## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. It is done by a
**fresh-context session**: an agent session that did not implement any of the milestone's work, so that nothing is
accepted on the word of the session that built it. It implements nothing. It regenerates the gate report from a
clean checkout, reads every receipt, checks that every gate of the milestone has a measured value and a caught
negative control, records a disposition for each, and records every kill decision due as an ADR (architecture
decision record).

M2 is the milestone that puts the batched engine under the CPU studio: one model on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal,
many designs in one batch, measured against the incumbent on the same cards. Its exit line is not "the engine is
fast"; it is that **the kill decisions for the kernel toolchain, contact, Metal and mixed batches are recorded as
ADRs** (KILL-KIR, KILL-CONTACT, KILL-METAL, KILL-PHYLUM, KILL-BUCKET), whichever way each went. This review is
therefore mostly a decision review: fourteen kill gates of the compiled register and four of later subsections are
due, and each needs measured values set beside a trigger that was registered before the measurement.

## Scope

In: the M2 exit record; dispositions for every gate part due; the eighteen kill decisions; the re-read of
KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER on our own profile; the variant in force (with or without devices); risks and open questions
due; the sections of the checks attached to the exit runner; the state of the upstream work items dated M2. Out: any implementation or repair (a defect found opens a bead and the review stays open); the measurements
themselves (their beads); the shared procedure (N40-05) and the query (N40-04), which this bead uses.

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (the shared protocol, in brief).** (1) Start a session with no context of M2; refuse if its identity
appears as implementer of any due row. (2) Clone at the exit candidate commit into an empty directory and build on
the pinned toolchain. (3) Run every M2 demonstration manifest through `frob --json` and through `frob-mcp`.
(4) Re-run the recurring gates and the full negative-control sweep at this commit. (5) Regenerate the gate report
and the README status table; both must equal the committed files. (6) List kill decisions with and without ADRs,
shared close reasons, controls that never failed, and every `not-run`, `blocked` or `fail` part. (7) Read each
receipt and record a disposition per part, from the six of the protocol: `accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill:
<fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`, `no-claim` (the `reality:` line of GATE-GROUND-01 with no dataset),
`not-run: <variant and reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Check each kill decision against its registered trigger
and amendments. (9) Read the risk register: the 41 rows readable by M2 (21 first read at M0, 9 at M1, and the 11
first readable here: RISK-05, -06, -09, -11, -12, -30, -39, -40, -45, -46, -58), each with a reading and, where
its earliest signal fired, one of the three outcomes (a kill gate decided, an ADR that changes a mechanism or a
scope, a written reason why neither applies). (10) Record open questions answered or defaulted (section 7), among
them OPEN-NUM-4 (N35-29: the ill-conditioning limit and
the `stiff` refinement count frozen for GATE-SPARK-18), and read the numeric hazard register's M2 rows (N35-20).
(11) Count active Moonshot items (at most four).
(12) Demonstrate the exit line, apply the stop rules, write `docs/delivery/exits/M2/EXIT.md`.

This is the exit protocol of N40-05 applied to the M2 data: the gate query is N40-04's, the runner is
`scripts/exit/review.sh M2`, and the exit-time checks that other beads attach to the runner
(`scripts/exit/checks.toml`) are run by it, each landing as a section of the exit record and each of its blocks
making the verdict `not exited`. This bead writes no second query and no second runner. The attached checks read
at M2, with their owners: `firewall` (N40-07; the foundations of M2 are FrankenSim as parity oracle, asupersync
for device jobs, `cudarc`, FrankenTorch, FrankenSymPy and franken_numpy, each admitted by its own record);
`tracker-check` (N41-16: no cycle, no orphan, every gate carried, every bead with a test plan); `principles`
(N00-21: a principle whose first-enforcement milestone is M2 or earlier must be enforced and proven by a caught
control, and an unproven one blocks; a principle not yet enforced blocks only from M3); `risk-review`,
`risks-complete` and `risk-plants` (N41-01, N41-32; with the M2 rows of N41-34 and N41-38, 39 primary plants are
runnable and must be `caught`); `premortem` (N41-PM); `open-complete` and `owner-calendar` (N41-03, N40-35);
`visibility-guard` (N41-05; `unverified` is a warning); `moonshot-cap` (N10a-02); the decision-record sweep
(NAB-22: `decision-sweep check --exit M2` on the M2 sweep record, which NAB-25 writes at the exit candidate
commit before this review); the bead-lifecycle check (N41-17) and the done report (N41-26).

  clones the commit that carries the recording. A failing run is recorded `fail` and blocks like any part; the
  review repairs nothing.
- GATE-FID-01 may be re-baselined once at M2 with a written rationale and is then frozen: the review checks that at
  most one re-baseline exists and that the frozen envelope's identity is in the ledger.
- GATE-FID-03: where referee coverage is below 90% for a reference class, an adjudicator is named, or claims are
  restricted to the classes the referee covers; BET-34 is then recorded as falsified for that class.

  GATE-CHRON-01, GATE-AUTOPSY-01, the digest part of GATE-TETHER-09) cite one receipt per backend (N40-08).
- Every Performance figure carries its disclosure block and the incumbent figure measured on the same card; a
  figure without a complete block is refused.
- A device result is admissible as evidence only if OPEN-SPARK-1 is ratified; otherwise it is labelled exploratory.

**6. The variant.** If OPEN-SPARK-1 is not ratified, if INT-CUDA-01 and INT-FT-01 both failed, or if no device
family holds a controlled determinism class, the exit is taken in the no-GPU variant (N40-33): everything lands on
`batch-cpu`; device gates are recorded `not run` with the reason; NS-10 and the device halves of NS-02 and NS-14
are recorded as not demonstrated; any device result that exists is labelled exploratory. The kill decisions are
still recorded, by the rule of N40-33: a kill gate whose record reads only parts that ran on CPU tiers is
decided as usual; one that reads only device parts is `deferred` by an ADR that names M3 and cites the variant
record; one with a clause the CPU parts decide and a clause that needs a device is `fired` if the CPU clause is
met and `deferred` otherwise; none is `not-fired` while a part it reads was not run. The exit line is then met
by those ADRs, the deferred ones included, and the exit record lists each deferred decision under "variants in
force". Outside the variant a `deferred` outcome for one of the five gates of the exit line does not meet the
line (DESIGN: the line asks for the decisions, whichever way they went, and five deferrals would meet it
vacuously); the review then records `not exited`, naming the gate. The other kill gates due may be deferred by
ADR under the rule of the kill-gate record (a second repair cycle is a deferral to a named later exit).

**7. Open questions due, with the default each takes if unanswered.** The list is the register's, not this
bead's: `due(M2)` of the open-question register (N41-03) returns every row whose deadline is M2 or earlier, and a

them, and OPEN-PROG-2 (`M2, before any sealed world family exists`); that is 41 questions, since the two aliases
resolve to OPEN-PROG-2 and move with it. Most are decided by a measurement of M2 in
their own decision beads, which is why those beads are prerequisites of this review; for each the review checks
that the row reads `answered` with its record, or `defaulted` by ADR where the measurement did not decide. The
owner's rows among them: OPEN-SPARK-1, listed a second time because this is the exit at which its default
bites (its deadline was M0; default: GPU results exploratory; the no-GPU variant). OPEN-SPARK-5 (no Metal binding
of our own; the device layer stays inside the gateway's limits and accepts KILL-METAL's fallback). OPEN-PHYLUM-3
(the one-device, one-hour rule; KILL-PHYLUM decided on the pilot alone). OPEN-PROG-2, with OPEN-TENFOLD-1 and -2
(a pinned incumbent CPU engine as adjudicator, run by a fresh-context session; ratios labelled
`self-adjudicated`; the record of N41-06 must exist before any sealed world family does). OPEN-ARENA-3 (the
owner alone holds `qual:admin`; one sealed family per task family, sized for 20 requests at 256 clusters).
OPEN-CHRON-4 (retention windows for failure captures and the default store budget on each machine: 30 days and
64 GB). OPEN-SAFE-2 (policy weights trained in a private or research-only scene inherit its mark and licence
set). OPEN-TENFOLD-3, -6 and -8 (internal advocate sessions with 40 device-hours per stratum and the label
`rival: internally advocated`; the agent the project is developed with, pinned, one agent only; the thresholds
as written). OPEN-TENFOLD-4 (nothing above the default compute class runs): it was put to the owner one
milestone earlier than the others because its default is the one that can hold a milestone. With it in force

exit if no stratum has one (the stratum rule of N41-15, named at that exit by the calendar's check with the
reason `open-default-blocks-exit`); the exit record prints that consequence beside the default. Not due here:
the retention of job logs, idempotency keys and retired confirmation clusters is OPEN-SAFE-4, with deadline M3
(default: logs and keys 30 days; retired clusters sealed indefinitely). The exit record also prints the
questions asked now for M3. Each default taken is recorded as an ADR with the capability it removed.

**9. Beads on one branch of a decision, and upstream work.** Every bead of M2 is a prerequisite of this review

gives for the untaken branch, citing the decision's ADR; the review checks the close reason against the ADR.
The upstream work items dated M2 (work in a sibling's repository) are the exception to the ancestor rule: no
milestone waits on a sibling change, each keeps its interim posture until its switch-over has run, and this
review does not depend on them. It records the state of each from `registries/upstream.toml` (adopted; landed
with the switch-over pending; open with its interim posture in force) and blocks on none.

## Considerations

- With M3, this is the largest exit by convergence parts (81). Budget the control sweep; shard it, never sample
  it.
- If the head-to-head could only run as a pilot (default of OPEN-PHYLUM-3), KILL-PHYLUM's ADR says so and the
  decision is recorded on the pilot.
- A review that cannot obtain device time records the device gates as `not run` with that reason; it does not
  accept implementer-supplied figures.
- Every bead of M2 that is not an upstream work item is an ancestor of this review (the plan's graph rule,
  checked by `tracker-check`), which is why its dependency list is long: it names each M2 bead that no other M2
  bead waits for, the decision-record sweep among them. It also names BET-12, the owner's M1 decision taken from
  BD-147's schedule gate: that bead has the M1 review among its ancestors, so it cannot precede BD-147 and is held
  to be an ancestor of this review instead (N41-16 check 3(b)). A bead discovered during M2 is added to it. The twenty
  upstream items dated M2 are in `related`, with the beads that follow one of them (the later stages NFp1-51 and
  NFp1-52; the switch-overs NFp2-01, NFp3-11 and NFp3-12): their own text says no milestone waits on them, and the tracker check's
  orphan rule exempts them for the same reason (N41-16 check 3(b)).
- A defect the review finds is a new bead of M2, added to this list; the review implements nothing and stays
  open until that bead closes (the protocol's rule).
- The record of a deferred kill decision is not a pass and not a waiver. It moves one decision to M3 by ADR and
  leaves the capability partial; the M3 review finds it due.
