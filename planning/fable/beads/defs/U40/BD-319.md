## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught negative control, records a
disposition for each, and records every kill decision due as an ADR. It implements nothing.

M3 lands places, tasks, adversarial worlds, autopsy and blinded qualification together, because the **partial
ten-fold gate** needs all of them, and then holds that gate: the first measured comparison of design-loop time
against the strongest composed incumbent, on the `legged-quad` and `wheeled` strata (and `envelope` and `sensing`
where ready), with three arms. The exit line is that **GATE-TENFOLD-06 is recorded, whatever the ratio**. A low
ratio is a result that redirects M4's funding; an unrecorded gate is a milestone that has not exited. This review
also carries the heaviest but one decision load of the program: 28 kill gates of the compiled register and 6 of
later subsections.

## Scope

In: the M3 exit record; dispositions for every part due; 34 kill decisions; the program-gate records of the
ten-fold test due at M3 and the application of its outcome rules; risks and open questions due; the sections of
the checks attached to the exit runner; the Augur slices due at M3; the state of the upstream work items dated M3.
Out: any implementation or repair (a defect found opens a bead of M3 and the review stays open); the measurements
(their beads); the decision-record sweep itself (NAB-26, which this review reads); the shared procedure (N40-05)
and query (N40-04), which this bead uses.

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (the shared protocol, in brief).** (1) Start a session with no context of M3; refuse if its
identity appears as implementer of any due row. (2) Clone at the exit candidate commit into an empty directory
and build on the pinned toolchain. (3) Run every M3 demonstration manifest through `frob --json` and through
`frob-mcp`. (4) Re-run the recurring gates and the full negative-control sweep at this commit. (5) Regenerate the
gate report and the README status table; both must equal the committed files. (6) List kill decisions with and
without ADRs, shared close reasons, controls that never failed, and every `not-run`, `blocked` or `fail` part.
(7) Read each receipt and record a disposition per part, from the six of the protocol: `accepted-pass`,
`accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`, `no-claim` (the `reality:` line of GATE-GROUND-01 with no
dataset), `not-run: <variant and reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Check each kill decision against its
registered trigger and amendments. (9) Read the risk register: the 56 rows readable by M3 (21 first read at M0, 9
at M1, 11 at M2, and the 15 first readable here: RISK-14, -15, -19, -26, -27, -34, -37, -41, -44, -47, -49, -50,
-51, -52, -59), each with a reading and, where its earliest signal fired, one of the three outcomes (a kill gate
decided, an ADR that changes a mechanism or a scope, a written reason why neither applies). (10) Record open
questions answered or defaulted (section 7). (11) Count active Moonshot items (at most four). (12) Demonstrate the
exit line, apply the stop rules, write `docs/delivery/exits/M3/EXIT.md`.

This is the exit protocol of N40-05 applied to the M3 data: the gate query is N40-04's, the runner is
`scripts/exit/review.sh M3`, and the exit-time checks that other beads attach to the runner
(`scripts/exit/checks.toml`) are run by it, each landing as a section of the exit record and each of its blocks
making the verdict `not exited`. This bead writes no second query and no second runner. The attached checks read
at M3, with their owners: `firewall` (N40-07; the foundations of M3 are FrankenSim's optimizers, racing,
statistics and spectral groups, asupersync for the daemon, FrankenSQLite for the qualification ledger and
fastmcp_rust for the full MCP surface, each admitted by its own record at today's pin; franken_numpy for the
Gymnasium-compatible adapter, frankentui, franken_threed and the drone-reconstruction schemas only if admitted);
`tracker-check` (N41-16: no cycle, no orphan, every gate carried, every bead with a test plan); `principles`
(N00-21: from M3 on, a principle not yet enforced blocks as well as one enforced without a caught control);
`risk-review`, `risks-complete` and `risk-plants` (N41-01, N41-32; with the M3 rows of N41-35, 54 of the 59
primary plants are runnable and must be `caught`, 5 are declared, and nine further observations run);
`premortem` (N41-PM); `open-complete` and `owner-calendar` (N41-03, N40-35); `suite-02-confirm` (N41-14);
`visibility-guard` (N41-05; `unverified` is a warning); `moonshot-cap` (N10a-02); the decision-record sweep (NAB-22:

## Considerations

- If the owner has not approved the compute for the partial gate (OPEN-TENFOLD-4 at its default), nothing above
  the default class runs: a partial gate whose every stratum is `not run` is no outcome, M3 does not exit, and the
  review records that state and stays open; CPU strata recorded with the device strata `not run`, each with its
  reason, are a recordable outcome. The review never substitutes a smaller experiment.
- 34 ADRs is a large reading load; the kill ledger beads (N40-25, N40-26, N40-28) and the cross-check N40-30 make
  it a comparison and not a search.
- A class withdrawn here stays withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh sealed families.
- Every bead of M3 that is not an upstream work item is an ancestor of this review (the plan's graph rule,
  checked by `tracker-check`), which is why its dependency list is long: it names each M3 bead that no other M3
  bead waits for, the decision-record sweep NAB-26 among them. A bead discovered during M3 is added to it. The
  three upstream items dated M3 are in `related`: their own text says no milestone waits on them.
- A defect the review finds is a new bead of M3, added to this list; the review implements nothing and stays open
  until that bead closes (the protocol's rule).
