## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught negative control, records a
disposition for each, and records every kill decision due as an ADR. It implements nothing.

M3 lands places, tasks, adversarial worlds, autopsy and blinded qualification together, because the **partial
ten-fold gate** needs all of them, and then holds that gate: the first measured comparison of design-loop time
against the strongest composed incumbent, on the `legged-quad` and `wheeled` strata (and `envelope` and `sensing`
where ready), with three arms. The exit line is that **GATE-TENFOLD-06 is recorded, whatever the ratio**. A low
ratio is a result that redirects M4's funding; an unrecorded gate is a milestone that has not exited. This review
also carries the heaviest but one decision load of the program: 28 kill gates of the compiled register and 6 of
later subsections.

## Scope

In: the M3 exit record; dispositions for every part due; 34 kill decisions; the program-gate records of the
ten-fold test due at M3 and the application of its outcome rules; risks and open questions due. Out: any repair;
the measurements; the shared procedure (N40-05) and query (N40-04).

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief).** (1) A session with no context of M3, whose identity is not an implementer of any due
row. (2) Clean clone at the exit candidate commit; build on the pinned toolchain. (3) Every M3 demonstration
manifest through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`. (4) Recurring gates and the full negative-control sweep at this
commit. (5) Regenerate the gate report and README table; both equal the committed files. (6) List kill decisions
with and without ADRs, shared close reasons, never-failed controls, and `not-run`, `blocked`, `fail` parts.
(7) A disposition per part from its receipt: `accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`,
`not-run: <reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Each kill decision against its registered trigger and amendments.
(9) The risk register: per risk, whether its earliest signal fired, and the outcome. (10) Open questions answered
or defaulted. (11) At most four active Moonshot items. (12) Demonstrate the exit line, apply the stop rules, write
`docs/delivery/exits/M3/EXIT.md`.

**3. Program gates of the ten-fold test due here.** Before the sealed suite is opened: GATE-TENFOLD-03 (the harness

**4. Outcome rules the review verifies were applied, per confirmatory cell.** `withdrawn` (R̂ below 3, or a

compute): KILL-TENFOLD-TIE; the layer ships over that engine for the stratum. `descriptive`: a cell whose
pre-analysis could not reach its verdicts (KILL-TENFOLD-REACH). `disqualified`: one false certificate
(KILL-TENFOLD-CERT) or a leak from sealed worlds (KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK). Also decided on the partial gate's record:
KILL-AUGUR-TIE and KILL-FAMILYCERT on the `envelope` and `sensing` strata; KILL-GYMADAPT; the incumbent arm that
BET-10 names; and whether the `train` phase still holds a removable share of at least 10% per class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m3::`: `due_counts` (6, 77, 28; 21 late parts; 6 late kill decisions; seven
ten-fold parts: -03, -04, -05, -06, -10, -11, -13); `exit_line_needs_tenfold_06` (no record → `not-ready` even with
every other part passed); `sealing_order_checked` (a ledger in which the suite opening precedes the freeze manifest
→ rejected); `standing_gates_start_here` (KILL-AUGUR-REUSE, KILL-TENFOLD-CERT, KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK due from M3 on).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m3.sh` wraps `scripts/exit/review.sh M3`: the six demonstrations through CLI and MCP; the
partial-gate numbers recomputed by the standalone scorer from ledgers and receipts and compared bit for bit with
the recorded ones; the control sweep; ADR listing. Logs: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, each
recomputed ratio and interval beside the recorded one, α_i spent per qualification submission, digests of replayed
`.fmcw` worlds, and on failure the first differing value and artifact paths; the summary names the gates touched.
No sealed content is logged before the reveal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the M3 column of the load table (6, 77, 28); 10.081, 9.677, 1.8215, 0.78568; 32, 46, 49, 182.
Controls the review must catch: a canary value from a sealed world in an arm's transcript; a freeze manifest dated
after the suite opening; a pilot printed as a whole gate; a kill ADR that cites no receipt; a self-certified PASS.

## Considerations

- If the owner has not approved the compute for the partial gate, it is `not run` and M3 does not exit; the
  review records that state and stays open. It does not substitute a smaller experiment.
- 34 ADRs is a large reading load; the kill ledger beads (N40-25, N40-26, N40-28) and the cross-check N40-30 make
  it a comparison and not a search.
- A class withdrawn here stays withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh sealed families.
