## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught negative control, records a
disposition for each, and records every kill decision due as an ADR. It implements nothing.

M4 builds the two levers that carry the thesis: deciding many designs per turn, and training few of them. The
second lever rests on two measurable things. **Rank agreement**: whether a cheap evaluation rung orders designs the
way trained specialists do (Kendall τ on the top band, lower 95% bound; at least 0.7 lets a rung select, at least
0.5 lets it discard the bottom half, below 0.5 or with no record it does neither). **Family policies**: whether one
policy conditioned on the design walks unseen designs of its class with little or no training. The exit line is
that **rank agreement is measured and the family-policy bets are decided**, not that either went well; M4 has a
complete variant with no family policy. This review carries the heaviest decision load of the program: 30 kill
gates of the compiled register and 9 of later subsections.

## Scope

In: the M4 exit record; dispositions for every part due; 39 kill decisions; the variant in force; the
re-stratification consequence for M7 if both levers fell; risks and open questions due. Out: any repair; the
measurements; the shared procedure (N40-05) and query (N40-04).

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief).** (1) A session with no context of M4, whose identity is not an implementer of any due
row. (2) Clean clone at the exit candidate commit; build on the pinned toolchain. (3) Every M4 demonstration
manifest through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`. (4) Recurring gates and the full negative-control sweep. (5)
Regenerate the gate report and README table; both equal the committed files. (6) List kill decisions with and
without ADRs, shared close reasons, never-failed controls, and `not-run`, `blocked`, `fail` parts. (7) A
disposition per part: `accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`, `not-run: <variant
and reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Each kill decision against its registered trigger and amendments. (9) The
risk register read, with outcomes for fired signals. (10) Open questions answered or defaulted. (11) At most four
active Moonshot items. (12) Demonstrate the exit line, apply the stop rules, write
`docs/delivery/exits/M4/EXIT.md`.

GATE-REFLEX-06, -07, -08; GATE-REFLEX-01 for the tracked and hybrid archetypes; GATE-GAIT-01, -02, -03;

**3. Checks particular to M4.**

- *Rank agreement is a record, whatever τ is.* GATE-CORTEX-12 passes when τ, top-10 recall and intervals are
  recorded per class and task family and a rung without a record is refused. The review checks that the thresholds
  0.7 and 0.5 were fixed before the data (the pre-registration's commit precedes the first training receipt), that
  each class's mode (`selective`, `discard-only`, `uninformative`) is the one the thresholds prescribe, and that an
  unmeasured stratum neither selects nor discards.
- *The family-policy bets are decided.* KILL-FAMILY and KILL-INHERIT each have an outcome. If the pre-funding pilot
  met its falsifier or OPEN-CORTEX-1 stayed at its default, the outcome is `not-reached` or `fired`, GATE-CORTEX-11
  is recorded as failed or `not run`, BET-15 as falsified or untested, NS-19 as not demonstrated, and the lost
  factor is recorded for the ten-fold arithmetic (variant bead N40-34).
- *Pilots before funding.* For every Frontier work package of M4 (family lanes, specialization, the paired design
  gradient, alternative learning algorithms, the fleet scheduler) a pilot record with a falsifier, a computed
  ceiling, a fallback and a result exists and predates the funding (GATE-GAUNT-08).
- *The frozen 30-amendment campaign* is one campaign read by GATE-ATEL-11 and GATE-AUGUR-12; the model-form check
  is one fixture read by GATE-ATEL-10 (with GATE-FID-05 and GATE-CORTEX-15 from M3).
- *Ceiling before speed* (GATE-AUGUR-14, GATE-TENFOLD-09): every registered speed comparison records measured f, r
  and h on the strong baseline and is refused when 1/(1 − f + f/r + h) is below the claimed ratio.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every gate of M4 has a recorded value and a caught negative control (the seed's done-condition), or a record
      under the no-family-policy variant with its reason.
- [ ] Rank agreement is measured (GATE-REFLEX-04, GATE-CORTEX-12, GATE-ATEL-07 have records per class) and the
      family-policy bets are decided (KILL-FAMILY and KILL-INHERIT have ADRs) (the seed's done-condition).
- [ ] All 39 kill decisions have an ADR with an outcome; fired gates print under their fallbacks' names.
- [ ] Every Frontier work package funded in M4 has a pilot record that predates its funding.
- [ ] If both levers fell, the recomputed ceilings and the M7 re-stratification bead exist.
- [ ] The gate report regenerated from a clean checkout equals the committed one and is `exit-ready`;
      `docs/delivery/exits/M4/EXIT.md` is written by the fresh-context session.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m4::`: `due_counts` (0, 49, 30; 16 late parts; 9 late kill decisions);
`exit_line_needs_rank_records` (remove the record of one class of GATE-ATEL-07 → `not-ready`);
`exit_line_needs_family_decisions` (KILL-FAMILY with no outcome → `not-ready`; with `not-reached` and the variant
record → ready); `rank_mode_from_thresholds` (lower bound 0.72 → `selective`; 0.55 → `discard-only`; 0.41 →
`uninformative`; no record → `uninformative`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m4.sh` wraps `scripts/exit/review.sh M4`: the seven demonstrations through CLI and MCP on `trj`;
`frob eval rank-agreement --json` re-read per class with τ and its bounds compared with the register rows; the
control sweep; the pilot-before-funding listing; ADR listing. Logs: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block,
τ with its bootstrap bounds beside 0.7 and 0.5, designs decided per hour, GPU-seconds per accepted decision, and
on failure the first differing value with artifact paths; the summary names the gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the M4 column of the load table (0, 49, 30); mode assignment at the two thresholds. Controls the
review must catch: thresholds for τ committed after the first data; a rung that discards with no rank record; a
family-policy return reported with no in-distribution check; a kill ADR recorded by an implementer; a work package
funded before its pilot.

## Considerations

- The variant with no family policy is a legitimate exit, not a failure to be hidden; the README then says "no
  family policy" under the fallback's name.
- KILL-ALTALGO is decided per algorithm and only when one is proposed for funding; algorithms never proposed are
  `not-reached`.
- M5 may be in progress during this review; nothing of M5 is read here.
