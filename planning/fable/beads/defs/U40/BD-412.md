## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught negative control, records a
disposition for each, and checks that every kill decision due is on record as an ADR written by its decision
bead. It implements nothing.

M5 strengthens what can be trusted and what can be built: certificates and Lean capsules, calibration against real
measurements, real-place ingestion, and a build packet. None of it is on the critical path, and every lane has a
fallback that ships under its own name. The exit line is the weakest of the nine by design: **the Reality lane has
a record or a recorded `no-claim`**. The rule this review must hold against temptation: a gate that needs a
physical board, a bench measurement or a building scan is an import lane whose resting state is `no-claim`;
`no-claim` is a record, a pass by default is not.

## Scope

In: the M5 exit record; dispositions for every part due; 18 kill decisions; the state of the Reality lane; the
lane-by-lane stop rules; risks and open questions due. Out: any repair; the measurements; the shared procedure
(N40-05) and query (N40-04).

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief).** (1) A session with no context of M5, whose identity is not an implementer of any due
row. (2) Clean clone at the exit candidate commit; build on the pinned toolchain (the Lean lane on a Linux host).
(3) Every M5 demonstration manifest through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`. (4) Recurring gates and the full
negative-control sweep; the attached checks of `scripts/exit/checks.toml` (N40-05), among them the
decision-record sweep `decision-sweep check --exit M5` on the record NAB-28 made at this candidate commit, and the
tracker check (N41-16), whose check 3(b) must list no M5 bead that is not an ancestor of this review. (5) Regenerate the gate report and README table; both equal the committed files. (6) List
kill decisions with and without ADRs, shared close reasons, never-failed controls, and `not-run`, `blocked`,
`fail`, `no-claim` parts. (7) A disposition per part: `accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill: <fallback>`,
`recorded-fail: blocks`, `no-claim`, `not-run: <reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Each kill decision against its
registered trigger and amendments. (9) The risk register read. (10) Open questions answered or defaulted. (11) At
most four active Moonshot items. (12) Demonstrate the exit line, apply the stop rules, write
`docs/delivery/exits/M5/EXIT.md`.

**2. Gate parts due (identifiers; criteria in brief are in EPIC-M5 and the generated report).** Admission:
INT-FLN-01, -02 (the Lean checker, on a Linux host); INT-FSIM-15 (the structure group, admitted now at today's
pin); INT-FDGR-01 for scan import (a sibling gate; it does not block). Lemma: GATE-LEMMA-01, -02, -05;
GATE-QUAL-03. Kinesis and Traction: GATE-TRAC-09 on `batch-cpu` (its GPU rows are M6). Habitat: GATE-HAB-03.
Ground: GATE-GROUND-02 to -09. Foundry: GATE-FOUNDRY-01 to -10. Formats: GATE-WS-02 complete for all thirteen.
Four of these are tagged M4 by their chapters and may already have passed there: GATE-FOUNDRY-01 to -03 and
GATE-GROUND-03; an early pass satisfies the part. Later subsections: GATE-GROUND-11; GATE-FOUNDRY-13, -14.
Recurring: GATE-GROUND-01, GATE-SAFE-01, GATE-TENFOLD-09, GATE-GAUNT-01, -02, -03, -05, -10; GATE-GAUNT-08 at
each funding decision (every package of M5 holds Frontier work: none of WP-31, WP-32, WP-68 and WP-70 to WP-72 is
funded without a pilot record in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` that has a falsifier, a computed
ceiling, a fallback and a result; the review reads `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable
--all --json`, the pilot register's example target of N38a-09). Tied to no milestone and never blocking: GATE-GROUND-10. Counts to expect: 4 admission parts, 25 convergence parts, 3 late parts.

**3. The Reality lane: what the review records.**

- GATE-GROUND-01 (recurring): every report prints its `reality:` line; with no dataset it reads `no-claim`; the
  negative battery of 50 planted misuses is refused in full.
- GATE-GROUND-10: if a real dataset was admitted (OPEN-GAUNT-1 answered), the first real cell has a frozen
  prediction scored on sealed data and a band issued, and the review checks the freeze-before-read order from the
  ledger. If no dataset was admitted, the review records `no-claim` as the state of the lane, checks that the
  second-simulator stand-in is labelled `synthetic` everywhere, and checks that no report or README line reads as a
  Reality claim.
- GATE-HAB-03: one real place ingested end to end, privately, with its scale label and unknown regions respected
  and at least one probe request emitted; a place for which no capture exists is `no-claim`, not a pass.
- The Ground verbs were exercised on the synthetic rehearsal and every such result carries `synthetic`.

**4. Kill decisions to check, each an ADR with measured values beside the trigger.** Each ADR is
`docs/adr/ADR-<gate>-M5.md` in N40-03's format, written by its decision bead (KILL-LEMMA N12-31, KILL-REFLECT
N12-32, KILL-SKEL-PORT N14a-24, KILL-TREEOP N16a-23, KILL-VARIATIONAL N16a-22, KILL-HARDCONTACT N18a-23,
KILL-DRESSING N19a-41, KILL-INGEST N19c-08, KILL-GROUND, -BAND, -CLOCK, -ASBUILT and -KIT N30b-17 to N30b-21,
KILL-TOPOPT, -LOADS, -FAB, -TOLERANCE and -PACKET N31b-15 to N31b-19); the review writes none. It checks each
against its registered trigger and dated amendments (procedure step 8), against the `decision` of its row in
`registries/gates.toml` (outcome `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or `deferred`), and against the session rule
(the recording session implemented none of the mechanism); a kill gate due with no ADR, or an ADR that disagrees

beyond the rehearsal waits if no dataset has been admitted.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every gate of M5 has a recorded value and a caught negative control (the seed's done-condition), a kill
      decision with its fallback adopted, or, for a Reality gate only, a recorded `no-claim`.
- [ ] The Reality lane has a record or a recorded `no-claim` (the seed's done-condition): GATE-GROUND-10's row
      reads `pass` with a sealed-data score and a band, or `no-claim`; it never reads `not-run` at this exit.
- [ ] All 18 kill decisions have an ADR `docs/adr/ADR-<gate>-M5.md` with an outcome that equals its row's
      `decision` in `registries/gates.toml`; fired gates print under their fallbacks' names.
- [ ] Every M5 bead other than epics is an ancestor of this review (N41-16 check 3(b)), except the upstream work
      items and switch-over beads that state no milestone waits on them (Considerations), each of which is listed
      in the exit record with its `registries/upstream.toml` status: the tracker check at the candidate commit
      lists no M5 `tracker-orphan`.
- [ ] GATE-GAUNT-08 is read at each M5 funding decision: every Frontier package of M5 has its pilot records with a
      falsifier, a computed ceiling, a fallback and a result.
- [ ] No private-scene data appears in any log, kit or export produced by the review.
- [ ] The count of consumed certificates that changed a decision is recorded.
- [ ] The Bets section of the exit report (N40-31) lists the three bets due at M5 (BET-11, BET-28, BET-29) with
      their deciders' values and exit states.
- [ ] The gate report regenerated from a clean checkout equals the committed one and is `exit-ready`;
      `docs/delivery/exits/M5/EXIT.md` is written by the fresh-context session.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m5::`: `due_counts` (4, 25, 18; 3 late parts; no late kill decision);
`reality_lane_record_or_no_claim` (GATE-GROUND-10 `not-run` → the exit line is unmet; `no-claim` → met; `pass` →
met); `no_claim_never_default` (GATE-HAB-03 with no capture receipt and status `pass` → rejected as
`evidence-unresolved`); `early_pass_counts` (GATE-FOUNDRY-01 passed during M4 satisfies its M5 part);
`bets_due_m5` (exactly three bets listed); `kill_adr_matches_row` (KILL-BAND with an ADR whose outcome is
`not-fired` while its row's `decision` reads `fired` → blocks naming the gate; a kill gate due with no ADR →
`not-ready`; all eighteen agreeing → ready); `scoped_entries_m5` (KILL-HARDCONTACT with an entry of scope
`backend:batch-cpu` and KILL-DRESSING with `item:assets` → ready; either row with no entry taken at M5 →
`not-ready`); `funding_without_pilot_blocks` (a Frontier bead of WP-70 with a first log line dated before its
pilot record → blocking finding; a package whose pilot's kill gate is still `to-register` is listed not
fundable by `pilots fundable`); `certificate_count_recorded` (an exit record with the count of consumed
certificates that changed a decision left empty → invalid naming the field; zero is a valid count and prints
the Lemma stop-rule reading); `upstream_items_listed_not_blocking` (a scratch upstream registry with FD-FSIM-23
`open` → listed in the record's upstream section with its interim posture, verdict unaffected; an ordinary M5
bead that is neither an ancestor of the review nor exempt → `not-ready` naming it). The rules behind these are
tested where they live (N40-03, N40-04, N40-05, N38a-09, N41-16); these tests run them on the M5 data.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m5.sh` wraps `scripts/exit/review.sh M5`: `frob prove kernel K-cay-step --json` and
`frob-verify` on a GPU-less host; the Ground rehearsal from `frob ground ingest <rehearsal dataset> --key
exm5-ingest --json` to `frob ground band --cell <cell> --metric <metric> --level 0.90 --json`; `frob foundry export
<design> --packet <dir> --key exm5-export --json` and the packet audit (every run and mutate verb with its own
key); a grep of every produced report for a `reality:` line. Logs through the
STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/exit_m5/<utc>.ndjson` (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` on every line):
command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the `reality:` value of each report, coverage beside 19/21, bundle digests, per kill gate due its ADR path beside its row's outcome and scope, the
upstream items listed with their status, and on
failure the first differing value with artifact paths; the `summary` line counts assertions passed and failed
and names the gates and kill gates touched and the verdict. Logs never contain private-scene data: the script asserts
that no path under a private scene's closure appears in its own output.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the M5 column of the load table (4, 25, 18). Controls the review must catch: a report with no
`reality:` line; a second-simulator result offered as Reality (refused); a `no-claim` lane printed as passed; a
private input to a replication kit; a build packet with a flag set to true.

## Considerations

- M5 may overlap M4 in time; this review still depends on the M4 review (seed dependency) so that exits are
  recorded in order.
- The Lean lane needs a Linux host; the review runs that part on `trj`.
- An admitted real dataset arrives with licence and provenance obligations; the review checks the licence join on
  every derived artifact.
- Upstream work items are read, not waited on (DESIGN, from the owner's ruling that every upstream work item
  keeps an interim posture and a switch-over rule, so that no milestone waits on a sibling change landing). The
  thirteen items dated M5 (FD-FLN-01, FD-FLN-02, FD-FSIM-23 to FD-FSIM-30, FD-FSYM-04, FD-FDGR-01, FD-FSS-01), the
  conditional item NFx-02 (register row FD-FSIM-41) and the two switch-over beads that wait on an item (NFp2-02
  on FD-FDGR-01, NFp2-03 on FD-FSS-01) each state that no milestone waits on them. These sixteen are therefore in
  `related`, not in the dependency list, where check 3(b) of the tracker check exempts them, as the M2, M3 and M4
  reviews do: the review lists each with its `registries/upstream.toml` status (`open` with the interim posture
  in force, `landed` with the switch-over pending, or `adopted`) and blocks on none. An entry of the tracker
  check's advisory list `tracker-exit-waits-on-upstream` (check 3(d): an item's bead still reached through
  another bead) is a warning in the exit record; the review records for each either the edge to move or why the
  in-repository bead on the path needs it.
