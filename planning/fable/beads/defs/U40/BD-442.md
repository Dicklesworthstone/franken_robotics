## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, records a disposition for each gate part, and records every kill decision due as an ADR. It
implements nothing.

M8 delivers the Moonshot items that would make the product unlike anything else, each behind a flag and each with a
stop rule; the plan works without every one of them. Seven epics: facility co-design and multi-robot handoff; skill
transport with learned residuals; continuation-based synthesis; firmware export; geometric-algebra tree policies at
scale; reachable-set tables for shields; the fused per-world step kernel. A Moonshot is funded only after a
paper-scale pilot with a falsifier, at most four are active at once, and none is on the critical path. The exit
line is that **every epic has its plan, its pilot result and its stop rule applied**, and every gate reached has a
recorded value and a caught negative control. This is the last exit of the program.

## Scope

In: the M8 exit record; per epic, the plan, the pilot record and the stop rule's application; dispositions for
every gate reached; the Moonshot cap; flag isolation against the release measured at M7. Out: any repair; the
pilots and measurements; the shared procedure (N40-05) and query (N40-04).

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief).** (1) A session with no context of M8, whose identity is not an implementer of any due
row. (2) Clean clone at the exit candidate commit; build with every M8 flag off, then with each funded epic's flag
on. (3) Every demonstration manifest of a funded epic through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`. (4) Recurring gates and
the full negative-control sweep. (5) Regenerate the gate report and README table; both equal the committed files.
(6) List kill decisions with and without ADRs, shared close reasons, never-failed controls, and `not-run`,
`blocked`, `fail` parts. (7) A disposition per part: `accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail:
blocks`, `not-run: <reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Each kill decision against its registered trigger and
amendments. (9) The risk register read. (10) Open questions answered or defaulted. (11) The Moonshot cap. (12)
Demonstrate the exit line, write `docs/delivery/exits/M8/EXIT.md`.

**5. Checks particular to M8.**

- *The cap* held at every commit of M6 and M8 together (the four slots are shared).
- *Pilots are pilots*: registered before running, with a falsifier and an outcome not known in advance.
- *Pretraining envelope*: any pretraining ran only under an owner-approved envelope of the pretraining class with
  its ceiling and kill rule written first; without approval the epic (tree policies in particular) was not funded.
- *Isolation*: with every M8 flag off, digests equal those of the release measured at M7.
- *No retroactive claims*: nothing delivered at M8 is cited in the tournament report.

**6. Open questions due, with defaults.** Funding per epic, and an envelope of the pretraining class for any
pretraining (default: the epic is not funded). OPEN-PROG-5 (the order under the cap; default: none before its pilot
passes, then the order of the table above).

**7. Stop rules to apply.** Per epic, by its stop rule. An epic that has not passed its pilot within its planned
weeks returns to the killed-idea register and frees its slot under the cap.

- [ ] The eight kill gates of the compiled register and the two late ones have outcomes.
- [ ] The Moonshot cap held at every commit; no pretraining ran without an approved envelope.
- [ ] Flags-off digests equal the M7 release reference.
- [ ] `docs/delivery/exits/M8/EXIT.md` is written by the fresh-context session.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m8::`: `due_counts` (0, 7, 8; 3 late parts; 2 late kill decisions);
`expired_epic_frees_slot` (an epic past its planned weeks with no passed pilot and still marked active →
violation); `unfunded_epic_does_not_block` (GATE-CORTEX-17 `not-run` with KILL-GATREE `not-reached` → ready);
`mega_decided_before_work_package` (work-package beads of the fused kernel started before KILL-MEGA's ADR →
violation).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m8.sh` wraps `scripts/exit/review.sh M8`: re-runs every pilot from its registered record; runs
each funded epic's acceptance fixture through `frob --json` with its flag on; runs the standard battery with all
flags off and compares digests with the M7 release reference. Logs: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block
with the tier label Moonshot, the falsifier's threshold beside the measured value, digests, and on failure the
first differing value with artifact paths; the summary names the gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the M8 column of the load table (0, 7, 8); seven epics; the fused-kernel pilot threshold 2.0.
Controls the review must catch: an unflagged lost continuation path; a root declared real by a tolerance on its
imaginary part; an epic funded with no pilot record; a fifth active Moonshot item; a flag that changes release
digests.

## Considerations

- This review depends on the M7 review (seed dependency), not on M6's: the two frontier milestones are independent
  apart from the shared cap.
- Several epics may never be funded; a milestone that exits with seven `not-reached` decisions has exited
  correctly.
- If the owner funds an epic after this exit is recorded, the exit record gains an addendum by the same procedure.
