## Background

Two kinds of overlap exist in the gate register, and both are deliberate. First, several gates record **one
experiment** from the side of the chapter that consumes it: the same fixture, run once, read by gates owned by
different subsystems, each compared with the threshold in its own row. Second, a reader meets **two numbers for what
looks like one quantity** under different gate identifiers; these are not conflicts, and the register changes none
of them, but each needs a stated reading so that nobody "fixes" one number to match the other.

This bead records both as data and checks them. Without it the first overlap wastes compute (six runs of a
1,000-episode co-tenancy experiment per milestone instead of one) or, worse, lets two copies of a fixture's
constants disagree; the second invites a silent relaxation (the looser of two bounds applied to both scopes).
DESIGN: one fixture identity per physical case; a fixture stated by two subsystems is one fixture with two rows.
Rejected: merging the gates. Each chapter owns its gate because each has a different consequence when it fails.

## Scope

In: the shared-fixture table, the related-threshold table, the check that a shared fixture has one identity and one
run per milestone, the check that no gate's threshold was edited toward its neighbour's. Out: the fixtures and the
mechanisms (their chapter beads; the batch-independence fixture and its record are BD-181's verb `spark.check
--invariance`, a dependency because this bead's end-to-end script and its M2-exit acceptance read that receipt); discrepancies that *were* conflicts and their resolutions (N40-09); for the
film squeeze times, the mechanism that makes the one bound structural: the single registration of the
known answer's tolerance with its owner, the gate rows that cite it by identifier, and the lint that rejects a
gate row restating a numeric tolerance for a cited known answer (NEG-12, at M3, which depends on this bead).
This bead's row for that quantity states the reading and its check confirms the number; it does not say where
the number is stored.

## Technical approach

The two record kinds below are top-level tables of `registries/gates.toml` added to the schema `frob.gates/1` by
this bead (additive: a file without them still loads; within them an unknown field fails the load, as for gate
rows).

**1. Shared fixtures (`[[shared_fixture]]` records in `registries/gates.toml`: `fixture`, `gates`, `owner`).**

| Fixture | Recorded by | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Batch independence of a world's digests (1,000 episodes solo and in random co-tenancies, capacities, slot assignments) | GATE-KIN-05, GATE-PHYLUM-02, GATE-SPARK-15, GATE-CHRON-01, GATE-AUTOPSY-01 and the digest part of GATE-TETHER-09 | One fixture, one run per milestone |
| The model-form check (planted cone and penetration exploits typed `model-conditional`; a genuine winner `model-robust`; cost at most 10% of the campaign) | GATE-FID-05, GATE-CORTEX-15, GATE-ATEL-10 | One fixture |
| The frozen 30-amendment campaign, over one revalidation engine | GATE-ATEL-11, GATE-AUGUR-12 | One campaign |
| The tier correction (type-I error at most α over 10⁴ null comparisons with optional stopping; a planted between-tier reversal not established) | GATE-FID-08, GATE-CHRON-09 | One fixture |

In each case the fixture is one, it is run once per milestone, and each gate is compared with the threshold in its
own row. Mechanically: each gate's part cites the same receipt hash; the exit query (N40-04) verifies equality of
the receipt and of the fixture identity across the group, and fails with `shared-fixture-diverged` otherwise.

**3. Checks (in `crates/frob-conformance/src/register/related.rs`).**

1. `one_identity`: every gate of a shared-fixture group names the same `fixture` identifier, and the fixture
   registry holds exactly one entry with that identifier.
2. `one_run`: at an exit, the parts of a group cite one receipt (or one receipt per tier where rows differ by tier).
3. `own_threshold`: each gate's verdict is computed against its own row's threshold; a group member whose
   threshold text equals a neighbour's when the seeded texts differ fails `threshold-borrowed`.
4. `numbers_frozen`: for each related-threshold row, the numbers named in "where it appears" are extracted from the
   gate rows and compared with the seeded values (1.25, 1.6, 0.70, 0.5, 2%, 10⁻³, 10⁻⁴, 0.7, 0.5, 1, 6, 1.5); a
   difference without an amendment (N40-03) fails. A gate's number is its *effective* threshold: where the
   row states the number, that number; where the row cites a known answer by identifier and states none, the
   tolerance registered with that known answer, resolved exactly as the gate runner resolves it. The second case
   is how GATE-TRAC-05 and GATE-HAB-06 read after NEG-12 (their rows then hold no number, by that bead's lint),
   and the check must keep finding 2% for both.
5. `scope_not_relaxed`: GATE-SINEW-02 and GATE-SPARK-05 keep 10⁻⁴ whatever GATE-CHRON-03 records.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Four shared-fixture records and ten related-threshold records load and equal the tables above.
- [ ] At the M2 exit the six gates of the batch-independence group cite one receipt per backend; a planted second
      run with different seeds for one member fails `shared-fixture-diverged`.
- [ ] Changing GATE-SINEW-02's bound to 10⁻³ without an amendment fails `numbers_frozen`.
- [ ] A fixture registered twice under two identifiers for one physical case fails `one_identity`.
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-03 and GATE-REFLEX-06 read τ ≥ 0.5 and no row reads the pair (0.6, 0.3).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance register::related::`.

- `shared_groups_are_four`: group sizes 6, 3, 2, 2.
- `related_rows_are_ten`: ten records; each names at least two identifiers.
- `one_identity_violation`: two fixture entries for the co-tenancy case → failure naming both.
- `one_run_violation`: two receipts within one tier for one group at one exit → failure.
- `numbers_frozen_values`: extracted set equals {1.25, 1.6, 0.70, 0.5, 0.02, 1e-3, 1e-4, 0.7, 1, 6, 1.5}.
- `effective_threshold_through_known_answer`: two gate rows that cite one known answer with a registered
  tolerance of 2% and state no number yield 0.02 for both; with the registration changed to 1% and no amendment
  the check fails for both rows at once.
- `scope_not_relaxed`: ledger bound 1e-3 recorded, port-balance bound still 1e-4.
- `screening_bound`: 1 − δ^(1/m) at δ = 0.05, m = 30 → 0.0950 (to four places), the figure "30 clean audited
  discards bound the rate at 9.5%".

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/shared_fixtures.sh`: runs the co-tenancy fixture once on `batch-cpu` through its owner's verb,
`frob spark check --invariance --backend batch-cpu --workload Q --episodes 1000 --cotenancies 100 --key shfx-run
--json` (BD-181, a dependency: the fixture and its `composition` record are that bead's; this bead only checks that
every member gate reads the one receipt; `spark.check` is a run verb, so each call carries its own key and a call
without one is `FROB-REF-805`), reads the stored record with `frob spark check --invariance --report --workload Q
--key shfx-report --json`, and asserts
that the six member gates resolve to that one receipt with `frob evidence trace <claim_id> --json`; then plants a divergent
receipt for one member and asserts the exit query reports `shared-fixture-diverged`; then edits GATE-SINEW-02's
bound to 10⁻³ with no amendment and asserts `numbers_frozen` fails naming the row. Logs through the STD-E2E helper
to `target/e2e-logs/shared_fixtures/<utc>.ndjson`: per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command lines,
exit codes, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism class, identities, seed), the
receipt digests compared, the extracted thresholds beside their seeded values as `metric` lines, and on failure the
first differing digest or number and the artifact paths; the `summary` line counts assertions and names the
thirteen member gates.

### Known answers and negative controls

N40-KA R-19 (the four groups), R-20 (the ten rows and their numbers). Negative controls N40-CX RC-25 (a member gate
re-run alone on different seeds), RC-26 (a tighter bound relaxed to its neighbour's), RC-27 (the pair (0.6, 0.3)
entered for a τ rule). Metamorphic: adding a gate to a group never changes the fixture's digest. Determinism: the
shared fixture is run in its declared class on each backend; the check itself is pure.

## Considerations

- Compute: the co-tenancy fixture is the costliest of the four (100 random co-tenancies per backend); one run per
  milestone is the saving this bead exists to secure.
- A gate that needs a superset of a shared fixture (GATE-PHYLUM-02 adds three bucket sizes) runs the superset once;
  the others read the subset from the same receipt.
- The strong-scaling pair is the likeliest to be "harmonized" by mistake: 1.25 is a tolerance that a gate holds;
  1.6 is a funding threshold that a kill gate reads.
