## Background

The register's subsystem tables were compiled at one point in time. Subsections added to the chapters afterwards
define further gates and kill gates. They are **binding under the same nine rules** as every other gate (N40-01);
they simply have no row in the compiled tables yet. Until the tables are regenerated, each such gate has its row in
the subsection that defines it, and that row governs its threshold; the milestone is the one its defining row
states; and a kill gate with no milestone of its own is decided at the milestone of the gate beside it.

Left as an index in prose, these gates are the ones most likely to be forgotten at an exit: they are in no
subsystem table, and one group of them is printed only in abridged form. This bead enters every one into
`registries/gates.toml` as a first-class row, so that the generated register and the exit reports include them and
the "until the tables are regenerated" state ends. Counted from the two indexes: 76 convergence gates (49 from the
first index, 27 more from the final-audit index) and 25 kill gates (22 and 3).

## Scope

In: the rows (identity, claim, part milestones, owner subsection, brief), the neighbour rule for kill gates, the
four amended rows, the two name collisions, the regenerated tables. Out: the full criteria (defining beads); the
triggers and fallbacks of the 25 late kill gates as ledger text (N40-29); the two related-threshold rows printed
beside the index (N40-08).

GATE-GAUNT-02, -05 and -10 recur at every exit; GATE-GAUNT-03 at every exit from M2; GATE-GAUNT-08 at each funding
decision; GATE-WS-08 at every release. GATE-GAUNT-11 has no single milestone: each of its rows lands at the
milestone of its owner section. GATE-TENFOLD-04 and -05 are restated by the final-audit index and are program rows
(N40-14), not new gates.

**2. Claims.** Each row takes the claim its defining row prints. A defining row that prints two (for example
"Known answer; Design comparison", "Performance; known answer (determinism)", "Known answer; Parity", "Known answer;
Fidelity") is entered with the claim of its governing part under rule 3 of N40-01, decided by this procedure: if the
row contains a comparison of two methods or arms with a threshold, `design-comparison`; else if it contains a figure
on named hardware with a threshold, `performance`; else if it compares with an oracle or another tier, `parity` or
`fidelity`; else `known-answer`. The reading entered is recorded per row with one sentence of reason, and the
defining bead's owner confirms it. Until confirmed the row is `blocked: claim reading`, which is not a pass.

**3. Kill gates and the neighbour rule.** The 25 late kill gates are entered with `mode = "exit"` (or
`pilot-before-funding` where the trigger is a pilot's falsifier) and `decided` equal to the milestone of the gate
beside them: KILL-DEVORACLE M1; KILL-ROWQUEUE, KILL-LOOPELIM, KILL-ANCESTOR, KILL-LIMBKERNEL M2; KILL-MPCRECORD,
KILL-FOIL, KILL-ITEMBANK, KILL-EXPODOMAIN, KILL-ENSEMBLE, KILL-REPLAYPACK M3; KILL-SKEL-CELL, KILL-VISIBILITY,
KILL-AUGUR-BRACKET, KILL-SYNTH-REGRESSOR, KILL-WEAKVAR, KILL-BUDGETCURVE, KILL-FREEPERIOD, KILL-STRIDESEQ M4;
KILL-COUPLING M4 for a violated transported bound and M8 for the tray clause; KILL-STEPADJOINT, KILL-FARFIELD,
KILL-SOILLIFT, KILL-SHORTGRAD M6; KILL-INTERACT M8. KILL-EXPODOMAIN's neighbour GATE-ARENA-14 has parts at M2 and
M3; the decision is entered at M3, the exit at which the gate's last part is due (DESIGN reading, recorded as an
ADR). GATE-TRAC-11 is the gate that KILL-RESPONSE (a kill gate of the compiled tables, decided at M4) reads.

**4. Four existing gates amended by the same subsections; they keep their rows.**

| Gate | Amended by | What the amendment adds |
|---|---|---|
| GATE-CORTEX-05 | "A threshold crossing is not attainment" | Every figure of the form "time to a target", including this gate's wall-clock to the return threshold, is an attainment claim that needs error control over every evaluation look that could have produced the crossing; a recorded score crossing alone is a descriptive event (its own gate is GATE-CORTEX-20) |
| GATE-TETHER-01, GATE-TETHER-13 | The diagnosis-and-restraint battery | The two gates score task completion; GATE-TETHER-15 complements them by scoring diagnosis and restraint on 30 wrong setups. Neither threshold changes |
| GATE-AUTOPSY-03 | The single-precision attribution lane | The planted factor ranks first in at least 90% of 200 failures in double precision, and in the single-precision lane for windows of at most 1 s (GATE-AUTOPSY-09 gates that lane) |

Each amendment is entered as an `[[amendment]]` record (N40-03) dated before any measurement of the gate.

**5. Two name collisions the loader guards.** KILL-EXPODOMAIN is the kill gate of the exposure-domain coupling of
random streams in Arena; it is not KILL-EXPOSURE, which is Sensorium's (camera exposure integration). GATE-ARENA-13
appears in both indexes; it is one row.

**6. Abridged statements.** The final-audit index prints its statements cut at a fixed width. The cut text is not
entered. The brief of each such row is taken from the defining row in full.

**7. Regeneration.** With these rows entered, `print_register` (N40-02) emits each late gate inside the table of its
subsystem and a section "Verification program" with GATE-GAUNT-01 to -11; the index sections disappear from the
printout.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 76 late convergence rows and 25 late kill rows are in the file; with the compiled tables the totals are 37
      admission, 355 convergence, 13 ten-fold, 1 schedule and 144 kill rows: 550 identifiers.
- [ ] Each late row's part milestones equal the index above; no late kill row has an empty `decided`.
- [ ] Every late row with a two-claim defining row carries exactly one claim and a recorded reason, or is `blocked`.
- [ ] The three amendments are present, dated before any measurement of their gates.
- [ ] A row `KILL-EXPODOMAIN` with Sensorium as owner, or a second `GATE-ARENA-13`, fails the load.
- [ ] No brief in the file ends in a cut word: every brief is byte-equal to its defining row's statement.
- [ ] The regenerated printout contains no "added after the register was compiled" section.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance register::late::`.

- `late_counts`: 76 and 25; totals 355 and 144; grand total 550.
- `late_parts_by_exit`: number of late convergence parts due at M0…M8 = 8, 12, 22, 21, 16, 3, 4, 0, 3 (89 parts
  over 76 gates; GATE-FID-09 and GATE-WS-07 have three parts each, nine gates have two).
- `late_kills_by_exit`: 0, 1, 4, 6, 9, 0, 4, 0, 2 (KILL-COUPLING counted at M4 and M8; 26 decisions for 25 gates).
- `neighbour_rule`: a late kill row with no `decided` and neighbour GATE-X at M4 → M4.
- `one_claim_or_blocked`: a row with claim text "Known answer; Design comparison" and no reading → `blocked`.
- `name_collisions`: KILL-EXPODOMAIN owner section is Arena's (24.22); KILL-EXPOSURE's is Sensorium's (20.21).
- `no_truncated_brief`: every late brief is found verbatim in its defining declaration.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/late_gates.sh`: regenerates the register printout and the nine exit reports; asserts that each late
gate appears in the report of each exit of its index row and in no other; plants (a) a late gate omitted from the
file and (b) a late kill gate with no decision point, and asserts the row-count test and the load fail. Logs
commands, exit codes, wall time, counts measured against expected per exit, the register digest and, on failure,
the first missing identifier.

### Known answers and negative controls

N40-KA R-22 (76, 25, 550), R-23 (parts by exit). Negative controls N40-CX RC-33 (a late gate missing from an exit
report), RC-34 (an abridged statement entered as the brief), RC-35 (KILL-EXPODOMAIN confused with KILL-EXPOSURE).
Determinism: pure function of the registry.

## Considerations

- The count 550 is a snapshot. The tooling recomputes it; a test that pins it must be updated by the amendment
  that adds a gate, which is the point: adding a gate is a visible act.
- Several late gates are due at M0 (GATE-WS-05, parts of GATE-WS-06, -07, -09, GATE-GAUNT-02, -05, -08, -10): the
  M0 exit review must see them, which is why this bead is an M0 bead.
- Fail-closed default for an unconfirmed claim reading: `blocked`, shown as open.
- A chapter unit that discovers a gate in its text that neither index lists adds it by amendment; the lint N40-09
  reports any gate declared by a crate and absent from the register.
