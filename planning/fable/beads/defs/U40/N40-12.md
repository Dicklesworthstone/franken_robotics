## Background

The program's central claim is a ratio: design-loop time with this system against the strongest composed incumbent
workflow. A ratio needs a denominator, and at planning time the denominator was *composed from published figures of
different stacks*, which is not a measurement. The **parity-baseline gate** exists so that the denominator is
measured before any mechanism is funded against it. It is a **program gate**: it decides whether the program
continues as planned and belongs to no subsystem. If it is not met, M0 does not exit, the thesis arithmetic stays
a HYPOTHESIS, and no work package is funded as a speed lever.

The gate has two halves. The **rival audit** pins every incumbent by revision, records what each one documents that
it can do, and freezes a warm configuration for each after tuning on reference tasks only. The **no-agent
baseline** times, on the Linux workstation `trj` (two RTX 4090 cards), the phases of the incumbent loop that need
no agent. These are the `trj` rows of the parity gate GATE-SPARK-21 that need no agent; this gate reads them into
the first phase ledger. Claim kind: Parity (published incumbent figures reproduced on our hardware). DESIGN
decision: incumbents are frozen *first*, so that no later tuning of ours is compared with an untuned rival.
Rejected: measuring the rival when our engine is ready, because every funding decision before that would rest on
the composed table.

## Scope

In: the row of GATE-TENFOLD-01 in the program-gate register; the completeness verdict `tenfold01`, which reads
the measured record and decides whether the gate may be entered as passed; the consequence when it is not met;
the M0 exit report's line for this gate; the rules on what the baseline may and may not change, with the M0
stop rule; the regeneration of the thesis table from the measured rows.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the rival audit and everything measured: the per-incumbent audit entries in `comparators.toml` (pin,
  licence, frozen warm configuration, capability table), the reading of the timed rows into the first phase
  ledger, the within-20%-or-explained check per published figure (implemented once, there), the measured
  record `frob.tenfold.gate01/1` and the script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_parity_baseline.sh` that produces it on
  `trj`: N39b-08. This bead depends on it, reads its record, and neither times an incumbent nor recomputes a
  reproduction;
- the capability register and the re-audit of documented absences and not-found items by execution (N00-13,
  N00-14), which N39b-08's record summarizes;
- the incumbent harness (seed BD-039) and the measurements (seed BD-040); the agent-driven loop
  (GATE-TENFOLD-02, bead N40-13); the rows on the Mac (they close with GATE-SPARK-21 at M1, seed BD-142).

## Technical approach

**1. What must be on record** (produced and measured by N39b-08; this section is the text of the register row,
against which the measured record is checked).

1. *Audit, per incumbent of the rival arms.* Exact revision; licence, and whether it permits publishing a
   comparison; a capability table; the warm configuration, frozen after tuning on reference tasks only; the
   environment recipe from which it runs on `trj`. A changed revision changes the harness identity.
   The capability table is the one N39b-08 builds, with the plan's eight rows of what an incumbent's own
   documentation says it can do: per-world model parameters in one batch; state-preserving model edits; opt-in
   run-to-run determinism; mixed topologies in one batch (heterogeneous worlds); electrical and thermal
   actuator models; sampling control without training; interior generators; agent access over MCP. It also
   carries, for each incumbent, restorable state and forks, a Metal path, and its fastest solver path (the
   first two read from the capability register of N00-13 and N00-14, the third from the frozen warm
   configuration). These five items are fields of N39b-08's entry in `comparators.toml`, under BD-039's schema
   (the only one of that file): revision is `release` with `source_revision`; licence is `licence` with
   `permits_publishing_comparison` (projected from the licence reading of N39b-32); the capability table is `capabilities`;
   the warm configuration is `warm_config`; the environment recipe is the recorded environment of the
   incumbent harness named by `container_digest`.
2. *Timed rows on `trj`, each with a closed phase ledger and a disclosure block.* (a) Cold start. (b) A parameter
   edit to the first device step. (c) A topology edit to the first device step. (d) Training to the return
   threshold on one card. (e) The same on two cards. Published context (FACT, from the incumbents' own
   publications): about 410 s on one RTX 4090 and about 325 s on two for a flat-ground quadruped policy, with a
   compile region of about 45 s inside those times.
3. *Reproduction check.* For every published figure that the thesis table uses: measured m, published p, and
   either |m − p| / p ≤ 0.20 or a written explanation of the difference; in the second case the measured figure
   replaces the published one in the thesis table.

**2. Ledger closure.** A phase ledger is `ledger-closed` when its exclusive phases sum to the wall clock within 2%;
otherwise the run is flagged `ledger-open` and leaves ceiling arithmetic. Only closed ledgers satisfy this gate.

**3. The check** `tenfold01(records) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/baseline.rs`:
complete iff every incumbent has the five audit fields, rows (a) to (e) each have a closed ledger, a disclosure
block and a pinned harness identity, and every published figure has its reproduction entry. The verdict lists what
is missing. The gate passes when the record is complete; it does not require the incumbent to be slow or fast.
Its input is the measured record `frob.tenfold.gate01/1` of N39b-08 together with the ledger records and
receipts that record names; the function resolves each identity (a figure whose receipt does not resolve is not
a measurement), reads the status of each reproduction entry (`reproduced` and `explained` satisfy it;
`unexplained` does not) and the closure flag of each ledger, and requires the re-audit counts of the record to
leave no documented-absence or not-found row without its M0 record. It computes no relative difference and no
closure residual itself: those are N39b-08's check and the ledger's closure check.

**4. Record.** Register row GATE-TENFOLD-01, kind `program`, claim `parity`, milestone M0; `measured` holds the
five timings with units and the count of figures reproduced within 20% against the count explained. Controls: the
three negative controls of the test plan, registered in the conformance registry and caught in the same run as the
recorded pass. The row has one writer (conventions of the gate register, N40-01 and N38b-19): the check's run
writes its verdict as the gate's receipt (the gate line `pass` or `fail`, the `measured` string, the hash of
the measured record it read, and each control with `caught`), the receipt is committed byte-identical at
`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`, and a session that ran none of the measurements enters the row from it with
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-01 --receipt <hash>`. No script
writes the row, and an incomplete verdict records `fail`, never `pass`.

**5. Consequence if not met.** M0 does not exit. The thesis arithmetic stays a HYPOTHESIS; the register marks every
work package's speed justification `unfunded: no measured denominator`.

**6. What the baseline may change, and what it may not.** It may change the ranking of the levers, the funding of
work packages, the mission deadlines H and the expected ranges per mission class. It may not change the estimand
or the outcome rules. If the measured ledger puts the ceilings under 3 for every mission class, the thesis chapter
is rewritten before any work is funded against it (an M0 stop rule).

**7. Other consumers of the pins.** The audit's pins also serve the instruments that other chapters borrow from
incumbents: the incumbent arms of the mixed-batch head-to-head and the proxy-adequacy pilot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every incumbent of the rival arms has revision, licence, capability table, frozen warm configuration and
      environment recipe on record; the harness identity changes when a revision changes.
- [ ] Rows (a) to (e) are timed on `trj`, each `ledger-closed`, each with a complete disclosure block.
- [ ] Each published figure used by the thesis table is reproduced within 20% or carries a written explanation,
      and the thesis table is regenerated from the measured rows.
- [ ] The register row is recorded by a session that did not run the measurements; a `ledger-open` run cannot
      satisfy it.
- [ ] With any one of the items of step 1 missing, the M0 exit report is `not-ready` and names it.
- [ ] An incumbent whose licence forbids publishing a comparison is replaced by the next strongest and the
      replacement is printed (default of the licence question).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::baseline::` (synthetic record sets).

- `complete_record_passes`: all fields, five closed ledgers → pass.
- `open_ledger_fails`: phases summing to 97% of the clock → `ledger-open` → fail; 98.5% → closed.
- `reproduction_rule`: a record whose entry for p = 410 s, m = 470 s (14.6%) has status `reproduced` → satisfied;
  an entry for m = 520 s (26.8%) with status `unexplained` → fail naming the figure; the same with status
  `explained` → pass, and the thesis table shows the measured figure in place of the published one. (The
  arithmetic of the 20% band is tested once, in N39b-08.)
- `reaudit_incomplete_fails`: a record whose re-audit counts leave one documented-absence row without its M0
  record → fail naming the row.
- `reads_one_record`: the verdict is a pure function of the measured record and what it names; with the record
  absent the verdict is `not-ready`, never a pass.
- `missing_two_card_row_fails`: row (e) absent → fail naming it.
- `revision_change_changes_identity`: two harness records differing in one incumbent revision → different
  identities.
- `no_disclosure_block_refused`: a timing without its block → refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_01.sh` (on `trj`): it does not time the incumbents a second time. It invokes
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_parity_baseline.sh` (N39b-08), which runs the incumbent harness for rows (a) to (e) and
writes the measured record, or reads the record of the most recent such run when one exists for the current
pins; then it runs the check `tenfold01` on that record, which writes the verdict receipt; it records that
receipt with `record_gate` into a scratch copy of the gate register (never the real one, whose row the
recording session enters from the same receipt) and asserts that a complete verdict is entered and an
incomplete one is refused as a pass; it regenerates the thesis table from the measured rows and the M0 exit
report. It then replays the negative controls
below on copies of the record and asserts each verdict. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_01/<utc>.ndjson`, per step: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block `{claim: parity, class, tier: trj, determinism, identities,
seed}`, the measured record's hash, each timing with unit beside the published
figure and the 20% band as read from the record, the ledger's closure residual beside 2%, harness identity and
revisions, the verdict with its list of missing items, and on failure the first missing or differing item with
artifact paths. The summary names GATE-TENFOLD-01 and GATE-SPARK-21.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the closure bound 2%; the reproduction band 20%; the published context 410 s, 325 s and 45 s as
context only (they are not thresholds). Negative controls: a ledger with 3% unattributed time (flagged and excluded
from ceilings); a published figure pasted as a measurement (no receipt resolves; refused); an incumbent configured
cold where a warm configuration exists (the audit's frozen configuration differs; refused).

## Considerations

- The two-card and all-core rows exceed the default compute envelope; without the owner's approval the
  single-card and eight-core rows are recorded, the others are `not run`, and this gate cannot pass.
- OPEN, incumbent licences (owner: project lead; blocks this gate): default, revisions and fetch instructions only.
  OPEN, comparator revisions: default, the revisions in the source register, frozen before any paired run.
- Incumbents run out of process, under a pin, on fixtures captured by script: they are comparison instruments,
  never dependencies and never baselines of record.
- A fast incumbent is not a failure of this gate. It is the information the gate exists to obtain.
