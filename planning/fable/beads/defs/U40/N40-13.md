## Background

At M0 the phases of the incumbent's loop that need no agent were timed (GATE-TENFOLD-01). A design loop, however,
is driven by an agent: it proposes a design, edits, launches training, reads results, decides. GATE-TENFOLD-02 runs
that whole loop for the **composed rival** (the strongest incumbent workflow) with the **common agent** (the same
agent model, revision and caps that every arm of the ten-fold test will use), end to end, and records a **phase
ledger**: wall-clock time split into exclusive phases (authoring, compile, training, evaluation, agent turns,
checking, and so on) that sum to the clock.

With that ledger the program can do arithmetic it could not do before. For every **lever** of the thesis (a
mechanism claimed to shorten the loop) it computes the **removable share** p: the fraction of the rival's
restricted time that the lever could remove. A lever whose p is under 0.10 cannot be a speed lever, because
removing a phase that holds 10% of the time speeds the loop up by at most 1/0.9 = 1.11. Such a lever is struck from
the speed case before M2 begins. This is a program gate: if it is not met, M1 does not exit, and the composed table
of published figures may not be cited as a denominator anywhere. Claim kind: Performance.

## Scope

In: the program gate as a check and a record: the function `tenfold02(records)` that the measurement on record is
complete and correctly derived; the row GATE-TENFOLD-02 of the gate register, written by a session that did not
run the measurement; the reading of the lever decision's record into that row; the consequence if the gate is not
met (M1 does not exit; the lint that refuses a report citing the composed table).

Out, with the bead that has it:
- The measurement and all arithmetic on it (seed BD-143, on which this bead depends): running the rival loop,
  the ledgers and their derivation, the pooled share table with intervals, the measured rows that replace the
  composed table, the nine ceilings (a) to (i) with their formulas and worked values, the removable share,
  interval and ceiling of each lever (each share recorded with its time base, so that a later product of lever
  factors can be checked), the repeat. This bead recomputes none of it; it checks that each is present, cites its
  ledgers and was computed on the rival's ledger.
- The order rule on products of lever factors, which BD-143's record does not form: N39a-38
  (`order-rule-violation`, FROB-REF-866).
- The decision of KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER at M1 (N39a-39, on which this bead depends): the rule by which a lever
  with a removable share under 10% is struck, its treatment of a share measured with noise, the statuses
  `funded-as-speed`, `struck` and `marginal` per lever and class, the strike list and the funding-reason field
  of each work package. This bead applies no strike rule of its own and reads the statuses from that record.
- The phase-ledger mechanism (Chronicle); the ceiling calculators (N00-17, N39a-37); the program-wide refusal of
  speed comparisons whose ceiling is too low (GATE-TENFOLD-09, bead N40-18).

## Technical approach

**1. What must be on record.**

1. The rival loop run with the common agent on at least 12 development missions of the strata `legged-quad` and
   `wheeled` (TARGET; the 12 public development missions of each stratum are the pool), on `trj`, cold and warm
   runs separated, at the revisions pinned by the audit of GATE-TENFOLD-01.
2. For every run a phase ledger that is `ledger-closed`: exclusive phases sum to the wall clock within 2%. A run
   that is `ledger-open` is kept, flagged, and excluded from ceiling arithmetic; it is never dropped.
3. The table of the thesis that composed published figures, regenerated from the measured rows; every ceiling of
   the thesis recomputed from the measured ledger.
4. For every lever of the thesis: its removable share p on the measured ledger with its interval and its ceiling
   (from BD-143's record), and its status per mission class from the decision record of N39a-39:
   `funded-as-speed`, `struck` or `marginal`. An earlier two-valued wording of this gate (`speed-lever` if
   p ≥ 0.10, `struck from the speed case` if p < 0.10) is replaced by those three: a share is measured with
   noise, and the decision bead reads it with its repeat range (struck when the upper end of the range is below
   1/10; `marginal` when the range straddles 1/10, kept until the M2 re-read; a degenerate range read at its
   point value, so p = 0.10 exactly is funded and p = 0.0999 is struck).

**2. The ceilings.** The gate requires every ceiling of the thesis recomputed from the measured ledger. The
formulas, their worked values and their tests are in BD-143 (removing a phase, at most 1/(1 − p); improving a part,
at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h); an infinitely fast environment step; one design per agent turn, at most (C + t)/t;
compute per decision; the unremovable cost, R ≤ RMT_rival ÷ u; the horizon, R ≤ H ÷ RMT_ours; completion,
R ≤ 1/q; the design cycle alone, the measured incumbent cycle ÷ our planning cycle of 120 s). Each is arithmetic on the rival's ledger, and each exists because a tempting comparison ignores it. What
this gate checks about them is in point 4: each is present, each cites the ledgers it was computed from, and each
was computed on the rival's ledger and not on ours.

**3. The funding rule, read here and decided elsewhere.** A mechanism whose removable cost is under 10% of the
measured loop is not funded as a speed lever (KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER; first reading at M1, re-read at M2 on our own
profile), because removing a phase that holds 10% of the time speeds the loop up by at most 1/0.9 = 1.11. It may
still be funded for what it does to trust, coverage or correctness, with that reason written in its work package
and no speed figure attached. The decision, its procedure and the strike list are N39a-39's; the order rule on
products of lever factors is N39a-38's check, applied to any product formed from the shares BD-143 records with
their time base (the funding decision of N39a-39 reads its refusals). This gate requires that the decision record exists for every lever and class with a measured ledger
before the row is written, and copies each status into the row.

**4. The check** `tenfold02(records) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/loop_baseline.rs` (the
gate's check; the derivation of tables and shares is BD-143's module `tenfold::baseline`): at
least 12 missions across the two strata with closed ledgers; the regenerated table present and derived only from
those ledgers (each cell cites a ledger identifier); p and a ceiling for every registered lever, each citing the
rival's ledgers; a status from N39a-39's decision record for every lever and class; for every (lever, class) with
status `struck`, the work-package registry showing no speed justification for it; the count of runs in the record
equal to the count launched.

**5. Record.** Register row GATE-TENFOLD-02, kind `program`, claim `performance`, milestone M1; `measured`: the
number of missions, the pooled restricted mean time per stratum with its interval, and the list of levers with p.

**6. Consequence if not met.** M1 does not exit. The composed table may not be cited as a denominator (a lint in
the report generator refuses a report that cites it).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 12 development missions of `legged-quad` and `wheeled` have closed ledgers for the rival loop with
      the common agent, cold and warm separated.
- [ ] The composed table is replaced by measured rows; every cell resolves to a ledger.
- [ ] Every lever has a removable share and a ceiling on record (BD-143) and a status per class from the decision
      record of N39a-39; every lever struck there is out of the speed case before any M2 bead starts, and its
      work package carries no speed figure; a `marginal` lever is listed as such in the row.
- [ ] The check refuses a record in which a ceiling was computed on our arm's ledger, a lever has a share and no
      status, or a struck lever's work package still carries a speed figure. (That the ceiling functions
      reproduce the worked values 2, 6.7, 10 at p = 0.9, 25/3 and 1.11 is an acceptance line of BD-143.)
- [ ] No run is dropped: the count of runs in the record equals the count launched; `ledger-open` runs are listed.
- [ ] The row is recorded by a session that did not run the measurements, with a caught negative control.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::loop_baseline::`.

- The five ceiling tests (`ceiling_remove_phase`, `ceiling_improve_part`, `ceiling_completion`,
  `ceiling_common_latency`, `ceiling_horizon`) are tests of the arithmetic and are in BD-143
  (`tenfold::baseline`), where they were moved from this bead; the order-rule tests are N39a-38's
  (`order_rule_pruning`, `order_rule_sequence`).
- `status_read_from_decision_record`: scratch decision records with degenerate ranges at p = 0.09 and p = 0.10
  give `struck` and `funded-as-speed` in the row; a range straddling 1/10 gives `marginal`; a lever with a share
  and no decision record fails the check.
- `struck_lever_with_speed_figure_fails`: a work-package record that still cites a struck lever as its speed
  justification fails the check.
- `ceiling_must_cite_rival_ledgers`: a ceiling whose inputs cite our arm's ledger fails the check with the
  registry's reason `ceiling-wrong-arm` (FROB-REF-866, N39-REF), and one citing a `ledger-open` run with
  `ceiling-needs-closed-ledger`; the check defines no reason name of its own.
- `open_ledger_excluded_not_dropped`: a ledger with 3% unattributed stays in the run count and leaves the ceiling
  inputs.
- `twelve_missions_minimum`: 11 closed ledgers → fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_02.sh` (on `trj`): replays the recorded rival-loop ledgers through the check, regenerates the
measured table and the lever table, and regenerates the M1 exit report; then replays the four negative controls
below on copies of the record and asserts each is refused with its reason. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_02/<utc>.ndjson`, per step: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code,
wall time, the evidence block `{claim: performance, class, tier: trj, determinism, identities (agent model and
revision, incumbent revisions), seed}`, per-mission ledger closure residual beside 2%, each lever's p and ceiling
with units and its status, the count of runs launched and recorded, digests of the ledgers, and on failure the
first offending ledger with its path. The `summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-02 and
KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the ceilings' worked values are BD-143's; this check uses one of the harness's own known answers
to guard against a ceiling computed on the wrong arm's ledger: a single-pair ledger whose ratio is 6.0 with
training removed giving 2.206 and an unremovable floor giving 8.571. Negative controls: a ceiling computed on our arm's ledger instead of
the rival's; a dropped censored run; a lever with p = 0.06 still listed with a speed figure; a report citing the
composed table.

## Considerations

- The measurement needs an agent, device time on `trj` and the owner's approval of the compute; without it the
  gate is `not run` and M1 does not exit.
- The agent is part of the measurement: the result is conditional on the common agent's model and revision, and
  the record says so.
- A lever struck at M1 may return at M2 if our own profile shows a removable share of at least 10%; the re-read
  is the same rule on a different ledger.
- Development missions are public; nothing sealed is touched by this gate.
