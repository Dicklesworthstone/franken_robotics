## Background

At M0 the phases of the incumbent's loop that need no agent were timed (GATE-TENFOLD-01). A design loop, however,
is driven by an agent: it proposes a design, edits, launches training, reads results, decides. GATE-TENFOLD-02 runs
that whole loop for the **composed rival** (the strongest incumbent workflow) with the **common agent** (the same
agent model, revision and caps that every arm of the ten-fold test will use), end to end, and records a **phase
ledger**: wall-clock time split into exclusive phases (authoring, compile, training, evaluation, agent turns,
checking, and so on) that sum to the clock.

With that ledger the program can do arithmetic it could not do before. For every **lever** of the thesis (a
mechanism claimed to shorten the loop) it computes the **removable share** p: the fraction of the rival's
restricted time that the lever could remove. A lever whose p is under 0.10 cannot be a speed lever, because
removing a phase that holds 10% of the time speeds the loop up by at most 1/0.9 = 1.11. Such a lever is struck from
the speed case before M2 begins. This is a program gate: if it is not met, M1 does not exit, and the composed table
of published figures may not be cited as a denominator anywhere. Claim kind: Performance.

## Scope

In: the completeness check of the measured loop; the replacement of the composed table; the removable share and
ceiling per lever; the strike rule; the gate record and consequence. Out: running the rival loop (seed BD-143);
the phase-ledger mechanism (Chronicle); the program-wide refusal of speed comparisons whose ceiling is too low
(GATE-TENFOLD-09, bead N40-18).

## Technical approach

**1. What must be on record.**

1. The rival loop run with the common agent on at least 12 development missions of the strata `legged-quad` and
   `wheeled` (TARGET; the 12 public development missions of each stratum are the pool), on `trj`, cold and warm
   runs separated, at the revisions pinned by the audit of GATE-TENFOLD-01.
2. For every run a phase ledger that is `ledger-closed`: exclusive phases sum to the wall clock within 2%. A run
   that is `ledger-open` is kept, flagged, and excluded from ceiling arithmetic; it is never dropped.
3. The table of the thesis that composed published figures, regenerated from the measured rows; every ceiling of
   the thesis recomputed from the measured ledger.
4. For every lever of the thesis: its removable share p on the measured ledger, its ceiling, and its status
   (`speed-lever` if p ≥ 0.10; `struck from the speed case` if p < 0.10).

**2. The ceilings (each is arithmetic on the rival's ledger; each exists because a tempting comparison ignores
it).** With RMT the restricted mean time (time capped at the deadline H) and R the ratio rival ÷ ours:

| # | Ceiling | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Removing a phase | If a phase holds the share p of the rival's restricted time and a mechanism removes it entirely, the speed-up is at most 1/(1 − p). Removing half the loop gives 2; removing 85% gives 6.7; ten needs p ≥ 0.9 |
| 2 | Improving a part | If the share f is improved by the factor r and the mechanism adds overhead h, the speed-up is at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h); all three are measured on the rival's critical path |
| 3 | Unremovable cost | If our arm's time has a floor u that no mechanism removes (authoring, the common agent's turns, adjudication, checking, serialization), then R ≤ RMT_rival ÷ u. When u exceeds one tenth of the rival's time, a ratio of ten needs another stage changed or the claim changed |
| 4 | Completion | If our arm misses the share q of deadlines, RMT_ours ≥ q·H and RMT_rival ≤ H, so R ≤ 1/q. With 12% unfinished the ratio cannot exceed 25/3 ≈ 8.33 |
| 5 | Common latency | With the same agent latency t per turn in both arms and a rival cost C per decision, deciding one design per turn gives at most (C + t)/t |
| 6 | The horizon | R ≤ H ÷ RMT_ours, because the rival's restricted time cannot exceed H |

**3. The funding rule applied here (KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER, first reading; re-read at M2 on our own profile).** A
mechanism whose removable cost is under 10% of the measured loop is not funded as a speed lever. It may still be
funded for what it does to trust, coverage or correctness, with that reason written in its work package and no
speed figure attached. **The order rule**: a pruning ratio is never multiplied by a speed-up measured on work the
pruning already removed; each factor in a product of levers is measured on the time that the earlier factors left.

**4. The check** `tenfold02(records) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/loop_baseline.rs`: at
least 12 missions across the two strata with closed ledgers; the regenerated table present and derived only from
those ledgers (each cell cites a ledger identifier); p and a ceiling for every registered lever; every lever with
p < 0.10 marked struck, and the work-package registry showing no speed justification for it.

**5. Record.** Register row GATE-TENFOLD-02, kind `program`, claim `performance`, milestone M1; `measured`: the
number of missions, the pooled restricted mean time per stratum with its interval, and the list of levers with p.

**6. Consequence if not met.** M1 does not exit. The composed table may not be cited as a denominator (a lint in
the report generator refuses a report that cites it).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 12 development missions of `legged-quad` and `wheeled` have closed ledgers for the rival loop with
      the common agent, cold and warm separated.
- [ ] The composed table is replaced by measured rows; every cell resolves to a ledger.
- [ ] Every lever has a removable share and a ceiling on record; every lever with p < 0.10 is struck from the
      speed case before any M2 bead starts, and its work package carries no speed figure.
- [ ] The ceiling functions reproduce the worked values: 2, 6.7 (to one decimal), 10 at p = 0.9, 25/3, 1.11.
- [ ] No run is dropped: the count of runs in the record equals the count launched; `ledger-open` runs are listed.
- [ ] The row is recorded by a session that did not run the measurements, with a caught negative control.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::loop_baseline::`.

- `ceiling_remove_phase`: p = 0.5 → 2; p = 0.85 → 6.667; p = 0.9 → 10; p = 0.10 → 1.111.
- `ceiling_improve_part`: f = 0.8, r = 4, h = 0.05 → 1/(0.2 + 0.2 + 0.05) = 2.222.
- `ceiling_completion`: q = 0.12 → 25/3.
- `ceiling_common_latency`: C = 420 s, t = 60 s → 8.
- `ceiling_horizon`: H = 4 h, RMT_ours = 0.5 h → 8.
- `strike_rule`: levers with p = 0.09 and 0.10 → struck and kept.
- `order_rule`: a product of two lever factors where the second was measured on the full ledger and not on the
  remainder is rejected (`factor-on-removed-work`).
- `open_ledger_excluded_not_dropped`: a ledger with 3% unattributed stays in the run count and leaves the ceiling
  inputs.
- `twelve_missions_minimum`: 11 closed ledgers → fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_02.sh` (on `trj`): replays the recorded rival-loop ledgers through the check, regenerates the
measured table and the lever table, and regenerates the M1 exit report. Logs per step: command, exit code, wall
time, per-mission ledger closure residual beside 2%, each lever's p and ceiling with units, the count of runs
launched and recorded, digests of the ledgers, and on failure the first offending ledger with its path. The
summary names GATE-TENFOLD-02 and KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the six ceilings' worked values above; a single-pair ledger whose ratio is 6.0 with training
removed giving 2.206 and an unremovable floor giving 8.571 (the harness's own known answer; it guards against a
ceiling computed on the wrong arm's ledger). Negative controls: a ceiling computed on our arm's ledger instead of
the rival's; a dropped censored run; a lever with p = 0.06 still listed with a speed figure; a report citing the
composed table.

## Considerations

- The measurement needs an agent, device time on `trj` and the owner's approval of the compute; without it the
  gate is `not run` and M1 does not exit.
- The agent is part of the measurement: the result is conditional on the common agent's model and revision, and
  the record says so.
- A lever struck at M1 may return at M2 if our own profile shows a removable share of at least 10%; the re-read
  is the same rule on a different ledger.
- Development missions are public; nothing sealed is touched by this gate.
