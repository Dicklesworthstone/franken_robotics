## Background

The ten-fold test compares design-loop time across arms on **sealed** mission families: missions that no arm has
seen, opened to an arm only when its clock starts. A sealed suite can be opened once. If the harness that scores it
is wrong, if an arm was tuned after the missions were fixed, or if the simulator that adjudicates answers is
unsound or was used by an arm during tuning, the suite is spent and the result is worthless. Three program gates
therefore stand **before the suite is opened**, at the partial gate of M3 and again at the tournament of M7:

- **GATE-TENFOLD-03**: the scoring harness reproduces its own known answers exactly and catches its negative
  controls.
- **GATE-TENFOLD-04**: the freeze-and-seal order was followed and every arm is frozen and published.
- **GATE-TENFOLD-05**: the adjudicating simulator is accepted.

If any is not met, **the suite is not opened**. They are program gates: they belong to no subsystem and decide
whether the comparison may proceed. Claim kind of all three: Known answer. DESIGN decision: step 2 of sealing (the
suite hash) precedes step 4 (arm freeze) so that no mission can be written against a frozen arm, and step 4
precedes step 6 (opening) so that no arm can be tuned against a mission. Rejected: freezing arms "about the same
time" as sealing, which leaves both directions of contamination open.

## Scope

In: the three gates as rows of the program-gate register, each with an M3 part and an M7 part; the pre-opening
verdict `may_open`, which is the conjunction of the three with GATE-TENFOLD-10 and GATE-TENFOLD-11 for one gate
instance and is the only thing that lets the harness open a sealed suite; the reading rules for the three
measured records (which part of each record the conjunction needs, and for which instance); the program-level
invariant checks that span both instances (role disjointness, no family reused between M3 and M7); how the two
kill rules these gates feed (KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ, KILL-TENFOLD-REACH) change what `may_open` requires; the open
questions that block them.

Out, with the bead that has it. Each of the three gates is measured once, and its record is written once, by
the ten-fold chapter's measurement bead; this bead reads those records and writes no second record, re-runs no
known answer and replays no control:
- GATE-TENFOLD-03: the module `tenfold_ka` of the known-answer test target `g1_known_answers` (there is no
  separate `tenfold_ka` target), the rows and controls run against the real harness, the mutant scorers, the
  script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_known_answers.sh` and the gate receipt, committed at `receipts/gates/<hash>.json`:
  N39b-22;
- GATE-TENFOLD-04: the checker of the four clauses (`frob-tenfold seal verify`, manifests and statements, the
  home-task reproduction and easy wins, the pre-analysis and reachability table), the record in its `pre-open`
  and `post-open` parts, its seven controls and the script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate04_freeze_seal.sh`:
  N39a-40;
- GATE-TENFOLD-05: the expressible known answers, the planted-answer sets (20 per stratum), the arm-usage
  search, the discordance sample, the record per stratum, its five controls and the script
  `scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate05_adjudicator.sh`: N39a-10;
- the decisions themselves: KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ (N39a-11) and KILL-TENFOLD-REACH (N39a-34);
- the harness, the suite, the arms and the adjudicator (the ten-fold chapter's beads); the pre-analysis known
  answers (GATE-TENFOLD-10, bead N40-19); the rival audit (GATE-TENFOLD-11, bead N40-20).

Sections 1 to 3 below state what each gate requires, as the text of its register row: it is what a reader of
the register must be able to check against the measured record, and it is the same content the measurement
beads implement. Where a number appears in both, the measurement bead's test is the one that exercises it.

## Technical approach

**1. GATE-TENFOLD-03: the harness (measured and recorded by N39b-22).** It reproduces exactly:

| Known answer | Expected | Guards against |
|---|---|---|
| The six-family card | R̂ = 2.647; the completion ceiling 3.75; per-family ratios 6.00, 7.00, 6.857, 7.714, 0.8125, 9.00 | Averaging ratios (6.23) and dropping a censored run (7.05) |
| The single-pair ledger | 6.0; training removed 2.206; unremovable floor 8.571 | A ceiling computed on the wrong arm's ledger |
| The warm card | R_warm at 1, 10, 100 reuses: 0.272, 2.120, 6.596; limit 8.617; N* = 5 | Quoting the limit as the warm ratio; omitting a standing asset |
| Exact guard | Critical counts 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12, 12 of 16, 17 of 24, 22 of 32 at 0.05; 18 of 24 at 0.0125; no count at m = 4 | An approximate test; a verdict that cannot be reached |
| Zero-count bound | 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 0.1174 | A zero-width interval from 24 zeros |
| Completion ceiling | 12% unfinished caps R at 25/3 | A ratio claimed above its ceiling |
| Monitor | Stops at eight consecutive families below 3 (25.6) and not at seven (17.1) | An early stop without anytime validity |

and catches each negative control: a planted false certificate (the arm is disqualified); a canary value from a
sealed world in a transcript (the cell is void); a ledger with 3% unattributed time (flagged, and excluded from
ceilings); a manifest whose freeze time follows the suite opening (the run is refused); a request for a
confirmatory cell with four families (refused as unreachable); a request for the fixed-n verdict at a monitor's
stopping time (refused); an arm that touched the adjudicating simulator during tuning (disqualified).

**2. GATE-TENFOLD-04: freeze and seal (measured and recorded by N39a-40).** The six steps appear in the ledger in order, each with a hash and a time:
(1) class descriptions and the development suite are published; (2) the adjudicator commits the hash of the sealed
suite and of the referee protocol; (3) arms are tuned on the development suite only; (4) each arm's freeze manifest
is published; (5) the pre-analysis is recorded; (6) the suite is opened to the harness, and a mission card is shown
to an arm only when that arm's clock starts. In addition: a published manifest and an advocate statement for every
arm; the frozen rival reproduces its home-task training time within 20% on `trj` and wins at least one easy-win
development family per stratum (so that the rival is demonstrably competent, not a strawman); the pre-analysis is
recorded, with every confirmatory cell meeting the **reachability conditions**: P(`ten-fold`) at least 0.8 at a
true ratio of 15; P(`withdrawn`) at most 0.05 at a true ratio of 5; the guard reachable at its adjusted level; and
the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development suite at least 10 (since R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A whatever the rival does). At
most four confirmatory cells are named per gate; the exact guard is applied with Holm's step-down procedure at
family level 0.05 across them (with four cells the first step needs 18 of 24). A cell that fails a reachability
condition is opened as `descriptive`, and the report says so before any sealed run (**KILL-TENFOLD-REACH**); it is
enlarged for the next gate.

**3. GATE-TENFOLD-05: adjudicator acceptance (measured and recorded by N39a-10).** The adjudicating simulator passes the known answers of the
verification program that it can express, inside its own stated tolerances; of 20 planted answers per stratum (10
that must pass, 10 that must fail) it classifies 20 correctly; no arm's logs show it in training or tuning; on a
5% sample of accepted answers the discordance with each arm's home referee is reported. If no neutral simulator
passes for a stratum (**KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ**): cross-home adjudication, labelled (each arm's answers are checked by
the other arm's referee); if neither home referee can run the other arm's answers, the stratum is `descriptive`.

**4. Invariants the checks enforce.** INV-TENFOLD-01: the builder, rival-advocate and adjudicator roles are
disjoint for the life of a gate, for people and for agent sessions. INV-TENFOLD-02: the six sealing steps appear in
the ledger in order, each with a hash and a time. INV-TENFOLD-03: no paired run exists whose arms were not all
frozen and published before it started; no arm changes after its freeze. INV-TENFOLD-06: a sealed family serves
one gate and is then retired; the M7 suite uses no family that M3 used. INV-TENFOLD-07: no output of a monitor
enters the fixed-n verdict, and the verdict is computed once, at n.

**5. The pre-opening verdict.** `may_open(suite) -> Result<(), Vec<Blocker>>` in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/preopen.rs`: Ok iff -03, -04, -05, GATE-TENFOLD-10 and GATE-TENFOLD-11 each
have a `pass` part for this suite's hash, recorded before the opening. The harness's `open` operation calls it and
refuses otherwise: the sealing-ledger bead (N39a-15, a dependency) builds `open` with a bare read of the five
gates' register statuses for this suite hash; this bead replaces that read with `may_open`, so that a status
counts only when the record it cites satisfies the reading rule of its row below, and the blockers are the
refusal's detail. Each of the three gates has two parts in the register: M3 (the partial-gate suite) and M7 (the
tournament suite); a pass at M3 does not carry to M7.

What "a `pass` part for this suite's hash" means for each record (DESIGN; the three records have different
shapes because they measure different things, and the conjunction must not paper over that):

| Gate | Record read | Bound to the suite by | Counts as `pass` for opening when |
|---|---|---|---|
| GATE-TENFOLD-03 | The receipt of the gate's tier run (N39b-22: `FROB_TEST_SEED=390322 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-TENFOLD-03`, module `tenfold_ka`), committed at `receipts/gates/<hash>.json`: commit, fingerprint, seed, per row observed and expected, per control `caught` | The receipt's commit is the harness commit frozen for the gate instance (`M3-partial` or `M7-tournament`) whose sealing step 2 committed this suite's hash | Every row reproduced, the control list is not empty and every control was caught in the same run |
| GATE-TENFOLD-04 | The record of N39a-40, part `pre-open` (steps 1 to 5 and clauses 2 to 4) | Its `suite` field equals this suite's hash | `verdict: "pass"` on the `pre-open` part with `violations: []` and every control caught. The `post-open` part (step 6 and the first `card-shown` entries) cannot exist yet and is not read here; it is checked by N39a-40 after the opening |
| GATE-TENFOLD-05 | The records of N39a-10, one per stratum in scope of the suite | Its `instance` is the suite's gate instance and its `sim` pin equals the adjudicator pin in the sealing ledger's step 2 | Every stratum in scope has `verdict: "pass"`: clauses 1 to 3 measured on the frozen arms (not `pending: freeze`) and the discordance table of the development phase present. The discordance on accepted sealed answers is `pending: after-close` at this point by construction and does not block the opening |

A record of another instance, a -04 record for another suite hash, a -05 record whose pin differs from the
ledger's, and any record entered after the `open` entry are each a `Blocker` naming the gate. Where
KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ has been decided for a stratum (N39a-11: cross-home adjudication, labelled, or the stratum
`descriptive`), `may_open` reads that decision record in place of the -05 pass for that stratum and carries the
label into the opening entry. Where KILL-TENFOLD-REACH has been decided for a cell (N39a-34), the cell is
`descriptive` in the pre-run notice and the -04 record's clause 4 passes without it.

## Acceptance criteria

Read from the measured records, at M3 and again at M7 (each line is satisfied by the record of the bead named,
never by a second measurement here):
- [ ] The harness reproduces all seven known answers exactly and catches all seven negative controls, at M3 and
      again at M7 on the release under test (the receipt of N39b-22).
- [ ] The sealing ledger of each suite shows the six steps in order with hashes and times; a ledger with step 4
      after step 6, or step 2 after step 4, is refused (the record of N39a-40).
- [ ] Every arm has a published manifest and an advocate statement; the frozen rival is within 20% of its
      home-task training time on `trj` and wins at least one easy-win development family per stratum (N39a-40).
- [ ] Every confirmatory cell meets the four reachability conditions; a cell that does not is `descriptive` before
      any sealed run; at most four cells are confirmatory (N39a-40, with the decisions of N39a-34).
- [ ] The adjudicator classifies 20 of 20 planted answers per stratum and appears in no arm's training or tuning
      logs; the discordance on a 5% sample is reported (the records of N39a-10).

Delivered by this bead:
- [ ] `open` refuses while any of the five pre-opening gates lacks a pass for this suite hash.
- [ ] `may_open` reads each record by the rule of its row in section 5: a -03 receipt at another commit, a -04
      record for another suite hash or with only its `post-open` part, a -05 record with `pending: freeze` or
      with a simulator pin that differs from the sealing ledger's, and a record entered after `open` each block
      the opening and are named.
- [ ] A -05 record whose only pending item is the after-close discordance does not block the opening.
- [ ] The register holds six parts (three gates × M3 and M7), each citing the measured record by hash; no part
      is entered by the session that implemented the mechanism it cites.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::preopen::`. The arithmetic of the gates is tested where it is measured:
the six-family card (2.647, not 6.23 and not 7.05; ceiling 3.75), the exact-guard counts and the zero-count
bound 0.1174 are rows of N39b-22's module `tenfold_ka` in the target `g1_known_answers`; the 720 orderings of the six steps, the reachability
boundary (0.79 against 0.80; horizon cap 9.9) and the first Holm step (18 of 24) are tests of N39a-40. This
bead's tests are those of the conjunction:

- `preopen_requires_five`: any one of -03, -04, -05, -10, -11 missing → `open` refused, naming it.
- `record_binding`: a -03 receipt at a commit other than the instance's frozen harness commit; a -04 record whose
  `suite` differs; a -05 record whose `sim` pin differs from the ledger's step 2: each is a blocker.
- `pre_open_part_is_what_is_read`: a -04 record with a passing `pre-open` part and no `post-open` part lets
  `open` proceed; one with `verdict: "fail: clause 3"` does not.
- `pending_freeze_blocks_after_close_does_not`: -05 with `pending: freeze` → blocker; -05 with clauses 1 to 3
  passed and discordance `pending: after-close` → not a blocker.
- `control_not_caught_blocks`: a receipt or record in which one control has `caught: false` is not a pass,
  whatever its rows say.
- `late_record_blocks`: a record whose ledger sequence follows the `open` entry is refused.
- `m3_pass_does_not_carry`: all five passes for the M3 instance, offered for the M7 suite hash → refused.
- `kill_adj_replaces_05_for_stratum`: a decided KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ record for a stratum stands in for that
  stratum's -05 pass and its label appears in the opening entry; an undecided one does not.
- `roles_disjoint`: one session identity in two roles → refused.
- `family_reuse_refused`: a family identifier present in the M3 suite and offered for M7 → refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_preopen.sh`: on a rehearsal gate instance (a synthetic suite of two strata with public
keys and two synthetic arms) it obtains the three measured records by running the measurement beads' own
entry points, never its own copy of them: the harness known-answer run of N39b-22
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=390322 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
GATE-TENFOLD-03`, whose receipt it reads by hash), the rehearsal mode of
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate04_freeze_seal.sh` (N39a-40: the sealing ledger, the two freezes, the pre-analysis)
and of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate05_adjudicator.sh` (N39a-10: the 20 planted answers per stratum); it adds
rehearsal records for GATE-TENFOLD-10 and -11 and calls `open`, which must succeed. It then removes or spoils
each of the five records in turn (absent; another instance; another suite hash; `pending: freeze`; one control
marked not caught; entered after `open`) and asserts that `open` is refused and names the gate. The seven
negative controls of the harness and the ordering violations of the ledger are replayed by the measurement
beads' scripts; this script asserts only that their outcome is in the records it reads. It then plants the four
conjunction controls below (an evaluator that accepts four of five, the M3 record for the M7 hash, a -04 record
read without its suite hash, `pending: freeze` read as a pass) and asserts each is caught. Logs through the
STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_preopen/<utc>.ndjson`: per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block `{claim: known-answer, class: Tested, tier: G0:host,
determinism: D0, seed}`, per gate the record's hash, instance, binding value and verdict, the result of
`may_open` with its blockers, each refusal with the gate it names, and on failure the first differing value with
artifact paths; the `summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04, -05, -10 and -11. Nothing
sealed exists in this script.

### Known answers and negative controls

As tabulated in step 1 (the ten-fold chapter's registry numbers them), exercised by N39b-22. Controls of the
three gates, each caught by its measurement bead and read here from the record: the seven of step 1 (N39b-22);
a strawman rival (a frozen rival that wins no easy-win family: refused by -04, N39a-40); an adjudicator present
in an arm's tuning log (refused by -05, N39a-10). Controls of this bead's own conjunction, which it must catch
itself: a `may_open` that accepts any four of the five gates; one that accepts a record of the M3 instance for
the M7 suite; one that reads a -04 record without comparing its suite hash; one that treats `pending: freeze`
as a pass. Determinism: `may_open` is a pure function of the ledger at the frontier of the `open` request
(class D0).

## Considerations

- OPEN questions that block these gates, with defaults: the adjudicator and neutral party (OPEN-PROG-2: a pinned
  incumbent CPU engine no arm trains in, run by a fresh-context session; ratios `self-adjudicated`); the rival
  advocates and their budget (agent sessions that have contributed nothing to this project, 40 device-hours per
  stratum, label `rival: internally advocated`); the deadline H per stratum (three times the frozen rival's median
  development time, rounded up to half an hour, raised until the horizon cap is at least 10); the agent model
  (the agent the project is developed with at the gate's date, pinned; one agent only); ratification of the
  thresholds 3 and 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5, the margin 0.05 and the limit of four confirmatory
  cells (as written).
- These thresholds are fixed when the first suite hash is committed, and none is changed after any sealed run.
- A pass here says the comparison may run. It says nothing about its result.
