## Background

The **partial ten-fold gate** is the first measured comparison of the whole design loop: an agent with this system
(arm A), the declared incumbent workflow (arm B) and an ablation arm (arm C: a short robust program or composed
layer over an incumbent engine) each answer the same sealed missions under the same agent, hardware and caps, and
the ratio of restricted mean times is estimated per stratum. It is held at M3, before the costly levers of M4
(family policies, inheritance, rank-agreement allocation) exist, on purpose: the gate measures the product
*without* the "train few" lever, and its record decides how much of M4 is funded as a speed lever.

It is a program gate with an unusual pass criterion: **it passes when the record exists and the outcome rules have
been applied, whatever the ratio.** M3 does not exit without it. The reason is that the dangerous outcome is not a
low ratio; it is no measurement, or a measurement whose consequences are negotiated after the number is known. So
the consequences are written in advance (the outcome rules below), each outcome is recorded as an ADR, and a kill
outcome is not re-tested on the same suite. Claim kind: Performance. HYPOTHESIS recorded before the run: on
`legged-quad` the ratio at M3 lies near the bottom of its expected range.

## Scope

In: the completeness check of the record; the application of the outcome rules per cell; the ADRs; the other
decisions taken on the gate's record; the consequence for M4's funding. Out: the protocol, arms, adjudication and
scoring (seeds BD-314 to BD-317 and the ten-fold chapter's beads); the pre-opening gates (N40-14, N40-19, N40-20);
the recovery ratios (N40-22).

## Technical approach

**1. Scope of the gate.** Cells (stratum, regime): (`legged-quad`, cold) and (`wheeled`, cold) as confirmatory
cells; (`envelope`, warm) and (`sensing`, cold) as confirmatory cells if their pre-analyses allow it, and as
descriptive cells otherwise. Tracked and hybrid briefs wait for M4 and are first measured at M7. Arms A, B and C
all run. Arm A at M3 has: the daemon with slot dispatch, mixed batches, the baseline controllers and the sampling
fallback, the ladder with fixed allocation, the qualification lane, Nemesis and Autopsy, the memo graph, and the
mission queries on the CPU. It does not have family policies, inheritance or rank-agreement allocation.

**2. What the record must contain, per cell.** The pooled ratio R̂ of restricted mean times with its interval
[L, U]; the verdict; completion and coverage for each arm; the tie test against arm C; one closed phase ledger per
arm; the regime, the family count m, the pair count n, the deadline H, the adjudication label and the freeze hashes
(a warm ratio also carries the reuse count S and the crossover N*). No run is dropped; a censored run contributes
H; an exclusion is a harness fault recorded before times are compared.

**3. The outcome rules, written in advance.** The thresholds 3 and 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5 and
the margin 0.05 are fixed when the first suite hash is committed, and none is changed after any sealed run.

| Outcome on a confirmatory cell | What the report says | What the program does |
|---|---|---|
| `withdrawn`: R̂ below 3, or a futility stop | "Measured R̂ with [L, U] on ⟨stratum, regime⟩. The speed claim for this class is withdrawn." | KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED. The class leaves the speed claim in the thesis, the README and every report. Work packages for the class whose only justification is speed are re-planned before the next milestone starts. The class may be measured again at M7 on fresh sealed families |
| `measured`: R̂ from 3 up to 10, or 10 and above without the guard | "⟨R̂⟩ times shorter, and at least ⟨L⟩ with 95% confidence, on ⟨stratum, regime⟩." The word "ten-fold" is not used for the class anywhere | The ledger names the binding ceiling. Lever work for the class continues only on phases whose removable share is at least 10% |
| `ten-fold` | "Ten-fold on ⟨stratum, regime⟩: measured R̂ with [L, U]", with S and N* for a warm cell | BET-13 is met for that stratum and for no other |
| Tie with the ablation arm: arm C reaches the same accepted scope and RMT_C ÷ RMT_A is at most 1.5; or the recovery ratio is at least 0.8 on elapsed time and on compute | "A short program over ⟨engine⟩ reached the same accepted answers within a factor of 1.5", or "a layer over ⟨engine⟩ recovers ⟨r_T⟩ of the saving in time and ⟨r_C⟩ in compute." | KILL-TENFOLD-TIE. Say so. Ship the layer over that engine for that stratum, behind the same verbs, schemas and checker. Keep engine work only for the gaps that are internal to an engine. Two are documented: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay, and the Metal tier. Two are unverified and are checked before they are relied on: replay of one world outside its batch, and solver-internal certificates. Spend the research budget on the first mission the program cannot resolve. The ratio against arm B is then the layer's ratio, and no claim is made that the mechanisms need this engine |
| Engine advantage not established: RMT_C ÷ RMT_A above 1.5 with its lower bound at or below 1.5 | "The advantage of the engine over a layer was not established." | The layer is offered beside the engine for that stratum. BET-14 is neither met nor failed there, and is re-tested at the next gate |
| The rival is faster: R̂ below 1 | The measured ratio | As `withdrawn`. Where licences allow, the rival's method is adopted behind the verbs |
| `descriptive` | The numbers, with the reason no verdict was possible | Nothing is cited as passing or failing BET-13. The cell is enlarged for the next gate |
| `disqualified` | The event, its scope and its evidence | One false certificate (KILL-TENFOLD-CERT): the arm has no ratio in every cell where it used that proof kind; for arm A the proof kind is suspended, its cells fall to `Tested`, and KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT decides whether the rule or its implementation was at fault. Any leak from sealed confirmation worlds (KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK): the touched cells are void for that arm, the families are retired at once, and if the extent cannot be bounded the whole gate is void and is re-run on a rotated suite |

Across strata: each stratum is its own result; a portfolio ratio with equal weights over the strata run is reported
as descriptive; BET-13 is met when at least one named stratum has the verdict `ten-fold`. A disqualification
awards nothing to the other arm: a cell whose rival is disqualified has no denominator, is reported as such, and
is re-run at the next gate. A breach of a resource cap is not a disqualification: the run is censored at the
instant of the breach and costs H.

**4. What else is decided on this record.** KILL-AUGUR-TIE and KILL-FAMILYCERT on the `envelope` and `sensing`
strata; KILL-GYMADAPT; the incumbent arm that bet BET-10 names; and whether the `train` phase still holds a
removable share of at least 10% for each class, which decides how much of the M4 work on the second lever is
funded as a speed lever. Under 3 on every class: speed work is no longer funded, the product is positioned on
replay, qualification, certificates and the evidence ledger, and the family-policy spend of M4 is decided again.

**5. What remains valuable if the speed claim is withdrawn.** Any world can be re-run alone on the CPU from its
address; comparisons carry error control that survives an agent's retries; family answers are checked by an
auditor that shares no code with their producer; failures arrive as minimal replayable worlds with an
interventional explanation; every result names its claim, its class and its evidence; and the energy ledger
closes. Under 3 the product is described by those and by its measured ratio.

**6. The check** `tenfold06(record) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/partial.rs`: every cell in
scope has every field of step 2; every confirmatory cell's verdict is the one the rules assign to its numbers;
every outcome has an ADR; the pre-opening gates were recorded before the first sealed mission was opened.

**7. Cost.** A cell costs m·c·Σ over arms of E[min(T, H)] device-hours (c: concurrent devices per run). With 24
families, three arms and a deadline of 4 h the worst case is 288 device-hours per cell, reached only if every run
is censored. HYPOTHESIS: about 70 device-hours per design cell at restricted mean times near 1.5 h, 1.0 h and
0.4 h for arms B, C and A. That is above the default envelope class.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every cell in scope has its ratio, interval, verdict, completion, coverage, tie test and one closed phase
      ledger per arm.
- [ ] Each confirmatory cell's verdict equals the rule's assignment for its recorded numbers, and each outcome is
      recorded as an ADR in M3.
- [ ] The decisions of step 4 are recorded: KILL-AUGUR-TIE, KILL-FAMILYCERT, KILL-GYMADAPT, the BET-10 arm, and the
      removable share of the `train` phase per class.
- [ ] The measured table replaces the expected ranges for the classes run.
- [ ] The count of runs recorded equals the count launched; no arm changed after its freeze.
- [ ] The gate's row is `pass` when the record is complete, with any ratio; with the record absent it is `not-run`
      and the M3 exit report is `not-ready`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::partial::` (synthetic records).

- `passes_at_any_ratio`: complete records with R̂ = 0.8, 2.4, 6.0 and 12.0 → the gate passes in all four; the
  verdicts are `withdrawn`, `withdrawn`, `measured`, and `ten-fold` only when the guard holds.
- `ten_without_guard_is_measured`: R̂ = 11 with 16 of 24 families above the guard floor (17 needed) → `measured`.
- `tie_rule`: RMT_C ÷ RMT_A = 1.4 with the same accepted scope → tie; 1.7 with lower bound 1.45 → "advantage not
  established"; 1.7 with lower bound 1.6 → no tie.
- `incomplete_record_not_run`: one arm's ledger `ledger-open` in one cell → the gate is not passed and names it.
- `censored_run_counts_h`: a censored run contributes H to the restricted mean; dropping it is rejected.
- `disqualified_has_no_ratio`: a cell with a false certificate from arm A → no ratio for A; nothing awarded to B.
- `verdict_mismatch_rejected`: a record that labels R̂ = 2.4 as `measured` → rejected.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_06.sh`: on a synthetic two-stratum suite with three synthetic arms and public keys, runs the
harness end to end (seal, freeze, pre-analysis, open, interleaved runs, close, reveal, score), then the check, and
regenerates the M3 exit report; then removes one phase ledger and asserts `not-ready`. Logs: command, exit code,
wall time, per cell R̂ and [L, U] beside the thresholds 3 and 10, completion and coverage per arm with the margin
0.05, the tie statistic beside 1.5, ledger closure residuals, the freeze hashes, and on failure the first
differing value with artifact paths. The summary names GATE-TENFOLD-06 and each kill rule read.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the six-family card (R̂ = 2.647; not the average of ratios 6.23; not 7.05 with the censored run
dropped); 17 of 24 at 0.05. Negative controls: a planted false certificate; a canary value in a transcript; a
record whose arm changed after its freeze; a verdict re-tested on the same suite after a kill outcome (refused).

## Considerations

- OPEN, the compute envelope for the gate (owner; blocks it): default, nothing above the default class runs, the
  gate is recorded `not run`, and M3 does not exit. This is deliberate: there is no smaller substitute.
- With no neutral party named, the gate runs and every ratio is labelled `self-adjudicated`; the partial gate is
  then a rehearsal and the report says so.
- A class withdrawn here stays withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh families; sealed families used here are
  retired and never serve again.
- If a physics row fell below 0.33 of the incumbent at M2 (KILL-PARITY), the arithmetic is redone with measured
  rates before this gate, and whether to ship the layer is decided here.
