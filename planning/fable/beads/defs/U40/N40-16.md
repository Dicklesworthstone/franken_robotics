## Background

The **tournament** is the measurement the program exists to make: every stratum of the sealed mission suite that
has a product to run, on sealed families that no earlier gate used, with every arm frozen and published, every pin
of every arm and every sibling frozen for its length, and neutral adjudication. GATE-TENFOLD-07 is the program gate
that records it. Like the partial gate at M3, **it passes when the report is published with its ledgers and its
kit, whatever it says.** If it is not met, M7 does not exit and no comparative claim is made anywhere.

The pass criterion is about publication because the failure being guarded against is a result that is measured and
then withheld, softened or re-run until it looks better. A tournament is not stopped by an interim number; it is
voided and re-run only for a breach of protocol. Claim kind: Performance.

## Scope

In: the gate itself: the completeness check of the published report; the check that the freeze window was
clean; the audit that each cell's verdict equals the written rule's assignment; the entry of the row of
GATE-TENFOLD-07, once, from a fresh-context session; the positioning that follows. Out: the conduct of the
tournament (running the ten steps with their commands, applying the outcome engine to produce each cell's
verdict and its ADR, and putting the four digests on file): N39b-13, which supplies what this gate's row cites
and enters no row of this gate; the mechanisms it runs (the enforcement of the ten-step order in the event log,
the content of the tournament's freeze record and its watcher, the classification of the three breaches by
extent with their void records, the refusal of an interim stop): the conduct module N39bp3-01; the freeze of the stack
(N33b-03); the suite, the arms, adjudication, the run and the report generator
(the work under the epics BD-428 to BD-433); the kit's recomputation (GATE-TENFOLD-08, bead N40-17); suite hygiene
(N40-21); the recovery ratios (N40-22).

An epic is a container and nothing depends on one, so this bead waits for the beads that produce what it checks:
the tournament's order of work, event log, freeze record and cell verdicts (N39b-13), its dress rehearsal on the development
pool (N39b-17), the simulated decision rule with its binding pre-analysis (N39b-16) and the adjudication dry run
(N38a-34). The six epics are kept in `related`.

## Technical approach

**1. Order of work the record must show** (the ten steps N39b-13 carries out with the commands of the conduct
module N39bp3-01, which refuses a step entered out of order; this bead checks that the event log shows them, in
order, each with a hash and a time). Refresh the rival audit; publish the enlarged development pool (it now
holds the families the partial gate retired); commit the suite and protocol hashes; tune; freeze and publish every
arm; record the pre-analysis and name at most four confirmatory cells; run with the arms interleaved; close and
reveal; publish the report and the kit; have an external party re-run the kit. DESIGN: adjudication at M7 is
external, or the word is not used until the external replication has passed.

**2. Strata.** `legged-quad`, `legged-biped`, `wheeled`, `tracked`, `hybrid`, `envelope`, `sensing`, `rebody`,
`failure`, `throughput`, and `facility` only if its Moonshot gate GATE-AUGUR-24 has passed (otherwise `not run`).
A stratum that needs a mechanism of M5 or M6 which has not landed is declared `not run` in the report, with the
reason. Sizes: 24 sealed mission families per cell by default; a cell that declares a population result needs at
least 32 independent families at α = 0.05, and 46 where four confirmatory cells share the level; a cell with fewer
declares a finite-suite result.

**3. What the published report must contain.** One table per cell: R̂, [L, U], the verdict, completion and
coverage for each arm, the tie test against arm C, N* and S for warm cells, the adjudication label. One phase
ledger per arm per cell, summing to wall-clock, with the six ceilings evaluated on the rival's ledger. Cold and
warm runs separately; the crossover reuse count; the audit of every submitted certificate by `frob-verify`; the
tie statement; the count of certificates that changed a decision. Every reported ratio carries its regime, m, n,
H, [L, U], the adjudication label and the freeze hashes.

**4. Written rules applied (no mechanism is killed at M7).** (a) The decision rule of BET-13 and its wording for
each range of the ratio: below 3 or a futility stop, `withdrawn` (KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED: the product is described for
that class by what does not depend on speed, and by its measured ratio); from 3 up to 10, or 10 and above without
the guard, `measured` and the word "ten-fold" is not used for the class; `ten-fold` only with the exact guard,
non-inferior completion and coverage (lower bound of the difference above −0.05) and Holm's procedure across at
most four confirmatory cells. (b) The tie rule of BET-14: arm C within a factor of 1.5 on the same accepted scope,
or a recovery ratio of at least 0.8 on time and on compute: say so and ship the layer over that engine for that
stratum (KILL-TENFOLD-TIE). (c) Disqualification of an arm from the scoped claim by one false certificate
(KILL-TENFOLD-CERT); voiding of cells touched by a leak (KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK). The word "ten-fold" is governed by
our arm against the stronger of the declared and the composed incumbent, never against the declared one alone.

**5. Voiding.** A tournament is voided and re-run only for a breach of protocol: a pin moved during the freeze,
sealed material read by an arm, or an arm changed after its freeze. A pin move or a toolchain train voids the runs
it spans (the freeze check of N40-07). The two statements are one rule, by the extent of the move (DESIGN, the
same in N33b-03, which owns the stack freeze, and in N39bp3-01, which classifies each breach by its extent and
writes the void records that N39b-13 acts on): with the stack freeze still open on the frozen
receipt and every affected run identified, the spanned runs and their cells are void and those cells are re-run
on fresh families; with the freeze closed as `aborted`, or a run that cannot be attributed to one receipt, the
tournament is void. This gate applies neither case; it refuses to pass while a void cell has not been re-run or
listed `not run` with its reason.

**6. The check** `tenfold07(report, kit, ledger) -> Verdict` in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/tournament/gate.rs`, the submodule `gate` of the conduct module `tournament`
that N39bp3-01 writes in `tournament.rs` (DESIGN: one file per bead, as `tenfold::hygiene::gate` is for
GATE-TENFOLD-12; an earlier text put this check in `tournament.rs` itself, which is now that bead's file):
the report exists at a public or owner-designated location with a content hash recorded in the register; every
stratum run has the fields of step 3; strata not run are listed with reasons; the kit's manifest hash is recorded;
the lock digest is equal at both ends of the window, and every run pooled into a cell carries the frozen receipt
hash, the `receipt` of the stack lock's `[freeze]` table (N33b-03); no cell is `void: stack moved`; no family of
the M3 suite appears in the sealed suite; each cell's verdict equals the rule's assignment; each outcome is an
ADR. The check reads the freeze record and the void records in the form N39bp3-01 writes them. When all of it
holds, its run writes the verdict as the gate's receipt, committed byte-identical at
`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`, and the fresh-context session enters the gate's row from it, once, with `cargo run
-p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-07 --receipt <hash>` (the one writer of a gate
row, N38b-19; this bead is the gate's one recorder); `measured` cites the event log's digest, the freeze
record, the report digest and the kit digest that N39b-13 put on file. Neither the check nor a script writes
the row.

**7. After publication.** The product is described by its measured ratio per stratum, and by auditability where
the ratio is under 3. The thesis's table of expected ranges is replaced by the measured table.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The report is published with one table per cell and one closed phase ledger per arm per cell, and the kit.
- [ ] Every pin and the toolchain were frozen for the tournament's length; no arm changed after its freeze; no
      family used at M3 is in the suite.
- [ ] Every stratum is either run with a complete record or listed `not run` with its reason.
- [ ] Each confirmatory cell's verdict equals the written rule's assignment; each outcome is an ADR in M7; at most
      four cells are confirmatory.
- [ ] Every submitted certificate was replayed by `frob-verify`; the count of certificates that changed a decision
      is in the report.
- [ ] The gate's row is `pass` once the report and kit are published, with any ratio; no comparative wording
      appears in the README or any report before that.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::tournament::gate::` (synthetic reports).

- `published_at_any_ratio_passes`: complete reports with every verdict kind → pass.
- `missing_ledger_fails`: one arm's ledger absent for one cell → fail naming it.
- `pin_move_voids`: lock digests differ across the window → the spanned runs are void and the gate cannot pass.
- `foreign_receipt_in_cell_fails`: one pooled run carrying a receipt other than the frozen one → fail naming the
  run; `void_cell_blocks_pass`: a cell still marked `void: stack moved` → the gate cannot pass.
- `family_reuse_fails`: a family identifier from the M3 suite in the M7 suite → fail.
- `word_needs_stronger_rival`: ratio 12 against the declared incumbent and 7 against the composed one → `measured`,
  the word is not used.
- `five_confirmatory_cells_refused`: five named cells → refused.
- `verdict_mismatch_fails`: a confirmatory cell with R̂ = 2.647 recorded as `measured` (the rule assigns
  `withdrawn`) → fail naming the cell, the recorded and the assigned verdict.
- `outcome_without_adr_fails`: every verdict correct but one outcome with no ADR in `docs/adr/` → fail naming it.
- `unpublished_report_not_passed`: a complete report with no published hash → `not-run`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_07.sh`: the rehearsal of the two-week freeze on a synthetic suite with public keys: freezes
pins, runs three synthetic arms interleaved, closes, reveals, generates the report and the kit, runs the check
and records its receipt with `record_gate` into a scratch copy of the gate register (never the real one);
then moves one pin mid-window and asserts the void; then plants an M3 family in the suite and a report without
its kit and asserts each is refused. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_07/<utc>.ndjson`:
per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block `{claim:
performance, class, tier, determinism, identities (suite hash, freeze receipt, arm manifests), seed}`, per cell R̂
and [L, U] beside 3 and 10, the lock digests at both ends, the freeze hashes, the report and kit hashes, and on
failure the first differing value with artifact paths; the `summary` line counts assertions and names
GATE-TENFOLD-07 and each kill rule read. No sealed content is logged before the reveal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the harness's own (six-family card R̂ = 2.647; 17 of 24 at 0.05; 18 of 24 at 0.0125). Negative
controls: an interim stop on a low number (refused: no cell is stopped on an interim number); an arm changed after
its freeze; a report published without its kit; comparative wording in the README before publication; a cell
whose recorded verdict differs from the written rule's assignment (the audit fails).

## Considerations

- The tournament needs the owner's approval of compute on both machines and an external party for replication;
  without approval fewer strata run and each is declared `not run` with the reason.
- A private repository cannot be independently confirmed; the default (private) prints that limit on the result.
- A low ratio is a result. The outcome is published with the same care either way: a losing experiment reproduces
  as readily as a winning one.
