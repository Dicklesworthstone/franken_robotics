## Background

A published ratio that nobody else can recompute is an assertion. The **replication kit** is everything needed to
recompute the tournament's result and to re-run it: a gate's result is published with its kit. GATE-TENFOLD-08 is
the program gate that tests the kit. If it is not met, **the result is labelled `unreplicated` and the word
"ten-fold" is not used**, whatever the tournament measured. Claim kind: Known answer (the recomputation is exact).

Two levels, for two different doubts. *Level 1, recompute*, answers "is the arithmetic right": from ledgers and
receipts alone, a scorer with no dependency outside the standard library reproduces every published ratio,
interval, count and verdict, bit for bit, on a clean machine. *Level 2, re-run*, answers "does it happen again":
from the frozen manifests, on hardware with a comparable fingerprint, one sampled pair per stratum is run again for
every arm. DESIGN decision: the scorer is dependency-free, like the offline certificate auditor, so that a third
party can audit it by reading it. Rejected: scoring inside the main binary, which would make replication depend on
building the system being judged.

## Scope

In: the check `tenfold08` that decides the gate from the kit and from the measurement log of the gate's script;
the receipt it writes, from which the row is recorded; the two sub-results `level1` and `level2`; the application of N39b-20's comparability function to the logged
fingerprints (the definition is N39b-20's); the entry of the
row of GATE-TENFOLD-08 in the gate register (the only entry of that row); the labelling consequence while the
row is not `pass`.

Out, with the bead that has it: running the measurement, that is building and shipping the kit, executing level
1 and level 2 on a second host, the refusal steps and the tamper plants, in `scripts/e2e/tenfold_kit.sh`
(N39b-21, a dependency; this bead runs neither level again and reads that script's log); building the kit and
the scorer (the ten-fold chapter's beads under the publication epic BD-433); the tournament itself (N40-16).

An epic is a container and nothing depends on one, so this bead waits for the kit builder (N39b-18), the level-2
re-run procedure (N39b-20) and the script that measures the gate (N39b-21); the standalone scorer (N39b-19) is
reached through them. The epics BD-432 and BD-433 are kept in `related`.

## Technical approach

**1. Kit contents (the check verifies presence and hashes).** The pre-registration and the pre-analysis. The
mission cards, the world generators and the revealed keys. The referee protocol and the adjudicator's acceptance
code. Every freeze manifest, with the scripts, wrappers, tag maps and agent guides it names. Every ledger, receipt
and agent transcript. The scorer. Hardware fingerprints and disclosure blocks. A manifest of hashes over all of it.

**2. Limits.** Incumbent code is not redistributed: the kit pins revisions and gives fetch instructions. A private
scene or dataset cannot enter a kit, and a kit built from one is refused. Every exported artifact carries the
no-authorization flags, all false.

**3. Level 1.** On a clean machine (an empty environment with a stock compiler), build the scorer from the kit and
run it over the kit's ledgers and receipts. Every published ratio, interval, count and verdict is reproduced bit
for bit. Any difference fails the level and names the first differing value.

**4. Level 2.** From the frozen manifests, on hardware whose fingerprint is comparable, one pair per stratum is
sampled by the public rule entered in the event log with the suite hash before the tournament closes, and run
again for every arm (with `frob-tenfold kit level2 <kit-dir> --party external|internal --out <dir> --json`,
the command of N39b-20, run on the second host by the script of N39b-21). TARGET: the acceptance outcome is the same and the time is within a factor of 1.5 in at
least 90% of the sampled pairs. "Comparable" is the function `comparable(gate_fp, replica_fp)` of the level-2
bead N39b-20, applied here to the two logged `frob.fingerprint/1` records, never restated: the same device name,
architecture and count per campaign and the same CPU core count; driver, runtime and compiler in the same major
release; the whole `arithmetic` block equal. The fingerprint's `identity` digest is not compared (it holds the
host and device identifiers, which differ on any other machine), and measured rates are description only.

**5. The check** `tenfold08(kit, log) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/replicate.rs`: manifest
hashes verify; no file's provenance closure contains a private mark; level 1 exact; level 2 at or above 90%.
Levels 1 and 2 are not executed by the check: it reads their outcomes from the structured log of
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_kit.sh` run on the tournament's kit (N39b-21: one assertion per published number for
level 1; one metric per re-run campaign and the share for level 2; every plant caught), verifies that the log's
kit digest is the digest of the kit at hand, and that the second host's fingerprint is comparable in the sense
of step 4.

**5a. The receipt and the row.** The log alone records nothing. The check writes its verdict as the gate's
receipt: the summary of that run of `tenfold_kit.sh`, with the kit digest, the scorer digest, the log's digest,
both hosts' fingerprints and the result of `comparable`; for `level1` the count of published numbers and the
count reproduced bit for bit; for `level2` the pairs re-run, the pairs with the same acceptance outcome and a
time inside the factor of 1.5, and the share; every plant with `caught`; and `gates_claimable`. The receipt is
committed byte-identical at `receipts/gates/<hash>.json` (N40-01) and the row is entered from it with `cargo
run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-08 --receipt <hash>` by a fresh-context
session (conventions of the register: one recorder per gate, and this bead is this gate's). The row carries
the two sub-results `level1` and `level2` as the receipt states them and passes only with both; a receipt
made from the rehearsal gate's log (`gates_claimable: false`) cannot record a pass, and a row offered with no
committed receipt is refused `evidence-unresolved`.

**6. Consequence.** While the row is not `pass`, the report generator stamps `unreplicated` on every figure of the
tournament report and the README, and the lint that guards wording refuses the word "ten-fold". With the default
answer to the adjudication question (self-adjudicated), the word additionally waits for an external party's re-run
of the kit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The kit's manifest verifies and lists every item of step 1.
- [ ] Level 1 reproduces every published number bit for bit on a clean machine with the dependency-free scorer.
- [ ] Level 2 re-runs one sampled pair per stratum for every arm, with the same acceptance outcome and a time
      within a factor of 1.5 in at least 90% of pairs (TARGET).
- [ ] A kit with a private input is refused; no incumbent code is in the kit.
- [ ] Every artifact in the kit carries the no-authorization flags, all false.
- [ ] While the gate is not passed, every figure is printed `unreplicated` and the word "ten-fold" appears nowhere.
- [ ] The row of GATE-TENFOLD-08 is entered with `record_gate` from the check's receipt, committed at
      `receipts/gates/<hash>.json`, and carries `level1` and `level2` with the kit digest, the level-1 counts and
      the level-2 share as the receipt states them; a row offered from the log alone, or from a rehearsal
      receipt, is not a pass.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::replicate::`.

- `level1_bit_exact`: a kit whose ledgers reproduce the published table → pass; one receipt with one time changed
  by 1 ms → fail naming the cell.
- `level2_threshold`: 10 sampled pairs, 9 within a factor of 1.5 with equal outcomes → pass (90%); 8 → fail.
- `outcome_must_match`: a pair within the time factor whose acceptance outcome flipped counts as a miss.
- `private_input_refused`: a kit whose closure contains a private scene mark → refused.
- `scorer_has_no_dependencies`: the scorer's manifest has an empty dependency table.
- `sample_seed_precommitted`: a level-2 sample whose seed was recorded after the close → refused.
- `unreplicated_stamp`: with the row `not-run`, the generated report contains `unreplicated` beside every ratio.
- `receipt_carries_both_levels`: the receipt written for a passing kit and log holds the kit digest, the level-1
  counts (published and reproduced, equal) and the level-2 share (9 of 10 → 0.9); the same log with its kit
  digest changed → verdict `fail`, receipt records the mismatch; a receipt with `gates_claimable: false` offered
  to the recorder for a pass → refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_08.sh`: it does not run either level itself (that is N39b-21's measurement). It invokes
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_kit.sh` (N39b-21) on the kit of the synthetic tournament of `tenfold_07.sh`, which builds the
kit, runs level 1 in an emptied environment and level 2 on one sampled pair per synthetic stratum, and writes its
structured log; then it runs the check `tenfold08` on that kit and log, which writes the verdict receipt, and
asserts the verdict, the two sub-results, the kit digest, the level-1 counts and the level-2 share in the
receipt; it offers the receipt to `record_gate` against a scratch copy of the gate register (never the real
one) and asserts that, being a rehearsal receipt (`gates_claimable: false`), it is refused as a pass. It then replays, on copies, the check's own refusals: a log whose kit digest differs from the kit at
hand; a level-2 share of 8 of 10; a rehearsal log (`gates_claimable: false`) offered for a pass; a second-host
fingerprint of another device family; a kit with a private mark; and asserts each is refused with its reason, and
that with the row `not-run` the regenerated report stamps `unreplicated` and the wording lint refuses "ten-fold".
Logs through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_08/<utc>.ndjson`: per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block `{claim: known-answer, class, tier,
determinism, identities (kit digest, scorer digest, both host fingerprints), seed}`, the counts of level-1
assertions read from the log, the level-2 time ratios beside 1.5 and the share beside 90%, manifest and kit
hashes, and on failure the first differing value with paths; the `summary` line counts assertions and names
GATE-TENFOLD-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the published table itself (level 1 is equality). Negative controls: a kit with a private input
(refused); a scorer that links the main crates (refused by the dependency check); a published number edited by
hand after scoring (level 1 fails); a level-2 pair chosen after seeing which pairs replicate (refused by the
precommitted seed).

## Considerations

- "Comparable hardware" is judged by fingerprint, not by model name alone; a third party without an RTX 4090 (one
  per campaign, the gate's matched-device envelope) can run level 1 and records level 2 with the status
  `incomplete` of N39b-20's replication record, so the row cannot pass on that party's run.
- Level 2's 90% is a TARGET on a small sample; its count is reported, not just pass or fail.
- Repository visibility is an owner question; default private, with the kit and the certificate auditor as the
  artifacts prepared for a third party.
