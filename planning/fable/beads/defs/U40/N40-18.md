## Background

**The ceiling is computed before any speed test.** Before a speed comparison is locked, the largest ratio it could
possibly show is computed from the work that both arms share or that the mechanism does not touch, and the test is
refused if that ceiling is below the ratio it sets out to show. The rule comes from a recorded mistake (FACT, a
paper-scale pilot recorded in the program's review log): a specialized solver was compared with a composed one and measured 1.345 times
faster warm and 1.088 cold; the faster arm's batch held a residual of 575.7 µs that the solver does not touch
(parsing, assembly, checking, serialization and harness, not separately timed), so a solver of zero cost could have
reached about 1.46 at most (842.8 ÷ 575.7). The experiment could not have confirmed the claim it was run to test,
and nobody had computed that beforehand.

GATE-TENFOLD-09 makes the rule program-wide and recurring. It is one of the four gates re-run at every milestone
exit after its first. It has two clauses: every work package funded as a speed lever cites a removable share of at
least 10% on a `ledger-closed` run of the strong baseline; and every registered speed comparison records its
ceiling and is refused when the ceiling is below the ratio it sets out to show. If it is not met, the speed
comparison is refused and the work package loses its speed justification (kill gate KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER). Claim
kind: Performance. Decision of record: ADR-GAUNT-05, ceiling first.

## Technical approach

**1. The criterion (what the register row states).** Two clauses.

*Clause A, work packages.* For every work package whose recorded justification is `speed-lever`: a removable
share p ≥ 0.10 on a `ledger-closed` run of the strong baseline (exclusive phases sum to the clock within 2%; the
rival's ledger at M1; our own profile as well from M2). Below 0.10 its whole effect is at most 1/0.9 = 1.11: the
justification is changed to `trust`, `coverage` or `correctness` with that reason written and no speed figure
attached, or the package is not funded (KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER; applied on the rival's ledger at M1 and re-read on
ours at M2).

*Clause B, comparisons.* Every registered speed comparison carries, before it runs, the ratio it sets out to
show, the identifier of a `ledger-closed` run of the strong baseline, and one of: p (the share of the baseline's
restricted time the mechanism removes entirely), giving the ceiling 1/(1 − p); or (f, r, h) (the share improved,
the improvement factor, the added overhead, all measured on the baseline's critical path), giving the ceiling
1/(1 − f + f/r + h). The other ceilings apply where relevant: an unremovable floor u gives
R ≤ RMT_baseline ÷ u; a missed-deadline share q gives R ≤ 1/q; the horizon gives R ≤ H ÷ RMT_ours. If the
ceiling is below the ratio, the comparison is **refused** before it runs, with the ceiling printed. A comparison
with no baseline ledger, or with a `ledger-open` baseline, is refused. **The order rule**: a pruning ratio is
never multiplied by a speed-up measured on work the pruning already removed; in a product of levers each
factor's quantities are measured on the time the earlier factors left, and a registration that violates this is
refused.

How those quantities are stored and checked is N39a-38's: the attachment
`tenfold/ceilings/<comparison-id>.json` holds the ratio as `goal_ratio`, the baseline as `baseline.ledgers` and
the quantities as `basis`, and the refusals carry the reasons of the ten-fold refusal registry (N39-REF), all
under the product code FROB-REF-866: `ceiling-below-goal`; `ceiling-without-baseline` and
`ceiling-needs-closed-ledger`; `order-rule-violation`. DESIGN: this bead uses those field and reason names and
defines none of its own (an earlier draft here called them `claimed_ratio`, `ceiling-below-claim`,
`no-closed-baseline` and `factor-on-removed-work`; one refusal must have one name, and the registry's is the
name of record).

**2. The verdict** `tenfold09(record, register) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/ceiling.rs`,
where `record` is the measured record `frob.tenfold.gate09/1` that N39a-38 wrote at this exit's commit. The gate
passes at an exit when: (i) `clause_a.failing` is 0: no work package is `speed-lever` with p < 0.10 or with no
cited closed ledger; (ii) every speed comparison registered since the last exit carries its ceiling, and
`clause_b.attached_after_first_receipt` is 0 (the ceiling was recorded before the first run receipt);
(iii) every comparison whose ceiling was below its goal was refused and none ran, and
`clause_b.cited_while_refused` is 0; (iv) the refusal is live, shown by the record's controls: the negative
controls below were each refused at this commit. A record written at another commit than the exit's is not
accepted.

**3. Consequence.** If the gate is not met, the speed comparison at fault is refused and the work package loses
its speed justification: its funding record (N00-09) is re-justified without a speed figure or the package is
unfunded, and the kill decision is recorded (KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER, N39a-39). The exit report states which.

**4. Record.** Register row GATE-TENFOLD-09, kind `program`, claim `performance`, `recurring = true`, first part
at M1 and one part at every later exit (M2 to M8). Each part cites the measured record by hash and carries
`measured`: counts of packages checked, comparisons checked and refused, and the smallest margin among the
comparisons that ran, where the margin is the quotient ceiling ÷ `goal_ratio` as the attachment defines it
(DESIGN: an earlier draft here reported the difference C − claim; one number must have one definition).

**5. Reports.** A speed figure in any report whose comparison is not in the experiment registry with an admitted
attachment is refused by the report generator; the check is part of the exit report's regeneration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every `speed-lever` work package cites p ≥ 0.10 on a closed ledger; any below is re-justified without a
      speed figure or unfunded, with the kill decision recorded.
- [ ] Every registered speed comparison has its ceiling recorded before its first run receipt.
- [ ] A comparison with ceiling below its claimed ratio is refused before it runs, at every exit's commit.
- [ ] The recorded pilot's case is reproduced as a known answer in the measured record's controls: residual
      575.7, total 842.8, ceiling 1.46 (to two places), goal above 1.46 → refused.
- [ ] The gate has a fresh part at each exit from M1 to M8, each recorded by the exit review's session and each
      citing a measured record of N39a-38 written at that exit's commit.
- [ ] A speed figure in any report whose comparison is not in the registry is refused by the report generator.
- [ ] This bead writes no ceiling, no attachment and no measured value of its own: every number in a part is
      read from the measured record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::ceiling::verdict`.

- `verdict_passes_on_clean_record`: a record with no failing package, every comparison attached before its first
  receipt, the refused ones not cited and all controls caught → pass.
- `verdict_fails_on_failing_package`: `clause_a.failing = 1` (a package at p = 0.099) → fail, naming it; at
  p = 0.100 the record has none failing and the verdict passes.
- `verdict_fails_on_late_ceiling`: `attached_after_first_receipt = 1` → fail.
- `verdict_fails_on_cited_refused`: `cited_while_refused = 1` → fail.
- `verdict_fails_when_control_missed`: the control "goal 2.0 against a ceiling of 1.46" recorded as admitted →
  fail (the refusal is not live).
- `record_from_other_commit_rejected`.
- `recurring_parts`: the row has parts at M1…M8.
- `margin_is_quotient`: a record whose smallest ceiling is 2.2 against a goal of 2 reports 1.1.
- `unregistered_figure_refused`: a report line with a speed ratio and no registry entry → refused.

The arithmetic and refusal tests this bead once listed (`pilot_ceiling`, `remove_phase`, `improve_part`,
`lever_floor`, `open_baseline_refused`, `order_rule`, `ceiling_before_first_receipt`) test the measurement and
are in N39a-38 under the same names (`lever_floor` as its `funding_threshold`, `order_rule` as its
`order_rule_pruning` and `order_rule_sequence`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_09.sh`: at the current commit it runs the measurement script of N39a-38
(`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate09_ceiling.sh`, which registers the synthetic comparisons and the planted `speed-lever`
package at p = 0.06 and writes the measured record), evaluates `tenfold09` on that record, regenerates the exit
report and asserts the gate's part for the current milestone, and asserts that a report line with an
unregistered speed figure is refused; then offers a copy of the record with one control marked admitted and a
copy written at another commit, and asserts the verdict fails on each. It registers no comparison of its own.
Logs through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_09/<utc>.ndjson`: per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block `{claim: performance, class, tier,
determinism, identities (commit, baseline ledger identifiers), seed}`, the record's hash, the four verdict
conditions with their values, per comparison the ceiling beside the goal ratio and the margin, per package p
beside 0.10, the ledger closure residual beside 2%, and on failure the first offending entry with its path; the
`summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-09 and KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 1.46 from 842.8 and 575.7 with measured 1.345 warm and 1.088 cold; 1/0.9 = 1.11. Negative controls
(kept in the control registry, run by the measurement at every exit, and required by the verdict's condition
(iv)): a comparison claiming 2.0 with a ceiling of 1.46 (must be refused); a ceiling computed from the
candidate's own ledger instead of the strong baseline's (must be refused); a `speed-lever` package at p = 0.06
(must fail clause A). Control of the verdict itself: a record in which one of those controls is marked admitted
must fail the gate.

## Considerations

- The gate judges whether a test *could* show its claim, not whether the mechanism is good. A mechanism refused as
  a speed lever may be the most valuable thing in its package for trust or coverage.
- "The strong baseline" is the strongest composed incumbent, warm; measuring the ceiling on a weak baseline
  inflates it.
- From M2 the check runs on two ledgers (the rival's and ours); a lever must clear 10% on the ledger that funds it.
- The order-rule refusal has one name, `order-rule-violation` (the ten-fold refusal registry's), used by this
  bead, N39a-38 and N40-13; the earlier name `factor-on-removed-work` appears in no bead.
