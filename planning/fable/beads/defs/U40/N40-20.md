## Background

A comparison is only as good as its rival. The easiest way to win a ratio is to race a rival that nobody tuned, and
the second easiest is to keep adjusting the rival's configuration until the numbers look right. The **rival audit**
closes both doors: before any sealed mission is opened, each rival arm is frozen; its configuration, exact
revisions, licence, wrappers, tolerances and optimizer scripts are committed to the experiment pack, *after* a
competent baseline engineer has improved the fastest rival on the development fixtures; and each arm receives the
same calibration data, hardware envelope, reusable prior answers, cache budget and access to the checker.

GATE-TENFOLD-11 is the program gate that checks the audit is complete before sealing: every arm's frozen
configuration reproduces its own development-fixture figure within 10% (TARGET) on the tournament hardware; the
baseline engineer's sign-off and the digest of the committed pack are recorded; no arm's configuration changes
afterwards, and a changed digest voids the arm. If the gate is not met, the suite is not sealed. It is due at M3
for the partial gate and again at M7. Claim kind: Known answer. DESIGN: the most serious rival may be a short
analytic program wrapped around a mature simulator; it is always an arm.

## Scope

In: the completeness check of the pack; the 10% reproduction check; the sign-off; the digest lock; the record at
M3 and at M7. Out: building and tuning the rival arms (seeds BD-315, BD-430); the M0 pinning of incumbents
(GATE-TENFOLD-01, bead N40-12); the sealing order (GATE-TENFOLD-04, bead N40-14).

## Technical approach

**1. Rival classes, and what the candidate must earn against each** (the audit pins a named system and revision
for each class; names come from the source register and are re-read at the audit).

| Problem | Strongest rival to compose | What the candidate must earn |
|---|---|---|
| GPU learning with sensing, control and transfer | The leading batched engines with their training stacks and system-identification workflows | More accepted designs per unit time, counting setup, training and independent evidence |
| Industrial and rich-world simulation | The industrial stacks and the batched manipulation engines | A checked family answer across several contact or sensing regimes at competitive total cost, at fixed scene diversity and sensor resolution |
| Joint synthesis of body and control | Grammar-based morphology search, differentiable co-design and learned design generators | A cheaper design under the same sealed constraints, or stricter checkable coverage at matched cost; the rival receives the same grammar, masses, symmetry knowledge and controller budget |
| Certified geometry, adversarial scenes, robust design | Certified configuration-space regions; a scenario language; linear and mixed-integer programs; interval, reachability and satisfiability tools | More of the mission decided with correct quantifiers and interpretable counterexamples |
| Digital twins and systems engineering | A multibody systems-engineering tool with a skilled author; an agent-built twin | Less engineering time from a changed mission to a reconciled portable answer |

**2. What the pack must contain, per arm.** The configuration; exact revisions; the licence; wrappers; tolerances;
optimizer scripts; the development-fixture figure the arm's advocates measured (with its ledger); the record of
equal provisioning (calibration data, hardware envelope, reusable prior answers, cache budget, checker access).

**3. Protocol controls the audit records.** Missions are paired across arms with isolated transcripts. Order,
operator, site and resource assignment are randomized and counterbalanced. Agent arms start from fresh contexts on
the same model revision, with the same caps on model calls and parallel workers; human operators are matched for
proficiency. An adjudicator who does not know which system proposed an answer releases paired receipts after both
arms close. Where a platform lacks a native path, a best-supported trial and a matched-device trial both run, and
a missing cell is published as `unavailable`. The fastest qualifying rival, all arms and the raw pairings are
published; a per-case best-of-rivals ensemble is reported only as a labelled extra lower bound on the ratio.

**4. The reproduction check.** For each arm a, with d_a its recorded development-fixture figure and m_a the figure
measured from the frozen configuration on the tournament hardware: |m_a − d_a| / d_a ≤ 0.10 (TARGET). An arm
outside the band is re-examined: either its frozen configuration is not the one that was tuned (a packaging
defect) or the tournament hardware differs from the development hardware; neither may be resolved by changing the
figure.

**5. Sign-off and lock.** The baseline engineer's sign-off (identity, date, statement that the fastest rival was
improved on development fixtures and that no further improvement is known to them) and the BLAKE3 digest of the
committed pack are recorded in the sealing ledger before step 2 of sealing (the suite hash). Afterwards no arm's
configuration changes: the harness recomputes the pack digest at the start of every run, and **a changed digest
voids the arm**.

**6. The check** `tenfold11(pack, ledger) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/rival_audit.rs`;
register row GATE-TENFOLD-11, kind `program`, claim `known-answer`, parts at M3 and M7 (the audit is refreshed for
the tournament; the M3 record does not carry over). `measured`: per arm, d_a, m_a and the relative difference.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every rival arm has the seven items of step 2 in the committed pack; the equal-provisioning record covers
      all five resources.
- [ ] Each arm's frozen configuration reproduces its development-fixture figure within 10% on the tournament
      hardware.
- [ ] The sign-off and the pack digest are in the sealing ledger before the suite hash.
- [ ] A run started with a pack whose digest differs from the recorded one is refused and the arm is marked void.
- [ ] A short analytic program over a mature simulator is present as an arm in every stratum where one can be
      written.
- [ ] The gate is recorded at M3 before the partial gate's suite is sealed and again at M7 before the
      tournament's.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::rival_audit::`.

- `within_ten_percent`: d = 1.00 h, m = 1.09 h → pass; m = 1.11 h → fail; m = 0.89 h → fail (faster than tuned is
  also outside the band and is investigated).
- `missing_item_fails`: pack without optimizer scripts → fail naming the item and the arm.
- `signoff_before_suite_hash`: sign-off time after the suite hash → refused.
- `digest_change_voids`: one byte of a wrapper changed after the lock → run refused, arm void.
- `unequal_provisioning_fails`: one arm with a larger cache budget → fail.
- `m3_record_does_not_cover_m7`: only the M3 part recorded → the M7 pre-opening verdict is blocked.
- `ensemble_is_labelled`: a best-of-rivals figure without its label → refused by the report generator.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_11.sh`: builds a synthetic pack for two rival arms with recorded development figures;
measures both from the frozen configurations through the harness; records the sign-off and the digest; runs the
check; then edits a wrapper and asserts the void. Logs: command, exit code, wall time, per arm d, m and the
relative difference beside 10%, the pack digest before and after, the ledger step times, and on failure the first
differing item with its path. The summary names GATE-TENFOLD-11.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the band arithmetic. Negative controls: a rival configured cold where a warm configuration was
tuned; a pack edited after the lock; a strawman arm with no baseline engineer's sign-off; an arm given a smaller
hardware envelope than ours.

## Considerations

- OPEN: who the rival advocates and the baseline engineer are, and their budget. Default: agent sessions that have
  contributed nothing to this project, 40 device-hours per stratum, and the label `rival: internally advocated` on
  every result.
- Incumbent licences may forbid publishing a comparison; such an incumbent is replaced by the next strongest and
  the replacement is printed.
- The 10% band is a TARGET; it is fixed before the measurement and changes only by a dated amendment.
