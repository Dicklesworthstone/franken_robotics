## Background

A comparison is only as good as its rival. The easiest way to win a ratio is to race a rival that nobody tuned, and
the second easiest is to keep adjusting the rival's configuration until the numbers look right. The **rival audit**
closes both doors: before any sealed mission is opened, each rival arm is frozen; its configuration, exact
revisions, licence, wrappers, tolerances and optimizer scripts are committed to the experiment pack, *after* a
competent baseline engineer has improved the fastest rival on the development fixtures; and each arm receives the
same calibration data, hardware envelope, reusable prior answers, cache budget and access to the checker.

GATE-TENFOLD-11 is the program gate that checks the audit is complete before sealing: every arm's frozen configuration
reproduces its own development-fixture figure within 10% (TARGET) on the tournament hardware; the baseline engineer's
sign-off and the digest of the committed pack are recorded; no arm's configuration changes afterwards, and a changed
digest voids the arm. If the gate is not met, the suite is not sealed: its opening, the step that completes the
sealing, is refused (N39b-37). It is due at M3 for the partial gate and again at M7. Claim kind: Known answer. DESIGN:
the most serious rival may be a short analytic program wrapped around a mature simulator; it is always an arm.

## Scope

In: the program gate's check and record: the function `tenfold11(pack, ledger) -> Verdict`, which reads the audit's
pack and the sealing ledger and decides completeness of the pack, the 10% band on the recorded figures, the order of
the sign-off and digest (after every arm's freeze entry, before the opening), and the digest lock; the register row
with its parts at M3 and at M7, and the recording of the M3 part. The protocol controls of step 3 are listed for
context only: they are built and recorded by N39b-38 (run plan, isolation, blind scoring, publication set and ensemble
label) under GATE-TENFOLD-04, -06 and -07, and the only part of them this check reads is the equal-inputs declaration
of step 2.

Out, with the bead that has each: the audit itself, that is the register of rival classes, the experiment pack
with its contents, layout and digest, the reproduction run that produces the rerun figure, the band function,
the equal-inputs declaration, the sign-off entry, the void rule with its two refusals and the M7 refresh
procedure (N39b-37, a dependency: this bead measures nothing and builds no pack); building and tuning the rival
arms (seeds BD-315, BD-430); the M0 pinning of incumbents
(GATE-TENFOLD-01, bead N40-12); the sealing order (GATE-TENFOLD-04, bead N40-14).

## Technical approach

**1. Rival classes.** The register of rival classes and of what the candidate must earn against each (five
problem classes, from batched GPU learning engines to systems-engineering tools) is the audit's, in N39b-37, where
each class is pinned to a named system and revision. The gate checks that every stratum's frozen rival names its
class from that register and that the short-analytic-program arm is present; it keeps no second table.

**2. What the pack must contain, per arm.** The pack is N39b-37's (`tenfold/<gate>/pack/`), and the gate checks
it against that bead's definition: for every arm and stratum the freeze manifest and every file it names, which
are the configuration; exact revisions and container digests; the licence reading; wrappers and tag maps;
numerical tolerances; optimizer and outer-loop scripts; the agent guide; the tuning log; and the development
scores, that is the development-fixture figure the arm's advocates measured (with its ledger); plus the
equal-inputs declaration (calibration data, hardware envelope, reusable prior answers, cache budget, checker
access) and the sign-off. (An earlier draft of this bead listed seven items; the nine kinds above are the
audit's list and the one the check uses.)

**3. Protocol controls (context; built and recorded by N39b-38, not checked here).** Missions are paired across arms with isolated transcripts. Order,
operator, site and resource assignment are randomized and counterbalanced. Agent arms start from fresh contexts on
the same model revision, with the same caps on model calls and parallel workers; human operators are matched for
proficiency. An adjudicator who does not know which system proposed an answer releases paired receipts after both
arms close. Where a platform lacks a native path, a best-supported trial and a matched-device trial both run, and
a missing cell is published as `unavailable`. The fastest qualifying rival, all arms and the raw pairings are
published; a per-case best-of-rivals ensemble is reported only as a labelled extra lower bound on the ratio.

**4. The reproduction check.** For each arm a, with d_a its recorded development-fixture figure and m_a the figure
the audit's reproduction run measured from the frozen configuration on the tournament hardware (N39b-37, step 4;
this bead reads both from the record): |m_a − d_a| / d_a ≤ 0.10 (TARGET), by the audit's function
`within_band`. An arm outside the band is re-examined: either its frozen configuration is not the one that was
tuned (a packaging defect) or the tournament hardware differs from the development hardware. DESIGN (one rule,
stated identically in N39b-37): a miss is never resolved by editing the declared figure. The configuration is
corrected, its development fixtures are run again, and that run produces a new declaration with its own ledger
entry, before sign-off and never after.

**5. Sign-off and lock.** The baseline engineer's sign-off (identity, date, statement that the fastest rival was
improved on development fixtures and that no further improvement is known to them) and the BLAKE3 digest of the
committed pack are one `audit-record` entry of sealing step 5, after the `freeze` entry (step 4) of every arm and
stratum the pack names and before the opening (step 6): arms are frozen only after the suite hash (step 2), and the
pack holds their freeze manifests, so no sign-off can precede the suite hash (N39b-37, which writes the entry with
`frob-tenfold seal step 5 --kind audit-record`; the ledger refuses it earlier with `seal-out-of-order`, or
`freeze-missing` for an arm with no freeze entry). This check verifies its presence, its order and that the digest it
records is the pack's. Afterwards no arm's configuration changes: the harness recomputes the pack digest at the start
of every run of a campaign and at close, and **a changed digest voids the arm** (the audit's refusal
`tenfold.frozen-arm-changed`, exit 9; N39-REF registers `arm-changed` only as an alias that is never emitted, so the
check and its tests match the emitted id; sealing without the entry is the audit's `tenfold.audit-incomplete`, exit
3).

**6. The check** `tenfold11(pack, ledger) -> Verdict` in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/gates/tenfold11.rs`,
calling the audit's module `tenfold::rival_audit` (N39b-37's file `rival_audit.rs`, which an earlier draft of this
bead also named as its own); register row GATE-TENFOLD-11, kind `program`, claim `known-answer`, parts at M3 and M7
(the audit is refreshed for the tournament; the M3 record does not carry over). `measured`: per arm, d_a, m_a and
the relative difference. Each part has one writer (N38b-19): the run of `tenfold11` on the suite's pack and
ledger writes its verdict as the part's receipt (the gate line `pass` or `fail`, the `measured` string, the pack
digest, the suite hash and the control TF-CX-20 with `caught`), the receipt is committed byte-identical at
`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`, and a fresh-context session that built no arm and no pack records the part with
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-11 --receipt <hash>` after the
`audit-record` entry and before the opening. This bead records the M3 part on the partial gate's suite. The M7
part is recorded the same way on the tournament's refreshed pack, at the tournament's step 5 (N39b-13, M7), with
this check unchanged; this bead does not wait for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every rival arm has the items of step 2 in the committed pack; the equal-provisioning record covers
      all five resources.
- [ ] Each arm's frozen configuration reproduces its development-fixture figure within 10% on the tournament
      hardware.
- [ ] The sign-off and the pack digest are one `audit-record` entry after every arm's freeze entry and before
      the opening; the check fails on a ledger whose entry precedes a freeze entry or is absent at the opening.
- [ ] A run started with a pack whose digest differs from the recorded one is refused and the arm is marked void.
- [ ] A short analytic program over a mature simulator is present as an arm in every stratum where one can be
      written.
- [ ] The M3 part is recorded with `record_gate` from the check's receipt before the partial gate's suite is
      opened, by a session that built no arm and no pack; a verdict that is not `pass` records `fail`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::gates::tenfold11::`.

- `verdict_applies_band`: d = 1.00 h, m = 1.09 h → pass; m = 1.11 h → fail; m = 0.89 h → fail (faster than tuned is
  also outside the band and is investigated). The band function itself is tested in N39b-37.
- `missing_item_fails`: pack without optimizer scripts → fail naming the item and the arm.
- `signoff_after_freezes`: an `audit-record` entry before the `freeze` entry of one arm → fail naming the arm
  (the ledger's `seal-out-of-order`); after every freeze entry and before the opening → pass; an opening with no
  `audit-record` entry → fail (`tenfold.audit-incomplete`).
- `digest_change_voids`: one byte of a wrapper changed after the lock → run refused with
  `tenfold.frozen-arm-changed`, exit 9, arm void; the alias `arm-changed` never appears in the output.
- `unequal_provisioning_fails`: one arm with a larger cache budget → fail.
- `m3_record_does_not_cover_m7`: only the M3 part recorded → the M7 pre-opening verdict is blocked.
- `rival_class_and_short_program_arm`: a stratum whose frozen rival names no class of the audit's register →
  fail naming the stratum; a stratum without the short-analytic-program arm, where the register says one can be
  written → fail naming the stratum.

The label of the best-of-rivals ensemble is a property of the publication set and is tested by N39b-38
(`ensemble_is_min_over_rivals_per_pair`), not here.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_11.sh`: with the audit's commands (N39b-37) it builds a synthetic pack for two rival arms with
recorded development figures, runs the reproduction from the frozen configurations and records the sign-off and the
digest; then it runs the check; then it edits a wrapper and asserts the void with `tenfold.frozen-arm-changed` and
exit 9; then, on scratch copies of the ledger and pack, it places the `audit-record` entry before one arm's freeze
entry, gives one arm a larger cache budget, and removes the short-analytic-program arm from one stratum, and asserts
each is refused or failed with the item named. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_11/<utc>.ndjson`: per step `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall
time, the evidence block `{claim: known-answer, class, tier, determinism, identities (pack digest, arm revisions),
seed}`, per arm d, m and the relative difference beside 10%, the pack digest before and after, the ledger step times,
and on failure the first differing item with its path; the `summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-11.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the band arithmetic. Negative controls: the registered TF-CX-20 of N39-CX (a pack digest changed after
sign-off, plant `pack_digest_changed` of N39b-37), caught in the same run as each recorded part; a rival configured
cold where a warm configuration was tuned; a strawman arm with no baseline engineer's sign-off; an arm given a smaller
hardware envelope than ours.

## Considerations

- OPEN-TENFOLD-3 (decided by N39b-28, a dependency): who the rival advocates and the baseline engineer are, and their
  budget. Default: agent sessions that have contributed nothing to this project, 40 device-hours per stratum, and the
  label `rival: internally advocated` on every result.
- Incumbent licences may forbid publishing a comparison; such an incumbent is replaced by the next strongest and
  the replacement is printed.
- The 10% band is a TARGET; it is fixed before the measurement and changes only by a dated amendment.
