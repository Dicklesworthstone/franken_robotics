## Background

A **kill gate** is the written exit of a bet: a trigger and a fallback, named before the measurement, decided at a
named milestone and recorded as an ADR. The **fallback ledger** is the register's list of every kill gate, once.
The chapter units write the decision bead for each gate; this ledger is the independent copy those beads are
checked against (N40-30), so that no trigger or fallback is quietly re-scoped.

This bead carries the second of four groups: **the body**, the engine's physics, scenes, sensors, GPU backends,
numerics and performance. It holds the four decisions on which the engine rests and which define the M2 exit: the
kernel toolchain (KILL-KIR), the contact law (KILL-CONTACT), the Metal tier (KILL-METAL) and mixed batches
(KILL-PHYLUM with KILL-BUCKET). Reading the columns: **Decided** is the milestone at whose exit the decision is
recorded as an ADR; for a gate whose trigger is a pilot, the earliest milestone at which the work package can be
funded. **Owner** governs trigger and fallback; a subsection in parentheses is a consumer. The first decisions of
this group fall at M2, so the rows are entered at M1, before the measurements they govern.

## Scope

One record per kill gate, written once, and for these thirty-five it is written here.

In: the thirty-five rows as kill records in `registries/gates.toml` (format: N40-03), each with `trigger`,
`fallback`, `decided`, `mode`, owner, consumers and `reads`, with the notes of step 3; the `scope_kind` and
`reread` fields of the rows decided per scope or more than once (step 1a); seven rows the plan's table does not
hold, entered by `introduce` amendments (step 1b).

Out, and which bead has it:

- Measurements and decisions: the decision bead of each kill gate, written by the unit that owns its subsection.
- The other groups of the ledger: N40-23, N40-25 to N40-29.
- The chapters' side of these rows, where a chapter has a register bead of its own: N16c-05 (Kinesis:
  KILL-SHADOW, KILL-VARIATIONAL, KILL-TREEOP), N19c-29 (Habitat: its ten rows here, from KILL-LIVING to
  KILL-BANK-BREADTH), N20b-21 (Sensorium: KILL-ALIAS, KILL-SCHED, KILL-EXPOSURE) and N21c-32 (Spark: KILL-KIR,
  KILL-BUCKET, KILL-D1, KILL-METAL, KILL-FLEET, KILL-SPECIALIZE, KILL-MEGA). Each keeps the map from its
  chapter's kill gates to their decision beads and checks that each has this ledger's record and exactly one
  owner declaration. None creates a kill record. N28c-25 (Atelier) states KILL-FDGRAD as a consumer.
