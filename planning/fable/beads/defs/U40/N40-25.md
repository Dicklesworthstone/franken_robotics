## Background

A **kill gate** is the written exit of a bet: a trigger and a fallback, named before the measurement, decided at a
named milestone and recorded as an ADR. The **fallback ledger** is the register's list of every kill gate, once;
the chapter units' decision beads are checked against it (N40-30).

The register's third table, "the mind", has 45 kill gates. This bead carries its first part: the 17 gates of
estimation and control (Reflex, with gaits and shields), learning (Cortex) and tasks and requirements (Arena). They
include the gates that decide the lever "train few" (KILL-FAMILY, KILL-INHERIT, KILL-DISTILL) and the M4 variant
with no family policy. The second part (mission queries, adversaries, autopsy, the studio) is N40-26. Reading the
columns: **Decided** is the milestone at whose exit the decision is recorded as an ADR; for a pilot-triggered gate,
the earliest milestone at which the work can be funded. **Owner** governs trigger and fallback.

## Scope

In: the seventeen rows as kill records in `registries/gates.toml` (format: N40-03), with decision points and
notes. Out: measurements and decisions (chapter units); the other groups.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance register::ledger::mind1::`: `seventeen_rows`; `text_equals_seed`;
`decided_by_exit` (1, 3, 10, 3); `family_trigger_is_conjunction` (no-adaptation return below 70% on 60% of the set
with fine-tuning at 15% of steps → not fired; with fine-tuning at 25% → fired); `distill_gap` (student at 80% of
teacher → GATE-CORTEX-14 `fail`, KILL-DISTILL not fired; at 65% → fired); `inherit_two_clauses` (median cost 65% →
fired; median 30% with 25% of edits more than 5% worse → fired; median 30% with 10% → not fired);
`altalgo_per_algorithm` (two algorithms proposed → two decision records).

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/kill_ledger.sh` (bead N40-30); for this group it logs the seventeen identifiers, seeded
hashes, decision exits and modes, and on failure the first differing row.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 17 rows; thresholds 50% (KILL-RESIDUAL), 70% (KILL-DISTILL), 70% and 20% (KILL-FAMILY), 60%, 5% and
20% (KILL-INHERIT), two times (KILL-LEARNKERN), 80% (KILL-SPEC-REWARD's margin-only clause). Negative controls:
RC-10, RC-11, RC-38, and RC-40 (KILL-FAMILY evaluated as a disjunction, which would fire it on one clause alone).

## Considerations

- KILL-FAMILY and KILL-RANK (in N40-26) together decide whether the lever "train few" exists; if both fire, the
  ceilings are recomputed and the M7 suite is re-stratified before sealing.
- Several gates here have a gap between the gate's threshold and the kill trigger; inside the gap the capability is
  partial and described as partial.
- KILL-GYMADAPT changes the rival's access path in the ten-fold test, so its decision must precede the freeze of
  the rival arm.
