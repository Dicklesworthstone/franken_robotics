## Background

The program is built on 37 **bets**: explicit hypotheses, each with a falsifier, about what will turn out to be
true (the algebra pays for itself; one source runs on three backends; a family policy walks unseen designs; the
sealed ten-fold ratio holds). A bet that nothing decides is a belief. The register therefore pairs every bet with
the **gates that decide it**, the **kill gate** that fires if it fails, and the **milestone** at which that happens.
A bet with no kill gate has its consequence written with the bet.

This bead enters that pairing as data and checks it at every exit: a bet whose deciding gates are due and recorded
gets a status (`held`, `falsified`, `untested`); a bet whose deciding gate has no record at its milestone is
reported; and no gate is cited as deciding a bet unless the register says so. It is the bridge between the thesis
(the bets, owned by the unit that writes the bet beads) and the delivery layer (the gates and kill gates).

## Scope

In: the 37 rows; the status computation; the exit-time check. Out: the bets' own statements and falsifiers (their
beads); the kill ledgers (N40-23 to N40-28); the fallback exits per milestone (N40-32).

Each bet is decided by a recorded gate. A bet with no kill gate (BET-08, BET-10, BET-12, BET-21) has its
consequence written with the bet and repeated in the row.

**2. Status of a bet at an exit** (`bet_status(bet, register, exit) -> Status`):

- `not-due`: no deciding gate has a part due at or before this exit.
- `untested`: a deciding gate's part is due and is `not-run` (for example under a variant: BET-15 with no compute
  envelope); the bet is recorded as untested with the reason.
- `held`: every deciding part due so far is `pass` and no kill gate of the row has fired.
- `falsified`: a kill gate of the row is `fired`; or, for a bet with no kill gate, a deciding gate is `fail` and
  the written consequence has been applied by ADR.
- `partly-falsified`: some kill gates of the row fired and others did not (BET-29, BET-36, BET-30 have several);
  the status lists which.
- `open`: a deciding part is `fail` and neither a kill decision nor the written consequence is on record. This
  status blocks the exit.

Notes that the status computation must respect: for BET-13, "held" needs at least one named stratum with the
verdict `ten-fold`; a `measured` verdict between 3 and 10 is neither held nor falsified for that stratum, and
`withdrawn` falsifies it for that class only. BET-14 is neither met nor failed where the engine's advantage over
the ablation arm is not established. BET-34 is falsified per robot class where referee coverage is below 90%.
BET-12 follows the schedule gate's three branches (N40-11). BET-04 is re-read at every exit while KILL-FSIM is
rolling.

**3. Checks at every exit.** (a) Every bet whose `at` includes this milestone has a status other than `open`.
(b) Every deciding gate named exists in the register, and every kill gate named exists in a ledger. (c) No bet
bead or report cites a gate as deciding a bet unless this table lists it. (d) The README's bets table is generated
from these rows and the statuses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 37 bet rows load; every identifier in `deciding_gates` and `kill_gates` resolves to a register row.
- [ ] Exactly four rows have no kill gate, each with its written consequence.
- [ ] `bet_status` returns the stated status on each fixture of the test plan.
- [ ] An `open` bet blocks the exit report; a `falsified` bet prints its fallback's name.
- [ ] The README's bets table regenerates from the rows; a hand edit fails `cargo xtask check-generated`.
- [ ] At each exit the set of bets with a decision due equals the `at` column (M0: 2; M1: 3; M2: 7; M3: 20;
      M4: 14; M5: 3; M6: 1; M7: 2; M8: 1, counting a bet at every milestone its row names).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance register::bets::`.

- `thirty_seven_rows`; `four_without_kill_gate` (BET-08, BET-10, BET-12, BET-21).
- `all_identifiers_resolve`: over the rows, 0 dangling.
- `status_held`: BET-09 with GATE-SPARK-04 `pass`, KILL-METAL `not-fired` → `held`.
- `status_falsified`: KILL-METAL `fired` → `falsified`, fallback "Metal becomes a development and replay tier".
- `status_partly`: BET-29 with KILL-TOPOPT `fired`, KILL-TOLERANCE and KILL-PACKET `not-fired` →
  `partly-falsified`.
- `status_untested_under_variant`: BET-15 with GATE-CORTEX-11 `not-run` under the no-family-policy variant →
  `untested`.
- `status_open_blocks`: BET-06 with GATE-REFLEX-02 `fail` and KILL-BASELINE undecided → `open`.
- `bet13_needs_ten_fold_verdict`: all strata `measured` at ratio 6 → not `held`, not `falsified`.
- `due_by_milestone`: counts 2, 3, 7, 20, 14, 3, 1, 2, 1.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/bets_registry.sh`: loads the rows, prints the status of every bet at each exit on a synthetic register
(first with no measurement: all `not-due` or `untested` or blocking where due; then with synthetic decisions),
regenerates the README bets table, and asserts the due counts. Logs: command, exit code, wall time, the status
vector per exit, counts beside expected, and on failure the first bet whose status differs with the rows it read.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 37 rows; four without a kill gate; the due counts (N40-KA R-27). Negative controls: N40-CX RC-46 (a
bet declared `held` while its deciding gate is `not-run`), RC-47 (a gate cited as deciding a bet that the table
does not list for it), RC-48 (a bet with a failed decider and no decision, passed over at an exit).

## Considerations

- The table is the register's and governs which gates decide which bet; a bet bead that wants a different decider
  proposes an amendment.
- Late gates may come to decide parts of bets (for example a late gate that measures a lever more directly); they
  are added to a row only by amendment, never implicitly.
- Bets are tested in an order that puts the cheapest decisive evidence first: BET-04 and BET-12 at M0 and M1.
