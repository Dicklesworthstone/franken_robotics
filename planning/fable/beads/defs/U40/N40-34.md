## Background

M4 builds the lever "train few": instead of training a controller for every candidate design, a **family policy**
(one policy per morphology class, conditioned on the design) walks unseen designs of its class with no training,
and cheap evaluation rungs screen designs before any specialist is trained. The family policy is the single most
expensive item of the program to try: it needs pretraining over a population of designs, far above the default
compute envelope, and the owner must approve that envelope (question OPEN-CORTEX-1; the proposal is one class, the
quadruped, 200 designs, at most 60 GPU-hours of pretraining and 40 for specialists, on the Linux workstation). It
is also a bet (BET-15) with a pre-funding pilot and a kill gate.

DESIGN: **M4 can exit without a family policy.** This bead defines that variant in advance, so that an unapproved
envelope, a pilot that meets its falsifier, or a fired kill gate costs one capability and not the milestone. The
milestone's exit line already allows it: "rank agreement measured; the family-policy bets *decided*". Rejected
alternative: making the family policy a precondition of M4 and of the tournament, which would make the program's
central measurement hostage to its costliest bet.

## Scope

In: the three conditions; the variant record; the rungs that remain and their modes; the records that change; the
stop rule when rank agreement also fails; what M7 inherits. Out: the family policy, inheritance and the ladder
(their beads); the exit review that applies the variant (seed BD-378); the rehearsal that the variant still exits
(N40-32).

## Technical approach

**1. When the variant applies (any one).** (a) OPEN-CORTEX-1 stays at its default, "no pretraining" (its row
reads `defaulted` in `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example open_questions -- due --exit M4` (the due query of
N41-03, an example target of the crate, which has no binary of its own), with the default ADR N41-03 writes, or an owner
ruling `decided` that approves no pretraining envelope; row R7 of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` is then not
approved). (b) The
pre-funding pilot of the family policy meets its falsifier. (c) KILL-FAMILY fires: at GATE-CORTEX-11, return with
no adaptation is below 70% of specialists on more than half of the held-out set *and* fine-tuning needs more than
20% of specialist steps (a conjunction); or the pilot's falsifier holds.

**2. The variant record** (an ADR plus a `[[variant]]` record `id = "m4-no-family-policy"` in
`registries/gates.toml`, with the condition and its evidence: the default ADR, the pilot record, or the kill
decision). The `[[variant]]` table, its loader and the module `exit::variants` are N40-33's (a dependency); this
bead adds the record kind `m4-no-family-policy` with its own condition rule and its `not_run` derivation (the
parts whose subject is the family policy), and reads KILL-FAMILY's row from N40-25 and KILL-RANK's from N40-26.

**3. The rungs that remain.** The static check (`design check`), the baseline controller, the sampling controller,
gait orbits, and per-design specialists that **inherit from the nearest parent** (inheritance is a separate bet,
BET-16, with its own gate GATE-CORTEX-13 and kill gate KILL-INHERIT; if that fires too, every child trains from the
class prior or from scratch). The ladder runs per class in the mode its measured rank agreement permits
(GATE-REFLEX-04, GATE-ATEL-07; one pair of thresholds everywhere): with a Kendall τ lower 95% bound of at least
0.7 a rung is `selective`, at least 0.5 it is `discard-only` (it may discard the bottom half), and below 0.5, or
with no record, it is `uninformative`: it neither selects nor discards by rank, and removes only designs that fail
outright, labelled `baseline-only: unranked`.

**4. What is recorded under the variant.**

| Item | Recorded as |
|---|---|
| GATE-CORTEX-11 | `fail` (if measured and short, with KILL-FAMILY `fired`: then printed `killed`) or `not run (variant: no family policy)` |
| BET-15 | `falsified` (conditions b or c) or `untested` (condition a) |
| KILL-FAMILY | `fired` (c), `fired` on the pilot (b), or `not-reached` (a) |
| NS-19, "a family policy walks an unseen design" | Not demonstrated, with the reason |
| The lost factor | Entered in the ten-fold arithmetic: the ceilings are recomputed with the measured trained fraction, and the difference from the planned ceilings is the factor lost |
| The capability's name | "Specialists with inheritance from the nearest parent, and baseline screens"; the README and `frob capabilities` say "no family policy" |
| Every other artifact of M4 | Lands unchanged: the rank-agreement record, the ladder and cascade, equivariant policies, gait orbits, optimization and the archive, mission queries at scale, synthesis lanes, tracks and soil, the retrofit catalogue, shields, experiment suggestion, the surface A/B, `solve.run`, the fleet scheduler |

Gates that depend on a family policy as an *input* (and not as their subject) take their fallback input:
distillation's privileged-teacher case remains (GATE-CORTEX-14 with baseline or specialist teachers); the cascade's
rung R-family is absent and its table says so.

**5. The stop rule this variant feeds.** If KILL-RANK fires for every class (GATE-ATEL-07 fails for every
morphology class tested) and KILL-FAMILY fires as well, the lever "train few" is gone. Then: the ceilings are
recomputed with the measured trained fraction; every class whose ceiling is under 3 leaves the speed claim; and
the sealed suite of M7 is re-stratified before it is sealed (a bead is opened for it and the M7 pre-opening gates
depend on it). M7 still runs, because its result is published whatever it is.

**6. What M7 inherits.** The released surface with no family policy; arm F's phase ledger then shows the `train`
phase at its measured share; the tournament report states the variant on every stratum it touches.

**7. The way back.** If the envelope is approved later, the pilot and the gate run then, by their own records, at
the exit then current; the variant is closed by an ADR; M4's record is not rewritten.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The variant record exists only with evidence of one of the three conditions.
- [ ] Under the variant GATE-CORTEX-11 is `not run (variant: no family policy)`, `fail` or `killed`, never `pass`
      and never silently absent; BET-15 is `untested` or `falsified`; NS-19 is listed as not demonstrated.
- [ ] The ladder's mode per class is derived from the recorded τ; a class with no record is `uninformative`.
- [ ] The recomputed ceilings and the lost factor are on record in the ten-fold arithmetic.
- [ ] With the variant record, the M4 exit report is `exit-ready` when every other part passes; without it, the
      same register is `not-ready`.
- [ ] If KILL-RANK (all classes) and KILL-FAMILY both fired, the M7 re-stratification bead exists and blocks the
      M7 suite hash.
- [ ] The README and `frob capabilities --json` name the fallback and do not list a family policy.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::variants::no_family::`.

- `condition_required`: no evidence → refused; default ADR → accepted (`untested`); pilot falsifier → accepted
  (`falsified`); KILL-FAMILY fired → accepted.
- `kill_family_is_conjunction`: clause one met, clause two not → the variant by condition (c) is refused.
- `ladder_mode_from_tau`: 0.72 → `selective`; 0.55 → `discard-only`; 0.41 → `uninformative`; none → `uninformative`;
  exactly 0.7 → `selective` and exactly 0.5 → `discard-only` (the bounds are inclusive); an `uninformative` rung
  still removes a design that fails outright and labels the rest `baseline-only: unranked`.
- `cortex11_never_pass_under_variant`: a `pass` on GATE-CORTEX-11 while the variant record is open → contradiction,
  refused.
- `both_levers_gone`: KILL-RANK fired for all classes and KILL-FAMILY fired → the M7 pre-opening verdict requires
  the re-stratification record.
- `inheritance_independent`: KILL-FAMILY fired, KILL-INHERIT not fired → specialists inherit; both fired →
  children train from the class prior or from scratch.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/m4_no_family_policy.sh`: runs an M4 campaign with the family-policy feature off
(`frob opt cascade --spec <spec> --population seeded-81 --budget <budget> --seed <s> --key nofam-cas-1 --json` on
the seeded population; `frob eval rank-agreement --designs <calibration set of the class> --pair <rung>,referee
--key nofam-ra-<class> --json` per class, one key per class), records the variant, and regenerates the exit
report (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test exit -- report --milestone M4`, N40-04). Asserts: `exit-ready`; GATE-CORTEX-11 printed under the variant; the
cascade report lists the rungs used and the absent family rung; the capability list names the fallback. Logs:
command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, τ with bounds beside 0.7 and 0.5, the mode per
class, the trained fraction and the recomputed ceiling per class, and on failure the first differing value with
artifact paths. Then, on copies, it plants a `pass` on GATE-CORTEX-11 with the variant open, a discard by a cheap
rung of a class with no rank record, and both levers fired with no re-stratification record, and asserts each is
refused. The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/m4_no_family_policy/<utc>.ndjson` (per
step `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`); the `summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-CORTEX-11,
GATE-REFLEX-04, KILL-FAMILY and KILL-RANK.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the three conditions; the τ thresholds 0.7 and 0.5; the conjunction in KILL-FAMILY. Negative
controls: a family-policy result reported while the variant is open; a cheap rung discarding with no rank record;
the variant declared with no condition evidence; a tournament suite sealed without re-stratification after both
levers fell.

## Considerations

- The default of OPEN-CORTEX-1 is the likeliest way into this variant; the question is asked at M3 because M4
  needs the answer on its first day.
- "Untested" and "falsified" are different records with different consequences for later funding; the variant
  record says which.
- A no-family-policy M4 still demonstrates deciding many designs per turn (NS-06); only "train few" is weakened,
  and inheritance may recover part of it.
