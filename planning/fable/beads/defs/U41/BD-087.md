## Background

Kinesis (the dynamics subsystem, crate `frob-dyn`, layer 3) integrates by default with semi-implicit Euler on the
group: velocities first, then q₊ = q·τ(h·v₊) with the Cayley map τ = cay as retraction. That integrator is cheap and
has no conservation theorem. To have something exact to test against, the design keeps one **structure-preserving
step for a single rigid body** as a Solid mechanism: a symmetric kick, drift, kick derived from a discrete
Lagrangian. For one free body it is second order in h, time-reversible to round-off, and conserves the discrete
momentum map exactly when there is no potential and no input. Those three properties are hand-checkable and give
the dynamics known-answer battery its free-flight rows (the gate GATE-KIN-01 asks for free-flight momentum
conserved to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps on this lane).

What is not claimed (DESIGN, and the reason the articulated version is a separate Frontier bead): for an articulated
tree the mass matrix depends on configuration, a kick and drift splitting is then no longer the variational step,
and no symplectic or energy-drift property is asserted; with dissipative contact impulses the property has no
meaning. If the articulated lane is killed (KILL-VARIATIONAL: its implicit solve costs more than twice a
semi-implicit step on `batch-cpu`, or it fails its momentum row after one repair cycle), the fallback named in
advance is exactly this bead: the free-body step stays as a known-answer oracle and every rollout uses the default
integrator. This bead therefore exists on both branches.

A second reason for the bead is a trap that the review of the mathematics found: the implicit solve of the step
needs the differential of the Cayley map in one particular trivialization, and using the other one leaves momentum
exactly conserved. A test that checks momentum alone passes a wrong integrator.

## Technical approach

Module (DESIGN): `crates/frob-dyn/src/oracle/free_body.rs`, host only, `f64`, no kernels, no device path.

**Objects and symbols.** g = (R, p) ∈ SE(3) is `world_from_body`. ξ = (ω, v) is the body twist. 𝕄 is the constant
6×6 spatial inertia in the body frame (symmetric positive definite). μ = (π, ℓ) is the body momentum (angular,
linear). h is the step. V is a potential, f(g) the body-frame wrench it exerts, u an applied body wrench. x̂ is the
3×3 cross-product matrix. cay is the Cayley map of `frob-lie`: for x = (ω, v), cay(x) has rotation
(I − ½·ω̂)⁻¹·(I + ½·ω̂) and translation (I − ½·ω̂)⁻¹·v. Dcay_x is its **left-trivialized** differential:
cay(x)⁻¹·δcay(x) = Dcay_x(δx). Its inverse in closed form:

```text
(Dcay_x)⁻¹ = [[ I + ½·ω̂ + ¼·ω·ωᵀ ,  0        ],
              [ ½·v̂ + ¼·ω̂·v̂      ,  I + ½·ω̂ ]]
```

The rotation block inverts to 2/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(2I − ω̂). The discrete Lagrangian is
L_d(g_k, g_k₊₁) = (h/2)·ξ_kᵀ𝕄ξ_k − (h/2)·(V(g_k) + V(g_k₊₁)) with g_k₊₁ = g_k·cay(h·ξ_k), and the discrete momentum
at node k is μ_k = (Dcay_{−hξ_k})⁻ᵀ·𝕄ξ_k − (h/2)·f(g_k).

**The step** `step(g_k, μ_k, u_k, u_k₊₁) → (g_k₊₁, μ_k₊₁)`:

1. Half kick: μ⁺ = μ_k + (h/2)·(f(g_k) + u_k).
2. Implicit solve (the discrete Legendre relation): find ξ_k with F(ξ) = (Dcay_{−hξ})⁻ᵀ·𝕄ξ − μ⁺ = 0. Newton's
   method started from the previous ξ (or 𝕄⁻¹μ⁺ on the first step), with the closed-form 6×6 Jacobian
   ∂F/∂ξ = (Dcay_{−hξ})⁻ᵀ·𝕄 + ∂[(Dcay_{−hξ})⁻ᵀ]/∂ξ applied to 𝕄ξ; the matrix is a polynomial of degree two in x,
   so the Jacobian is written out by hand and checked against central differences. Stop at ‖F‖ ≤ 10⁻¹³·‖μ⁺‖
   (TARGET: two to three iterations); a solve that has not converged in 12 iterations returns an error value, never
   a state. A solved ξ_k = (ω_k, v_k) with h·‖ω_k‖ > 0.2 also returns an error value (`SubstepBound`, carrying
   h·‖ω_k‖ and the substep count ⌈h·‖ω_k‖/0.2⌉: the condition of `FROB-REF-212`, which `frob-lie`'s `cay_step`
   raises; the oracle itself crosses no agent boundary), never a state: SPEC-C-36 bounds the step's use to h·‖ω‖ ≤ 0.2 per substep, because the Cayley step
   rotates by 2·atan(h·‖ω‖/2) and not by h·‖ω‖; the caller halves h.
3. Drift and transport: c = cay(h·ξ_k); g_k₊₁ = g_k·c; μ° = (Ad_c)ᵀ·μ⁺, the wrench pull-back
   (Ad_g)ᵀ(τ, f) = (Rᵀ(τ − p × f), Rᵀf) evaluated at c. This is the group coadjoint of c⁻¹, not of c, with the sign
   convention for which a free body obeys μ̇ = ad*_ξ μ, that is π̇ = π × ω + ℓ × v and ℓ̇ = ℓ × ω.
4. Half kick: μ_k₊₁ = μ° + (h/2)·(f(g_k₊₁) + u_k₊₁).

Step 3 states that the spatial momentum Ad*_g μ = (R·π + p × R·ℓ, R·ℓ) is unchanged by the drift, which is the
discrete Noether theorem for a free body. The map is a symmetric composition, hence time-reversible and of second
order.

**Monitors returned by `rollout`.** Spatial momentum at every step; kinetic energy ½·ξ_kᵀ𝕄ξ_k plus V; the Newton
iteration count and final residual; the result of running the map backwards (negate h, start from the end state)
and the distance of the recovered initial state from the true one.

**API** (DESIGN): `FreeBody { inertia, potential: Option<&dyn Potential> }`, `fn step(&self, s: State, h: f64, u0,
u1) -> Result<State, SolveError>`, `fn rollout(&self, s0, h, n) -> Trace`, `fn reverse(&self, s_end, h, n) -> State`.
Determinism class D2 on the host (bit-identical run to run; no parallelism). Evidence class of what the oracle
supports: `Tested`. Nothing crosses the agent boundary from this module directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] KA-172: the stated matrix (Dcay_x)⁻¹ times a central-difference left-trivialized differential is the identity
      within 10⁻⁷ (the registered finite-difference tolerance; the exact rational form is SPEC-C-36's
      `laws_c36_dcay`) on 10⁴ random x with ‖ω‖ ≤ 3; the rotation block times 2/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(2I − ω̂) is the
      identity within 16 ulp in `f64`.
- [ ] KA-355 and the GATE-KIN-01 row: free flight (no potential, no input) of the asymmetric body below over 10⁶
      steps at h = 2 ms conserves spatial momentum to 10⁻¹² relative (seed condition); the Legendre solve reaches
      10⁻¹³ relative in at most three iterations on every step (TARGET, recorded).
- [ ] KA-173: on the same body the energy error is bounded over the run and falls by a factor between 3.6 and 4.4
      for each halving of h from 2 ms to 0.25 ms; running the map backwards recovers the initial state to 10⁻¹⁰
      relative (the registered tolerance).
- [ ] A step whose solved velocity has h·‖ω‖ > 0.2 returns `SubstepBound`, never a state (SPEC-C-36's
      `substep_bound_refused`: h·‖ω‖ = 0.25 is refused).
- [ ] KA-174: with uniform gravity, vertical spatial angular momentum and horizontal linear momentum are constant to
      10⁻¹² relative over 10⁵ steps.
- [ ] The hand fixture holds at the first step: inertia diag(1, 2, 3) kg·m², ω = (1, 1, 0) rad/s gives π = (1, 2, 0)
      and a finite-difference π̇ within 10⁻⁵ of (0, 0, −1); the body-frame rate π̇ + ω × π is zero.
- [ ] The seed negative control: with step 2 evaluated at +h·ξ_k (the right-trivialized differential where the
      left-trivialized one is required) the four-step test **fails**, by its energy-order and reversal clauses,
      while its momentum clause still passes. The test asserts both facts.
- [ ] The additive-update control fails the four-step test. It is the plan's control for KA-355, an additive
      quaternion update, and it is one build, `additive-quaternion`: the drift of step 3 is replaced by
      q ← (q + h·½·q⊗ω⁻)/‖·‖ with ω⁻ the body angular velocity at the start of the step, taken from the
      momentum without the Legendre solve. DESIGN (the conflict settled, stated identically in N16c-03): an
      earlier draft of this bead wrote the control on the rotation matrix (R replaced by R + h·R·ω̂ with
      renormalization) and asserted that it fails the momentum clause, while N16c-03 wrote it on the quaternion
      and registered "at least one of the three properties fails". The plan names the quaternion form, so that
      is the one mutant. Its registered observable is: at least one of the three clauses (energy order,
      reversal, momentum) fails; which clauses fail is measured at the first run and pinned in the control row
      from then on (HYPOTHESIS until run: the halving factor falls to about 2 and reversal fails; the momentum
      clause is recorded, not presumed). The update must use ω⁻ and not the solved ξ: normalizing
      q + h·½·q⊗ω is exactly the Cayley rotation of h·ω (half-angle tangent h·‖ω‖/2), so an "additive" update
      fed the solved velocity reproduces the correct drift and is a control that nothing can catch.
- [ ] The step agrees with the independent D.2 compendium oracle (N12-42) to 10⁻¹² relative after 10³ steps on
      100 seeded states, and the `right-trivialized-differential` mutant disagrees with it by more than 10⁻⁶.
- [ ] The oracle is called by the dynamics battery for its free-flight rows; the value measured here is handed to
      that bead's gate record with commit and seed.
- [ ] `crates/frob-dyn/CONTRACT.md` states the three claims, the two non-claims and the convention of step 3.
- [ ] The law suites of the two specifications this bead implements run unchanged against its production types,
      bound through each suite's subject trait: `laws_c36_dcay` and `laws_c36_step` of SPEC-C-36 (the Cayley
      differential and the four-step map) and the retraction laws of SPEC-C-05 that apply to the Cayley map on
      SE(3). A law that fails here is a defect of this bead, not of the suite.

## Test plan

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/free_body_oracle.rs`; no mocks; all inputs seed-explicit.
- `dcay_inverse_matches_finite_difference` (KA-172); `dcay_rotation_block_inverse`.
- `legendre_newton_converges`: 10⁴ random (𝕄, μ⁺) with ‖h·ξ‖ ≤ 0.2: residual ≤ 10⁻¹³ relative in ≤ 3 iterations;
  `legendre_jacobian_matches_central_difference` to 10⁻⁷ relative.
- `substep_bound_refused`: a free body whose solved ω gives h·‖ω‖ = 0.25 returns `SubstepBound` with that value;
  at h·‖ω‖ = 0.2 the step returns a state.
- `free_flight_momentum_1e6_steps`: 𝕄 = diag(1, 2, 3 kg·m²; 1, 1, 1 kg), ω₀ = (1, 1, 0) rad/s, v₀ = (0.3, −0.2, 0.1)
  m/s, h = 2 ms: max relative drift of spatial momentum ≤ 10⁻¹².
- `energy_error_order_two`: h ∈ {2, 1, 0.5, 0.25} ms over 10 s: ratios of the maximum energy error in [3.6, 4.4].
- `reversal_to_roundoff`: 10⁴ steps forward, then backward: state error ≤ 10⁻¹⁰ relative.
- `gravity_symmetry_momenta` (KA-174): centre of mass offset (0.05, 0, 0.02) m, g = 9.81 m/s².
- `euler_poincare_first_step_fixture`: the (0, 0, −1) rate.
- `mutant_other_trivialization_keeps_momentum_breaks_energy_and_reversal` (the seed control; counterexample
  CX-087).
- `mutant_additive_quaternion_fails_four_step_test`: asserts that at least one clause fails and records which;
  `additive_with_solved_velocity_equals_cayley` (the reason for ω⁻: with the solved ξ the normalized additive
  update equals the Cayley drift to round-off on 10⁴ random states).
- `nonconvergence_is_an_error`: an inertia with a 10⁻³⁰ eigenvalue returns `SolveError`, not a state.
- `agrees_with_compendium_d2_oracle`: the trapezoidal-variational-step row of the mathematics compendium D.2
  (N12-42, `frob_conformance::compendium::d2`, an independent reference implementation, taken as a
  dev-dependency; the comparison is over plain `f64` arrays) and this step, from the same 100 seeded initial

  with the `right-trivialized-differential` mutant built in they disagree by more than 10⁻⁶ relative within the
  same run (two implementations that both conserve momentum but differ in the differential are told apart here
  as well as by the energy clause). The oracle is never regenerated from this step.

### End-to-end script and logging
No verb is defined on the oracle. `scripts/e2e/kinesis_free_body_oracle.sh` runs `cargo test -p frob-dyn --test
free_body_oracle` in debug and release, twice each, then each control build once in debug (`cargo test -p frob-dyn
--features nc-<nnnn> --test free_body_oracle -- mutant_`, one per control; a mutant does not build under the
release profile), and writes the shared newline-delimited JSON log under
`target/e2e-logs/kinesis_free_body_oracle/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: one `metric` line per known answer
(momentum drift, energy-error ratios per halving, reversal error, Newton iterations and residual, each with its
threshold and unit), one `assert` line per control stating which clause caught it, digests of the final states for
the twice-run comparison, and a `summary` naming GATE-KIN-01 as touched (partial: the gate is recorded by the
battery bead).

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-172 (left-trivialized differential identity; registered tolerance exact in rationals, 10⁻⁷ for the
  finite-difference form).
- KA-173 (four-step map on a free asymmetric body: momentum 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps, energy error ÷ 4 per
  halving with ratio in [3.6, 4.4], reversal 10⁻¹⁰ relative).
- KA-174 (with gravity: two conserved momenta). KA-355 (order by step halving, reversal, momentum without
  potential; 10⁻¹² over 10⁶ steps; Legendre solve to 10⁻¹³).
- CX-087: "if the variational step conserves momentum, its implicit solve is right" is false: the transport of
  step 3 conserves momentum for any ξ, so the wrong differential is invisible to a momentum test and is detected
  only by the energy bound and by reversal.
- Metamorphic: the map commutes with a rigid change of world frame (left multiplication of g); scaling 𝕄 by a
  positive constant leaves the trajectory of g unchanged in free flight.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine and debug against release, bit-identical final states; Linux against
  macOS inside D2 for one compiler (recorded, not asserted across compilers).

## Considerations

- The energy clause needs the run long enough to show the bound (10 s at the listed steps) and short enough to stay
  inside the default machine envelope; 10⁶ steps of a 6×6 Newton solve take seconds on one core.
- The Cayley map is not the exponential: at h·‖ω‖ near 2 the rotation angle is large and the step loses accuracy
  without losing its structure; the step therefore refuses h·‖ω‖ > 0.2 (SPEC-C-36) and a rollout reports its
  maximum h·‖ω‖.
- This oracle must never be regenerated from the integrator it checks: its expected values are the conservation
  laws and the hand fixture, not stored trajectories.
- What would make us revisit: a second retraction on the oracle lane (the exponential), which needs its own
  differential and its own identity test.
