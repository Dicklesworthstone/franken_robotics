## Background

Stage 9 of the batched step integrates the configuration on its group: q₊ = q·τ(h·v₊), where v₊ is the end-of-step
velocity returned by the solve, h the physics step and τ a retraction from the Lie algebra to the group. On the
batched tiers τ is the **Cayley map**, cay(x) = (I − x/2)⁻¹·(I + x/2), because it is a rational function: it needs
no transcendental function (built-in elementary functions differ across libraries and GPUs and are banned in
batched kernels) and its result lies on the group in exact arithmetic. Adding a quaternion increment and
renormalizing instead needs an order-h² projection every step and is the control that these kernels must beat.

At M0 the derivation tool produced `K-cay-step` and `K-normalize` for one free body. This bead delivers the
**articulated** versions in `frob-kernels` (Kinesis, tier Solid): one group update per coordinate class of a robot
(floating base, revolute, prismatic, spherical and multi-column joints), one normalization per step, and
`K-anchor`, which keeps world positions representable in single precision. Single precision resolves about 7.6 µm
at 100 m from the origin, too coarse for contact; a world position is therefore an integer cell index plus an `f32`
offset from the centre of a cell of side 8 m, and the offset lies in [−4, 4), where resolution is at worst 2⁻²² m
(about 0.24 µm). Quantities that are differences (gaps, link-relative poses, velocities) never touch absolute
position.

Decision recorded here (ADR-NUM-06, DESIGN): the offset is centred. With offsets kept in [0, 8), a body that
drifts to −2⁻²⁵ m must add 8; the number 8 − 2⁻²⁵ is not an `f32` (the spacing just below 8 is 2⁻²¹), so the sum
rounds to 8.0: the position has moved and has left the cell's range. The centred convention never adds the cell
side to a small number.

## Scope

emitted as Rust (`batch-cpu`) and dialect source; their manifests; the integer turn counter; the group-membership

## Technical approach

**Coordinate classes and index ranges.** The packed model lowering gives, per shape class, contiguous ranges of
coordinates by class. Each kernel entry point runs over one range with shape worlds × coordinates; no kernel reads
a class tag or branches on it.

| Class | Stored state | Update (a = h·ω dimensionless, w = h·v metres) | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floating base, SE(3) | Unit quaternion q, anchored position (cell, offset) | q′ = q ⊗ (1, a/2) (increment in the body frame, multiplied on the right); d = 1 + (a·a)/4; u = (w + ½·a × w + ¼·a·(a·w))/d, which is (I − â/2)⁻¹·w; p′ = p + R(q)·u | `K-cay-step/se3` |
| Spherical joint, SO(3) | Unit quaternion | The rotation part of the row above | `K-cay-step/so3` |
| Revolute (and the angle of helical and cylindrical joints), SO(2) | Unit complex z = (c, s) and a signed 32-bit turn counter | z′ = z·((1 − a²/4) + i·a), d = 1 + a²/4 | `K-cay-step/so2` |
| Prismatic (and the travel of helical and cylindrical joints), ℝ | Scalar d in metres | d₊ = d + h·ḋ₊ | `K-cay-step/r1` |
| Planar joint, SE(2) | Unit complex and two offsets | Product of the one-parameter updates in screw-basis order | composed |

A multi-column joint is the product of one-parameter updates in the order of its screw basis. The rotation of q′/√d
is q composed with cay(â); a step rotates by 2·atan(‖ω‖·h/2) instead of ‖ω‖·h, which is why a substep with
‖ω‖·h above 0.2 is refused before this stage (`FROB-REF-212`, repairs: substep; use the exponential on the referee).

**`K-normalize`** (once per step, after all updates). Quaternion: n = ((q_w·q_w + q_x·q_x) + q_y·q_y) + q_z·q_z in
that association; s = √n; outputs the four components divided by s. Unit complex: n = c·c + s·s; divide both by √n.
Division and not multiplication by a reciprocal: one correctly rounded division has one rounding where
reciprocal-then-multiply has two. The summation order is fixed at derivation time; reassociation is not an allowed
rewrite. The unnormalized q′ and its d are both available, so the kernel normalizes q′ directly and never divides
by √d separately.

**Turn counter** (exact-integer arithmetic, class D0). The cut is the negative real axis. With (c, s) before and
(c′, s′) after normalization: if c′ < 0 and s ≥ 0 and s′ < 0 the counter increments; if c′ < 0 and s < 0 and s′ ≥ 0
it decrements; by selects on integer masks. Because a ≤ 0.2 the argument moves by less than 0.2 rad per step, so
one crossing per step is the most that can happen. The joint angle reported to consumers is 2π·turns + arg(z),
formed on the host; periodic laws read z, limit rows read the turn-resolved angle.

**`K-anchor`** (after `K-cay-step/se3`). Per axis, with offset o (an `f32`) and cell index k (signed 32-bit):
if o ≥ 4 then o ← o − 8.0 and k ← k + 1; if o < −4 then o ← o + 8.0 and k ← k − 1; by selects. For 4 ≤ |o| ≤ 16 the
shifted value is exact by Sterbenz's lemma (the difference of two floats within a factor of two of each other is
representable); proof obligation PO-NUM-01 is discharged exhaustively over the 2²⁵ values of two binades and two
signs on each backend. A body cannot cross 12 m in one step, so the precondition holds; an offset with |o| ≥ 16
after the update, or a cell index outside the scene's declared bounds, marks the world with `FROB-REF-249` and
freezes it (never integrated silently). The update p′ = p + R(q)·u is applied to the offset only; the difference of
two anchored positions is the float difference of offsets plus 8 times the integer difference of cells, formed only
between neighbours.

**Manifests.** Class floating for the updates and normalization, exact-integer for the counter and cell index;
determinism D2 (bit-identical across worker counts, batch size, slot, co-tenants and bucket capacity); inputs and
outputs with shapes and physical dimensions (angles dimensionless, w in metres); read and write footprints; no
aliasing except in-place state where the manifest declares the same slab for input and output of one coordinate;
padding: padded coordinates hold the identity (q = (1, 0, 0, 0), z = (1, 0), d = 0, zero velocity) and are updated
to the identity bit for bit. The bans hold: no fused multiply-add, no fast-math, no built-in transcendental (`sqrt`
is correctly rounded and allowed).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Group-membership test (seed condition): the reference quadruped (floating base and 12 revolute joints) and
      the humanoid (floating base, 29 joints) driven for 10⁶ steps at h = 2 ms with seeded velocities satisfying
      ‖ω‖·h ≤ 0.2 keep |‖q‖² − 1| and |c² + s² − 1| within four units in the last place at every step, in `f32`
      and in `f64`, with offsets always in [−4, 4).
- [ ] The Cayley identities hold bit for bit against the M0 single-body kernels on the base, and against the host
      `f64` reference of the integrator on every class.
- [ ] Turn counters equal those of a host reference that accumulates the exact rotation angle, on a joint spun
      through ±3.5 turns and on 10⁴ random joint histories.
- [ ] `K-anchor`: a body driven 1 km along each axis re-anchors 125 times per axis with zero position error
      against the `f64` reference beyond the offset's own rounding; the hand cases below hold; the uncentred
      mutant is caught.
- [ ] A world whose offset would leave [−16, 16) in one step is marked with `FROB-REF-249` and frozen.
- [ ] Bitwise equality across worker counts, batch sizes, slot permutations and two bucket capacities.
- [ ] The additive-increment mutant, the no-normalization mutant and the reciprocal-multiply mutant are caught.
- [ ] Manifests validate and the dialect lint passes on emitted sources.
- [ ] Open clauses of the definition of implemented: device differential checks (Spark check), reachability through
      `sim.run` on a batched tier (BD-159), the gate record of the precision battery (GATE-SPARK-05, BD-179).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-kernels/tests/integrate_kernels.rs`; no mocks; seeds explicit.
- `se3_quarter_turn_known`: q = identity, ω = (0, 0, 2) s⁻¹, v = (1, 0, 0) m/s, h = 1 s: q′ = (1, 0, 0, 1), d = 2,
  normalized to a quarter turn about z; u = (½, ½, 0) m.
- `so2_quarter_turn_known`: z = 1, a = 2: z′ = (0, 2), d = 2, normalized to i.
- `rotation_angle_is_two_atan`: a = 0.2 rotates by 2·atan(0.1) = 0.19934 rad, not 0.2.
- `normalize_association_fixed`: the emitted program sums in the stated order (golden program hash).
- `turn_counter_three_and_a_half_turns`: a joint spun to just short of 3.5 turns reports counter 3 and an argument
  just below π; spun the other way, counter −3 and an argument just above −π; in both cases
  2π·turns + arg(z) equals the reference angle within 10⁻⁶ rad in `f32`.
- `anchor_shift_known`: offset 3.75 m advanced by 0.5 m is 4.25 m, re-anchored to −3.75 m exactly with the cell
  index raised by one; offset −4.0 m is kept (the range is closed below); 4.0 m becomes −4.0 m in the next cell.
- `anchor_centred_small_negative` (KA-790): offset −2⁻²⁵ stays −2⁻²⁵, no shift.
- `anchor_out_of_range_refuses_249`.
- `group_membership_1e6_steps_{quadruped, humanoid}_{f32, f64}`.
- `padding_is_identity_bitwise`; `worker_count_and_slot_permutation_bitwise`.
- Mutants: `additive_quaternion_increment` (fails the comparison with the Cayley identity at a = 0.2 by more than
  10⁻³ in angle); `no_normalize` (norm drift beyond four units in the last place within 10⁶ steps);
  `reciprocal_multiply` (golden hash and last-bit comparison); `uncentred_anchor` (returns 8.0 on the KA-790
  input).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/integrate_kernels.sh`: runs the kernel tests in debug and release, twice; once the batched step
exists, `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu --task fixtures/spin-and-translate.ftask --steps 1000000 --seed 3 --json` at
two worker counts, asserting equal state digests and reading the group-membership residual from telemetry
(skipped and logged as such before the step lands). Logs: `metric` lines for the maximum membership residual in
units in the last place (threshold 4), the re-anchor count, the turn counts; digests; `assert` lines per mutant;
`refusal` lines for the two refusal fixtures; a `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-052 (the Cayley retraction lies exactly on the group; on SE(3) rotation cay(ω̂) and translation
  (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·v; the step rotates by 2·atan(‖ω‖·h/2); control: an additive quaternion increment followed by
  renormalization). KA-053 (membership residual within four units in the last place over 10⁷ steps with
  per-step normalization on; growth no faster than a random walk with it off).
- KA-790 (centred offsets; control: the uncentred shift returns 8.0), KA-791 (PO-NUM-01, exhaustive, run by the
  representation bead on each backend), KA-792 (resolution figures).
- CX-369 (offsets cannot be kept in [0, 8)), CX-370 (absolute positions cannot be held in single precision).
- Metamorphic: a rigid change of world frame commutes with the base update; integrating +ω then −ω for one step
  each returns the start state within two units in the last place after normalization.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; device families later, each
  against its class (the M0 gate already compared the single-body kernel on three backends).

## Considerations

- The membership bound is in units in the last place, so it is stated per precision; the 1 km precision battery
  re-uses this test at distance.
- The turn counter makes a circle-valued joint's history observable; a joint with bounded travel under one turn
  still stores it, so that all revolute coordinates share one layout.
- Cell crossings are exact but frequent for fast bodies near a cell face; the kernel has no hysteresis because the
  shift is exact in both directions.
- What would make us revisit: a kernel class that needs the exponential on devices (it would need deterministic
  elementary functions written in the dialect), or scenes larger than 8 m × 2³¹.
