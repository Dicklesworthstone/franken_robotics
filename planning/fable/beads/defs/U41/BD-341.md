## Background

In the design studio (Atelier, crate `frob-opt`, tier Solid) agents are the discrete optimizer: they choose
topology, leg count and actuator families. The studio supplies what agents are poor at: fast continuous
optimization over the numbers a design has declared free, and honest evaluation underneath. This bead delivers the
campaign verbs `opt.start` and `opt.status` with two mechanisms.

**Continuous optimization over levers.** Optimizers move declared levers only (ADR-SKEL-07; INV-SKEL-08: an
optimizer changes only declared levers, inside their bounds and on their manifolds), because an optimizer that can
touch every number finds the numbers nobody meant to free. The methods come from FrankenSim through the airlock
(FACT: `fs-dfo` has CMA-ES with restart schedules, `fs-bo` has trust-region and multi-fidelity modules,
`fs-ascent::riemann` has Riemannian quasi-Newton for static objectives on manifold-valued parameters).

**Racing.** Candidates in a generation are compared by e-processes and the dominated ones are cancelled
mid-evaluation, so compute follows the survivors. The construction is the mixture race of the sibling `fs-race`
(FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-race/CONTRACT.md`): rounds are the only clock; every survivor consumes one
observation per round in index order; a candidate's elimination evidence is the average of its pairwise
e-processes against all original opponents; and an elimination needs that mixture to reach at least 1/α, so the
true best is eliminated with probability at most α. An implementation that eliminates on any single pairwise
crossing must instead run each comparison at α/(N − 1).

What racing is expected to buy (stated before any measurement): the sibling's own battery asserts a saving of at
least 2 on a separated field and below 1.5 on an inseparable one, with 11.7 and 1.03 measured on its fixtures
(FACT). The expectation here is two to five times fewer evaluations when candidates are separated and nothing
otherwise (HYPOTHESIS, settled per campaign by the saving the race ledger records). Racing is therefore never
described as a speedup in general; each campaign prints its own ledger.

Rejected alternatives: fixed-budget evaluation of every candidate (pays full price for dominated designs);
eliminating on a t-test at interim looks (not valid under optional stopping); paired finite-difference design
gradients as the default search direction (paired worlds decouple under contact; that route is a gated Frontier
mechanism with its own kill gate and its fallback is the rank-based search of this bead).

## Technical approach

Module (DESIGN; one layout for the crate, the same in N28b-07 and N28b-08): this bead writes
`crates/frob-opt/src/campaign.rs` and `crates/frob-opt/src/race/{mod.rs, events.rs}`; the optimizers are in
`crates/frob-opt/src/optimize/{space.rs, iface.rs, cma.rs, bo.rs, riemann.rs, realize.rs}` (N28b-07) and the
ledger in `crates/frob-opt/src/race/ledger.rs` (N28b-08). Verb handlers in `frob-verbs`.

**Campaign.** `CampaignId` = hash of (specification identity, base `DesignId`, lever set, optimizer and its
options, objective, race parameters, address set, kernel and model identities). A campaign is a job with
generations.

1. Variables: the selected levers of the base design. Each lever has bounds or choices and a manifold kind (ℝ, ℝ₊,
   S¹, S², SE(3), the inertia cone, or a finite set). The continuous levers form the parameter space of N28b-07
   (`ParamSpace::from_design`), which holds the chart and the retraction of each manifold kind and maps the
   optimizer's coordinates to the manifold and back. A lever of kind `finite` is handled by enumeration inside a
   generation, not by rounding (DESIGN, the same statement in N28b-07): the parameter space excludes every lever
   with `choices`, so no optimizer sees one and no continuous coordinate is ever rounded to a choice. By default
   a `finite` lever is left to the agent, who is the discrete optimizer. When the request selects one in
   `--levers`, this loop enumerates its choices: each candidate the optimizer proposes is realized once per
   combination of choices, so the generation holds N × (the product of the choice counts) designs, all counted
   against the budget, and the optimizer is told, for each of its N candidates, the score of its best
   combination. Reason: a catalog choice has no neighbourhood for a continuous step, and rounding a coordinate
   to the nearest choice would hide the discontinuity from the optimizer.
2. Proposal: the optimizer proposes N candidates through `ask`; each becomes a design by `set-lever` edits through
   `realize` (typed edits, so each candidate has a `DesignId` and a lineage edge). A candidate outside bounds is
   never created: one whose edits the design language refuses is told back to the optimizer as `Refused`, never
   clipped.
3. Evaluation: the objective is a task metric with a declared direction and a declared span (the range of the
   per-address loss; it comes from the task definition and must not be estimated from the race data). Candidates
   run on the same world addresses (common random numbers), in one mixed batch where the designs are parameter
   variants of one topology; `--family` [F] also admits topology neighbours in one dispatch.
4. Race (below) decides which candidates continue to consume addresses.
5. Update: survivors return scores; cancelled candidates return their elimination round. The loop calls `tell`
   with status `Scored` for a survivor and `Cancelled` with the elimination round for a raced-out candidate; what
   each adapter does with a cancelled candidate (CMA-ES uses a rank bound; Bayesian optimization a censored
   observation) is specified in N28b-07. No score is ever fabricated for a cancelled candidate.
6. Stop: budget exhausted, the optimizer's own convergence test, or cancellation. Finalists are `provisional`
   until promoted on the referee by the promotion rule.

**Race** (per generation, N candidates, level α, at most T rounds).

1. Round t gives every survivor the outcome at address a_t; addresses are taken in index order.
2. For an ordered pair (i, j) the paired difference d_t = loss_j − loss_i at address a_t lies in [−span, span]. The
   pairwise e-process E_ij tests the hypothesis "j is at least as good as i" and grows when i beats j:
   E_ij(t) = Π_{s ≤ t} (1 + λ_s·d_s/span), with betting fractions λ_s chosen from past data only, inside [0, 1).
3. Elimination evidence of j: M_j(t) = (1/(N − 1))·Σ_{i ≠ j} E_ij(t), over all original opponents, including those
   already eliminated (their processes are frozen at their last value).
4. Eliminate j at the first round where M_j(t) ≥ 1/α; its remaining evaluations are cancelled at the next
   kernel-batch boundary and its slot is freed.
5. Guarantee (proof obligation PO-ATEL-04): if j is a best candidate, each E_ij is a nonnegative supermartingale
   starting at 1, so is their average, and by Ville's inequality the probability that M_j ever reaches 1/α is at
   most α.
6. Events and counts per generation. The race emits one event per round (the round, the survivors, the
   candidates eliminated with their mixture value and the threshold) and keeps the counts of evaluations started,
   completed and cancelled (started = completed + cancelled), the rounds each candidate survived, N, T and α.
   That is all this bead records. The race ledger is N28b-08's: it folds these events into the record
   `frob.race-ledger/1` when the race ends or is cancelled, and it owns the saving (the N·T observations of a
   non-racing generation divided by the observations consumed, with a second ratio in measured GPU-seconds that
   charges episodes cancelled part-way), the separation diagnostic, and the expected saving registered before
   round 1 and scored afterwards.

The statistic is a pure function of per-address outcomes taken in address order (INV-ATEL-02): it does not depend
on completion order, preemption, co-tenancy, shard count or memo hits.

**Verbs.**

- `opt.start` (kind `run`, first milestone M4, capability `opt:run`). CLI: `frob opt start --task <registered
  task> --design <id> --levers thigh,knee_ratio --method cma-es --objective cot-elec:min --population 16
  --race alpha=0.05,rounds=64 --budget gpu_s=3600 --key … --json`; `--family` evaluates a family per dispatch.
  Parameters (DESIGN: one set of names for the verb; the scripts of N28b-07 and N28b-08 use them): `--task <id>`
  for a registered task or `--spec <file>` for a task or mission specification file, exactly one of the two;
  `--method` ∈ {`cma-es`, `tr-bo`, `mf-bo`, `riemann-qn`} (the four adapters of N28b-07, which lists the same
  names; a method that was not admitted is refused with exit code 5); `--population` N, the candidates per generation (default: the batch
  capacity); `--generations` G, an optional cap on generations; `--race alpha=…,rounds=…` with T = `rounds`;
  `--seed`; `--budget` in the program's budget units (`gpu_s`, `cpu_core_s`); `--key`, the idempotency key.
  Input schema `frob.opt.start.in/1`; output `frob.opt.start.out/1` with `campaign_id` and the job handle (exit
  code 8 when accepted and still running; the bounded synchronous wait applies).
- `opt.status` (kind `read`, capability `opt:read`). Output `frob.opt.status.out/1`: state, generation,
  evaluations (started, completed, cancelled), survivors with lever values and score intervals, best so far with
  its `DesignId` (reported as the best provisional candidate), the optimizer state hash of the generation, spend
  by tier in device-seconds against the budget, the envelope class in force, warnings and `next_actions`; and an
  optional block `race_ledger`, filled by N28b-08 (the identities of the ledger records and the campaign roll-up
  with the pooled saving), which is absent until that bead lands. Every number carries the claim product (tier,
  determinism class, model identity, seed; evidence class set by the weakest input; flags all false) and results
  are labelled `provisional`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed condition (PO-ATEL-04): a null race of 81 identical candidates replayed 512 times at α = 0.05 eliminates
      the designated candidate in a number of replays within the exact binomial acceptance bound for a rate of at
      most α: at most 42 of 512, the 0.999 quantile of Binomial(512, 0.05), the same bound N28c-28's admission
      reading of `fs-race` uses (computed in exact integer arithmetic in the test; the null race keeps the best
      with probability at least 1 − α).
- [ ] Seed condition: a race of 16 candidates with one planted winner (better by a margin fixed from the race's
      pre-analysis so that sole survival has probability at least 0.9 within 64 rounds) returns it: the winner is
      never eliminated in at least 97 of 100 seeded races and is the sole survivor in at least 80 of 100.
- [ ] The single-crossing mutant (eliminate on any pairwise e-process reaching 1/α) fails the null-race bound.
- [ ] Verdicts and the round events (and therefore the ledger N28b-08 folds from them) are identical under random
      preemption, reordering of completions, co-tenancy and one or two shards (PO-ATEL-09 on 1,000 episodes in
      100 schedules).
- [ ] A CMA-ES campaign on a static objective with a known optimum (the thigh-length lever of the worked quadruped
      against a quadratic synthetic objective, labelled `synthetic`) converges to the optimum within the lever's
      step; no candidate is ever created outside lever bounds; every candidate has a lineage edge.
- [ ] `opt.start` returns a job handle with exit code 8 and `opt.status` reports progress with denominators; a
      repeated idempotency key returns the same campaign; cancellation drains at a kernel-batch boundary and leaves
      the counts consistent (and a final round event from which N28b-08 writes a ledger with `stopped_by:
      cancelled`).
- [ ] Each refusal in the table is produced by its planted request; a campaign above the envelope class is refused
      before any world is created.
- [ ] Every generation emits its round events in address order, and its counts satisfy started = completed +
      cancelled. (The ledger record, the saving printed for every generation and the registered expectation
      beside it are acceptance criteria of N28b-08, which reads these events.)
- [ ] A campaign that selects a `finite` lever with three choices and a population of 4 evaluates 12 designs per
      generation, each with one of the three choices exactly, and none with a rounded value.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-opt` documents the race, its guarantee and its limits; both verbs have registry
      entries, schemas and exit codes, and are reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`.
- [ ] This bead's row of GATE-ATEL-13 (the null calibration of the race, PO-ATEL-04) is recorded with the measured
      elimination count, the replay count and the exact binomial bound by `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
      record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-13 --receipt <hash of the null-race test output>`, marked partial. The rest of
      GATE-ATEL-13 is assembled by BD-349 and GATE-ATEL-16 (the cascade end to end) by BD-352; both depend on this
      bead, so neither is an acceptance condition here.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/opt_race.sh`: `frob opt start` on the worked quadruped with two levers, a flat-ground velocity task and
`--method cma-es --population 16 --race alpha=0.05,rounds=64` on `batch-cpu` inside the default envelope; polls
`frob opt status <campaign_id> --json` (the campaign is the verb's one input) until done; asserts a non-empty survivor set, counts that add up (started = completed +
cancelled) and `provisional` labels; repeats `opt.start` with the same `--key` (same `campaign_id`); cancels a
second campaign mid-generation with `frob job cancel` and asserts consistent counts; runs the refusal fixtures,
one per row of the table (111, 542, 543, 556, 545 with `--method mf-bo` on an unmeasured stratum, 445 with its
witness attached, 761 in process with zero worlds created, 704 for the same over-envelope campaign queued through
`frobd` with no approved envelope, and `foil-as-candidate` unless KILL-FOIL has fallen back), each
asserting its code and exit code 3 (4 for 761 and 704; where `mf-bo` was not admitted in the build the 545 fixture
asserts the row's answer for a method not admitted, `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, instead); repeats
one status call over `frob-mcp`. Every `opt.start` call carries `--key`: the repeat reuses the first campaign's key,
and the cancelled campaign and each refusal fixture have keys of their own, so no call is answered from another's
stored receipt. Logs: `cmd`, exit codes (8, 0, 3, 4), wall time, evidence block,
`metric` lines for evaluations and α, `refusal` lines, and a `summary` naming GATE-ATEL-13 (partial). The ledger
records, the printed saving, the restart of a killed worker and the equality of two seeded runs are asserted by
`scripts/e2e/opt_race_campaign.sh` (N28b-08).

- The sibling's battery figures (saving at least 2 on a separated field, below 1.5 on an inseparable one) as a

- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts and shard counts; `batch-cpu` against a device
  family at the level of verdicts where the class permits a paired statistic.

## Considerations

- One race is valid under optional stopping; it does nothing about an agent that runs fifty campaigns on the same
  held-out worlds until one passes. Results meant as claims go through the blinded qualification lane.
- The saving depends on separation; a campaign whose candidates are inseparable pays the full price and its ledger
  says so. The surface A/B and the cascade count racing as a lever only by measured ledger savings.
- A rung may select or discard only where rank agreement is on record; this bead's race runs on one rung and
  inherits that rung's mode label on its results.
- Campaigns above the default envelope (one device, one hour) need the owner's approved class; under the default
  the verbs still work at reduced budget.
- If the sibling optimizers fail admission, the local CMA-ES of N28b-07 runs against the same known answers and
  the other three methods are reported as not admitted (exit code 5 on request); the adapters are written against
  a small trait so that the choice changes no schema.
- What would make us revisit: e-processes with a variance-adaptive bet that keep the guarantee and cut rounds.
