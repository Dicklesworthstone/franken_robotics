## Background

The ten-fold test compares arms (this product; the strongest composed incumbent tooling, advocated by sessions
whose job is to make it strong) on the time from a brief to an **accepted** answer. Acceptance cannot be decided by
either arm's own simulator, because each arm's tools agree with themselves. Two things are therefore needed that
no measurement inside the program can supply, and the register carries them as one owner question:

- **the adjudicating simulator**: an evaluator, independent of both arms, that runs the acceptance batteries, for
  each stratum of missions (and that can represent that stratum's terrain, films and tracks);
- **the neutral party**: who seals the suite, holds the receipts, runs acceptance on a sealed key and releases
  the paired results only after every arm has closed.

How acceptance works, so that the roles are concrete (DESIGN): a submission is committed before any world is
drawn; each submission draws fresh world clusters; the i-th submission on a mission is tested at level
α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] with α = 0.05; the arm receives a receipt holding only the decision, the index, the level
spent, the units used and a hash; a rejected submission returns no per-world outcome; where the format allows, the
adjudicator scores an answer without being told which arm produced it.

Design intent: the adjudicator is an external party, a person or a team with no authorship in any arm.
Fail-closed default if none can be engaged: **an incumbent CPU engine at a pinned version that neither arm trains
in, run by a fresh-context agent session with no write access to any arm, operating from the protocol document
alone under a named human, with every action logged; every ratio so measured is labelled `self-adjudicated`.**
What the default costs: the words "independently accepted" and "ten-fold" are not used for the result until an
external party has re-run the replication kit, and the partial gate at M3 is a rehearsal.

The answer is needed at M2, before any sealed world family exists, because whoever seals the families must exist
before they are drawn; it is confirmed again before the M7 suite is sealed.

## Scope

In (this bead is the owner packet of OPEN-PROG-2; the two decisions it carries are made in the ten-fold unit's
decision beads): the page put to the owner at the M1 exit; the register rows of OPEN-PROG-2 and its aliases,
which move together; the configuration record `registries/tenfold/adjudication.toml`, generated from the
decision record, and its validator; the checks wired to the record (neutrality, simulator independence, sealing
order, label agreement, provisional status); the default record that is in force until the owner answers; the
second confirmation at M6.

Out, with the bead that has it:

- The decision OPEN-TENFOLD-2, who the adjudicator is: the options, what an external party needs, the role
  statement, the set-up of the default session, the two party kinds and the label each takes, and part 1 of the
  decision record (N39b-27, which depends on this bead: the owner answers on this bead's page, and N39b-27
  records the answer).
- The decision OPEN-TENFOLD-1, the simulator per stratum: the four conditions with their evidence, the candidate
  table, the representability probes, the decision rule, and part 2 of the decision record (N39b-26, likewise).
- The decision KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ per stratum (N39a-11); this bead's record carries its outcome and decides
  nothing.
- The acceptance battery and gate (the program-gate bead N40-14); the adjudication dry run (N38a-34); the rival
  advocates, agent model, thresholds and compute (N41-15); repository visibility (N41-05).

## Technical approach

**What is put to the owner at the M1 exit** (one milestone early), generated from the register:

1. *Neutral party.* The three options of N39b-27, quoted from that bead and not restated differently here: (a) an
   external person or team engaged for both gates; (b) external for the tournament only, with the default
   session under a named human for the partial gate, which is then a rehearsal; (c) the default session for
   both, with an external re-run of the kit sought after publication. For an external party: who, their access
   (read access to the protocol, the sealed key and the kit; no write access to either arm; no authorship in
   either arm), and their availability at M3 and M7.
2. *Adjudicating simulator per stratum.* A table of the strata the protocol registers (the partial gate at M3
   uses sealed legged and wheeled briefs; the tournament adds its own) against candidate engines from the oracle
   register, with for each candidate: pinned version and container digest; whether either arm used it for
   training, tuning or screening on that stratum (a disqualifier); whether it can express the stratum's
   terrain, films and tracks; its own stated tolerances. The table is the candidate table of N39b-26, with that
   bead's four conditions (not used by an arm on the stratum; contact model derived independently of both arms;
   deterministic on a CPU; pinned by revision) and its probe results where they exist; the page shows it and
   adds no candidate.
3. *Where no single engine can represent a stratum.* The pre-declared fallback is cross-home adjudication,
   labelled: each arm's accepted answers are run on the other arm's referee. If neither home referee can run the
   other arm's answers, the stratum is `descriptive`.

**Recording.** The one decision record is the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-02-adjudication.md`: the party, the human
responsible, the simulator per stratum with pins, the access list, and the date. Its part 1 (the party) is
written by N39b-27 and its part 2 (the simulator per stratum, one section per gate) by N39b-26; until the owner
answers, this bead writes the file as the default ADR (the default of both halves, marked `default in force`),
which the two decision beads then replace part by part. No other decision file exists for either half. Register
rows OPEN-PROG-2 and its aliases OPEN-TENFOLD-1 and -2 move together: an alias cannot be closed while the other
half is open, and the loader refuses a state in which they differ.

**The configuration record and its commands (DESIGN).** The record is a projection of the ADR, generated and
never edited by hand. The ADR carries one fenced `toml` block per part, the first line of each a comment
`# part 1: party` or `# part 2: simulators`; the generator reads those two blocks and nothing else (prose outside
them is for people), so what N39b-27 and N39b-26 write into their blocks is exactly what the record holds.

```toml
# registries/tenfold/adjudication.toml, schema frob.tenfold.adjudication/1
adr        = "docs/adr/ADR-PROG-02-adjudication.md"
adr_digest = "b3:…"                  # BLAKE3 of the ADR file the record was generated from
[gate.partial]                       # one table per gate: `partial` (M3) and `tournament` (M7)
record = "default"                   # default | owner
option = "c"                         # a | b | c of N39b-27; the default record is option c
external_replication = ""            # identifier of the record of an external re-run of the replication kit
confirmed = ""                       # tournament only: the date of the confirmation given after the M6 page
[gate.partial.party]                 # from part 1 of the ADR
kind      = "internal-session"       # external | internal-session
name      = ""                       # the external person or team; empty for the default session
human     = "<the named human>"      # required when kind is internal-session
statement = "b3:…"                   # digest of the signed role statement
access    = ["read:protocol", "read:schemas", "read:public-descriptions", "own:sealed-store"]
label     = "self-adjudicated"       # derived from kind by `adjudication_label`, never typed: internal-session
                                     # gives self-adjudicated, external gives external
[[gate.partial.stratum]]             # from part 2 of the ADR, one table per stratum
stratum     = "legged-quad"
mode        = "simulator"            # simulator | cross-home | descriptive; the last two come only from a
                                     # KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ decision (N39a-11) and need kill_adr
simulator   = "<engine>"
pin         = "<revision>"
container   = "<digest>"
tools       = []                     # family-answer strata: the direct evaluation tools named by N39b-26
provisional = true                   # until the adjudicator-acceptance gate has passed for the stratum
kill_adr    = ""
```

An unknown field fails the load. The two party kinds and their labels are the vocabulary of the decision
OPEN-TENFOLD-2 (N39b-27). The function that maps one to the other,
`frob_conformance::tenfold::adjudication::adjudication_label(kind)`, is defined here with the schema, because
this bead must write the default record before that decision bead runs; N39b-27 tests it on its three options,
and at M3 the role registry (N39a-02) writes its `adjudication` field by calling it: `external` gives `external`,
`internal-session` gives `self-adjudicated`.

Two subcommands of the ten-fold operator binary `frob-tenfold` (N39a-01; an operator tool, not a `frob` verb, and
there is no `frob-conformance` command) are added here:

- `frob-tenfold adjudication generate`: reads the two blocks of the ADR, validates them (the checks below) and
  writes the record with the ADR's digest; exit 3 naming the block and field at fault.
- `frob-tenfold require-adjudication [--gate <partial|tournament>] --json` (the function
  `frob_conformance::tenfold::adjudication::require_record(gate)`): exit 0 printing
  `{gate, digest, record, party_kind, label, wording, strata: [{stratum, mode, provisional}], adr}`; exit 3
  naming the missing half (`party`, `simulators`) or the stale digest. `wording` is `unlocked` when the party
  kind is `external` or `external_replication` names a record, and `locked-pending-replication` otherwise. With
  no `--gate` it reads every gate that has a record and prints the least permissive reading: `party_kind` is
  `internal-session` if any gate's is, and `wording` is `unlocked` only if every gate's is. That form is the
  reader of the two wording rows below.

**Checks wired to the decision** (in the ten-fold harness of `frob-conformance`; the validator of the record
above):

- *Neutrality.* The rule is the role statement of N39b-27 (no authorship in any arm; one role per gate). The
  validator applies it to the party named in the record: the party's identity must not appear as an author,
  implementer or reviewer of any bead of either arm, nor in either arm's training or tuning logs; it joins the
  party record against the tracker export and the arms' manifests. (The role registry refuses an author as
  adjudicator when a role is registered at M3; that is the same rule at its other point of application, built
  and tested in N39a-02.)
- *Simulator independence.* For each stratum, no arm's manifest lists the adjudicating engine of that stratum
  among its training, tuning or screening environments for the stratum (the first of the four conditions of
  N39b-26, re-checked from the manifests at every freeze). An engine an arm uses on one stratum may adjudicate
  another.
- *Sealing order.* No sealed family may be created, or bound into a suite, while the adjudication record is
  absent. This bead provides the check, `require_record(gate)` and its command `frob-tenfold
  require-adjudication --gate <partial|tournament> --json`. It has two call sites, both in beads that depend on
  this one. The family registry's writer (N39a-13) calls it before it creates a family with `pool: "sealed"` and
  refuses the creation with the check's refusal, for the two gates that hold sealed material (`M3-partial` as
  `partial`, `M7-tournament` as `tournament`); a `rehearsal-<name>` store holds no sealed material and is
  exempt. The freeze-and-seal checker of N39a-40, at M3 and at M7, calls it again as a precondition of sealing
  step 2 (`commit-suite`, the entry that binds the sealed families) and lists in its gate record any family of
  the sealed pool created before the record's date, which after the first call site must be none.
- *Confirmation before the M7 seal.* `require-adjudication --gate tournament` also requires `confirmed` to be
  dated after the page regenerated at the M6 exit; a record confirmed only before M6 fails with that reason.
- *Label.* The label `self-adjudicated` is derived from the party kind (`internal-session`) by
  `adjudication_label`, and from nothing else. This bead checks agreement: the record's `label` equals the
  derivation from its `kind`, at M3 the role registry's `adjudication` equals it too, and if the party is the
  default session every ratio, table and report line produced by the harness carries `self-adjudicated`.
- *Wording.* The words wait for an external party. This bead enters two `[[conditioned_phrase]]` rows in the
  claims-wording registry of N00-15 (beside the two rows N41-05 enters for OPEN-PROG-1), both with `owner =
  "N41-06"`, `reader = "frob-tenfold require-adjudication --json"`, `field = "wording"` and `allowed =
  ["unlocked"]`; a reader that fails (no record) is the least permissive value, so the phrase is refused.
  Row `adjudication-independent`: patterns "independently accepted", "independently adjudicated", "accepted by
  an independent (party|adjudicator|evaluator)". Row `adjudication-tenfold-result`: the word as a stated result,
  patterns "ten-?fold (faster|quicker|speed-?up|advantage|improvement|reduction)",
  "(ten|10) times (faster|quicker)", "10[x×] (faster|quicker|speed-?up)" and
  "an order of magnitude (faster|quicker)". The second row is
  what "unqualified" means here: the patterns match a claimed result and do not match the name of the test,
  of its gates or the registered bracket "[Hypothesis until the ten-fold report.]", which stay writable.
  DESIGN: the condition is the adjudication clause of rules W1 and W3 of the rule set `tenfold-wording`
  (N39b-15, from M3), and both read this record, so the two lints cannot disagree; whether a measured ratio
  has earned the word at all (the cited cell's verdict, replication, the citation) is that rule set's alone,
  and these rows add nothing to it. They exist because the record is in force from M2 and the rule set from
  M3.
  DESIGN: the label depends on who adjudicates only. Whether a stratum's simulator has passed its
  acceptance gate is a separate field of the record (`provisional`), and a stratum without a qualified
  simulator gets its own label from the kill decision (`adjudication: cross-home`, or `descriptive`); an earlier
  test name here read as if a passed gate were also needed to clear `self-adjudicated`, which would have given
  one label two meanings.
- *Qualification of the simulator.* The record is provisional until the adjudicator-acceptance gate passes for the
  stratum (known answers it can express inside its own tolerances; 20 planted answers per stratum, 10 that must
  pass and 10 that must fail, classified correctly; no arm's logs show it in training or tuning; discordance with
  each arm's home referee reported on a 5% sample).

**Kill gate KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ** (decided at M3 and again at M7). Trigger: no neutral simulator passes the
adjudicator-acceptance gate for a stratum. Fallback named in advance: cross-home adjudication, labelled; if neither
home referee can run the other arm's answers, the stratum is `descriptive`. The decision per stratum is the bead
N39a-11's, with its ADR; this bead's configuration record takes the stratum's adjudication mode from that
decision and the harness renames the stratum's result type accordingly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before any sealed world family exists there is an adjudication record that is either the owner's answer or
      the default ADR (this bead writes the default at M2); `frob-tenfold require-adjudication` exits 3 without
      it and 0 with it, and no script or document of this bead names a `frob-conformance` command.
- [ ] `frob-tenfold adjudication generate` writes a record equal to the two fenced blocks of the ADR with the
      ADR's digest; an edit of the ADR's prose changes nothing but the digest, and an unknown field in a block
      is refused.
- [ ] The validator rejects a party who appears in an arm's authorship or logs, and an engine listed in an arm's
      training environments.
- [ ] Under the default, every harness output carries `self-adjudicated`, and the claims-wording lint, through
      the two `[[conditioned_phrase]]` rows, refuses "independently accepted" and "ten-fold faster" in a report
      fixture, allows both once the record's party kind is `external` or an external replication is recorded,
      and in every state leaves "the partial ten-fold gate" and the registered bracket of differentiator N13
      unflagged.
- [ ] The validator accepts a stratum only with a simulator assignment or a recorded KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ outcome
      (cross-home, or `descriptive`) with its ADR, and refuses a stratum with neither (on fixture records; the
      real per-stratum outcomes are written at M3 and M7 by N39a-11); the configuration record is generated from
      the decision record, so it equals what N39b-26 and N39b-27 write into their parts (each of those beads
      asserts its part after writing it), and the page shown to the owner quotes their options and table from
      their files unchanged.
- [ ] The page is regenerated from the register at the M6 exit, and `require-adjudication --gate tournament`
      refuses without a confirmation dated after it (the check the M7 sealing step of N39a-40 calls).

`stratum_without_simulator_requires_kill_decision`;
`aliases_move_with_prog_2`; `m7_seal_requires_fresh_confirmation`; `generate_reads_only_fenced_blocks` (prose

`record_unknown_field_refused`; `reader_without_gate_is_least_permissive` (partial `internal-session`,

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_prog2.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): on a scratch tournament directory, runs
`frob-tenfold require-adjudication --gate partial --json` with no record (expects exit 3 naming both
halves); writes the default ADR and runs `frob-tenfold adjudication generate`; the same check exits 0 with the
record digest; with `--gate tournament` and a confirmation dated before the scratch M6 page it exits 3, and
after a fresh confirmation 0; produces a synthetic ratio table (labelled `synthetic`) and asserts the
`self-adjudicated` label on every line; runs the claims-wording lint on a scratch report holding "independently
accepted" and "ten-fold faster" (refused), then again after the scratch ADR's party kind is changed to
`external` and the record regenerated (allowed); plants a party who is an implementer (expects rejection);
records a stratum with no qualifying engine and asserts the cross-home label. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/owner_prog2/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript, every line with `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`: `cmd` lines with the exact command line, exit code and wall time; the digest of the record in force
and of the ADR it was generated from; `refusal` lines with the reason and exit code; one `assert` per check
with expected and observed values; per-stratum assignment as `metric` lines; `assert` lines of kind
`negative-control` with `caught` for the three plants below; on failure the first differing value and the
scratch paths; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed, naming KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ as touched when the
fallback path is exercised.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] with α = 0.05 (first three levels 0.025, 0.00833…, 0.004166…); 20 planted answers
per stratum. Negative controls: a sealed family created before the record; a self-adjudicated ratio printed
without its label; an arm's own engine used as adjudicator.

## Considerations

- The default is honest but weak: it supports a measured ratio and not the word that makes the claim interesting.
  The cost is stated on the decision page so that the owner chooses with it in view.
- An external party needs lead time; asking at the M1 exit leaves one milestone to engage one before M2 closes.
- The adjudicating engine's own limits (contact model, terrain support) bound what can be accepted; a stratum it
  cannot represent is never forced through it.
- If the party changes between M3 and M7 the M3 partial gate remains a record of what it was; it is not re-labelled.
