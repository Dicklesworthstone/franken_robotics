## Background

The thesis names eight levers, and the second is "train few": evaluate a population on cheap rungs (static
analyses, a baseline controller, a shared family policy) and spend per-design training only on survivors. The
same measurement also decides half of the third lever, "spend where it matters". That
lever works only if a cheap rung orders designs roughly as the trained, promoted rung does. Whether it does is
unknown, is the single most decisive early measurement of the program, and has a name: the **rank-agreement
experiment**. It trains about forty designs of one morphology class individually and compares their ranking with
the ranking each cheap rung gives the same designs, as a top-weighted rank correlation τ with a confidence bound.
The thresholds are fixed before data: a rung may **select** in a stratum where the lower bound of τ is at least
0.7, may only **discard** from 0.5, and is `uninformative` below 0.5 or with no record.

The experiment costs more than the default compute envelope allows. FACT (arithmetic recorded with the envelope
proposal): about forty per-design trainings are about 45 device-hours, or 5 to 7.5 device-hours where the
specialists already trained for the learning chapter are reused. The default envelope is 16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour
and one device per job. So the experiment needs the owner's approval, and it is asked at M1 because every later
decision about the ladder leans on its result.

Fail-closed default: **not run above the default envelope; every cheap rung is `uninformative`, so it neither
selects nor discards, and results are labelled as unranked.** What the default costs: the decisive early
experiment of the program is late, and the lever "train few" stays a hypothesis; with most designs trained, the
compute ratio against incumbents is near 1 for that lever. A pilot of the same measurement on incumbent tools is
due at M3 regardless.

## Scope

In: the decision record; the request as pre-registered; the approval record and what it must contain; what the
default does to rung modes and labels; the check that no rung selects or discards without a record. Out: the
experiment's design and pre-registration (BD-145); running it (the pilot at M3 and the measurement on our engine at
M4); the envelope classes themselves; the family-policy pretraining envelope (a separate owner question).

## Technical approach

**What is put to the owner at the M0 exit** (one milestone early), generated from the pre-registration:

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Measure τ between each cheap rung and the trained, promoted rung, in one morphology class and task family |
| Design set | About forty designs, frozen before data, including planted winners and planted defects from the design corpus |
| Thresholds | 0.7 to select, 0.5 to discard, fixed before data; reachable verdicts enumerated at the planned n |
| Compute, variant A | Reuse specialists already trained for the learning gates: about 5 to 7.5 device-hours; envelope class E1 (a named gate measurement, at most 12 device-hours) |
| Compute, variant B | Train all forty: about 45 device-hours; envelope class E2 (a design campaign, 48 device-hours) |
| Kill rule written first | DESIGN proposal, for the owner to amend: stop if the first ten trainings fail to reach the task threshold within twice the incumbent's time; report τ on what exists, labelled incomplete |
| If not approved | The default above; the M3 pilot on incumbent tools still runs inside the default envelope if it fits, else it is recorded `not run` |

**Recording.** ADR `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-04-rank-agreement-compute.md` with the variant, ceiling in device-hours,
machines, the kill rule and the date; an approval entry in the envelope approvals registry that jobs cite. The
register row moves to `decided` or `defaulted`.

**What the answer switches** (existing mechanisms, checked here end to end):

1. *No approval.* A rank-agreement job whose estimate exceeds the class in force is refused before it starts
   (`FROB-REF-761`). The rank-agreement ledger has no record for the stratum, so every rung's mode there is
   `uninformative`; a request to select or discard with such a rung is refused (`FROB-REF-537`, repair: run the
   experiment, or accept unranked results); reports print "unranked" and describe finalists as "k survivors".
2. *Approval.* The job cites the approval; its spend is charged against the ceiling and it stops at the ceiling or
   on the kill rule; its result is a ledger record with τ, its bound, n and the identities; rung modes follow from
   the thresholds.
3. *Either way* the phase ledger of the ten-fold test records the fraction of designs trained, so the cost of the
   default is visible in the measured ratio.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The register row is `decided` or `defaulted` with an ADR at the M1 exit; the exit record states which, and
      under the default lists "rung selection and discarding" under capabilities removed.
- [ ] Under the default: a rank-agreement job above the default envelope is refused with `FROB-REF-761`; a rung
      asked to select or discard is refused with `FROB-REF-537`; a campaign report prints "unranked".
- [ ] Under an approval: a job citing it runs; a job exceeding the approved ceiling is stopped and recorded; the
      kill rule is evaluated from recorded values and its outcome written.
- [ ] An approval with no ceiling or no kill rule is rejected by the approvals loader.
- [ ] The decision page is generated from the pre-registration and shows both variants with their arithmetic.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_prog4.rs`: `default_refuses_job_761`; `default_rungs_uninformative`;
`select_without_record_refused_537`; `approval_requires_ceiling_and_kill_rule`; `job_stops_at_ceiling` (synthetic
job charging device-seconds); `kill_rule_evaluated_from_records`; `report_prints_unranked_under_default`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_prog4.sh`: with no approval, submits the pre-registered job through `frob eval rank-agreement
--json` (expects exit 3 and `FROB-REF-761`), runs a small cascade on `batch-cpu` and asserts the "unranked" label
and "k survivors" wording; then writes a scratch approval for a toy-sized variant that fits the default envelope
(labelled `synthetic`), runs it, and asserts a ledger record and a mode derived from the thresholds. Logs: `cmd`,
exit codes, evidence block, the envelope class in force and requested, `refusal` lines, spend against ceiling as
`metric` lines, a `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 45 and 5 to 7.5 device-hours; classes E1 (12 device-hours per gate) and E2 (48 device-hours);
thresholds 0.7 and 0.5. Negative controls: a rung that selects with no record; an approval without a kill rule; a
job that hides its estimate below the class and overruns (stopped at the class limit, recorded as an overrun).

## Considerations

- This is one of the six questions that matter most and the cheapest of them to answer: variant A costs a few
  device-hours and decides whether a whole lever exists.
- A low τ is a result, not a failure of the experiment: it fires the kill gate for rank agreement, puts rungs in
  `discard-only` or `uninformative`, and the lost factor is recorded in the ten-fold report.
- The approval is for this experiment only; campaigns and pretraining have their own requests.
