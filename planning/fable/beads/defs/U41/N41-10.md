## Background

No hardware is in the scope of this program. The design treats reality as an **import lane**: every report prints
a reality line; where no real data has been admitted the line reads `reality: no-claim`, and that is recorded as
the state and not as a pass. A band (a statement that a simulated quantity matches a real one within stated limits)
is issued only inside a calibrated cell, a named robot, surface and task for which real logs were admitted,
predictions were frozen before they were compared, and held-out data scored them.

Two questions decide whether that lane ever carries anything, and only the owner can answer them because they
involve acquiring things, licences and money:

- **OPEN-GAUNT-1.** Which real-robot dataset, if any, is admitted to the Reality lane first, with what licence and
  provenance. (Its engineering half, whether any public paired simulation-and-reality dataset has enough robots per
  class to test exchangeability across units, belongs to the Ground lead; none was identified at planning time.)
  Fail-closed default: **`reality: no-claim` everywhere; the second-simulator stand-in is labelled `synthetic`.**
  Cost: no Reality claim; the first-real-cell gate stays an import lane.
- **OPEN-GROUND-2.** Whether any bench hardware is acquired. Fail-closed default: **none.** Cost: the optional bench
  packages never run. Sourcing may start at M0, and nothing waits for it.

Both are needed by M5, when the calibration subsystem lands; the risk "calibration data never arrives" is answered
by making the resting state harmless: it blocks nothing.

## Scope

In: the decision records; the candidate table for datasets and its admission conditions; the bench packages as
options with their costs and completion conditions; what each answer switches on; the label rules under the
defaults. Out: dataset admission mechanics, calibration, frozen predictions and bands (the Ground beads); the
replication kit; card catalogues' data sources (other owner questions).

## Technical approach

**OPEN-GAUNT-1: what is put to the owner at the M4 exit.** A candidate table produced by the Ground lead's survey,
one row per dataset: source and licence; whether the licence permits use, redistribution in a kit, or neither;
robots per class and units per robot; signals logged (currents, encoders, whole-body pose, contact); measurement
uncertainty stated or not; provenance (who measured, when, with what instrument); which cells (robot, surface,
task) it could calibrate. A dataset lacking licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty cannot be
admitted whatever the answer (`FROB-REF-873`, repair: supply the missing fields). The owner chooses one dataset,
several in an order, or none.

**OPEN-GROUND-2: the bench packages offered** (all optional, none scheduled against a milestone, none gating a
release; each leaves `reality: no-claim` in place if it never runs):

| Package | Rig | Measures | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Actuator stand | One actuator of a catalogue class on a stand with a known payload | Current, encoder, optionally torque | A sealed prediction of a held-out trajectory is scored; the card gains `measured` fields |
| B. Ramp and material pairs | An incline and a drag sled | Slip angle, drag at two normal loads | Friction per pair with its interval; a named identifiability alias shown separated |
| C. Instrumented cart | The cart-and-sill fixture with 60 mm and 75 mm wheels | Current, encoder, crossing time | A frozen prediction and the A/B ranking are scored on a sealed day |
| D. One built link | A printed or machined link with declared fiducials | A scan and a scale reading | The as-built loop closes and the tolerance audit is produced |
| E. Calibrated release | Named robot, surface and task cells | Whole-robot logs | Bands issued per cell from held-out predictions, with limits stated |
| F. Revalidation | Independently acquired traces for the cells of E | Whole-robot logs | Each ranking reversal and calibration limit reported |

The page lists for each package an estimated cost band and lead time supplied by the Ground lead (labelled
`assumed` until quoted), and which catalogue cards would move from `assumed` to `measured`.

**Recording.** ADRs `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md` and `ADR-PROG-07-bench-hardware.md`; register rows
OPEN-GAUNT-1 and OPEN-GROUND-2 move to `decided` or `defaulted`. The engineering half OPEN-GROUND-1 is closed by
the survey's record, not by this bead.

**What the answers switch** (existing mechanisms, checked here):

1. *Defaults in force.* Every report's reality line is `no-claim`; the calibration lane's rehearsal uses a second
   simulator as stand-in and every artifact from it is labelled `synthetic`; a request for a band is refused; the
   first-real-cell gate is recorded as "import lane, no data admitted", which is a state and not a pass or a fail.
2. *A dataset admitted.* It enters through the admission verb with its manifest; cells it covers become eligible
   for calibration; nothing outside those cells changes its reality line.
3. *A bench package acquired.* Its rig and protocol are registered; its logs are admitted like any dataset; its
   completion condition is the acceptance of that package's bead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both register rows are `decided` or `defaulted` with ADRs at the M5 exit; the exit record states which.
- [ ] Under the defaults: every report fixture prints `reality: no-claim`; a band request is refused; rehearsal
      artifacts carry `synthetic`; the first-real-cell gate's record reads as an import lane with no data.
- [ ] A dataset manifest missing licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty is refused with
      `FROB-REF-873` under any answer.
- [ ] A report that states a Reality band for a cell with no admitted data is refused (the negative control of
      the reality line).
- [ ] The candidate table and the package page are generated from the Ground survey's records, with `assumed`
      labels on unquoted costs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_reality.rs`: `default_reality_line_is_no_claim`;
`band_request_refused_without_cell`; `rehearsal_artifacts_synthetic`; `dataset_without_licence_refused_873`;
`import_lane_record_is_state_not_pass`; `admitted_dataset_changes_only_its_cells` (synthetic manifest labelled
`synthetic`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_reality.sh`: under the defaults runs `frob report run <fixture-run> --json` and asserts the
reality line; requests a band through the calibration verb and asserts the refusal; ingests a synthetic dataset
manifest with a missing licence (expects `FROB-REF-873`) and a complete one (labelled `synthetic`), and asserts
that only its cell changes. Logs: `cmd`, exit codes, evidence block with the reality field, `refusal` lines, a
`summary` naming GATE-GROUND-01 and GATE-GROUND-10 as touched (state only).

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the default texts; the six packages with their completion conditions. Negative controls: a planted
Reality band with no data; a rehearsal artifact without its `synthetic` label; a dataset admitted without
uncertainty.

## Considerations

- A recorded `no-claim` satisfies the program's definition of done for the ground clause; no calibration against
  hardware is required for done. The owner's answer changes what can be said, not whether the program finishes.
- Licences decide whether a dataset can travel in a replication kit; one that cannot is cited by hash and
  acquisition protocol.
- Bench package C uses the plan's first fixture and is the cheapest path to one calibrated cell.
- Privacy: logs from a private place inherit the private mark and never enter a kit.
