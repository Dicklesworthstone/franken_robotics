## Background

No hardware is in the scope of this program. The design treats reality as an **import lane**: every report prints
a reality line; where no real data has been admitted the line reads `reality: no-claim`, and that is recorded as
the state and not as a pass. A band (a statement that a simulated quantity matches a real one within stated limits)
is issued only inside a calibrated cell, a named robot, surface and task for which real logs were admitted,
predictions were frozen before they were compared, and held-out data scored them.

Two questions decide whether that lane ever carries anything, and only the owner can answer them because they
involve acquiring things, licences and money:

- **OPEN-GAUNT-1.** Which real-robot dataset, if any, is admitted to the Reality lane first, with what licence and
  provenance. (Its engineering half, whether any public paired simulation-and-reality dataset has enough robots per
  class to test exchangeability across units, belongs to the Ground lead; none was identified at planning time.)
  Fail-closed default: **`reality: no-claim` everywhere; the second-simulator stand-in is labelled `synthetic`.**
  Cost: no Reality claim; the first-real-cell gate stays an import lane.
- **OPEN-GROUND-2.** Whether any bench hardware is acquired. Fail-closed default: **none.** Cost: the optional bench
  packages never run. Sourcing may start at M0, and nothing waits for it.

Both are needed by M5, when the calibration subsystem lands; the risk "calibration data never arrives" is answered
by making the resting state harmless: it blocks nothing.

## Scope

This bead is the owner packet of the two questions: what is common to them. In: the one page
`docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-1.md` that puts both questions to the owner at the M4 exit, with each default, what
the default costs and the latest milestone at which an answer changes the outcome; the generation of that page
from the records of the beads below, so that no table is typed twice; the dated record that it was put; the
checks, by unit test and end-to-end script, that the defaults are in force and that each answer switches only
what it should; the label rules under the defaults.

Out, each in the bead of the chapter that owns the question, which writes the question's record and its
register row at the M5 exit:

| Question | Bead | What it holds that this bead does not |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN-GROUND-1 (the engineering half of the dataset question) | N30b-25, which this bead depends on | The survey; the one candidate table (thirteen fields per candidate: source, licence and what it permits, robots and units, signals and rates, instrument uncertainty, provenance, clocks, the denominator of attempts, assignment of conditions, whether a holdout can be sealed, the cell it could calibrate, what could not be learned, checklist result and class of support); the admission checklist; the Ground lead's finding; `docs/adr/OPEN-GROUND-1.md` |
| OPEN-GAUNT-1 (the owner's half) | N38b-26 | The forms of the answer (one dataset, several in an order, or none); the conditions an answer must satisfy; the record `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md`; the status of the Reality gates under each outcome; the tombstone |
| OPEN-GROUND-2 | N30b-26 | The options table of the six bench packages A to F (rig, measures, completion condition, what becomes sayable, what stays `no-claim`); the value statement; cost bands and lead times; the forms of the decision; the record `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-07-bench-hardware.md`, which lists the packages whose real rig runs are funded (none under the default); the package beads close at M5 on their protocols and dry runs whatever the decision says, so no close waits on a rig |

Also out: dataset admission mechanics, calibration, frozen predictions and bands (the Ground beads); the
Reality lane's record and the row of GATE-GROUND-10 (BD-397); the replication kit; card catalogues' data
sources (other owner questions).

## Technical approach

**The page** (`docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-1.md`, DESIGN: one page for the owner's M5 input on the Reality lane;
it is the supporting file of the record of OPEN-GAUNT-1). Generated by `frob-conformance` from three sources and
from nothing typed by hand:

1. the two register rows (question, default, cost of the default, deadline), from the open-question register;
2. for OPEN-GAUNT-1, the candidate table of the survey: the machine-readable block of
   `docs/adr/OPEN-GROUND-1.md` (N30b-25), one row per dataset with the fields that bead defines, followed by
   the Ground lead's finding. The page states beside it the one rule no answer can lift: a dataset lacking
   licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty cannot be admitted whatever the answer
   (`FROB-REF-873`, repair: supply the missing fields);
3. for OPEN-GROUND-2, the options file `docs/decisions/OPEN-GROUND-2.md` (N30b-26), included unchanged: the six
   packages with what each measures, completes and makes sayable, with cost bands and lead times labelled
   `assumed` until quoted. All six are optional, none is scheduled against a milestone, none gates a release,
   and each leaves `reality: no-claim` in place if it never runs.

A source that does not exist yet is printed as "not on file" with the bead that owes it, never as an empty
table. The page is regenerated whenever a source changes, and the copy put to the owner is recorded with its
date and the digests of its three sources.

**Recording.** This bead writes no decision record. The records are `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md`
(OPEN-GAUNT-1, written by N38b-26) and `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-07-bench-hardware.md` (OPEN-GROUND-2, written by
N30b-26); those beads move the register rows to `decided` or `defaulted`. The engineering half OPEN-GROUND-1 is
closed by the survey's record (N30b-25). The exit record of M5 states, from the register, which of the three
rows were decided and which defaulted.

**What the answers switch** (existing mechanisms, checked here):

1. *Defaults in force.* Every report's reality line is `no-claim`; the calibration lane's rehearsal uses a second
   simulator as stand-in and every artifact from it is labelled `synthetic`; a request for a band is refused
   (`FROB-REF-889` of `ground.band`, BD-393; that refusal under the defaults is checked by BD-397, whose lane
   record and script already run the band verb, not here); the
   first-real-cell gate is recorded as an import lane with no data admitted, which is a state and not a pass or
   a fail: in the gate register its row (written by BD-397) has the terminal status `no-claim` with the reason
   `no-dataset-admitted`, and before either half of the dataset question is recorded it reads `blocked` with
   `detail = "OPEN-GROUND-1"` (the same words as N30b-25 and N38b-26).
2. *A dataset admitted.* It enters through the admission verb with its manifest; cells it covers become eligible
   for calibration; nothing outside those cells changes its reality line.
3. *A bench package acquired.* Its rig and protocol are registered; its logs are admitted like any dataset; its
   completion condition is the acceptance of that package's bead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The page exists at the M4 exit, was put to the owner (a dated record with the digests of its sources),
      and is byte-equal to its generation from the register rows, the survey record and the options file.
- [ ] Each of the two register rows names its decision bead (N38b-26, N30b-26) and its record
      (`ADR-PROG-06`, `ADR-PROG-07`). The rows are moved to `decided` or `defaulted` by those beads at the M5
      exit; this bead's checks are written against both outcomes on fixture register states (defaulted; a
      dataset admitted; a package acquired) and are run by the exit runner over the recorded result.
- [ ] Under the defaults: every report fixture prints `reality: no-claim`; rehearsal artifacts carry
      `synthetic`; the GATE-GROUND-10 row reads
      `blocked` with `detail = "OPEN-GROUND-1"` while neither half of the dataset question is recorded, then the
      terminal status `no-claim` with reason `no-dataset-admitted` (a state, never `pass` or `fail`, and never
      `not run`), checked on fixture register states for both moments.
- [ ] A dataset manifest missing licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty is refused with
      `FROB-REF-873` under any answer.
- [ ] A report that states a Reality band for a cell with no admitted data is refused (the negative control of
      the reality line).
- [ ] The candidate table on the page has one row per candidate of the survey record and the package section
      is the options file unchanged, with `assumed` labels on unquoted costs; a hand edit of the page is
      detected.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_reality.rs`: `default_reality_line_is_no_claim`;
`rehearsal_artifacts_synthetic`; `dataset_without_licence_refused_873`;
`import_lane_record_is_state_not_pass`; `admitted_dataset_changes_only_its_cells` (synthetic manifest labelled
`synthetic`); `ground10_blocked_then_no_claim` (the two fixture states, and a mutant that writes `not run`
caught); `page_equals_generation` (the page is byte-equal to its generation from a fixture register, a
fixture survey block of two candidates and a fixture options file, and a hand edit is detected);
`missing_source_printed_as_not_on_file`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_reality.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): under the defaults runs `frob report run
<fixture-run> --json` and asserts the reality line; reads the GATE-GROUND-10 row of a scratch gate register before and after
a fixture default record of the dataset question (`blocked`, detail OPEN-GROUND-1; then `no-claim`, reason
`no-dataset-admitted`); ingests a synthetic dataset
manifest with a missing licence (expects `FROB-REF-873`) and a complete one (labelled `synthetic`), and asserts
that only its cell changes. Logs: `cmd`, exit codes, evidence block with the reality field, `refusal` lines, a
`summary` naming GATE-GROUND-01 and GATE-GROUND-10 as touched (state only).

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the default texts; the page's package section equal to the options file of N30b-26 (the six
packages with their completion conditions are known answers of that bead). Negative controls: a planted
Reality band with no data; a rehearsal artifact without its `synthetic` label; a dataset admitted without
uncertainty; a candidate row typed onto the page that is not in the survey record.

## Considerations

- A recorded `no-claim` satisfies the program's definition of done for the ground clause; no calibration against
  hardware is required for done. The owner's answer changes what can be said, not whether the program finishes.
- Licences decide whether a dataset can travel in a replication kit; one that cannot is cited by hash and
  acquisition protocol.
- Bench package C uses the plan's first fixture and is the cheapest path to one calibrated cell (stated on
  the options file of N30b-26).
- Privacy: logs from a private place inherit the private mark and never enter a kit.
