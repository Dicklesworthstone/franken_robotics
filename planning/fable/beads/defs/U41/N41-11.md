## Background

Four policy questions are put to the owner as one group, because they are all of the form "how careful by
default", they have safe defaults that cost nothing but caution, and they fall due between M2 and M4. Each default
is already the conservative choice, so the program proceeds under it; the owner's answer can relax one, with the
consequences stated.

| Question | Fail-closed default | Blocks | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPEN-SAFE-2: do policy weights trained in a private or research-only scene inherit its mark and licence set? | They inherit both | GATE-SAFE-04, GATE-SAFE-05 | M2 in the register: put to the owner at the M1 exit and decided or defaulted at the M2 exit; the two gates are M3 gates, so N37-20 records the option in force before the M3 exit review measures them |
| OPEN-CHRON-4: retention windows for failure captures and the default store budget on each machine | 30 days and 64 GB | GATE-CHRON-12 | M2 |
| OPEN-SAFE-4: retention periods for job logs, idempotency keys and retired confirmation clusters | Job logs and keys for 30 days; retired clusters sealed indefinitely | M3 | M3 |
| OPEN-SAFE-3: are sandboxed custom reward terms admitted, and in what sandbox? | Not admitted; the closed expression language only | M4 | M4 |

What the defaults cost: nothing beyond the defaults themselves. Why they are the owner's: a scan of a real place
is private data, and whether a network trained in it carries that privacy is a judgement about leakage through
weights, not a measurement the program can take; retention trades disk against the ability to explain an old
decision; a sandbox for agent-written code is an attack surface the owner must choose to open.

Terms. The *private mark* is a label on a scene ingested from a real place; every artifact derived from the scene
(stills, fixtures, telemetry, reports) inherits it, an export checks it, and a purge removes the scene and what
depends on it. A *licence set* is the join of the licences of the assets in a pack. A *failure capture* is the
retained high-rate telemetry around a failure. A *retired confirmation cluster* is a sealed set of worlds of the
blinded qualification lane that has been used and must never be reused or revealed early. The *closed expression
language* is the fixed grammar in which reward and requirement terms are written, with no user code.

## Scope

This bead is the owner packet of the grouped row: what is common to the four questions. In: the one page that
puts the four questions to the owner as a group, with the consequence of each relaxing answer and the calendar
on which each is put; the rule, enforced by the ADR validator, that a relaxing answer names its basis; the
generated record `registries/policy/data-policy.toml` of the four decided or defaulted values; the member
`custom_reward_terms` of the capability report `frob capabilities --json` (DESIGN: a constant `"not-admitted"`
in `frob-verbs`, changed only by the ADR of N37-09 and the mechanism bead that ADR creates; no other bead adds
it, and the cross-check below reads it); the cross-check that the configuration in force on a machine equals
that record and that the record equals the ADRs; the end-to-end script that shows the defaults in force.

Out, each in the decision bead of the chapter that owns the mechanism, which depends on this bead and writes
the question's ADR and its register row at its own deadline:

| Question | Decision bead | What it holds that this bead does not |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN-SAFE-2 | N37-20 | Options A to D, the two configuration keys and the parents-table rule, the gate rows and purge closure under each option, the tests `weights_inherit` |
| OPEN-SAFE-3 | N37-09 | Options A to D, the ceiling, the paper pilot over at least 30 task definitions with its falsifier, the conditions on any admitted form, the kill condition |
| OPEN-SAFE-4 | N37-30 | The three configuration keys, the start-up constraints (key period ≥ log period and the others), what collection does, the options with their costs, the virtual-clock tests at 29 and 31 days |
| OPEN-CHRON-4 | N29b-12 | The five measurements per machine, the days-to-full arithmetic, the six parameters priced (window, store budget, memo budget, eviction order, over-budget behaviour, private scenes), `store.toml`, the policy version |

Also out: the mechanisms (retention and tombstones, BD-209; private marks and export checks, BD-305; the reward
compiler, BD-194; sealed clusters, N24b-17; the owner-held configuration file, N37-02), which belong to their
own beads.

## Technical approach

**Where the values live** (DESIGN: one place per value). The mechanisms read each value from exactly one place,
the configuration its own chapter defines:

| Question | Value read by the mechanism from | Keys and defaults |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN-SAFE-2 | the owner-held safety configuration `$FROB_STATE/safety.toml` (N37-02; decided by N37-20) | `private.weights_inherit = true`, `licence.weights_inherit = true` |
| OPEN-SAFE-4 | the same file (decided by N37-30) | `retention.job_log_days = 30`, `retention.idempotency_key_days = 30`, `retention.retired_clusters = "sealed-indefinitely"` |
| OPEN-CHRON-4 | `store.toml` under the daemon's state root (the collector of BD-209; decided by N29b-12) | `[window] failure_capture_days = 30`; `[budget] store_bytes` and `memo_bytes`, 64 GB (64 × 10⁹ bytes) each; `[evict] order`; per-machine overrides keyed by hardware fingerprint |
| OPEN-SAFE-3 | no configuration key (decided by N37-09) | the capability report `frob capabilities --json` carries `custom_reward_terms: "not-admitted"`; a reward term supplied as a program is refused with `FROB-REF-828` |

Rejected alternative: the store collector, the exporter, the trainer's artifact writer and the task compiler
reading the values from a policy file in the repository. That would be a second source for values
the safety chapter keeps in a file only the owner of a machine may write (its rule is that a verb never reads a
second file) and the store keeps per machine; and a key that switches custom reward terms on would be an off
switch for the rule that no verb accepts a program, which only an ADR and a new mechanism bead may lift. The
repository file is therefore kept as a record and never read by a mechanism.

**Policy record** (DESIGN): `registries/policy/data-policy.toml`, schema `frob.datapolicy/1`, generated from the
four ADRs and from nothing else, never edited by hand and never read at run time by the collector, the exporter,
the trainer or the task compiler. It lists the expected value of each key above under the key's own name, so no
value has two names:

```toml
schema = "frob.datapolicy/1"
[safety]                                             # expected values of keys of safety.toml
"private.weights_inherit" = true                     # OPEN-SAFE-2, ADR-PROG-08
"licence.weights_inherit" = true
"retention.job_log_days" = 30                        # OPEN-SAFE-4, ADR-PROG-10
"retention.idempotency_key_days" = 30
"retention.retired_clusters" = "sealed-indefinitely"
[store]                                              # expected values of keys of store.toml
"window.failure_capture_days" = 30                   # OPEN-CHRON-4, ADR-PROG-11
"budget.store_bytes" = { default = 64000000000 }     # 64 GB; per machine; a named machine may override
"budget.memo_bytes" = { default = 64000000000 }      # 64 GB
[capabilities]
custom_reward_terms = "not-admitted"                 # OPEN-SAFE-3, ADR-PROG-09
```

The store budgets are recorded in bytes, as `store.toml` holds them: "64 GB" is 64 × 10⁹ bytes (the unit rule
stated in N29b-12).

**What is put to the owner** (at the M1 exit for the two M2 questions, then one milestone before each of the
others), with the consequence of each alternative:

1. *Weights do not inherit* (OPEN-SAFE-2 answered no): weights trained in a private scene could be exported and
   published; the page states that whether weights can leak a place is unmeasured, and that the licence join of
   research-only assets would no longer bind the weights. Any answer other than the default must name its basis:
   for the privacy mark (options C and D of N37-20) the leakage test it relies on; for the licence set alone
   (option B) the datasets and licence terms it was decided for.
2. *Other retention values* (OPEN-CHRON-4, OPEN-SAFE-4): shorter windows free disk and shorten the period in which
   a failure can be re-examined at full rate; longer ones need the store budget raised. Pinned decision-relevant
   chunks are kept whatever the window, and a collected object leaves a tombstone. Retired confirmation clusters
   may be destroyed instead of sealed only by an explicit answer; they may never be released for reuse.
3. *Custom reward terms admitted* (OPEN-SAFE-3 answered yes): the answer must name the sandbox (process isolation,
   no network, no file system, a time and memory cap, deterministic arithmetic) and accept that results from
   custom terms carry a label and are excluded from qualification.

**Recording.** One ADR per question, each written by the question's decision bead at its own deadline (DESIGN:
the four owner questions take the program's consecutive numbers, so that one identifier names each record in
every bead): `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-08-weights-inherit.md` for OPEN-SAFE-2 (N37-20),
`ADR-PROG-09-custom-reward-terms.md` for OPEN-SAFE-3 (N37-09), `ADR-PROG-10-retention-periods.md` for
OPEN-SAFE-4 (N37-30) and `ADR-PROG-11-store-retention.md` for OPEN-CHRON-4 (N29b-12; the chapter-prefixed
identifier ADR-CHRON-11 is not used for it). Each ADR carries its decided values in a machine-readable front
matter block; the policy record is regenerated from the four blocks, with the default for a question whose ADR
does not exist yet; the register rows move to `decided` or `defaulted` at their own deadlines, by the decision
beads. The ADR validator of this bead runs on all four: a relaxing answer for OPEN-SAFE-2 must name the leakage
test it relies on (privacy mark) or the datasets it was decided for (licence set alone), and a relaxing answer for OPEN-SAFE-3 must name the option and, for a general sandbox, the
sandbox with its five properties (item 3 above) and the pilot table's hash.

**Checks that the policy is in force** (each mechanism's own tests and each decision bead's tests exercise it;
this bead adds the cross-check that the running configuration, as printed by `frob doctor --json`,
`frob store status --json` and `frob capabilities --json`, equals the policy record and that the policy record
equals the ADRs; a value in force that differs from the record with no owner ADR stating it is a failed check
that blocks the exit record):

- weights written by training in a scene carrying the private mark carry the mark and the licence set, and their
  export is checked like any derived artifact;
- the collector removes unpinned failure captures older than the window, never a pinned chunk, and stays within
  the budget by collecting oldest first; a store over budget with only pinned content reports it and refuses new
  captures rather than deleting pinned ones;
- job logs and idempotency keys older than their window are collected; a retired cluster is unreadable by every
  verb of a discovery session at any clock value (the owner's audit scope, whose use is what retires a cluster,
  aside), as N37-30 specifies;
- a task file containing a custom term is refused by the compiler (`FROB-REF-828`) while the capability report
  says `custom_reward_terms: "not-admitted"`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner page with the four questions, their defaults and the consequence of each alternative is on file
      and was put to the owner at the M1 exit for OPEN-CHRON-4 and OPEN-SAFE-2 (a dated record); the calendar
      entries for OPEN-SAFE-4 (put at the M2 exit, due M3) and OPEN-SAFE-3 (put at the M3 exit, due M4) exist.
- [ ] The policy record exists with the defaults at M2, uses the configuration keys' own names, and is
      byte-equal to its generation from the ADRs.
- [ ] Each of the four register rows names its decision bead (N37-20, N37-09, N37-30, N29b-12) and its ADR
      identifier (`ADR-PROG-08` to `ADR-PROG-11`); the rows are moved to `decided` or `defaulted` by those
      beads at their own deadlines, and this bead's cross-check passes on the result each time.
- [ ] The values printed by `frob doctor --json`, `frob store status --json` and `frob capabilities --json`
      equal the policy record on both machines; a planted difference is reported with the key's name.
- [ ] Under the defaults, with the mechanisms that exist at M2: a 31-day-old unpinned failure capture is
      collected and a pinned one is kept (BD-209); a store over budget with only pinned content refuses new
      captures; a task with a custom reward term supplied as a program is refused with `FROB-REF-828` (N37-03)
      while `frob capabilities --json` says `custom_reward_terms: "not-admitted"`. The clauses whose mechanisms
      land at M3 (weights from a private scene carry mark and licence, BD-305; a 31-day-old job log and key are
      collected and a retired cluster cannot be read, N24b-17) are acceptance criteria of the decision beads
      N37-20 and N37-30, which depend on this bead and run its cross-check after they record their answers.
- [ ] A hand edit of the policy record with no ADR is detected and refused by the loader; a non-default value
      in `safety.toml` or `store.toml` with no owner ADR stating it fails the cross-check.
- [ ] A relaxing answer for OPEN-SAFE-2 without a named leakage test (privacy mark) or named datasets (licence
      set alone), or for OPEN-SAFE-3 without a named option and, for a general sandbox, a named sandbox, is
      rejected by the ADR validator.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/data_policy.rs`: `defaults_match_register`; `policy_equals_adrs`;
`hand_edit_detected`; `record_uses_configuration_key_names` (every key of the record exists in the schema of
`safety.toml` or `store.toml`, or is the capability name); `running_configuration_equals_record` and
`unrecorded_non_default_value_fails` (a scratch `safety.toml` with `retention.job_log_days = 7` and no ADR);
`unpinned_capture_collected_after_30_days` and `pinned_capture_kept` (store clock advanced through the store's
test hook); `budget_exceeded_by_pinned_refuses_new_captures`; `custom_reward_term_refused_828`;
`capabilities_reports_custom_reward_terms` (the member is present with `"not-admitted"`, and a planted value
that differs from the record fails the cross-check naming `custom_reward_terms`);
`relaxing_answer_needs_named_basis`; `record_defaults_for_missing_adrs` (with no ADR on file the record holds the
four defaults). The tests of mark inheritance (`weights_inherit`) and of retired clusters are N37-20's and N37-30's.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_data_policy.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): prints the three reports and compares them with
the policy record; plants `retention.job_log_days = 7` in a scratch `safety.toml` and asserts the cross-check
fails naming the key; fills a scratch store past 64 GB of unpinned synthetic captures (sparse files) and runs
`frob store collect --key <k> --json` (a real collection: without a key the call is a dry run that removes
nothing, VERBS-store), asserting the budget and the tombstones, then a store full of pinned captures refusing a new
one; submits a task with a custom term supplied as a program and asserts `FROB-REF-828`. Logs: `cmd`, exit codes, the policy digest, `refusal` lines, store bytes before and after as `metric`
lines against the budget, a `summary` naming GATE-CHRON-12 as touched (defaults in force only; GATE-SAFE-04 and
GATE-SAFE-05 are touched by N37-20's script).

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 30 days, 64 GB, sealed indefinitely, inherit both, not admitted. Negative controls: exported weights
without the mark; a pinned chunk collected; a retired cluster read; a custom term compiled; a policy record whose
key has no counterpart in a configuration schema; a non-default value in force with no ADR. The clauses on marks
and retired clusters are run by the decision beads N37-20 and N37-30 at M3, where their mechanisms exist; this
script does not carry them.

## Considerations

- The defaults add up to the cautious product; none blocks a milestone, which is why the question can wait for the
  owner without holding work.
- The store budget is per machine; the large-memory machine that holds the world bank may need more for its own
  captures, set by a per-machine override in the same ADR.
- Retention never overrides a legal or licence obligation to delete; a purge of a private scene removes its
  derived artifacts regardless of windows.
