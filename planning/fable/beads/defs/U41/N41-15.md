## Background

The ten-fold test is designed so that the party proposing a result never checks it, and so that the rival is as
strong as an honest advocate can make it. Before anything can be frozen for the partial gate at M3, four inputs
are needed that the test's own chapter assigns to the project owner. They are adopted into the open-question
register with the defaults that chapter states.

| Question | Fail-closed default | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN-TENFOLD-3: who the rival advocates are, and their budget | Agent sessions that have contributed nothing to FrankenRobotics, 40 device-hours per stratum, and the label `rival: internally advocated` on every result | GATE-TENFOLD-04 (freeze and seal) |
| OPEN-TENFOLD-4: the compute envelope for the partial gate and the tournament (a campaign class for each run, and the pretraining class where an arm builds a standing asset) | Nothing above the default class runs, the gate is recorded as `not run`, and M3 does not exit | GATE-TENFOLD-06 and GATE-TENFOLD-07 |
| OPEN-TENFOLD-6: the agent model, its revision and its caps | The agent the project itself is developed with at the gate's date, pinned; one agent only, with the result stated as conditional on it | Every freeze |
| OPEN-TENFOLD-8: ratification of the thresholds 3 and 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5, the margin 0.05 and the limit of four confirmatory cells | As written | The first suite hash |

Why these are the owner's. A *rival advocate* is whoever is responsible for making the incumbent arm strong:
choosing its tools, its warm configuration and its tuning within a budget; if the advocate is weak the ratio is
inflated, so who they are and what they may spend is a credibility decision. The *agent model* drives both arms;
the result is conditional on it, and choosing it is choosing what the result is about. The *thresholds* define what
words the report may use. The *compute* is the owner's money.

One of the four defaults is unlike every other default in the register: OPEN-TENFOLD-4 at its default **blocks the
M3 exit**. The partial gate must be recorded with its outcome; "not run" for lack of compute is not an outcome the
milestone can exit on. It is therefore asked two milestones early and repeated.

Each of the four questions has its own decision bead in the ten-fold unit, which holds the substance of the
decision: N39b-28 (OPEN-TENFOLD-3: the options for advocates, what they receive, the budget rule, eligibility,
the under-built objection), N39b-29 (OPEN-TENFOLD-4: the cost arithmetic per cell, the request, the options and
what each answer means for the gates), N39b-31 (OPEN-TENFOLD-6: the model pin, the caps and how they are chosen,
revision drift) and N39b-33 (OPEN-TENFOLD-8: the block of parameters, the evidence shown, the binding to the
first suite hash). This bead is the program's side of the same four questions: when they are put to the owner,
how the four answers reach the harness as one configuration record, and the two checks that span all four (a
freeze with an open row, and the M3 exit on the envelope). It does not restate the decisions.

## Scope

In: the schedule on which the four questions are put to the owner (the envelope notice at the M0 exit, the
one-page request at the M1 exit); the numbering of the four ADRs and the movement of the four register rows; the
configuration record `registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml`, generated from the four decisions and read by the
harness at every freeze, with its digest in the suite manifest; the freeze precondition on the four rows; the
M3-exit rule for the envelope; the end-to-end script over all four.

Out, and where it is:
- the content of each decision (options, arithmetic, evidence, the supporting file `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-<n>.md`)
  and the enforcement that belongs to one question alone: N39b-28 (advocates: eligibility, the budget rule, the
  label `rival: internally advocated`, the under-built objection), N39b-29 (envelope: cost per cell, the request,
  scheduling to the approved cap, refusal `FROB-REF-761`), N39b-31 (agent: equal caps across arms, the revision
  check, cap breaches), N39b-33 (thresholds: the block in exact rationals, its digest in the event-log entry that
  commits the first suite hash, the refusal `tenfold.parameters-frozen`). N39b-28, N39b-29 and N39b-33 depend on
  this bead for the record format; this bead depends on N39b-31, which is decided at M1;
- the adjudicator and neutral party (N41-06); the deadline per stratum, incumbent licences and the fourth arm (the
  ten-fold unit's questions); the freeze procedure, the arms and the decision rule themselves.

## Technical approach

**Configuration record** (DESIGN): `registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml`, generated from the four ADRs and their
supporting files, never edited by hand, and read by the ten-fold harness at every freeze. It is a projection of
the four decisions, not a second place where they are made: each table names the ADR it was generated from, and
a value that differs from its ADR fails the digest check.

```toml
[advocates]                         # from ADR-PROG-15 (N39b-28)
adr     = "ADR-PROG-15"
kind    = "internal-sessions"       # or "external", with names and affiliations, or "mixed" by stratum
budget_floor_device_hours_per_stratum = 40   # the budget per stratum is max(this, the builders' logged compute)
label   = "rival: internally advocated"
[agent]                             # from ADR-PROG-17 (N39b-31)
adr      = "ADR-PROG-17"
model    = "<pinned identifier>"    # the agent the project is developed with at the gate's date
revision = "<pinned revision>"
token_cap     = "<cap>"             # per campaign, the same for every arm
tool_call_cap = "<cap>"             # per campaign, the same for every arm
caps_status   = "provisional"       # provisional | final (N39b-31, step 2): provisional from the M1 decision
                                    # until the caps are re-derived from the measured baseline; a freeze needs final
single_agent = true
[thresholds]                        # from ADR-PROG-18 (N39b-33); ratified or as written
adr           = "ADR-PROG-18"
block_digest  = "<digest of the ratified block in canonical JSON>"
withdraw_below = "3"
confirm_at    = "10"
guard_floor   = "5"
tie_factor    = "3/2"
margin        = "1/20"
confirmatory_cells_max = 4
[envelope]                          # from ADR-PROG-16 (N39b-29)
adr          = "ADR-PROG-16"
partial_gate = "E0"                 # until approved; a campaign class per run once approved
tournament   = "E0"
pretraining  = "none"
approved_device_hours = 0           # per gate; the harness stops scheduling at the cap (N39b-29)
```

Two points on which this record follows the freeze manifest and the ratified block (DESIGN: a projection must
use the names and the number forms of what it projects). The agent's caps are the two the freeze manifest's `agent` block carries, `token_cap` and
`tool_call_cap` per campaign (FACT about the manifest: its fields are `model`, `revision`, `token_cap`,
`tool_call_cap`), with the status N39b-31 gives them (`caps_status`; the record is where the harness reads which
caps are in force and whether they are still provisional); a per-turn output limit, a context limit and a cap on parallel workers are recorded by N39b-31
where the provider exposes them, and no turn count or wall-clock cap is an owner input (the wall-clock limit of
a campaign is the deadline H of its stratum). The thresholds are written as exact rational strings (`"3/2"`,
`"1/20"`), never as binary floating-point literals, because the block they are generated from is serialized in
exact rationals and its digest must be reproducible; the block also carries the recovery thresholds 0.8 and 0.5,
the warm reuse count 10 and the level 0.05, which the harness reads from the block itself through
`block_digest`.

**What is put to the owner, and when.** At the M0 exit: an envelope notice (so that a refusal is known two
milestones before it would block). It uses the planning values of the cost arithmetic (288 device-hours per cell
in the worst case, about 70 expected per design cell), because the measured incumbent baseline does not exist
yet; the full request of N39b-29, derived from measured baseline times, replaces it before the partial gate's
suite hash. At the M1 exit: all four, as one page, each item a summary of its decision bead's options with the
default printed beside it.

1. *Advocates* (substance in N39b-28). External advocates with named affiliations, the default internal sessions,
   or a mix by stratum; the budget per stratum; the rule that an advocate has contributed nothing to this product;
   the advocate statement each arm publishes at the freeze. Under the default every result line carries
   `rival: internally advocated`.
2. *Envelope* (substance in N39b-29). The partial gate's campaigns on both arms (a design-campaign class per run;
   the pretraining class only where an arm builds a standing asset such as a family policy), the tournament's
   campaigns (24 mission families per cell by default, at least 32 for a cell that declares a population result,
   on both machines), a ceiling per run and a kill rule written first. If not approved: fewer strata at the
   tournament, each declared `not run` with the reason; at M3, the rule below.
3. *Agent* (substance in N39b-31). The model and revision to pin, and its caps, identical for all arms. The
   report states the result as conditional on that agent. More than one agent is a larger test; the default is
   one.
4. *Thresholds* (substance in N39b-33). The block of parameters, to ratify or amend **before the first suite hash
   exists**. After that they are part of the sealed protocol and cannot change for that suite.

**The envelope at its default, and the M3 exit (DESIGN; the same rule is stated in N39b-29).** The ten-fold
chapter gives the default as "nothing above the default class runs, the gate is recorded as `not run`, and M3
does not exit"; the work-package table gives the consequence of no approval as "the partial gate runs its CPU
strata". The two are reconciled as follows. With no approval, a campaign runs only if it fits the default class
(16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device); a campaign above it is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`). The
CPU strata (`envelope`, `sensing`) run whatever campaigns fit; the device strata are `not run`, with the reason.
If no stratum has a record, GATE-TENFOLD-06 is `not run` and M3 does not exit. If the CPU strata are recorded and
the device strata are `not run` with the reason, that is a recordable outcome and M3 may exit on it. Reason: this
keeps both statements of the plan true, adds no capability under a default, and never lets a milestone exit on a
gate with no record.

**Recording.** One decision has one record of authority (DESIGN, the same statement in N41-15 and in the four
decision beads N39b-28, N39b-29, N39b-31 and N39b-33): the ADR that the open-question register requires, which
the register row's `adr` field cites. The ADRs are `ADR-PROG-15` (OPEN-TENFOLD-3, advocates), `ADR-PROG-16`
(OPEN-TENFOLD-4, envelope), `ADR-PROG-17` (OPEN-TENFOLD-6, agent) and `ADR-PROG-18` (OPEN-TENFOLD-8, thresholds),
under `docs/adr/`. The file `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-<n>.md` is the ADR's supporting file: the request, the
options, the figures and the evidence. The ADR cites it by path and digest, and it states no decision that the
ADR lacks. Reason: the register's rule is that a decision or a default is an ADR, while the request and its
arithmetic are too long for one and change until the decision is taken.
The ADR of a decision is written by its decision bead; a default that is reached at its deadline is written by
the register's default writer (N41-03). In both cases the register rows move to `decided` or `defaulted`, the
configuration record is regenerated, and its digest enters the suite manifest at the freeze.

**Checks in the harness.**

Two checks are specified here, because each spans all four questions:

- A freeze refuses to start unless all four rows are `decided` or `defaulted`, the configuration digest
  matches its generation from the ADRs, and the agent's caps are `final`. This bead provides the check as
  `frob_conformance::tenfold::owner_inputs::require_frozen_inputs()` and the command `frob-tenfold
  require-owner-inputs --json`, a subcommand of the ten-fold operator binary of N39a-01 (there is no
  `frob-conformance` command): exit 0 with the configuration digest; exit 3 naming each open row, the
  mismatching table, or provisional caps with the refusal `tenfold.agent-caps-provisional` (the code and its
  repair, re-derive the caps from the baseline runs by the twice-p90 rule and record them `final`, are
  N39b-31's; the same refusal is raised at the freeze itself by the equal-terms check of N39a-19, and both read
  the caps through one module, `tenfold::agentpin`, so the two cannot disagree). The freeze-and-seal checker
  of N39a-40, which depends on this bead, calls `require_frozen_inputs()` before every sealing step (through
  the step-precondition registry of the sealing ledger, N39a-15) and through the freeze-check hook of the
  freeze manifest (N39a-18).
- The M3 exit: this bead supplies the stratum rule for OPEN-TENFOLD-4 and nothing else. The rule reads the
  partial gate's record: `not run` for every stratum is no record of the gate; CPU strata recorded with device
  strata `not run` (with the reason) is a record, and a recordable outcome. The block is the gate query's (a
  `not-run` gate blocks, N40-04), and the defaulted question behind it is named by check (f) of the owner
  calendar (N40-35) with the harness reason `open-default-blocks-exit`, which has named such a question at
  every exit since M0. This bead adds no second check, message or reason: in its own change it makes check (f)
  of N40-35 (a dependency, landed at M0, which cannot call a function of this M2 bead itself) call the rule,
  `owner_inputs::partial_gate_has_record(record)`, to decide whether GATE-TENFOLD-06 counts as recorded.

Four more are specified elsewhere and read the configuration record; they are not specified twice. The agent
record's check (N39b-31, a dependency) is exercised here by this bead's tests and script; the other three are
tested by the decision beads N39b-28, N39b-29 and N39b-33, which depend on this one for the record format, against
a configuration record it generates:

- a parameter of the ratified block changed after the first suite hash is refused with
  `tenfold.parameters-frozen` (N39b-33's block, enforced by the outcome engine N39b-05: the block's digest is in
  the event-log entry that commits the first suite hash; the suite manifest embeds the configuration digest as
  well);
- a campaign of the partial gate above the class in force, or beyond the approved total, is refused before it
  starts with `FROB-REF-761` (N39b-29);
- an advocate identity that appears among this product's implementers is rejected, and under internal advocacy
  the label is required on every result line (N39b-28);
- the agent pin and caps are equal across arms: the record carries one cap of each kind, so a record with a
  per-arm cap field is refused (N39b-31, `record_has_one_cap_per_kind`); the freeze-time equality of caps and
  revision across the arms' manifests is enforced by the equal-terms check of N39a-19.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The envelope notice was put to the owner at the M0 exit and the one-page request at the M1 exit; both are
      on record with their dates. (They are the owner calendar's rows of M1 and of M2, N40-35: each exit prints
      the next milestone's rows as "asked now".)
- [ ] All four register rows are `decided` or `defaulted` with ADRs `ADR-PROG-15` to `-18` before the first
      freeze; the configuration record equals its generation from the ADRs and names the ADR of each table.
- [ ] The configuration record carries `token_cap` and `tool_call_cap` for the agent and the thresholds as exact
      rational strings; its `[thresholds]` values equal the ratified block whose digest it names.
- [ ] `frob-tenfold require-owner-inputs`, the check every freeze calls, refuses with any row `open`, naming the
      row; with a configuration table that differs from its ADR, naming the table; and with `caps_status =
      "provisional"`, with `tenfold.agent-caps-provisional`. No script or document of this bead names a
      `frob-conformance` command.
- [ ] With OPEN-TENFOLD-4 at its default and no stratum recorded, the M3 exit is blocked and the owner calendar's
      check (f) names the question with `open-default-blocks-exit`; with the CPU strata recorded it is not
      blocked. No other reason or message for this case exists in the harness.
- [ ] Through this bead's script, the agent check of N39b-31 fires on the configuration record: an `[agent]`
      table with a per-arm cap is refused. The other three question-specific checks (`tenfold.parameters-frozen`
      after the first suite hash, N39b-05 and N39b-33; `FROB-REF-761` for a campaign above the class in force,
      N39b-29; the internal advocacy label and the implementer-as-advocate rejection, N39b-28) are tested by
      those decision beads against a configuration record generated by this bead; the freeze-time equality of
      caps across arm manifests is tested where it is enforced (N39a-19).
- [ ] With no ADR on file the generated record holds the four defaults (internal sessions with a floor of 40
      device-hours; class E0 with 0 approved device-hours; the developing agent pinned, its caps `provisional`;
      the block as written).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/tenfold_owner_inputs.rs`: `freeze_refuses_with_open_row`;
`config_digest_matches_adrs`; `config_tables_name_their_adrs`; `config_thresholds_equal_ratified_block` (the
strings `"3/2"` and `"1/20"` parse to the block's rationals, and a record holding `1.5` as a float is rejected);
`config_agent_caps_are_token_and_tool_call`; `provisional_caps_refuse_the_freeze` (`caps_status =
"provisional"` → exit 3 with `tenfold.agent-caps-provisional`; `final` → accepted; a value outside the two is a
load error); `m3_exit_blocked_when_all_not_run`; `m3_exit_allowed_with_cpu_strata` (both through
`partial_gate_has_record`, and through check (f) of N40-35 on a scratch M3 exit, where the reported reason is
`open-default-blocks-exit` and nothing else); `defaults_are_as_written` (3, 10, 5, 3/2, 1/20, 4; a floor of 40
device-hours). The tests of the four question-specific checks live with their decision beads
(`tenfold::advocates` in N39b-28, `tenfold::envelope` in N39b-29, `tenfold::agentpin` in N39b-31,
`tenfold::params` in N39b-33); this file re-runs the case of the one that is a dependency against a generated
configuration record, `per_arm_cap_refused`, and checks `require_owner_inputs_exit_codes` (3 with an open row or
a hand-edited table, naming it; 0 with the digest) and `record_defaults_without_adrs`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/owner_tenfold_inputs.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): on a scratch tournament directory runs
`frob-tenfold require-owner-inputs --json` with open rows (exit 3 naming them), writes the four
default ADRs, regenerates the configuration, runs the check again (exit 3: the default caps are `provisional`),
replaces the scratch ADR-PROG-17 by one whose caps are recorded `final`, regenerates and runs it again (exit 0
with the digest), hand-edits
`tie_factor` in the record (exit 3 naming the table), plants a per-arm `token_cap` in the `[agent]` table (refused
by N39b-31's one-cap-per-kind check), and runs a scratch M3 exit in the two states of the envelope (all strata
`not run`: blocked, the calendar's check (f) naming OPEN-TENFOLD-4 with `open-default-blocks-exit`; CPU strata
recorded: allowed). Logs to `target/e2e-logs/owner_tenfold_inputs/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript, every line
with `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`: `cmd` lines with the exact command line, exit code and wall time;
the configuration digest before and after each regeneration; `refusal` lines with the reason and exit code; one
`assert` per check with expected and observed values; `assert` lines of kind `negative-control` with `caught`
for the planted states; the exit check's verdict and the reason it reported; on failure the first differing
value and the scratch paths; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed, naming GATE-TENFOLD-04 and
GATE-TENFOLD-06 as read.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the six constants; 40 device-hours per stratum; 24 and 32 families. Negative controls: a freeze with
an open row; a freeze with provisional caps; a hand-edited configuration table; an M3 exit on an all-`not run`
partial gate; a per-arm cap. (A
post-hash threshold change, an unlabelled internally advocated result and unequal caps across arm manifests are
the negative controls of N39b-05 and N39b-33, N39b-28 and N39a-19.)

## Considerations

- Ratifying thresholds before any data exists is the whole point of asking early; an owner who wants different
  numbers can have them at no cost before the first suite hash and at the cost of a new suite after it.
- The agent pin makes the result conditional on one model at one revision. That is a limit of the claim, printed
  with it, not a defect to hide.
- External advocates need a budget and time to prepare; asking at the M1 exit leaves a milestone to engage them.
