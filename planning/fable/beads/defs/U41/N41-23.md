## Background

The product's thesis is a ratio against incumbent tools. A ratio has a denominator, and the plan's rule is that
**the incumbent is measured before a mechanism is funded against it**, on our own card, at pinned versions, in its
strongest warm configuration. The reason is arithmetic: if an incumbent already trains a flat-ground quadruped in
minutes, then no speedup of training alone can make a design loop ten times faster, and the ceiling of each lever
must be computed from the measured phases before any speed test is designed. One planning-time pilot showed how a
speed test can be incapable of confirming its own claim: a specialized solver measured 1.345 times a composed one
with a residual of work it did not touch, so that no solver could have shown more than about 1.46 times.

Step 6 of the first session takes the first measured entries of that denominator. No FrankenRobotics code is
compared with anything in this step; the point is to know the denominator before building against it. The
published context for the last figure (a FACT from the source register, to be re-read at its primary source) is
about 410 s to the return threshold of a flat-ground quadruped velocity task on one RTX 4090, of which about 45 s
is compilation, so the run fits the default envelope of one device and one hour.

Session rules apply (default envelope; a failing or surprising result is recorded as it is; nothing is repeated
until it looks better).

## Scope

In: the session's first reading of the denominator: the first repetitions of four rows of the parity baseline,
for one pinned incumbent stack on one card, through the out-of-process harness; the disclosure block; the
session record; the opening of the incumbent phase ledger with its first row.

Out: the harness and its pinned environment (BD-039); the oracle register and the capture scripts under
`tools/oracle` (N38a-04); the disclosure block schema `frob.perf.disclosure/1` with its completeness check and
refusal FROB-REF-862 (N36-13); the logging helper (STD-E2E); the session-record validator and its test file
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/session_record.rs` (N41-18), to which this step adds its cases; the reason
`session-record-invalid` (registry N41-REF, which names this step as a raiser). The parity baseline itself is BD-040,
which depends on this step and takes over the ledger it opens: the definitions of the rows, their full repeat
counts and statistics (the warm starts, 20 edits per edit row, five runs to threshold), the physics, full-step
and training-rate rows on both workloads, the two-card row, the reproduction judgement of each published figure
with its explanation file, the target register and the derived limits. This step defines no row of its own: its
four measurements are rows of BD-040's table, timed at the boundaries that table states. Also out: the Mac-side
baselines (the M1 completion bead, BD-142); any comparison with our own code.

## Technical approach

The incumbent runs **out of process**, driven by a script under `tools/oracle`, from a recorded environment
(container digest or lockfile hash); no incumbent code is linked into any product binary, and its outputs are
typed as Parity context, never as Reality.

**Rows and counts (DESIGN, the same statement in BD-040).** The four measurements are four rows of the parity
baseline: cold start; edit to first step for a parameter edit; edit to first step for a topology edit; time to
threshold on one card. BD-040's table defines what each times and how many repetitions the gate's row has. The
session takes the first repetitions only: all 5 cold starts of the cold-start row (and none of its 10 warm
starts), 5 of the 20 parameter edits, 5 of the 20 topology edits and 1 of the 5 runs to threshold. The counts are
reduced so that the step fits one session inside the default envelope (five runs to threshold would be about 35
minutes of training at the published figure; one is about 7), and each count is written into the disclosure
block, as the plan requires of the repetition count. BD-040 then completes the rows to their full counts under
the same harness identity and keeps the session's values as their first repetitions; if the harness identity
has changed in between, the session's values stay in the ledger as a row of their own and the campaign measures
the full count afresh. Nothing the session measured is discarded or replaced.

Procedure on `trj`, one RTX 4090 (artifacts under `docs/delivery/sessions/first/step-6/`):

1. *Pin and disclose.* Record the incumbent stack's name, revision and environment digest from its row of
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml` (BD-039; the oracle register's row for incumbent
   simulators points there); the driver and card; the host load (load average and other GPU tenants) at the start and end of each
   measurement; whether caches were warm; the repetition count.
2. *Cold start to the first step.* From a fresh process with cold kernel and asset caches: wall-clock from launch
   to the completion of the first simulation step of the flat-ground quadruped task at the stack's own
   recommended world count. Repeat five times (a DESIGN count, written into the disclosure block; caches cleared
   each time); record every value.
3. *A parameter edit to the first step.* With the process warm: change one link length of the robot model by its
   own tools' intended route, and measure wall-clock from acceptance of the edit to completion of the first
   device step of the edited model. Repeat 5 times (the first 5 of the row's 20).
4. *A topology edit to the first step.* The same for an edit that adds one link and joint. Repeat 5 times (the
   first 5 of the row's 20).
5. *Wall-clock to the return threshold.* Train its flat-ground quadruped velocity task with the stack's own
   reference configuration to the return threshold that the stack's documentation or the published comparison
   uses; record wall-clock, split into compilation and training where the stack reports it, the threshold, the
   world count and the step count. One run (the first of the row's 5); a second only if the first was
   interrupted (recorded).
6. *Enter the ledger.* Each figure goes into the incumbent phase ledger as the first repetitions of its row with
   its disclosure block, beside the published figure where one exists, with the relative difference; a
   difference above 20% from a published figure is noted in the record with its likely cause (configuration,
   hardware, version) and is never reconciled by tuning. The reproduction judgement of the figure (inside or
   outside [0.8, 1.2] on the row's full count, with the explanation file under `docs/perf/explanations/`) is
   made by BD-040, not by this step.

**Disclosure block** (attached to each figure): incumbent revision and environment digest; driver; card; host
load; cache state (cold or warm); repetition count and every repetition's value; world count and step size; the
command line; the date. The six items the session rule names (revision, driver, card, host load, cache warmth,
repetition count) are the minimum; the block is written as a `frob.perf.disclosure/1` record with `subject:
"incumbent"` and its `comparator` group (`name`, `revision`, `container_digest`, `configuration`) copied from that
comparators row (`source_revision` as `revision`) and the configuration hash the launcher verified, as N36-13
requires, and it must pass N36-13's completeness
check: a field the incumbent does not expose is a named gap or an allowed `na` (for example `energy` as a named
gap under OPEN-PERF-3), never a zero and never omitted. A block that fails the check is refused with FROB-REF-862
(exit 3) and the figure is not entered.

**The record** (`step-6/record.json`, schema `frob.sessionstep/1`): `observation` = { `cold_start_s`: [5 values],
`param_edit_to_first_step_s`: [5], `topology_edit_to_first_step_s`: [5], `time_to_threshold_s`, `compile_s`,
`threshold`, `worlds`, `published_context_s`: 410, `relative_difference` }, each with its disclosure block;
`decides` = ["the first row of GATE-SPARK-21", "the denominator of BET-13", "the ceilings for the quadruped
class", "the planning value of GATE-CORTEX-05"]; `verdict` (`recorded`; there is no pass or fail for a
measurement of somebody else's software).

What the observation decides: the denominator of the ten-fold bet; the ceilings stated before anything is built
for the quadruped class (the class was given an expected range of 3 to 10, and the measured training time is what
that range rests on); the planning value for the training-time parity gate. It is also the answer to the first
"every gate passed and the claim still failed" reason: nothing confirmable exists without an incumbent
measurement on record at the M0 exit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four measurements are recorded with every repetition and a complete disclosure block each.
- [ ] The incumbent ran out of process from the pinned environment; a changed revision would change the harness
      identity (asserted by re-reading the identity after the run).
- [ ] The time to threshold is entered beside the published figure with the relative difference; a difference
      above 20% has its likely cause written in the record (the reproduction judgement and its explanation file
      are BD-040's).
- [ ] The four measurements are entered as the first repetitions of BD-040's rows with their counts (5 cold, 5 of
      20, 5 of 20, 1 of 5) in the disclosure blocks; the step defines no row and no timing boundary of its own.
- [ ] The run stayed inside the default envelope (one device, one hour per job, 16 GiB, 8 cores), or the step
      records exactly which measurement could not and was not run.
- [ ] No FrankenRobotics figure appears in the record.
- [ ] The first row of GATE-SPARK-21 is recorded with measured values, the commit, the hardware fingerprint if it
      exists yet, and the seed where the incumbent exposes one.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The ledger writer's tests (owned by the harness bead) are re-run. This step adds validation cases in
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/session_record.rs`: `disclosure_block_required_per_figure` (a step-6 record whose
`time_to_threshold_s` has no block, or a block missing `host_load`, is rejected with FROB-REF-862);
`repetitions_all_kept` (a record with a mean but no per-repetition values, or with 4 cold-start values where the
block says 5, is rejected); `no_own_code_figures_in_step_6` (a record holding any block with `subject: "ours"` is
rejected with `session-record-invalid`); `relative_difference_computed` (a time to threshold of 493 s beside the
published 410 s gives a relative difference of +0.2024 and requires a written likely cause; 492 s gives +0.2000,
which is not above 20% (the band of BD-040 is the closed [0.8, 1.2]), and 451 s gives +0.1000, and neither does); `verdict_is_recorded_only` (a step-6 record with verdict `pass` or `fail` is rejected; `recorded` is
the only verdict).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/session/first/step-6.sh` (run on `trj`) sources the shared logging helper of STD-E2E and writes
`target/e2e-logs/step-6/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N41-23`), `step` and `event`. It logs `start`; one `cmd` line per command, with the incumbent's exact command
lines, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines for each measured value with unit (s), repetition index, host load
at start and end and cache state, and for the published context beside it with the relative difference and the
20% note threshold; `assert` lines for "environment digest equals the pinned comparators row" (both digests), "harness
identity unchanged after the run", "disclosure complete" and "no own-code figure"; on failure the first missing
field or differing digest and the paths of the record and the raw harness output; `end`; and a `summary` with
assertions passed and failed, naming GATE-SPARK-21 as touched (first row). Evidence fields: claim class Parity
context, tier as reported by the incumbent, seed where the incumbent exposes one. Logs carry no private-scene
data.

### Known answers and negative controls
There is no known answer for a measurement. The published context (about 410 s, about 45 s of it compilation) is a
FACT to compare with, not a target (K41-30). Negative controls: a record with a figure lacking its disclosure block
is rejected (FROB-REF-862); a harness run with a different incumbent revision has a different identity and its
figures cannot be entered in the same ledger row; X41-37, a figure for this product's speed planted beside the
incumbent's phases in a scratch copy of the record, is rejected with `session-record-invalid`.

## Considerations

- The loaded host makes single measurements noisy; repetitions are kept individually so that later analysis can
  use medians or paired lower bounds. No figure is dropped as an outlier in this step.
- Licences: whether the incumbent's licence permits publishing a comparison is not settled here. Fail-closed
  default: the figures stay in the internal admission record (the M0 rubric row: "none, an admission record,
  internal") and nothing is published until the licence has been read and recorded by the bead that publishes
  the baseline (BD-040).
- Compute: the step runs inside E0 (one device, one hour per job). If one run to threshold does not finish within
  the hour (more than 3,600 / 410 ≈ 8.8 times the published figure), the run is stopped, recorded as `not run` with the
  elapsed time, and no larger class is used without the owner's approval under OPEN-SPARK-2 (FROB-REF-761, exit 4,
  for a job above the envelope in force).
- The edit-latency measurements depend on what counts as "the stack's intended route" for an edit; the route used
  is written into the disclosure block so that a rival advocate can later challenge it.
- If the incumbent is faster than its published figure, that is the more important result, and the ceilings are
  recomputed from it.
