## Background

The first implementation session has eight steps. Steps 1 to 7 each produce an observation about the foundations:
whether an out-of-tree workspace builds; whether FrankenSim consumed by path computes what it computes for itself;
how many asupersync packages and working compilers there are, and whether fastmcp_rust passes its own checks on
the chosen toolchain; whether one algebraic kernel agrees bit for bit on Metal and on CUDA; what one incumbent's
loop costs on our card; and whether two exact known answers reproduce with a negative control caught. Step 8 is
what makes the session a record instead of an afternoon: **every result, passing or failing, is written into
`DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` with commands and outputs**: the admission records of steps 1 to 5, the incumbent
measurement of step 6 with its disclosure block, and the fixture counts of step 7 with the hash of the program
that produced them.

The rule that governs the step is the session's own: a failing result is recorded as failing, and no step is
repeated until it happens to pass. A session whose record shows only successes after several silent retries has
measured nothing.

## Scope

In: assembling the session record from the seven step records; rendering it into the admission document; the table
of what each observation decides, filled with the session's outcomes; the session-level check. Out: the steps
themselves; the admission record format and its generator; the M0 exit review; decisions that the observations
feed (the choice of T, the kill gates), which have their own beads.

## Technical approach

Inputs: `docs/delivery/sessions/first/step-N/record.json` for N = 1 … 7 (schema `frob.sessionstep/1`), each with
its saved command outputs. Tool (DESIGN): `scripts/session/first/step-8.sh`, which calls the admission record
writer; nothing is typed into the document by hand.

1. *Validate* each step record: commands with exit codes and output digests present; observation fields complete
   for the step; a verdict of `pass`, `fail` or `recorded`; the output files' digests match. A missing step record
   is written into the session record as `not run` with the reason; it is never omitted.
2. *Check for silent retries.* Each step directory keeps every attempt (`attempt-1`, `attempt-2`, …). The session
   record lists the number of attempts per step and the verdict of each; a step whose final verdict is `pass`
   after a failed attempt must carry a note stating what changed between attempts (a code or configuration change
   with its commit), otherwise step 8 fails.
3. *Render* into `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`, one section per observation, each with the exact commands, the
   essential output, the commit, the toolchain, the machine, and the verdict:
   - steps 1 to 5 as admission records (the workspace build with the first receipt; INT-FSIM-01 with digests,
     cold and warm check times, peak memory and package count; the asupersync count, the `fs-qty` cells and the
     fastmcp_rust result on T; the Metal run; the CUDA run with the three-way table);
   - step 6 as the incumbent measurement with its full disclosure block (revision, driver, card, host load, cache
     state, repetition count and every repetition);
   - step 7 as the fixture counts (561, 344, 217, 9; nine cells changed by the control; the witness results; 1/65 m;
     the eight analytic rows) with the hash of the program that produced them.
4. *Fill the decision table* from the records:

   | Step | Observation | Decides or informs |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Whether an out-of-tree workspace builds with the three repeated settings | The hypothesis that patches and build environment behave as profiles do; the first receipt |
   | 2 | Digest equality with FrankenSim; build times and memory | INT-FSIM-01; BET-04; whether KILL-FSIM's worker process is needed at once |
   | 3 | The number of asupersync packages; which nightlies build `fs-qty`; whether fastmcp_rust builds and passes its own checks on T | INT-ASUP-01 and FD-ASUP-01; the choice of T and the decision rule for the units escape hatch; FD-FMCP-01, and with it whether INT-FMCP-01 can pass in the core graph |
   | 4, 5 | Bit agreement of one algebraic kernel on three backends; the square-root probe | GATE-SPARK-00; the first half of GATE-SPARK-01; each family's best attainable determinism class (KILL-D1) |
   | 6 | The incumbent's cold start, edit latency and training time on our card | The denominator of BET-13; the ceilings for the quadruped class; the planning value of GATE-CORTEX-05 |
   | 7 | An exact family answer reproduced with a caught negative control; the analytic rows of the first fixture | The fixtures of GATE-AUGUR-03 and GATE-ARENA-04; the rule that a strict decision is computed exactly; the shape of the auditor |

   Each row gains a column "outcome in this session" (the verdict and the one-line result) and a column "next
   bead" (the bead that takes the decision on this evidence).
5. *Session summary,* generated: the four measurements (does FrankenSim consume cleanly; does fastmcp_rust build
   and pass its own checks on T; do the three backends agree on one algebraic kernel; what does the rival's loop
   cost on our card), the two sets of exact known answers with the control caught, and the two-second humanoid
   rollout on the referee, each as `yes`, `no` with the reason, or the measured value. The summary states plainly
   that the session did not create crates beyond what the steps needed.
6. *If step 2 failed,* the summary's first line says so: the failure is the session's most important result, and
   steps 3 to 8 are present because none of them depends on it.
7. *Check the siblings.* Write a fresh resolution receipt (N33a-04) and compare each sibling's head and tree hash
   with step 1's receipt. A sibling changed by this session (a commit whose author is the session's identity, or a
   working-tree edit) other than the FD-FMCP-01 change of step 3 in fastmcp_rust's repository, made under that
   repository's own rules with its files reserved, fails the step. A sibling head moved by other agents is listed
   as drift with the commits' authors and is not a failure; the receipt records it either way.

**Refusals.** Step 8 fails with exit 3 and the first line `reason=<id>` (harness reasons of N41-REF):
`session-record-invalid` (a step record without commands, output digests, observation fields or a verdict; a
saved output that does not match its digest; a summary that claims a pass for a step whose verdict is `fail`);
`session-silent-retry` (a pass after a failed attempt with no recorded change); `session-sibling-changed` (item 7).
A missing step record is not a refusal: it is rendered as `not run` with its reason.

**Record this step leaves:** `docs/delivery/sessions/first/session.json` (schema `frob.session/1`: step records by
reference with digests, attempts, verdicts, the decision table) and the regenerated `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`,
whose session sections are marked as generated and are compared with the JSON by the generated-file check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` contains a section for each of the seven observations with commands, outputs,
      commit, toolchain, machine and verdict, generated from the step records.
- [ ] A failed step appears as failed with its output; a step that was not run appears as `not run` with the
      reason; nothing is omitted.
- [ ] Every step's attempts are listed; a pass after a failed attempt without a recorded change makes step 8 fail.
- [ ] The decision table is filled, with the next bead named for each row.
- [ ] The session summary's seven items are present with their values.
- [ ] Regenerating the document from the records gives identical bytes; a hand edit of a generated section is
      caught.
- [ ] No sibling repository was changed by the session except fastmcp_rust's repository by the FD-FMCP-01 change
      of step 3, made under that repository's own rules (no local patch, no fork); the step-8 receipt shows it, and
      a planted edit in a scratch sibling fails with `session-sibling-changed`.
- [ ] Each failure prints its registered reason on its first line and exits 3.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/first_session.rs` on scratch step records (labelled `synthetic`):
`all_pass_renders_seven_sections`; `failed_step_is_rendered_as_failed`; `missing_step_is_not_run_with_reason`;
`silent_retry_detected` (two attempts, fail then pass, no change note); `retry_with_change_note_accepted`;
`decision_table_has_six_rows_seven_steps`; `summary_lists_four_measurements_two_known_answers_one_rollout`;
`generated_sections_match_json`; `hand_edit_detected`; `output_digest_mismatch_rejected` (a saved output edited
by one byte gives `session-record-invalid`); `summary_pass_over_failed_step_rejected`;
`sibling_change_by_session_detected` (a scratch sibling with a commit authored by the session identity gives
`session-sibling-changed`; the same commit by another author is listed as drift and passes);
`step2_failure_leads_summary` (a failed step 2 is the summary's first line); `crates_without_observation_rejected`
(a step record listing created crates and no observation is `not run`, never `pass`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/first_session_record.sh` sources the shared logging helper of STD-E2E and writes
`target/e2e-logs/first_session_record/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N41-25`), `step` and `event`. It runs `scripts/session/first/step-8.sh` on the real step
records, validates the document, then re-runs it on five scratch variants (a failed step 2; a missing step 5; a
silent retry in step 4; a saved output altered by one byte; an edit in a scratch sibling) and asserts the expected
renderings and refusals. It logs `start`; `cmd` lines with the exact command line, exit code and wall time; one
`assert` line per section with the step's verdict and the digest of its record; `metric` lines for attempts per
step; `refusal` lines with the reason identifier for each scratch variant that must fail; on failure the first
step whose record is invalid, the first differing field and the paths of the record and the document; `end`; and a
`summary` with assertions passed and failed, listing the admission gates and known-answer gates the session
touched (INT-FSIM-01, INT-ASUP-01, INT-TOOLCHAIN-01, INT-FMCP-01, INT-FT-01, INT-CUDA-01, GATE-SPARK-00,
GATE-SPARK-01, GATE-SPARK-21, GATE-GAUNT-08), each marked partial. A second run on the same records produces the
same document bytes.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers (K41-27): the document's structure (seven sections, six table rows); the step 7 counts repeated in
the record (K41-31). Negative controls: X41-32, the silent retry; X41-33, a record with crates created and an
observation missing; X41-36, an edit in a sibling outside the upstream work item; the hand edit of a generated
section; a step record whose saved output does not match its digest; a session summary that claims a pass for a
step whose verdict is `fail`.

## Considerations

- The session record is evidence for the M0 exit review, which is done by a fresh-context session that did not
  run these steps; the record must therefore be readable cold, with every command reproducible from the document.
- Outputs can be long; the document holds the essential lines and the digests, and the full outputs stay in the
  session directory under the same commit.
- Nothing in this record is a gate PASS. Gates are recorded by their own beads from their own runs; the session
  shows what is true on day one.
