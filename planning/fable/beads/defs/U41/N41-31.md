## Background

Gate GATE-WS-07 requires that each of four late-found risks has a planted instance on which its earliest signal
fires in 100 of 100 runs: two build rows at M0 (N41-02), a sampler row at M2 (N41-38) and a suite row at M3. This
bead measures the suite row and, with it, closes the gate from all four rows. The suite row is a case where a
plausible check passes while the thing it should protect is wrong.

The sampler row (RISK-58: a sampler with correct marginals and a wrong joint law, detected by a pairwise
sign-agreement frequency outside ½ ± 0.005) was drafted together with this bead and now has its own bead, N41-38,
because it is due at the M2 exit and this bead cannot be finished before M3. Everything about that row (its 100
seeds, the curve comparison under channel reordering, its positive control and the gate's M2 part) is there.

The gate register holds GATE-WS-07 as one record with three parts, one per milestone at which a part is due
(N40-10; one part per milestone, N40-01): M0 with scope "the two build rows", M2 "the sampler row", M3 "the suite
row". The plan's four rows are therefore three parts, and the M0 part's `measured` carries both build counts.

**The suite row (RISK-59).** A rare slow mission can be absent from a sealed sample, and a resampling interval then
certifies a ratio the population does not have. The hand-checkable case: deadline H = 1,000 s; the rival always
takes 20 s; the candidate takes 1 s on 99% of mission families and 999 s on 1%. Both always complete. The
population ratio of restricted mean times is 20/10.98 = 1.8215. A suite of 24 families contains only fast missions
with probability 0.99²⁴ = 0.78568, and then every percentile-bootstrap resample reports 20: resampling cannot show
a tail the sample does not contain. Signals: the pre-analysis reports a family count below the count its
registered bound needs; every sealed mission of an arm finishes near its fastest time. Mitigation: the bounded
paired contrast; two result types (a population result needs at least ln(1/α)/ln(1.1) families: 32 at α = 0.05,
46 at α = 0.0125, 49 at α = 0.01; a finite-suite result states the ratio on the sealed missions as a fact about
that suite and carries no population wording).

## Scope

In: the 100-run harness of the suite row, its positive controls, the tier entry and receipt of the gate's M3 part,
and the check that GATE-WS-07 is closed from its three parts. Out: the recording of the part (the recorder of
N38b-19, run by a session that did not build this harness); the sampler row with its harness and the M2 part
(N41-38); the
ten-fold mode of the pre-analysis with the necessary family count and the population refusal (N39b-34), its
per-cell reachability conditions (N39a-33) and the program-gate record of its known answers (N40-19); the
detectors themselves (the count refusal of the pre-analysis, N39b-34; the completion-spread detector and its
label `no-tail-observed`, in the scorer's contrast module, N39b-35, item 8); the build rows (N41-02).

## Technical approach

Harness (DESIGN): `crates/frob-conformance/src/controls/ws07_late.rs` (module `suite_row`; the module `sampler_row`
of the same file belongs to N41-38); script `scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh --row suite`.

**Suite row (recorded at the M3 exit, before the partial gate).**

1. For seeds 1 to 100, generate a ten-fold study plan with result type `population`, a family count n drawn from
   8 to 31, a level α from {0.05, 0.0125, 0.01} and a random cluster structure, and submit it to
   `frob eval preanalyze --json`.
2. Signal: the pre-analysis reports the family count as below the necessary count and refuses the population
   result with `suite-too-small-for-population-claim` (exit 3; `observed` n, `required` both n_min and n_bound;
   the refusal of N39b-34, matched by identifier). Expected: 100 of 100.
3. For the same seeds, simulate the rare-tail candidate at n = 24 and keep the samples that contain no slow
   family (about 79 of 100), and score each through the scorer's contrast module (`frob-tenfold-score`, N39b-35)
   with the rare-tail rival. On each: the completion-spread detector reports that every candidate mission finished
   within a factor of 1.5 of the fastest (DESIGN threshold for "near its fastest time") while n is below the
   necessary count, and labels the cell `no-tail-observed`; the contrast test does not certify ten-fold; the
   percentile bootstrap, run as the negative control, reports 20 and would certify.
4. Positive control: a plan with result type `population` at α = 0.05 under the registered bound
   `bounded-paired-contrast-hoeffding/1` is admitted at n = 182, where it meets both the necessary count 32 and
   the 182 its registered bound requires (other reachability conditions aside); at n = 32 the pre-analysis
   reports the necessary count as reached and still refuses for the bound's 182, and that plan is counted as a
   refusal, not as a false alarm; a plan with n = 24 and result type `finite-suite` is accepted. (The verb
   admits population wording only at n ≥ max(n_min, n_bound), N39b-34; an earlier draft of this step had the
   n = 32 plan accepted.)

**Gate record.** The script writes no row of `registries/gates.toml` (one recorder per gate, N38b-19). The suite
row has an entry in the conformance crate's tier target named by GATE-WS-07's record, built on the module
`suite_row`: it runs steps 1 to 4 in process and, under `--features gauntlet-mutations`, the row's registered
control, seeded from N41-CX's row X41-08 (the percentile bootstrap on a tail-free sample, which reports 20 and
would certify where the contrast test must not). The script runs the entry with the helper `e2e_tier <tier>
GATE-WS-07 --mutations`, which is `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features
gauntlet-mutations --test <tier> -- GATE-WS-07`, and logs the receipt's hash. The recording session, which did not
build the harness, enters the M3 part (scope "the suite row") from that receipt with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-WS-07 --receipt <hash> --part M3`. `measured` carries the
fired count of 100, the positive-control counts, the count of tail-free samples and of bootstrap certifications
among them; the receipt carries commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds. A fired count below 100 is a `fail` line
in the receipt and is recorded as `fail` with the first failing seed.

**Closing the gate.** A gate's status is derived from its parts and is never stored (N40-01): with any due part
unrecorded the gate prints "not run". This bead's closing check reads the register after the M3 part is recorded
and requires all three parts to be `pass`: the M0 part of N41-02 with both build counts at 100 of 100 in its
`measured`, the M2 part of N41-38, and the M3 part. A `fail` part, or a part still `not-run`, leaves the gate
open and is named. The register-wide clause of the gate ("no row of the register lacks a planted instance") is
checked by N41-02 at M0 and held in the M0 part's receipt; the firing of every row's plant at each exit is the
exit-time check of N41-32 and, from M5, the sweep of N41-37. Neither is repeated here.

Determinism: the plans are generated from addressed draws (D0); the pre-analysis is exact arithmetic. Evidence
class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pre-analysis refuses a population result on 100 of 100 undersized plans, naming the necessary count (32,
      46 or 49 for the three levels).
- [ ] On the tail-free rare-tail samples the cell is labelled `no-tail-observed`, the contrast test certifies
      ten-fold in none, and the bootstrap control certifies in every one.
- [ ] The positive controls pass (n = 182 population at α = 0.05 admitted; n = 32 population reported as reaching
      the necessary count and refused for the registered bound's 182; n = 24 finite-suite accepted).
- [ ] The tier run of the suite row writes a receipt whose GATE-WS-07 line is `pass` with the control of X41-08
      `caught`, and the M3 part is recorded from it with `record_gate --part M3`; the script itself records
      nothing.
- [ ] The closing check passes only if the gate's three parts are all `pass` (the M0 part of N41-02 with both
      build counts at 100 of 100, the M2 part of N41-38, the M3 part); on a scratch register with one part removed
      or `fail` it names the part, and the gate prints "not run" or `fail`, never pass.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ws07_late.rs` (suite half); the real pre-analysis, no mocks.
- `necessary_counts_known`: 32, 46, 49 from ln(1/α)/ln(1.1) rounded up; 182 for the distribution-free bound at
  α = 0.05.
- `undersized_population_plan_refused` (n = 24, α = 0.05); `finite_suite_plan_accepted`;
  `population_plan_admitted_at_182`; `count_32_reached_but_bound_unmet`.
- `rare_tail_numbers`: population ratio 1.8215; probability of a tail-free suite 0.78568.
- `tail_free_sample_bootstrap_reports_20`; `contrast_does_not_certify`.
- `no_tail_observed_label_set`.
- `gate_closed_needs_three_parts`: on a scratch register, with the M0, M2 or M3 part `not-run` the derived status
  prints "not run" and the closing check names the part; with one part `fail`, or the M0 part's `measured` showing
  99 of 100 for one build row, the check fails; with all three `pass` it passes.
- `gate_closed`: the same check on the register of record; it names the open part until the M3 part is recorded
  and passes afterwards.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh --row suite` sources the shared logging helper of STD-E2E and writes
`target/e2e-logs/ws07_late_plants/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N41-31`), `step` and `event`. It runs the 100 seeds through the real binary (`frob eval preanalyze --plan
<plan> --json`), logging `start`; a `cmd` line per invocation with the exact command line, exit code, wall time and
the envelope's evidence block (claim kind known-answer, class, determinism D0, seed); one `assert` line per seed
(seed, n, α, signal value, necessary count, verdict); `refusal` lines with the identifier
`suite-too-small-for-population-claim`, `observed` and `required`; for step 3 a `cmd` line per scorer invocation
and one `assert` line per tail-free sample (seed, label `no-tail-observed` present, contrast LB against 0, the
bootstrap control's figure); `metric` lines for the fired count against
100, the false-alarm count against 0, the tail-free count (expected about 79) and the bootstrap certifications
among them; the digest of each generated plan; on failure the first seed that did not fire, its plan path and the
envelope; the `cmd` line of the tier run with its `receipt` field; `end`; and a `summary` with assertions passed
and failed naming GATE-WS-07 and the receipt of its M3 part. The closing check is run by the recording session
after the part is recorded (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test ws07_late -- gate_closed`), not by the script,
which records nothing.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers (K41-08, K41-10): 1.8215; 0.78568; 32, 46, 49, 182. Negative controls: X41-08, the percentile
bootstrap on a tail-free sample; an undersized population plan. A pre-analysis with the count rule disabled is the mutant that must fail
this bead's assertions.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The spread threshold of 1.5 is a detector for the second signal, not a statistical claim; the binding protection
  is the count rule.
- If the partial gate moves, the suite row still has to be recorded before any sealed family is opened.
- The rare-tail simulation and the refusal of an undersized population claim are also exercised by the
  measurement of the program gate of the pre-analysis (the script of N39b-36, whose receipt N40-19 records as
  GATE-TENFOLD-10); this bead does not own those checks, it fires the plant 100 times and records the row of
  GATE-WS-07.
