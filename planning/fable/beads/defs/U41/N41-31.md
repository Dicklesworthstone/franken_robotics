## Background

Gate GATE-WS-07 requires that each of four late-found risks has a planted instance on which its earliest signal
fires in 100 of 100 runs: two build rows at M0 (N41-02), a sampler row at M2 and a suite row at M3. This bead
records the last two. Both are cases where a plausible check passes while the thing it should protect is wrong.

**The sampler row (RISK-58).** A noise sampler can have correct marginals and a wrong joint law. The sibling's
Gaussian action sampler is reported to have exactly this defect (FACT to recheck; its pairwise sign-agreement
frequency is 0.6655 where independent channels give ½). Consequences: the likelihood ratio a policy-gradient method
computes is for a variable that was not sampled, and exploration is correlated across channels. A test of each
channel's mean and variance passes. Signals: a pairwise sign-agreement frequency outside ½ ± 0.005 in the sampler
battery; a learning curve that changes when channels are reordered. Mitigation: the joint-law rule for sampled
actions and its gate; counter-based draws keyed per channel; no sibling sampler is linked.

**The suite row (RISK-59).** A rare slow mission can be absent from a sealed sample, and a resampling interval then
certifies a ratio the population does not have. The hand-checkable case: deadline H = 1,000 s; the rival always
takes 20 s; the candidate takes 1 s on 99% of mission families and 999 s on 1%. Both always complete. The
population ratio of restricted mean times is 20/10.98 = 1.8215. A suite of 24 families contains only fast missions
with probability 0.99²⁴ = 0.78568, and then every percentile-bootstrap resample reports 20: resampling cannot show
a tail the sample does not contain. Signals: the pre-analysis reports a family count below the count its
registered bound needs; every sealed mission of an arm finishes near its fastest time. Mitigation: the bounded
paired contrast; two result types (a population result needs at least ln(1/α)/ln(1.1) families: 32 at α = 0.05,
46 at α = 0.0125, 49 at α = 0.01; a finite-suite result states the ratio on the sealed missions as a fact about
that suite and carries no population wording).

## Scope

In: the two 100-run harnesses, their positive controls, the two gate rows, and the closing of GATE-WS-07. Out: the
planted sampler and the sampler battery (N33c-37, N23b-30); the pre-analysis and its known answers (N40-19); the
detectors themselves; the build rows (N41-02).

## Technical approach

Harness (DESIGN): `crates/frob-conformance/src/controls/ws07_late.rs`; script `scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh`.

**Sampler row (recorded at the M2 exit).**

1. For seeds 1 to 100, run the sampler battery on the planted linear-phase sampler with n = 10⁶ addressed draw
   pairs per channel pair (standard error of a sign-agreement frequency about 0.0005, a tenth of the band).
2. Signal: at least one channel pair has sign agreement outside ½ ± 0.005. Expected: fires on 100 of 100 seeds,
   with the observed frequency near 0.6655 recorded per seed.
3. Second signal, on 10 of the seeds (it costs a short training run each, inside the default envelope on
   `batch-cpu`): a reference task trained with the planted sampler under two channel orders gives learning curves
   that differ beyond the seed-to-seed band; with the product sampler the curves under the two orders are
   bit-identical (draws are keyed per channel, so order cannot matter).
4. Positive control: the product sampler stays inside the band on 100 of 100 seeds.
5. Linking check: no sibling sampler symbol is present in any `frob` binary (the dependency check's deny list).

**Suite row (recorded at the M3 exit, before the partial gate).**

1. For seeds 1 to 100, generate a ten-fold study plan with result type `population`, a family count n drawn from
   8 to 31, a level α from {0.05, 0.0125, 0.01} and a random cluster structure, and submit it to
   `frob eval preanalyze --json`.
2. Signal: the pre-analysis reports the family count as below the necessary count and refuses the population
   result. Expected: 100 of 100.
3. For the same seeds, simulate the rare-tail candidate at n = 24 and keep the samples that contain no slow
   family (about 79 of 100). On each: the completion-spread detector reports that every candidate mission finished
   within a factor of 1.5 of the fastest (DESIGN threshold for "near its fastest time") while n is below the
   necessary count, and labels the cell `no-tail-observed`; the contrast test does not certify ten-fold; the
   percentile bootstrap, run as the negative control, reports 20 and would certify.
4. Positive control: a plan with n = 32 at α = 0.05 and result type `population` is accepted by the pre-analysis
   as to its count (other reachability conditions aside); a plan with n = 24 and result type `finite-suite` is
   accepted.

**Gate record.** GATE-WS-07 rows `m2-sampler` and `m3-suite`: fired counts of 100, positive-control counts, the
observed sign-agreement frequencies (minimum, median, maximum), the count of tail-free samples and of bootstrap
certifications among them, commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds. With these and the two M0 rows the gate's PASS
is recorded; if either count is below 100 the gate is recorded failing with the first failing seed.

Determinism: draws are addressed (D0); the battery statistic is exact given the draws; the pre-analysis is exact
arithmetic. Evidence class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted sampler's sign-agreement signal fires on 100 of 100 seeds; the product sampler stays inside
      ½ ± 0.005 on 100 of 100.
- [ ] On 10 seeds the planted sampler's learning curves differ under channel reordering and the product sampler's
      are bit-identical.
- [ ] The pre-analysis refuses a population result on 100 of 100 undersized plans, naming the necessary count (32,
      46 or 49 for the three levels).
- [ ] On the tail-free rare-tail samples the cell is labelled `no-tail-observed`, the contrast test certifies
      ten-fold in none, and the bootstrap control certifies in every one.
- [ ] The positive controls pass (n = 32 population at α = 0.05; n = 24 finite-suite).
- [ ] GATE-WS-07 rows `m2-sampler` and `m3-suite` are recorded with measured values, and the gate's status is
      computed from all four rows.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ws07_late.rs`; real sampler battery and real pre-analysis, no mocks.
- `planted_sampler_fires_each_seed` (seeds 1 to 100, reduced to n = 10⁵ in the unit test with the band check kept
  valid: 0.6655 is more than 100 standard errors from ½).
- `product_sampler_inside_band`.
- `necessary_counts_known`: 32, 46, 49 from ln(1/α)/ln(1.1) rounded up; 182 for the distribution-free bound at
  α = 0.05.
- `undersized_population_plan_refused` (n = 24, α = 0.05); `finite_suite_plan_accepted`.
- `rare_tail_numbers`: population ratio 1.8215; probability of a tail-free suite 0.78568.
- `tail_free_sample_bootstrap_reports_20`; `contrast_does_not_certify`.
- `no_tail_observed_label_set`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh --row sampler|suite`: runs the 100 seeds of the chosen row through the real
binaries (`frob train start` for the ten curve seeds, the sampler battery command of its own bead, `frob eval
preanalyze`), with one `assert` line per seed (seed, signal value, threshold, verdict) and `metric` lines for the
fired count against 100, the false-alarm count against 0 and, for the suite row, the tail-free count and the
bootstrap certifications. `refusal` lines carry the pre-analysis refusal text. The `summary` names GATE-WS-07 and
the row recorded. The curve runs are labelled with their envelope class (default).

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 0.6655 (FACT, re-measured by the planted copy); 1.8215; 0.78568; 32, 46, 49, 182. Negative
controls: the planted sampler; the percentile bootstrap on a tail-free sample; an undersized population plan. A
battery that checks marginals only, and a pre-analysis with the count rule disabled, are the mutants that must
fail this bead's assertions.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; `batch-cpu` and device families for the sampler
row, since integer draws are D0 everywhere.

## Considerations

- The 0.6655 figure is a property of the sibling defect as reported; the planted copy is this program's own code
  written to reproduce it, so the test does not depend on the sibling staying broken.
- The spread threshold of 1.5 is a detector for the second signal, not a statistical claim; the binding protection
  is the count rule.
- If the partial gate moves, the suite row still has to be recorded before any sealed family is opened.
