## Background

Each row of the risk register names an earliest signal and a **planted instance**: a constructed case in which the
hazard is present, registered in the negative-control registry, on which the row's detector must report the
signal. Gate GATE-WS-07 requires that no row of the register lacks one. N41-32 supplies the layer (binding to a
registered control, staging by a `due` milestone, the command that fires plants, the exit-time check); N41-33 to
N41-35 carry the rows due at M1 to M3. This bead carries the three rows whose detectors arrive at M4 and one
further observation.

How a plant works, in brief. A row's `planted` field holds a slug, a text, a `due` milestone and a state
(`declared`, then `runnable` once it names a control whose last outcome is `caught`). Binding is reuse first: a
control already registered by the detector's owner for the same hazard is named and left unchanged; otherwise a
control seeded `RISK-nn`, with the gate list `["GATE-GAUNT-01"]`, is registered with a fixture under
`tests/controls/risk/<slug>/`, labelled `synthetic`. Plants run on a scratch root only.

Why M4: this is the milestone at which designs are decided without training each one. Three hazards decide
whether that is sound. A family policy may not transfer, or may lose plasticity along a lineage of edits. A cheap
rung may discard the design that would have won. A search or a gradient may exploit an artifact of contact order.
Each has a signal that the M4 mechanisms produce; this bead makes each signal fire once on purpose.

## Scope

In: the three plants and one further observation below, bound and runnable; the counts of the `risk-plants`
section of the M4 exit record. Out: the layer (N41-32); the detectors (named per row); the last two rows, due at M5, and the full sweep
(N41-37).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the three rows is `runnable` at the M4 commit: its `planted.control` names a control (reused
      unchanged, or registered with `seed = "RISK-nn"` and `gate = ["GATE-GAUNT-01"]`), and it is `caught` with
      the observable in the table; RISK-28 has all three parts.
- [ ] The further observation of RISK-49 is runnable and caught; no row has a declared further observation left.
- [ ] `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example risks -- plants --exit M4 --json` (N41-32's subcommand of the
      risk register's example target) reports 57 runnable and 2 declared primary plants and 10 of 10 further
      observations.
- [ ] RISK-37's screen, with the planted winner left in, passes its audit (the positive control), and with the
      winner discarded fails it.
- [ ] If a kill gate of the family-policy work has fired, the record names the fallback and the plants bound to
      it; no plant is skipped because a mechanism was withdrawn.
- [ ] The M4 exit is blocked when any one of the three is set back to `declared` on a scratch copy.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/risk_plants_m4.rs`: `plant_risk_28_out_of_class_refused`;
`plant_risk_28_resets_disabled_fails_chain`; `plant_risk_28_default_envelope_refuses_job`;
`plant_risk_37_discarded_winner_found`; `plant_risk_37_kept_winner_passes` (positive control);
`plant_risk_47_gradient_across_switch_unreliable`; `plant_risk_47_smooth_case_reliable` (positive control);
`plant_risk_49_agent_ignores_injection`; `fallback_state_binds_fallback_controls` (KILL-FAMILY, KILL-INHERIT
and KILL-FDGRAD fired in turn on a scratch register: each plant is bound to the fallback's control or mode and
none is skipped); `m4_counts` (57, 2, 10).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/risk_plants.sh --exit M4`: fires the 57 primary plants and ten further observations on a scratch
root, calling `policy.apply` (RISK-28 (i): the out-of-class design, refused with `FROB-REF-534` by BD-320's
membership rule, which that verb calls before scoring), `train.family`, `train.inherit`, `opt.cascade`,
`phylum.gradient` and the agent session harness for this bead's rows, each run-verb call with its own `--key` (`risk-<row>-<part>`, so that no plant is answered
from another's stored receipt). The two training plants use the smallest registered fixture and stay inside the default
machine envelope (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device); if a plant would exceed it the script stops with the
estimate and records `not-run: over-envelope`, never a pass. Logged with the shared helper of STD-E2E as in
N41-32, to `target/e2e-logs/risk_plants/<utc>.ndjson` (every line with `ts`, `script`, `bead` `N41-36`, `step`,
`event`): `start`; `cmd` lines with the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the evidence block of each verb call (claim, class, tier, determinism,
seed); one `assert` per plant with row, part, slug, control, detector, expected and observed signal and outcome;
`metric` lines for the decline along the chain, the audit twin's gap against 5%, whether the audit lane reported
the removed winner, and runnable against 57, declared against 2 and further observations against 10; `refusal`
lines with codes and exit statuses (534 and 540 exit 3, 761 exit 4); on failure the first plant whose outcome
differed, its fixture path and the scratch root; `end`; `summary` with assertions passed and failed naming
GATE-WS-07.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: ten edits in the chain and the 5% band of its audit twin; counts 57 and 2; ten further
observations. Negative controls: the plants;
the two positive controls (a kept winner, a smooth gradient) which must not fire; a plant recorded as caught from
a CPU run where its row names a device (refused). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; the training plants
are judged against their declared determinism class, not bit for bit.

## Considerations

- These three rows are the thesis failing lever by lever; their fallbacks ship under their own names. A plant
  that fires says the detector works, and says nothing about whether the lever does.
- RISK-28's chain plant is the costliest in the register. It is kept small on purpose: its job is to show that
  disabling resets is detected, and the gate's own run measures the effect.
- Compute: no plant here is admitted above the default envelope. A plant that cannot run inside it is redesigned
  as a smaller fixture; an approval is not requested for a control.
