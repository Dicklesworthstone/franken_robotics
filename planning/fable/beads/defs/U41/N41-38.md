## Background

Gate GATE-WS-07 (workspace gate, a TARGET, defined with four risks that were found late) requires that each of
those four risks has a planted instance on which its earliest signal fires in 100 of 100 runs, and that no row of
the risk register lacks a planted instance. The gate closes in parts: two build rows at M0 (N41-02), a sampler row
at M2 and a suite row at M3 (N41-31). This bead measures the **sampler row**, which is the gate's M2 part (the
register holds one part per milestone: M0, M2 and M3; N40-10, N40-01), at the M2 exit. It is a
test bead of the Gauntlet (the conformance harness in `frob-conformance`), tier Solid, on the critical path only in
the sense that the M2 exit lists the part.

The risk is RISK-58, severity High: **a sampler has correct marginals and a wrong joint law**. A reinforcement
learner that explores with Gaussian action noise scores each sampled action with a product of per-channel
densities. If the channels are in fact correlated, the likelihood ratio the learner computes belongs to a random
variable that was never sampled, and exploration is correlated across joints. A test of each channel's mean and
variance passes all the same. The sibling simulator's own Gaussian action sampler has this defect (FACT, to be
re-read at the pinned commit: its phase is a linear function of a counter, so adjacent channels agree in sign with
frequency 0.6655 where independent channels give ½). The program does not link that sampler; a planted copy of the
defect, written in this program's own code, is the negative control (N33c-37).

Earliest signals of the risk, as the register states them: (1) a pairwise sign-agreement frequency outside
½ ± 0.005 in the sampler battery; (2) a learning curve that changes when the action channels are reordered.
Mitigations named by the row: the joint-law rule for sampled actions (rule P-12: the joint law of the sampled
action equals the law the likelihood computation assumes) with its gate GATE-CORTEX-18; counter-based draws keyed
per channel (draws by world address, BD-180); no sibling sampler linked into any binary.

Why a separate bead at M2. The sampler row is due at the M2 exit, and the register-wide count of runnable plants
at that exit (39: 30 from earlier exits, eight of N41-34, and this one) includes it. The suite row needs the
ten-fold pre-analysis, which exists only at M3. One bead for both rows would be an M3 bead that an M2 exit cannot
wait for, so the two rows are two beads. Rejected alternative: firing the plant once at the exit, as the other 55
rows are fired. The gate asks for 100 of 100 for its four late rows, and since the detector is deterministic for a
fixed seed plan, the 100 runs are 100 seeds in which the defect enters through different draws.

## Scope

In: the 100-seed harness for the sampler row; the second signal (curves under channel reordering) on ten seeds;
the positive control on the product sampler; the linking check; the tier entry and the receipt of the gate's M2
part (scope "the sampler row"). Out: the recording of the part (the recorder of N38b-19, run by a session that did
not build this harness); the planted sampler itself (N33c-37); the joint-law battery, its statistics, bands, seed plan and
receipt (N23b-30); the qualified product sampler (N23b-29); addressed draws (BD-180); the trainer and the reference
task used for the curve runs (BD-198); the build rows (N41-02); the suite row and the closing of the gate from its
four rows (N41-31); the other M2 plants of the register (N41-34).

## Technical approach

Harness (DESIGN): `crates/frob-conformance/src/controls/ws07_late.rs` (module `sampler_row`); script
`scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh --row sampler` (the same script serves the suite row of N41-31 with `--row
suite`). The harness adds no statistic of its own: it calls the battery of N23b-30 as a library, inside the
conformance crate. The Gauntlet has no `frob` command; the row's entry is run as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo
test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test <the tier named by GATE-WS-07's record> --
GATE-WS-07`, and that entry loops over the 100 seeds.

**The sampler row.**

1. For seeds 1 to 100, run the joint-law battery on the planted linear-phase sampler with n = 10⁶ addressed draw
   pairs per channel pair. Under independence a sign-agreement frequency has standard deviation ½/√n = 0.0005, a
   tenth of the band ½ ± 0.005.
2. First signal: at least one channel pair has sign agreement outside ½ ± 0.005. Expected: fires on 100 of 100
   seeds, with the observed adjacent-channel frequency near 0.6655 recorded per seed (the value follows from the
   phase step of 0.16724512 of a turn per draw: 1 − 2·0.16724512 = 0.66551).
3. Second signal, on 10 of the seeds (each costs one short training run, inside the default envelope of 16 GiB,
   8 cores, one hour and one device, on `batch-cpu`): the reference task (flat velocity tracking of the reference
   quadruped) trained with the planted sampler under two channel orders gives learning curves that differ beyond
   the seed-to-seed band of the task; with the product sampler the curves under the two orders are bit-identical,
   because draws are keyed per channel and order cannot matter. DESIGN: the product-sampler runs go through
   `frob train start`; the planted-sampler runs are driven by the harness inside the conformance build, calling
   the same trainer as a library. Reason: the planted sampler is compiled only into the conformance build, and
   `train.start` refuses a sampler that has no qualification receipt, which the plant by construction cannot
   obtain. Those run records carry the labels `synthetic` and `planted` and are never offered as training
   results.
4. Positive control: the product sampler stays inside the band on 100 of 100 seeds (no false alarm).
5. Linking check: no symbol of the sibling's sampler is present in any `frob` binary (the deny list of the
   dependency check, read here as a listing; this bead adds no check to `xtask`).

**Gate record.** GATE-WS-07's M2 part (scope "the sampler row"). The tier run above writes the receipt; the script
logs its hash and writes no row of `registries/gates.toml` (one recorder per gate, N38b-19). The recording
session, which did not build the harness, enters the part with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
record_gate -- --gate GATE-WS-07 --receipt <hash> --part M2`. `measured` carries the fired count out of 100, the
positive-control count out of 100, the observed sign-agreement frequencies (minimum, median, maximum over seeds)
and the ten curve comparisons with their distances; the receipt carries commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds.
The part's registered control is the planted sampler, seeded from N41-CX's row X41-07, and it must be `caught` in
the same run. A fired count below 100, or any false alarm of the product sampler, is a `fail` line in the receipt
and is recorded as `fail` with the first failing seed. The gate's status is derived from its three parts
(N40-01); N41-31 records the last one and checks that the gate is closed.

**Binding in the risk register.** RISK-58's `planted` record in `registries/risks.toml` names the control seeded
from N41-CX's entry X41-07 (plant `linear-phase-sampler`, which is N33c-37's one fixture), with `due = "M2"`: the
binding rule of N41-32, reuse first, so that no control with a `RISK-` seed is registered for the row. The same
fixture also backs the controls that the Cortex catalogue's late entry on the joint law and the suite's entry
seed for GATE-CORTEX-18 and GATE-WS-06 (one control per entry, N38b-05); the risk row names the one whose gate
list holds GATE-WS-07. This bead sets the plant's state to `runnable` once the row has been recorded `caught`,
which is what the plant command of N41-32 reads at the M2 exit.

Determinism: draws are addressed (class D0, exact integers); the battery statistic is exact given the draws.
Evidence class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted sampler's sign-agreement signal fires on 100 of 100 seeds; the observed adjacent-channel
      frequency is within 0.005 of 0.6655 on every seed.
- [ ] The product sampler stays inside ½ ± 0.005 on 100 of 100 seeds.
- [ ] On 10 seeds the planted sampler's learning curves differ under channel reordering and the product sampler's
      are bit-identical.
- [ ] No sibling sampler symbol is found in any `frob` binary.
- [ ] GATE-WS-07's M2 part is recorded with measured values at the M2 exit, from the tier run's receipt with
      `record_gate --part M2`, and the script itself records nothing; RISK-58's plant is `runnable` and reported
      `caught` by the plant command, so the M2 count of runnable primary plants is 39.
- [ ] A battery that checks marginals only (the mutant) lets the planted sampler pass, and this bead's assertion
      on the fired count fails for it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ws07_late.rs` (sampler half); the real battery and the real planted sampler, no
mocks; fixtures labelled `synthetic`.
- `planted_sampler_fires_each_seed`: seeds 1 to 100, reduced to n = 10⁵ in the unit test with the band check kept
  valid (0.6655 is more than 100 standard errors from ½ at that n).
- `product_sampler_inside_band`: the same seeds, zero alarms.
- `adjacent_agreement_is_0_6655`: the measured frequency against 1 − 2·0.16724512, within 0.005.
- `row_fails_on_99`: a synthetic result with 99 fired seeds records the row as failing and names the seed.
- `plant_binds_existing_control`: RISK-58's plant names the control seeded from X41-07, whose fixture is
  N33c-37's and whose gate list holds GATE-WS-07, and no control with a `RISK-` seed exists for the row.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ws07_late_plants.sh --row sampler`: runs the row's entry with the cargo command above (the 100
battery seeds, and the planted-sampler curve runs, which the harness drives in process) and the product-sampler
curve runs through the real binary, `frob train start <reference quadruped> --task <flat velocity task in one
of two channel orders> --backend batch-cpu --seed <s> --key ws07-<order>-<s> --json` followed by `frob train
status <run> --json` (two fixture tasks that differ only in the order of their action terms), sourcing the shared logging helper of STD-E2E and writing
`target/e2e-logs/ws07_late_plants/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: `start`; one `assert` line per seed
(seed, signal value, threshold, verdict); one `assert` per curve pair (seed, channel order, distance, the task's
seed-to-seed band, and for the product sampler the two curve digests, which must be equal); `metric` lines for the
fired count against 100, the false-alarm count against 0 and the minimum, median and maximum frequency; the digest
of each battery report; `end`. Each line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (N41-38), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
evidence block; the curve runs are labelled with their envelope class (default). On failure the log gives the
first failing seed and the path of its battery report. The `summary` line names GATE-WS-07 and the hash of the
receipt of its M2 part.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers (K41-08, K41-09): 0.6655 (FACT about the sibling's recurrence, re-measured here on the planted
copy); the band ½ ± 0.005; standard deviation 0.0005 at n = 10⁶. Negative controls: the planted sampler itself
(X41-07); the marginals-only battery, which must fail this bead's assertion. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts;
`batch-cpu` and each device family once the backends exist, since integer draws are D0 everywhere.

## Considerations

- The figure 0.6655 is a property of the sibling's defect as reported; the planted copy is this program's own code
  written to reproduce it, so the test does not depend on the sibling staying broken. When the upstream fix lands,
  the corrected sibling sampler is re-run through the battery before it may serve as a comparison baseline; the
  plant stays.
- The second signal costs ten short training runs; under the no-GPU variant of M2 it still runs, because the curve
  runs are on `batch-cpu`.
- The same planted fixture serves the workspace gate GATE-WS-06, which requires rule P-12 to reject it, and the
  battery's own negative control; this bead only counts 100 firings and records the row.
- What would make us revisit: a change of the battery's band or seed plan, which changes the meaning of "fires"
  and needs the row to be re-recorded.
