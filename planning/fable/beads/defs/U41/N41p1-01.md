## Background

A design in `frob-design` (Skeleton, layer 2, tier Solid) is a term whose internal nodes are **constructors**
(`attach`, `chain`, `serial`, `mirror`, `radial`, `scale`, `replace`, `closure`). Constructor terms stay unexpanded
in the canonical bytes (canonical-form rule 7); the expanded instance tree is a compile product. The seed known
answer of the constructor bead BD-056 is that a design built with `mirror` equals its hand-expanded form, and the
plan carries the same statement as construction C-12's law C-12.S7 and as metamorphic relation MR-C11 (whose
registered mutation, a mirror that reflects positions and leaves revolute axis signs unchanged, N38a-15 carries at
M2).

BD-056 can test that equality only on its own output, the placed-component sets, because the elaborate stage
(N14a-07), the kinematic functor (BD-061), the inertia functor (BD-062) and the verbs that read a design
(`design.check` BD-110, `design.view` BD-057, `design.show` N14a-21) all depend on it. This bead closes the loop
after them: the same equality on elaborated trees and compiled models, the mass factor of `scale`, and the
end-to-end script through the `frob` binary that BD-056 specified but could not run. It exists so that the
user-visible claim (an agent who writes `mirror` gets exactly the robot it would have got by writing four legs)
is checked where the user meets it.

Terms. *Hand-expanded form*: `fixtures/quad-a-expanded.fbot`, written by BD-056: the four legs of `quad-a` as
explicit `attach` limbs on four torso ports, each written with BD-056's reflection table (axes as axial vectors,
det(P)·P·a, with the same coordinate, limits and gear sign). *Parity*: +1, or −1 for an image under an odd number
of reflections; a record of the constructor, absent (+1) in the hand-expanded form.

## Scope

In: a `frob-model` integration test comparing the two designs after elaboration and after compilation; the
`scale` mass test; the script `scripts/e2e/design_constructors.sh`.

Out: the constructors, their expansion and reflection rules, the fixtures (BD-056); the elaborate stage (N14a-07);
the kinematic and inertia functors (BD-061, BD-062); the verbs (BD-057, BD-110, N14a-21); the registered mutation
of MR-C11 in the Gauntlet's suite (N38a-15, M2); the archetype-wide gates (GATE-SKEL-03, BD-109, N14b-06).

## Technical approach

**Test 1, elaboration** (`crates/frob-model/tests/constructors_compiled.rs`). Elaborate both designs (N14a-07).
Pair instances by placement: sort each tree's instances by (kind, exact placement, parent placement) and compare
pairwise. Equal means: the same count of instances of each kind; equal exact placements; equal typed nodes
(Region, fill, material, joint class, screw basis, origin, limits, actuator card, transmission, contact law).
Excluded from the comparison, and only these: instance identifiers, source sets, and the parity record.

**Test 2, compilation.** Compile both. Compare, bit for bit in binary64 after the single rounding from exact:
- kinematic section (BD-061): for every paired body, `parent_from_joint`, screw axes and pitches, limits, home;
- frames at 100 seeded configurations (seed 20261123, joint values uniform inside the limits, written once by this
  bead to `fixtures/constructors/configs.jsonl` keyed by `quad-a`'s joint identifiers, and translated for the
  expanded form through the hand-written pairing `fixtures/constructors/pairing.toml`, identifier ↔ identifier):
  world poses of every body and every foot frame;
- inertial section (BD-062): per-body mass, centre of mass and inertia about it, and the whole-robot roll-up.
`ModelId`s may differ (the kinematic section records parity); this test asserts nothing about them and logs both.

**Test 3, scale.** `fixtures/constructors/scale2-solid.fbot` (BD-056) wraps a solid capsule body in
`scale("2", ·)`. Its compiled mass is exactly 8 times the unscaled body's in binary64 (every length factor is a
power of two, so the product of the scaled lengths and the density rounds exactly as the unscaled one, times 8);
its rotational inertia about the centre of mass is exactly 32 times; a 2 mm shell body under the same scale gives
mass exactly 8 times. A `declared` body under `scale` keeps its mass properties and carries the warning
`scale-left-declared-mass`.

**The script** `scripts/e2e/design_constructors.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; seed explicit; idempotent):

| Step | Command | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `frob design check fixtures/quad-a.fbot --json` (stages 1 to 6) | exit 0 or 2, no refusal |
| 2 | the same for `fixtures/quad-a-expanded.fbot` | exit 0 or 2, no refusal |
| 3 | `frob design view fixtures/quad-a.fbot --as json --json` | both `mirror` keys and one `chain` in the bytes; BLAKE3 of the bytes equals `bound.design` |
| 4 | `frob design show <each> --section instances --json` | 17 bodies each; placements equal pairwise as in test 1 |
| 5 | `frob design show <each> --section frames --at @<configs> --json`, the expanded form's file produced from `configs.jsonl` through `pairing.toml` | frame poses of paired frames equal bit for bit at 100 configurations |
| 6 | `frob design show <each> --section mass --json` | per-body mass properties and roll-up equal bit for bit |
| 7 | `frob design show fixtures/constructors/scale2-solid.fbot --section mass --json` | between each scaled body and its bare twin in the same design: solid capsule mass ratio 8 and inertia ratio 32, shell mass ratio 8, exact; the `declared` body's mass properties equal and the warning `scale-left-declared-mass` |
| 8 | `frob design check <f> --json` for each of the six `fixtures/constructors/refuse_*.fbot` | exit 3 and the code of `expect.toml` (101 bad port; 102 zero scale; 103 for `serial` k = 0, for `radial` n = 1 and for a `replace` path naming nothing; 123 mount on the mirror plane), raised at stage 1 for all six (constructors are read and expanded there), and a non-empty first repair |

Logging (STD-E2E standard): one NDJSON line per event to `target/e2e-logs/design_constructors/<utc>.ndjson` and a
transcript beside it; each line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N41p1-01`), `step`, `event`, the exact command
line, exit code, wall time in ms, the envelope's `evidence` block, the two `DesignId`s and `ModelId`s; `metric`
lines for the mass and inertia ratios with their expected values; `assert` lines with the digests of the compared
sections; on a mismatch, the first differing instance pair, field and both values, and the paths of the two
fixtures; `refusal` lines with code, pointer and first repair; a final `summary` with counts passed and failed.

Determinism: exact elaboration (D0); compiled sections bit-identical across two runs, debug and release, Linux and
macOS.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Test 1 passes: elaborated trees of `quad-a` and `quad-a-expanded` are equal up to identifiers, source sets
      and the parity record.
- [ ] Test 2 passes: kinematic and inertial sections and frames at 100 seeded configurations are bit-identical.
- [ ] Test 3 passes: mass × 8 and inertia × 32 exactly under `scale("2")`; the warning on the declared body.
- [ ] The three planted mutants of BD-056 (axis mapped by P·a; parity sign on the pseudo-inertia; an expanding
      writer), BD-056's mutation features `mutant-axis-polar`, `mutant-inertia-parity`, `mutant-expand-writer` of
      `frob-design`, built in one at a time in a debug build (`cargo test -p frob-model --features
      frob-design/<feature> --test constructors_compiled`), each make test 1, test 2 or step 3 fail, naming the
      first differing field.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/design_constructors.sh` exits 0 with all eight steps passing and writes its NDJSON log and
      summary; run against a debug `frob` built with the axis mutant (`cargo build -p frob --features
      frob-design/mutant-axis-polar`), it exits non-zero at step 5.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-model --test constructors_compiled`: `mirror_equals_hand_expanded_elaborated`,
`mirror_equals_hand_expanded_compiled`, `mirror_frames_equal_100_configs`, `scale2_mass_times_8`,
`scale2_inertia_times_32`, `scale2_shell_mass_times_8`, `scale_declared_warns`. Real fixtures, no mocks.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/design_constructors.sh`, as above.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answer: C-12.S7 (the statement of metamorphic relation MR-C11, tier G3, site `host`, N38a-15), mirror-built
  equals hand-expanded, exact after elaboration and bit-identical after compilation, here in this crate test.
- Known answer: `scale("2")` of a solid gives mass × 8 and inertia × 32, exact.
- Negative controls: the three mutants of the acceptance criteria; the refusal fixtures each yield their own code
  and no other.
- Metamorphic: relabelling the hand-expanded limbs' body names changes no compared field.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- A mismatch in test 2 with test 1 passing locates the defect in a functor (BD-061, BD-062), not in the
  constructors; the log says which section differed first.
- Mesh Regions under `mirror` flip triangle winding (BD-056's `mirror_mesh_flips_winding`); `quad-a` has no mesh
  body, so a winding defect in a functor is not this bead's to catch.
- Runs in seconds on a laptop; no GPU.
