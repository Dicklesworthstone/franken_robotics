## Background

**The row.** WP-03: Marrow core. Crates: `frob-lie`, `frob-algebra`. Milestone: M0, M1. Key gates: GATE-MARROW-01,
-02. Waits for: WP-02.

Marrow is the algebraic core that every layer above computes with. `frob-lie` provides the Lie groups and their
algebras (SO(3) and SE(3) over `fs-ga`, SO(2) as a unit complex number with a turn counter, SE(2), SE₂(3), SE₂₊ₖ(3),
product groups), the Cayley and exponential retractions with their derivatives, joint subgroups with the Hervé
classification and generated joint maps, and frame-typed transforms, twists and wrenches with the three typed maps
between frames. `frob-algebra` provides finite groups from generators with classes, characters and projectors, orbits,
Burnside counts, subgroup lattices and wreath products, the `Cone` trait with the orthant, second-order and
semidefinite cones, the inertia cone and the mass-support predicate, posets with antichains and Kleene iteration, and
the small dense cone-program solver with end states and dual certificates.

It is one unit of work because conventions must be fixed once and tested as laws before any crate above layer 1 names
a twist or a wrench: the workspace forbids a bare `f64` physical quantity in a public signature above layer 1, so a
convention error made here would be copied into every later crate. The two crates share the layer and the law-test
discipline (10⁶ cases per law).

**Order.** It waits for WP-02 because SO(3) and SE(3) are built over `fs-ga`, which is reached by path through the
stack that WP-02 admits. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would
block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-lie` (layer 1; Solid; cap 12): Lie groups and algebras, subgroups, retractions, frame typing, conventions.
- `frob-algebra` (layer 1; Solid; cap 14): Finite groups and representations, cones and the cone solver, posets, the
  inertia cone.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** `frob-lie` traits, SO(3), SE(3) and SO(2); both retractions with derivatives and their laws; the coadjoint
  convention; the fixture of proof obligation PO-MARROW-01; GATE-MARROW-01 for SO(3) and SE(3). The three theorem
  cards T-01, T-03 and T-04 that the gate cites are certified in WP-05.
- **M1.** The remaining groups, joint subgroups and generated joint maps; frame-typed quantities; all of
  `frob-algebra` including the cone-program solver; GATE-MARROW-02.

Out of scope: equivariant linear maps by irreducible blocks and symmetric controllers (WP-29); the symmetry groups
Γ_mech and Γ_obs of the eighteen archetypes, GATE-MARROW-03 (WP-38); guarded predicates on devices, GATE-MARROW-05
(WP-46); the exact and guarded kernel classes (WP-35).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **A reversed convention is silent.** The admission test of the `fs-ga` façades (INT-FSIM-02, in WP-37) requires a
  deliberately reversed adjoint to fail at least three fixtures; the same discipline applies to every law here.
- **Exact character tables can exceed their bit budget.** That is the trigger of KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS, decided at M4
  with the equivariant maps (WP-29); nothing in this package depends on its outcome.
- Law tolerances are TARGETs: at most 64 ulp in `f64` and 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations.
