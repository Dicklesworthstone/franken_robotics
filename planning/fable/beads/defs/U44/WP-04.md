## Background

**The row.** WP-04: Spark spikes. Crates: the kernel dialect, `spark/frob-spark-cuda`, `spark/frob-spark-metal`, the
backend trait. Milestone: M0. Key gates: INT-CUDA-01, INT-FT-01, -02; GATE-SPARK-00, -01. Waits for: WP-01.

The spike answers, with the least code that can answer it, whether one kernel source can run on three back ends inside
a controlled determinism class (BET-02: one source, three backends). It delivers the shared dialect prelude and its
lint, the backend trait with the kernel manifest, the `batch-cpu` harness, version 0 of the CUDA annex (driver load,
NVRTC compile, cache, launch, on a `cudarc` that builds without a CUDA toolkit), version 0 of the Metal annex over
FrankenTorch's `ft-kernel-metal` gateway with resident buffers and device completion receipts, `spark.probe` with the
IEEE probe kernels and their report, and `K-cay-step` running from one dialect source on `batch-cpu`, on both GPUs of
`trj` and on this Mac.

It is one unit of work because the two device families are asked the same question on the same kernel, and the answer
is one table: the probe sets each device's best attainable determinism class, which every later package reads.

**Order.** It waits for WP-01, which provides the annex workspaces and T. The annexes keep their own lockfiles because
of the unsafe and foreign-interface policy (a third-party driver binding, FFI capsules, FrankenTorch's `block` patch),
never because of the compiler. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic
would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope review) and role:
- `spark/frob-spark-cuda` (annex workspace; Solid; cap 8): CUDA backend, arena lease, binary kernel cache.
- `spark/frob-spark-metal` (annex workspace; Solid; cap 7): Metal backend.
- The kernel dialect (`kernels/`): the shared-dialect prelude v0 for CUDA and Metal, and the dialect lint.
- The backend trait: `KernelBackend`, the kernel manifest with its stage signature and the `batch-cpu` harness. The
  trait is defined in the core and implemented in the annexes, so the core never depends on an annex.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** Both annexes at version 0; the prelude, lint, trait, manifest and harness; INT-CUDA-01, INT-FT-01 and
  INT-FT-02; `spark.probe` on both device families (GATE-SPARK-01); `K-cay-step` from one source on three back ends
  (GATE-SPARK-00, which is also the test of INT-FSIM-11).

Out of scope: the full CUDA and Metal backends (WP-18, WP-19); the hardware fingerprint and the exact and guarded
kernel classes (WP-35); derivation of the kernel arithmetic (WP-05); the determinism services and the decision KILL-D1
(WP-46).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate):
- M0: BD-013 Shared dialect prelude v0 for CUDA and Metal; the dialect lint; BD-014 `KernelBackend` trait, the kernel
  manifest with its stage signature, and the `batch-cpu` harness; BD-015 INT-CUDA-01 and `spark/frob-spark-cuda` v0:
  driver load, NVRTC compile, cache, launch; BD-016 INT-FT-01 and `spark/frob-spark-metal` v0 over the
  `ft-kernel-metal` gateway, with …; BD-017 `spark.probe`: the IEEE probe kernels and report; BD-020 GATE-SPARK-00 and
  INT-FSIM-11: one dialect source for `K-cay-step` on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and …; BD-028 INT-FT-02: resident buffers and
  device completion receipts through the Metal gateway.
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The package is Solid. One pilot is
parented here: 'is Metal a rollout tier?', the predicted quadruped throughput on the M5 Ultra computed from the
fingerprint. Its result must be on record before KILL-METAL is read at M2 (WP-19).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every child bead is closed with a typed reason citing its own test and that test's output; no close reason is shared; no child is closed by the session that implemented it.
- [ ] Every gate below has a row in `registries/gates.toml` (commit, command, output, receipt hash) with status `pass`, or `killed: fallback adopted` with its ADR. `not run` is not a pass. Thresholds are TARGETs until measured.
- [ ] INT-CUDA-01 (admission of `cudarc`; first needed M0): The pinned `cudarc` builds without a CUDA toolkit, loads the driver on `trj`, and runs a kernel on both GPUs.
- [ ] INT-FT-01 (admission of frankentorch `ft-kernel-metal`; first needed M0): The gateway builds in the Metal annex, with FrankenTorch's `block` patch repeated at the annex root, and runs a kernel on this Mac.
- [ ] INT-FT-02 (admission of frankentorch `ft-kernel-metal` gateway; first needed M0): A buffer stays resident across two command buffers; the gateway returns a completion receipt from the device; a device tag alone earns zero operator coverage.
- [ ] GATE-SPARK-00 (Known answer; earliest M0): One dialect source for `K-cay-step` compiles and runs on CUDA (`trj`) and on Metal (this Mac) and matches the Rust reference inside its class.
- [ ] GATE-SPARK-01 (Known answer; earliest M0): IEEE probe per device family: exhaustive bit comparison of `f32` square root and reciprocal over all 2³² inputs; 10⁹ sampled operand pairs for +, −, ×, ÷ with subnormal and overflow edges; the contraction detector; subnormal and NaN behaviour recorded. The outcome sets each device's best attainable determinism class.
- [ ] Each crate above ships `CONTRACT.md` with its ten sections and `contract.toml`; `xtask check-contracts` reports its line count under 115% of its cap.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **OPEN-SPARK-1** (owner; due at the M2 exit): ratification of ADR-SPARK-01, that GPU backends live in annex
  workspaces with registered FFI capsules outside the Franken-only core. Fail-closed default: GPU results are labelled
  exploratory, no Performance or Parity claim rests on a device result, and M2 exits in its no-GPU variant.
- **OPEN-SPARK-4**: whether the pinned `cudarc` exposes graph capture and the asynchronous pool calls. Default:
  preallocated transient buffers and per-launch submission, with the throughput cost disclosed.
- **OPEN-SPARK-5**: if INT-FT-01 fails, stay inside the gateway's limits and accept KILL-METAL's fallback; no Metal
  binding of our own without an owner decision.
- **Stop decision of M0**: a device family is dropped from M2 if the probe shows that it can hold no controlled
  determinism class.
- Unsafe code exists only in annex capsules: each is registered in `registries/capsules.toml`, under 300 lines, behind
  a safe API, with a `SAFETY.md`, and counted by `check-unsafe`.
