## Background

**The row.** WP-05: Derivation spike. Crates: `frob-kir`, `derive/frob-derive`, `frob-lemma`. Milestone: M0. Key
gates: INT-FSYM-01, -02; GATE-LEMMA-00. Waits for: WP-03.

The spike tests at the smallest scale the claim that kernel arithmetic is derived and certified, not written by hand
(BET-01, the algebra pays for itself; BET-11, proof-carrying kernels). It delivers `frob-kir` v0 (straight-line
programs, common-subexpression elimination, the Rust and dialect emitters, the dimension checker),
`derive/frob-derive` v0 building on T against the FrankenSymPy crates by path and deriving `K-cay-step` and
`K-normalize`, and `frob-lemma` v0: claims and instance certificates for theorem cards T-01, T-03 and T-04 on the M0
kernels, checked by FrankenSymPy and written as `.frobcert` bundles whose `poly-identity` and `rational-ldlt` checkers
live in `frob-cert`, so that the certificates replay in the offline auditor.

It is one unit of work because derivation, emission and certification are one pipeline, and a derived kernel whose
certificate does not replay is not evidence for either bet.

**Order.** It waits for WP-03: the kernels derived are Marrow's Cayley step and normalization, and T-01, T-03 and T-04
are identities over `frob-lie`. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic
would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-kir` (layer 1; Solid, Frontier; cap 14): Straight-line programs with bounded loops and resource metadata,
  dimension checking, differentiation, emitters; the exact-integer kernel class.
- `derive/frob-derive` (annex workspace; Frontier; cap 14): Symbolic derivations, certificate production, the
  exact-synthesis subprocess.
- `frob-lemma` (layer 6; Solid, Frontier; cap 10): Claims, certificate production, bundle writing, theorem cards, the
  reflection route.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** `frob-kir` v0; `derive/frob-derive` v0 with INT-FSYM-01; the two derived kernels emitted for `batch-cpu` and
  both dialect preludes; `frob-lemma` v0 and the bundle writer; GATE-LEMMA-00. The second admission gate of the row,
  INT-FSYM-02 (the planar two-link Jacobian against closed forms at a regular and a singular configuration), is
  first recorded here at M0 (N33a-32); it is recorded again at its own pin on the three-joint leg by the bead that
  first uses sparse Jacobians in an analysis (BD-105, WP-11, M1).

Out of scope: the kernel library and the measurement that decides KILL-KIR (WP-15); certificate coverage across
derived kernels and Lean capsules (WP-31); the exact and guarded kernel classes in `frob-kir` (WP-35); the bundle
reader and the binary `frob-verify` (WP-36).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **The derivation annex is build-time only.** FrankenSymPy never enters the core's lockfile; generated arithmetic is
  committed with digests and never edited (`check-generated` regenerates and compares).
- **The dependency firewall of M1.** If the symbolic foundation fails admission, M1 still ships: CLI only and no
  kernel certificates, because the mission slice's auditor uses the standard library alone.
- No constructor of `Proved` evidence exists outside `frob-lemma` (compile-fail test of GATE-LEMMA-06, in WP-36).
