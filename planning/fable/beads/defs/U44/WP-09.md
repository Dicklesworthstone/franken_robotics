## Background

**The row.** WP-09: Actuators. Crates: `frob-actuate`. Milestone: M1. Key gates: GATE-SINEW-01, -02, -05 to -08. Waits
for: WP-07.

Sinew turns a commanded torque into what a real drive can deliver. The package delivers actuator, battery and
transmission cards (the `.fcard` schema with per-field provenance and the generic catalog), actuator levels L0 and L1
on the referee and L2 with winding current, the thermal network and the battery, winding temperature with
temperature-dependent resistance and thermal runaway, the voltage limit and field weakening, transmissions (gear,
belt, screw, linkage, cable and coupling matrices with loss laws), the power bus and the battery with brownout events,
the port energy-balance check on the referee, and joint-level stiction, backlash and hard stops as rows in the referee
solve. Verbs: `actuator.list`, `actuator.show`, `actuator.check`, `actuator.thermal`, `actuator.envelope`,
`actuator.bus`.

It is one unit of work because all of it is one family of port models bound by two shared laws: the port energy
balance closes, and every loss and every entropy production is nonnegative.

**Order.** It waits for WP-07: designs reference cards, and the `.fbot` fields carry the power bus. (The order between
packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry
the real dependencies.)

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **OPEN-SINEW-1**: which real actuator parts ship in the catalogue, and under what data licence. Default: generic
  classes only; a card field without measured provenance is `assumed`.
- **A constant-resistance thermal check is unsound**: the worked card's 20 A point is admitted by it and refused by
  the corrected check with temperature-dependent resistance. Both are tested (GATE-SINEW-05).
- A demand above the bus ceiling E²/(4·R) must produce a brownout event and never a completed step, 100 of 100 trials
  (GATE-SINEW-07).
