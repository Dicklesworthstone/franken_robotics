## Background

**The row.** WP-10: Referee tier. Crates: `frob-dyn`, `frob-contact`. Milestone: M1. Key gates: GATE-KIN-01, -02;
GATE-TRAC-01, -02. Waits for: WP-02, WP-09.

The referee is the tier every other tier is judged against, and M1 is a design studio that runs on it alone. The
package delivers collision primitives and contact sets generated from `fs-query` (with its admission test
INT-FSIM-08), the referee step, which is FrankenSim's contact step run on our generated contact sets with compliant
feet, the free-body variational step kept as an oracle, the dynamics known-answer battery, contact-free forward
dynamics in parity with the pinned oracle, and the contact known-answer battery on the referee.

It is one unit of work because dynamics and contact meet in one step function, and the M1 studio needs that function
whole: a rollout that the static analyses, the controllers and the mission slice can all stand on.

**Order.** It waits for WP-02 (the airlock and `RefereeDynamics`) and WP-09 (actuator levels act through the step, and
joint stiction, backlash and hard stops are rows in the referee solve). (The order between packages is recorded in
`related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-dyn` (layer 3; Solid, Frontier; cap 20): The batched step, integrators, tiers, the trust ladder; the
  transactional event-located step with witness migration; the variational lane.
- `frob-contact` (layer 3; Solid, Frontier; cap 22): Collision geometry, detection, contact laws (convex compliant,
  the four-dimensional cone, joint rows, film friction, the hard-contact law), wheels, tracks, terrain mechanics.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** The Kinesis refusal rows (N16-REF), registry data entered ahead of the mechanisms that raise them.
- **M1.** The referee step and both known-answer batteries; GATE-KIN-01 and -02; GATE-TRAC-02; the referee rows of
  GATE-TRAC-01, with the list of rows FrankenSim's solve can express recorded beside the gate; the turn count as
  referee state and the referee rows of GATE-MARROW-06 (N13-35, N13-36; the device and teleport rows are N13-37 in
  WP-51 at M2).
- **M2.** Two upstream work items done in FrankenSim's repository, each with its interim posture here: native
  spherical, universal, planar and cylindrical joints (FD-FSIM-01; until then products of one-axis joints, and its
  switch-over re-runs GATE-KIN-02 and -03), and an arc-aware swept bound in the broad phase (NF-10; until then the
  arc-enclosing bound that `frob-contact` builds itself for rotating links, which GATE-TRAC-12 measures in WP-39).

Out of scope: the transactional, event-located step with witness migration (WP-39); the batched step (WP-15); contact
on batched tiers (WP-16); the four-dimensional cone, joint rows and scaling (WP-45); the trust ladder (WP-22).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **The referee's contact cap.** FrankenSim's sustained solve handles at most 21 contacts; an over-capacity set
  refuses by name until the upstream item FD-FSIM-13 lands. Archetypes whose contact count does not fit are not
  demonstrated on the referee at M1.
- **Joint types.** Spherical, universal, planar and cylindrical joints are lowered to products of one-axis joints
  until the upstream item FD-FSIM-01 lands; its switch-over rule re-runs GATE-KIN-02 and -03.
- The referee claims same-platform determinism only, until deterministic elementary functions land upstream
  (FD-FSIM-07).
- KILL-CONTACT is decided at M2 with the batched contact law (WP-16), not here.
