## Background

**The row.** WP-12: Reflex I: invariant filter and baseline controllers. Crates: `frob-control`. Milestone: M1. Key
gates: GATE-REFLEX-01, -03. Waits for: WP-10.

A design studio must make a design stand before anything is learned. The package delivers the invariant filter behind
`control.estimate`, the legged baseline controller, the wheeled baseline controller, and `control.synth` with
three-outcome reporting together with `control.show`. With it each first archetype stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m
of flat ground on the referee.

It is one unit of work because estimation and control close one loop, and the loop is the acceptance: the filter is
judged by consistency inside χ² bounds on the simulated IMU and a position drift under 2% of distance on a flat trot,
and the controllers by the stand-and-traverse row.

**Order.** It waits for WP-10: the controllers act through the referee step. (The order between packages is recorded
in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real
dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **Three outcomes, never a bare failure.** `control.synth` returns `not-synthesizable` with the reason,
  `synthesized-failed` with the failure taxonomy, or `ok`. A design that fails under its baseline is not thereby a bad
  design, and the report says so.
- The comparator's `overconfident` flag must fire on a planted bias (GATE-REFLEX-03).
