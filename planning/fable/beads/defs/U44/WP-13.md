## Background

**The row.** WP-13: Chronicle I: telemetry, energy ledger, explain. Crates: `frob-chronicle`. Milestone: M1. Key
gates: GATE-CHRON-03. Waits for: WP-10.

Chronicle is the record: nothing reaches an agent as a bare number, and everything an agent is told can be traced to
what produced it. The package delivers the ledger schema, `.frun` episode files, telemetry levels T0 to T2 with their
streams and channel kinds, the energy ledger on the referee with stored heat, separate braking modes and the first
offending event, the failure taxonomy with outcome kinds, first-cause ordering and budgeted summaries
(`telemetry.summary`, `telemetry.failures`, `telemetry.energy`, `telemetry.query`), and `explain.run` and
`explain.design`.

It is one unit of work because the M1 studio needs one coherent record of a rollout: what happened, where the energy
went, what failed first, and an explanation an agent can read within a budget.

**Order.** It waits for WP-10: there must be a referee rollout to record, and the energy ledger is closed against the
referee's own port flows. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would
block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate):
- M1: BD-090 `frob-chronicle`: the ledger schema, `.frun` episodes, telemetry levels T0 to T2 with streams …; BD-092
  The energy ledger on the referee, with stored heat, separate braking modes and the first …; BD-094 Failure taxonomy
  with outcome kinds, first-cause ordering and budgeted summaries …; BD-112 `explain.run` and `explain.design`.
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** None. Every mechanism of this package
is Solid.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **The ledger's foundation.** The ledger runs on FrankenSQLite (admitted by INT-FSQL-01 in WP-02). Its named fallback
  is an append-only JSON-lines ledger with the same schema.
- **Layering.** The evidence sink and the trace source are traits defined in `frob-core` and implemented here, so that
  lower crates can emit evidence without depending on layer 6.
- Three energy tolerances exist with different scopes and none relaxes another: 10⁻³ relative for the whole ledger on
  single-precision tiers, 10⁻⁴ per episode for the port balance, 10⁻⁴ on the precision battery.
