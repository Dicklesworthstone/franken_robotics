## Background

**The row.** WP-16: Traction, batched. Crates: `frob-contact`. Milestone: M2. Key gates: GATE-TRAC-01, -03. Waits for:
WP-15, WP-17.

Contact decides whether a legged or wheeled design's simulated behaviour means anything, and the program's bet is that
a convex compliant law is good enough on the fast tiers (BET-03). The package delivers `K-geom-pose`, `K-np-prim`,
`K-bp-sort` and `K-bp-pairs` (poses of primitives, primitive pairs, the deterministic broad phase), `K-jac-rows`,
`K-cone-setup` and `K-cone-eval` for contact law CL-1, `K-np-sdf` with overlays and the pooled brick directory,
`K-np-thin` for thin barriers, the contact known-answer battery on the batched tiers, and the tunneling and capacity
audits behind `contact.audit` and `contact.capacity`.

It is one unit of work because detection and the law are measured together: the battery passes or fails on the whole
chain, and the tunneling audit compares the fast tiers with the certified detector.

**Order.** It waits for WP-15 (the batched step and the kernel library) and WP-17 (the scene fields and the pooled
directory that the field narrow phase reads). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-CONTACT** (decided M2, in WP-22, by N18b-33). Trigger: GATE-FID-01 fails on slip or creep after parameter
  mapping and one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: CL-2 becomes the default at a published lower throughput; if that also fails,
  locomotion claims are restricted to the referee tier and the fast tiers are labeled exploratory.
- **Stop decision of M2**: the legged strata of the partial ten-fold gate are re-planned if both fallbacks of
  KILL-CONTACT are exhausted.
- On the M2 battery the fast tiers must miss fewer than 1 contact per 10⁶ world-steps that the certified detector
  finds (GATE-TRAC-03).
- The broad phase is deterministic by construction (sort, then pairs), because batch-composition invariance
  (GATE-KIN-05, GATE-SPARK-15) forbids any order that depends on co-tenants.
