## Background

**The row.** WP-19: Metal backend. Crates: `spark/frob-spark-metal`. Milestone: M2. Key gates: GATE-SPARK-02, -04,
-06. Waits for: WP-04, WP-15.

The Metal backend runs the same kernels on this Mac through FrankenTorch's `ft-kernel-metal` gateway. The package
delivers packed arguments, batch chains, snapshot blits and receipts. Its bet is BET-09: Metal throughput is at least
25% of one RTX 4090 at matched settings on the M5 Ultra, reported beside an incumbent with a Metal path measured on
the same machine.

It is one unit of work for the same reason as the CUDA backend: agreement inside the class, the Metal performance row,
and fault injection are judged on the whole backend.

**Order.** It waits for WP-04 (the annex at version 0 and the gateway admission) and WP-15 (the kernels and the step).
(The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the
child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-METAL** (decided M2). Trigger: GATE-SPARK-04 fails after one optimization pass, or the Metal backend cannot
  hold run-to-run determinism. Fallback, named in advance: Metal becomes a development and replay tier; CUDA is the
  training tier; the Mac trains on `batch-cpu`.
- The gateway's limits bind the design: resident buffers across command buffers and device completion receipts were
  admitted by INT-FT-02; anything beyond them waits for an upstream change in FrankenTorch or an owner decision
  (OPEN-SPARK-5).
- If KILL-METAL fires, Metal becomes a development and replay tier, CUDA is the training tier and the Mac trains on
  `batch-cpu`; no gate of another package is blocked.
- OPEN-SPARK-1 applies as in WP-04 and WP-18: until ADR-SPARK-01 is ratified, results from this backend are labelled
  exploratory and support no Performance or Parity claim; with neither device family ratified M2 exits in its no-GPU
  variant.
- The three upstream beads (FD-FT-01, NFp1-51, NFp1-52) are done in FrankenTorch's repository; no gate of this
  package waits on them. Until each switch-over has run, the backend stays inside the gateway's admitted limits.
