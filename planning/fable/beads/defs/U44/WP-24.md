## Background

**The row.** WP-24: Arena: task families, scorecards, the design corpus and benchmark. Crates: `frob-arena`.
Milestone: M3. Key gates: GATE-ARENA-01, -02, -07, -09. Waits for: WP-21, WP-23.

Arena fixes what a design is asked to do and how the answer is counted. The package delivers the task families of M2
(velocity tracking, terrain traversal, with reference-robot scorecards) and of M3 (interior navigation, payload
transport, low-friction crossing, tight-space maneuvering); requirement verdicts on batched tiers by streaming
past-time monitors (`task.verdict`, `task.settle`, schema `frob.verdict/1`); metrics, scorecard accounting and the
batteries `standard`, `full`, `hard` and `covering`; the design corpus of about 200 designs with frozen labels,
planted defects, planted winners and planted single causes; the requirement-writing test with a fresh agent; and the
pilot of a requirement-derived reward term.

It is one unit of work because a scorecard is honest only when the task, the verdict and the denominator are defined
together: a planted infeasible scenario must appear in its column and in the all-scenario denominator, and a silently
shortened battery must change identity.

**Order.** It waits for WP-21 (trained reference policies and the environment that tasks run in) and WP-23 (the
interiors the M3 task families are set in). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-SPEC-REWARD** (decided M3). Trigger: In the training pilot the arm with the added requirement term does not
  improve success over the shaped reward across five seeds. Fallback, named in advance: Requirements stay acceptance
  metrics and search scores only; the added-term option leaves the task schema; margin-only training is offered only
  if it reaches 80% of the shaped arm's success.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-FOIL: detection below 60%, or findings on
  more than 30% of clean tasks, or the sampling controller on the actual design finds the same loopholes at the same
  cost; fallback, the in-campaign check on winning episodes only, with `task.audit` running the actual design alone.
  KILL-ITEMBANK: a factor under 2 for the 20 closest
  pairs, or coverage more than 2 points below nominal, or held-out prediction no better than per-item means; fallback,
  stratified sampling by interior archetype with proportional allocation. KILL-EXPODOMAIN (not KILL-EXPOSURE, which is
  a sensor gate): under a factor of 2 in contrast variance on the held-out battery, or an automatic coupling choice
  that fails its test; fallback, the keyed random streams as they stand with explicit per-stream adapters, the four
  coupling checks kept as a test.
- **The corpus is frozen before it is used.** The hash of the label file is committed before any analysis is tuned,
  and a relabel changes the corpus identity; otherwise GATE-ATEL-01 and GATE-REFLEX-02 would be tuned to their own
  labels.
- BET-24 (a requirement language) is decided by GATE-ARENA-03 (WP-41), GATE-ARENA-09 (here) and GATE-CORTEX-10 (the
  streaming monitors).
