## Background

**The row.** WP-25: Atelier: compare, explain, promote, detect. Crates: `frob-opt`, `frob-learn`. Milestone: M3. Key
gates: GATE-ATEL-01, -04; GATE-FID-02; GATE-CORTEX-04. Waits for: WP-22, WP-24.

The studio loop turns on four judgments, and each must be safe against the way an agent actually works: looking
repeatedly and stopping when it likes. The package delivers the anytime-valid paired comparison on devices
(`eval.compare`, `study.compare`, `explain.compare`), the trust ladder's promotion rule with the DC-01 table
(`eval.promote`), the exploitation detectors (`eval.detect`), validation of static analysis on the design corpus, and
the reports `report.design`, `report.run`, `report.campaign` and `report.mission`.

It is one unit of work because the four judgments share their statistics and their evidence: a comparison's type-I
error must hold under optional stopping, a promotion must catch a planted tier-sensitive policy, and a detector must
flag its planted exploit and pass an honest policy.

**Order.** It waits for WP-22 (the fidelity ledger and replay that promotion stands on) and WP-24 (tasks, scorecards
and the corpus that comparisons and the static-analysis validation run on). (The order between packages is recorded in
`related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-STATIC** (decided M3). Trigger: GATE-ATEL-01 fails (BET-05). Fallback, named in advance: `design check`
  verdicts are downgraded to advisory lints and the pruning claim is withdrawn.
- **BET-05 (static analysis)** is decided here: area under the ROC curve at least 0.8 and at least 0.05 above the
  heuristic baseline at a 95% paired lower bound, with planted defective designs flagged and a planted sound design
  passed.
- The bisect row of GATE-ATEL-04 (the planted edit found in at least 95 of 100 trials) is due at M4 and closes in
  WP-28.
- A comparison between arms of different device families, or with an arm trained under class D4, is refused as a
  paired comparison (GATE-SPARK-17, in WP-58).
