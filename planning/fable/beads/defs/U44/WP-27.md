## Background

**The row.** WP-27: Reflex II: baselines for every archetype, the sampling controller, skill artifacts [S], [F].
Crates: `frob-control`. Milestone: M3, M4. Key gates: GATE-REFLEX-02, -04, -05, -06, -08. Waits for: WP-12, WP-15.

The baseline controller is the cheap rung between static analysis and training: if a design can be made to walk or
roll without learning, most designs never need a trained policy (BET-06), and where no baseline exists a
sampling-based predictive controller is the fallback (BET-27). The package delivers baseline controllers for every
archetype (multi-legged, biped balance, steered, omnidirectional, tracked, hybrid, arm on a base), validation of
baseline synthesis on the design corpus, control programs with layer records, infeasibility certificates and fallback
events, the sampling controller (`control.mpc`; `K-fork`, `K-cost`, `K-weight`), an inspectable baseline plus a
learned residual (`control.ladder`), and skill artifacts `.fskill` with their exact layer (`skill.compile`,
`skill.check`, `skill.show`).

It is one unit of work because these are the rungs of one ladder inside one crate, and they are judged against one
reference: agreement in rank with trained specialists, with thresholds fixed before data.

**Order.** It waits for WP-12 (the filter and the first baselines) and WP-15 (the batched step: corpus validation and
the sampling controller's forks need a batch). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`:
a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:

What lands at each milestone:
- **M2.** The pilots on record first: Frontier baseline routes I (N22c-05), the sampling-controller pilot (N22a-22,
  BET-27) and the residual pilot (N22a-27), whose verdicts fund or block the M3 and M4 mechanisms.
- **M3.** Baselines for all eighteen archetypes except tracked and hybrid; control programs; the sampling controller
  as a fallback; GATE-REFLEX-02 and -05; GATE-REFLEX-04 for the legged and wheeled classes; the decision
  KILL-BASELINE.
- **M4.** Tracked and hybrid baselines with their contact laws; the sampling controller at its gate (GATE-REFLEX-06);
  the residual rung; the skill exact layer (GATE-REFLEX-08); GATE-REFLEX-04 for tracked and hybrid; the decisions
  KILL-MPC and KILL-RESIDUAL.

Out of scope: gait orbits and continuation (WP-64); the energy-budget tier and runtime shields (WP-65); symmetric
controllers (WP-29); skill transport with learned residuals and reachable-set tables, which are Moonshot items
(WP-79).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The plan's envelope table registers no request for this package; two of its pilots need more than the
  default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device), and the envelope register (N44-01) reserves a row for each, with
  `packages = ["WP-27"]`, filed by the pilot's bead before its first run above E0. Row **R25**, filed by N22a-22 (the
  sampling-controller pilot): class E2, scope `campaign:pilot-reflex-mpc`, the pilot's own ceiling of 15
  device-hours written in the row, for the ten specialists; if not approved, the E0 form: the 90 episodes are run and
  recorded, no specialist is trained, the rank clause is not evaluable, and the verdict is `fallback` if the success
  or cost clause holds and `undetermined` otherwise (BD-252, N22a-23 and N22c-30 stay unfunded, and N22c-16 records
  the fallback in force). Row **R26**, filed by N22a-27 (the residual pilot) only if its smoke run shows a training
  run longer than one hour (otherwise the row is never filed and the identifier is not reused): class E1, scope
  `gate:KILL-RESIDUAL`, at most 2 hours per run and 12 device-hours in all; if not approved, the pilot is closed
  `withdrawn` (`not run: no envelope`, citing R26), N22a-16 and BD-330 are not funded, and N22c-15 records the
  fallback in force. Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval is recorded on the row in
  `registries/compute_envelope.toml`; a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-BASELINE** (decided M3). Trigger: GATE-REFLEX-02 fails (BET-06). Fallback, named in advance: Baseline
  synthesis is limited to the morphology classes that pass; others report `not-synthesizable` and route to training.
- **KILL-MPC** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-REFLEX-06 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: The
  sampling controller is kept as a single-world CPU debugging aid; demonstrations come from the baseline and from
  specialists.
- **KILL-RESIDUAL** (decided M4). Trigger: The pilot's falsifier holds, or at M4 the residual rung needs more than 50%
  of a specialist's steps on the corpus. Fallback, named in advance: The residual rung is removed; the baseline
  remains a behaviour-cloning warm start.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-MPCRECORD (delivered-controller lane): more
  than 10% of delivered-controller verdicts reverse at the referee, or no named computer meets the deadline in 99% of
  ticks for any class on the pilot set. Fallback: the sampling controller is used for screening and fallback only, and
  a mission that requires it returns `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable` with its screening episodes.
- **One pair of thresholds for rank agreement everywhere**: a Kendall τ lower 95% bound of at least 0.7 lets a rung
  select, at least 0.5 lets it discard the bottom half, and below 0.5 it removes outright failures only
  (`baseline-only: unranked`).
- The sampling controller's cost thresholds are those of BET-27: a median of at most 1 GPU-minute per 20 s quadruped
  episode and 6 per humanoid episode; its digest must not change with the fork count.
- GATE-REFLEX-02 needs its potency control: every corpus design with a planted control-relevant defect must fail.
