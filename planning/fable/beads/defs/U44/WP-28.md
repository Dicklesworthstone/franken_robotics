## Background

**The row.** WP-28: Optimization, lineage, experiment suggestion and the surface A/B [S], [F]. Crates: `frob-opt`.
Milestone: M4. Key gates: NS-06; GATE-ATEL-03, -15; GATE-TETHER-13. Waits for: WP-25.

This package is where the studio becomes a search. It delivers optimization and racing (`opt.start`, `opt.status`),
lineage with bisect and merge (`design.bisect`; the lineage graph, `design.diff` and `design.blame` land at M1 with
the design language, in WP-07), registered predictions (`predict.register`, `predict.report`), experiment suggestion
ranked by value of information (`experiment.suggest`), the one-call pipeline `solve.run`, the mission session with a
fresh agent, and the pre-registered A/B of agent surfaces on the design benchmark.

Its North-Star scenario is NS-06: an agent runs thirty design-check-simulate-explain-edit cycles on a wheel-leg hybrid
in one hour on `trj`; each cycle dispatches a family of at least ten variants in one batch, so at least three hundred
designs are decided in the hour; every design version is content-addressed, every comparison is a declared study, and
`frob design bisect` finds the edit that broke stair climbing. The unit of the story is designs decided per hour: the
cycle count alone is worth only 3.0 to 3.5 times against an incumbent quadruped loop.

It is one unit of work because the surface A/B (BET-10, structure for agents) measures exactly these verbs in an
agent's hands, so they must land and be measured together.

**Order.** It waits for WP-25: optimization races candidates by declared comparisons, and bisect and predictions cite
promoted results. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block
every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request R8 of the envelope register (shared with WP-62): Design campaigns for the cascade and the surface A/B. Class
  asked: E2 per campaign. If not approved: Campaigns sized to the default, with fewer briefs and wider intervals.
  Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval is recorded on row R8 of
  `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts
  (`FROB-REF-761`). GATE-ATEL-03 run on the fallback states the smaller brief count and its wider interval, and is not
  reported as the registered-size result.
- **KILL-VOI** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-ATEL-15 fails. Fallback, named in advance: Value of information is a stop
  rule only; `experiment.suggest` lists proposals unranked by value.
- **KILL-SOLVE** (decided M4). Trigger: The ceiling of §32.6.2 is under 1.4-fold, or agents with `solve.run` do no
  better than agents driving the verbs. Fallback, named in advance: The prompts, that is the MCP workflow prompts
  (`studio_loop`, `compare_designs`, `mission_query`), which describe the same sequence as guidance; agents drive the
  verbs. The decision has two clauses: the paper ceiling, read before `solve.run` is funded (N32ap1-01; if it fires,
  the verb is never built), and the agent comparison, the second arm of GATE-TETHER-13.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-VISIBILITY (sensor mounts as optimizer
  variables): the gate of the mount optimizer fails on both counts, or the pilot attributes under a fifth of
  estimated-state failures to visibility or geometry. Fallback: `sense.visibility` stays a report and a stage-7 check,
  and mounts are not optimizer variables.
- **BET-10 has no kill gate**: if the A/B shows no effect, the surface is reduced to the verbs that showed one.
- KILL-FDGRAD (paired design gradients) is owned by the Phylum chapter and carried by WP-63; if it fires, optimization
  here uses rank-based search (CMA-ES, racing).
- No instruction taken from a poisoned import may be followed in the mission session (GATE-TETHER-13).
