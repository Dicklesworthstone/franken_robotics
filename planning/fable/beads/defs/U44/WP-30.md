## Background

**The row.** WP-30: Wheels, tracks, hybrids, moving surfaces, soil [S], [F]. Crates: `frob-contact`, `frob-analyze`.
Milestone: M4. Key gates: GATE-TRAC-08; known answers of §18.16. Waits for: WP-16, WP-11.

Not every archetype walks: wheeled, tracked and hybrid bodies need contact laws of their own. The package delivers
tyre laws, tracks and wheel-leg hybrids on all tiers, soil mechanics (plate sinkage, drawbar pull, ruts as per-world
mutable state; `contact.soil`), the contact response batch with per-column bounds and reroutes (`contact.response`),
and moving surfaces with the task families of M4: conveyors, stair and curb climbing for wheels and tracks, reach and
carry.

It is one unit of work because these laws share the contact crate, the closed forms they are checked against, and a
consequence: GATE-REFLEX-01 closes for the tracked and hybrid archetypes only when their contact laws exist.

**Order.** It waits for WP-16 (batched contact, into which the laws are added as rows and cards) and WP-11 (the
maneuverability and static analyses that the wheeled and tracked archetypes are screened by). (The order between
packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry
the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-contact` (layer 3; Solid, Frontier; cap 22): Collision geometry, detection, contact laws (convex compliant,
  the four-dimensional cone, joint rows, film friction, the hard-contact law), wheels, tracks, terrain mechanics.
- `frob-analyze` (layer 4; Solid; cap 20): Static analyses and preflight.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M4.** The laws on all tiers, soil, the response batch and the M4 task families; GATE-TRAC-08; the contact known
  answers for wheels, tracks and soil; the decisions KILL-SOIL, KILL-RESPONSE and KILL-TYRE.

Out of scope: the contact laws of the remaining archetypes that land at M3 with the archetypes (`belt`, `skid`, `pad`,
`roller-wheel`, `wheel-rolling`; WP-38); soft soil at scale, link-chain tracks and granular media, which are M6 items
(WP-74); a lifted planning model for a tracked base on soil (WP-33).

structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).

structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.

Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-SOIL** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-TRAC-08 fails after one repair cycle, or no soil card can be sourced with
  provenance. Fallback, named in advance: A rigid floor with rolling resistance and a drag card labeled `assumed`;
  soft-ground results labeled exploratory.
- **KILL-RESPONSE** (decided M4). Trigger: Mode guards reroute more than half the columns on battery states; or (the
  second trigger, §18.19) a bound violation or a false mode acceptance that survives one repair cycle. Fallback, named
  in advance: Full solves per query.
- **KILL-TYRE** (entered in the gate register by amendment, N40-24; decided at M4 by N18bp4-02, at the latest at the
  M4 exit). Trigger: the tyre-law pilot's falsifier holds (N18b-03: no pair of the at least 12 designs of the vehicle
  corpus swaps order against a rigid wheel with a paired difference resolved at the 5% level under both arms, 64
  worlds per design per arm), or the tyre rows of GATE-TRAC-08 fail after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in
  advance: rigid wheels with the friction cone and a rolling-resistance row; the tyre laws N18b-04 close with reason
  `fallback`. Funding of N18b-04 is read from the pilot record alone.
- **Ruts are per-world mutable state.** Rut leak across resets must be zero over 10³ resets, or a world inherits
  another world's terrain and batch-composition invariance is lost.
- **KILL-RUT** (entered in the gate register by dated amendment at M4, by N38a-37 in WP-14, before the rut field
  N18b-17 of this package is funded; decided at M6 by N38a-38 under the epic BD-417, from the pilot N18b-16 of this
  package and the batch-scale measurement N38a-29). Trigger: the pilot's falsifier holds (a leak above zero, a marked
  world's digest that differs from its solo run, or a planted leak that goes undetected), or its funding flags stand
  (more than 256 KiB per world on its fixture, or a paired lower bound of the step-cost ratio above 1.25), or at batch
  scale any leak in 10³ resets on a claimed backend, rut buffers that break the fixed launch shape or the determinism
  class of a batch, or a paired lower bound above 1.25 of the step-cost ratio with ruts on against ruts off on the
  reference tracked workload. Fallback, named in advance: persistent deformation off; terrain resets each episode and
  sinkage is computed per step from the soil law with no memory.
- A soil card with no provenance cannot be used for a claim; if no card can be sourced, KILL-SOIL's fallback (a rigid
  floor with rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`) applies and soft-ground results are labelled
  exploratory.
- The second trigger of KILL-RESPONSE (a bound violation or a false mode acceptance surviving one repair cycle) is
  measured by a later gate of the contact chapter (GATE-TRAC-11, §18.19, M4).
