## Background

**The row.** WP-32: Real-place ingestion [F]. Crates: `frob-scene`, `frob-conformance`, `frob-analyze`. Milestone: M5.
Key gates: GATE-HAB-03. Waits for: WP-23, WP-22.

Generated interiors are proxies; this package admits a real one. It delivers real-place ingestion (`scene.import`,
`scene.fit`) through the schema adapter of the drone-reconstruction lane (INT-FDGR-01), with a scale label, coverage
masks that become the scene's unknown-space label, privacy inheritance, and at least one probe request emitted for the
place; and asset-populated interiors with their licences, if their pilot funds them.

It is one unit of work because a real place is a Reality claim with its own failure modes (no metric scale, unseen
regions, private content, restricted licences), and all of them are handled in the one lane that admits the place.

**Order.** It waits for WP-23 (the interior grammar, which is also the fallback representation: a surveyed proxy
authored in the grammar) and WP-22 (an ingested place is used only through the trust ladder and replay, like any other
scene). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child,
so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-scene` (layer 2; Solid, Frontier; cap 28): Scene packs, the scene compiler, terrain and interior generators,
  import and acceptance; optical and fixture properties; clearance complexes; the retrofit catalog; living-world
  state.
- `frob-conformance` (layer 7; Solid; cap 24): The Gauntlet harness, oracle fixtures, known answers, the
  counterexample catalogue, the ten-fold harness.
- `frob-analyze` (layer 4; Solid; cap 20): Static analyses and preflight.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M5.** The import and fit verbs, the probe request, asset-populated interiors where funded; GATE-HAB-03;
  INT-FDGR-01 for scan import (its schema fixtures are due at M3); the decisions KILL-INGEST and KILL-DRESSING.

Out of scope: the private mark, licence joins and `scene.purge` themselves (WP-58); probe and instrument design
(WP-59); as-built ingestion of a robot, which is a Ground item (WP-68).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M5: BD-398 Real-place ingestion (`scene.import`, `scene.fit`); INT-FDGR-01; BD-399 Asset-populated interiors with
  their licences
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The package is Frontier. Pilots on
record first: inspect one twin package for landmarks, a scale witness and coverage; and a paper-scale test of scene
dressing (populated interiors, kinematic actors) against the structural proxies, which decides whether dressing
exposes any failure class the proxies do not.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-INGEST** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-HAB-03 cannot pass with a scale label of `witnessed` or better after one
  repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: The place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey,
  and the pack says it is a proxy.
- **KILL-DRESSING** (decided M5). Trigger: A dressing item breaks the fixed launch shape or the determinism class of a
  batch, adds more than 25% to step cost, or exposes no failure class beyond the proxies on the M5 battery. Fallback,
  named in advance: Structural proxies and static clutter only.
- **RISK-12**: a scan of a private place leaks through a still, a fixture or policy weights. Trigger: a derived
  artifact without the private mark. Mitigation: inheritance of the private mark, the export flag, `scene.purge`,
  GATE-SAFE-04; weights inherit the mark by default.
- **No capture, no pass.** A place for which no capture exists is recorded as `no-claim`. A geometry generation whose
  scale status is not metric is refused.
- OPEN-HAB-5: which reconstruction package and version is the import contract. Default: the walkthrough twin package,
  and the drone route when it publishes geometry.
