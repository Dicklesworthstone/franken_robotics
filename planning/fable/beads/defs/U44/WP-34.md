## Background

**The row.** WP-34: Incumbent phase ledger and parity baseline. Crates: `frob-conformance`, `tools/oracle`. Milestone:
M0, M1. Key gates: GATE-SPARK-21. Waits for: WP-01.

The program's thesis is a ratio, and a ratio needs a measured denominator: the incumbent is measured before a
mechanism is funded against it. The package delivers the incumbent harness, the parity baseline on `trj` (the phase
ledger for cold start, physics and full step on workloads Q and H, time to the return threshold on one and two cards,
and edit to first step for a parameter edit and a topology edit), the completion of the baseline on this Mac (CPU
MuJoCo at 1, 24 and 36 threads; an incumbent with a Metal path) and at native settings on `trj`, and the incumbent
design-loop phase ledger: one agent-driven round timed by phase, cold and warm separated, with the ceilings of the
ten-fold arithmetic recomputed from the measured shares.

It is one unit of work because the phase ledger is one instrument read at two milestones: the rows that need no agent
at M0, and the loop with the common agent at M1.

**Order.** It waits for WP-01 only: the harness drives incumbents out of process and needs nothing of our engine. (The
order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child
beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-conformance` (layer 7; Solid; cap 24): The Gauntlet harness, oracle fixtures, known answers, the
  counterexample catalogue, the ten-fold harness.
- `tools/oracle/`: the incumbent harness as an out-of-process oracle: pinned revisions, the strongest warm
  configurations, and the disclosure block on every figure. No incumbent enters a production dependency graph.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** The harness and the `trj` rows of GATE-SPARK-21 that need no agent; these are what the parity-baseline gate
  GATE-TENFOLD-01 reads. If that gate is not met, M0 does not exit and no package is funded as a speed lever.
- **M1.** GATE-SPARK-21 complete, including this Mac; the incumbent design loop with closed phase ledgers
  (GATE-TENFOLD-02), which replaces the published table of the ten-fold arithmetic by measured rows.

Out of scope: our own performance harness and parity rows (WP-47); the rank-agreement pilot and the partial ten-fold
gate (WP-60); the tournament's rival arms (WP-76).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01). GATE-TENFOLD-09 (ceiling before speed, program-wide) is first recorded here at
M1 on the rival's ledger and recurs at every exit: a package funded as a speed lever cites a removable share of at
least 10%.

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request R1 of the envelope register (shared with WP-47): Incumbent and parity measurements on both cards and on all
  cores. Class asked: E1 per gate (GATE-SPARK-21 here). If not approved: Single-card and eight-core rows only; the other
  rows are recorded `not run`. Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval is recorded on row R1 of
  `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01); a job above the class in force, including an incumbent arm launched out
  of process with its declared demand, is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`). Because R1 is first needed at M0,
  its ruling is part of the M0 exit; the rows of GATE-SPARK-21 and GATE-TENFOLD-01 that need two cards or more than
  eight cores read `not run` with the envelope reason until it is approved. OPEN (owner, at the M0 exit, with
  OPEN-SPARK-2): whether GATE-TENFOLD-01 with its two-card rows `not run` lets M0 exit. Fail-closed default: those
  rows count as missing, not as met; no figure, ratio or speed-lever funding rests on a two-card row until it is
  measured; the single-card rows are recorded and read as such.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER** (decided M1 on the rival's ledger; M2 on ours). Trigger: A mechanism's removable cost is
  under 10% of the measured loop. Fallback, named in advance: It is not funded as a speed lever; it may be funded for
  trust, coverage or correctness with that reason written and no speed figure.
- **Stop decision of M0**: if the measured ledger puts the ceilings of the ten-fold arithmetic under 3 for every
  mission class, that arithmetic is rewritten before any work is funded against it.
- **OPEN-PERF-2**: which incumbent revisions and configurations are the comparators. Default: the revisions in the
  source register, frozen before any paired run.
- A figure without a complete disclosure block is refused; cold and warm runs are never mixed in one row.
