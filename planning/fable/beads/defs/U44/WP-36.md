## Background

**The row.** WP-36: Claim product, flags and the offline auditor. Crates: `frob-core`, `frob-cert` (binary
`frob-verify`). Milestone: M0, M1. Key gates: GATE-LEMMA-06, -03; GATE-WS-03; GATE-SAFE-01, -08. Waits for: WP-01.

Every result carries a claim, and seven kinds of claim are never conflated: Proof, Known answer, Parity, Fidelity,
Design comparison, Reality, Performance. The types that enforce this live in `frob-core` and are built with the
workspace (WP-01): the evidence classes, the claim-product type (the envelope's class is the meet of its inputs, and
no conversion from a product to one of its factors compiles) and the no-authorization flags (three fields, all false,
with no setter). This package holds their acceptance, and it delivers in `frob-cert` the independent auditor: a
canonical JSON reader, BLAKE3, exact integer and rational arithmetic, outward-rounded intervals, the checkers of the
instance-certificate proof kinds and of the mission proof kinds (`lp-primal`, `lp-farkas`, `finite-game`,
`alias-pair`, `partition`, `interval-box`, `trace-witness`), and the binary `frob-verify`.

It is one unit of work because the type that says how far a result may be trusted and the program that checks a
certificate without trusting anything are two halves of one guarantee, and both must exist before the first verb
answers.

**Order.** It waits for WP-01 only. `frob-cert` has an empty dependency table and its own stable toolchain file, so it
builds alone. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every
child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-core` (layer 0; Solid; cap 8): Identities and hashing; evidence classes and the claim-product type; refusals;
  schema versions and format-registry types; artifact-graph identifiers; the `qty` re-export; the no-authorization
  flags.
- `frob-cert` (layer 6; Solid; cap 12): The certificate bundle reader and checkers; the `frob-verify` binary; standard
  library only.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** GATE-LEMMA-06 on the `frob-core` types (compile-fail tests, and the three flags, all false, on every
  artifact the conformance suite exports); `frob-cert` with the reader, the arithmetic and the first checkers.
- **M1.** The checkers of the mission proof kinds; GATE-LEMMA-03 (one accepted specimen and at least three rejected
  mutations per proof kind); GATE-WS-03 (the auditor's independence); GATE-SAFE-01 and -08.

Out of scope: certificate production and bundle writing (WP-05, WP-42); the flags on every exporting verb and the
claim product at every verb boundary, which are re-tested at M3 over the full surface (WP-58); replay of qualification
campaigns by the auditor (WP-31); the checker-independence campaigns (WP-42, WP-59).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **Independence is structural.** `frob-cert` forbids unsafe code at the crate root, has no dependency outside the
  standard library, shares no source file with any proposer, and only `frob-verbs`, `frob-cli` and `frob-conformance`
  may depend on it.
- GATE-SAFE-01 recurs at every milestone exit after its first record.
- KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT (standing from M1, carried by WP-42) bears on this crate: one certificate accepted by
  `frob-verify` and shown false removes its proof kind if the rule, not the implementation, is at fault.
- Build packets carry a fourth flag, `fabrication_released`; the flag test covers every flag an artifact kind carries.
