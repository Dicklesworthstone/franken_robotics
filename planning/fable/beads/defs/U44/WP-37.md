## Background

**The row.** WP-37: Worker fallback, file-by-file admission tests, the plan-time pilots. Crates: `frob-fsim` (binary
`frob-fsim-worker`), `worker/`; `frob-units` only if forced. Milestone: M0. Key gates: INT-FSIM-02 to -11,
INT-FSIM-18; INT-FNP-01, -02. Waits for: WP-02.

An admission is never inherited: a foundation is used above the airlock only after its own test has a record at the
pin of that day. The package delivers the first admission tests of the `fs-ga` Lie façades and of `fs-mbd` articulated
dynamics; the foundation-defect admissions found at plan time (the `planar_drive` near-straight case with an
independent chord formula, three probes of the sibling's PPO update, the fixed-witness sustained step and the contact
capacity through the airlock, the typed stride converter at the interchange boundary); the worker-process fallback
exercised before it is needed; the upstream work items opened as beads labelled `upstream` and mirrored in the sibling
trackers; and the plan-time pilots re-run in this repository and recorded.

It is one unit of work because all of it protects one boundary: nothing above the airlock may rest on a sibling
behaviour that has not been tested from this side, with a planted failure that the test catches.

**Order.** It waits for WP-02: the airlock crate and the pinned stack that the tests exercise. (The order between
packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry
the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-fsim` (layer 0; Solid; cap 20): The airlock: adapters to FrankenSim's volatile crates in feature groups; the
  `remote` mode and the worker binary.
- `worker/`: the workspace manifest that re-targets `frob-fsim` as the binary `frob-fsim-worker` (the `remote` mode of
  the airlock). It builds on T, except when the fallback keeps the worker at an older pin and it builds on that pin's
  toolchain.
- `frob-units` (conditional; layer 0; cap 3): exists only if the toolchain gate forces it.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M0.** The admission tests, the worker fallback with INT-FSIM-18, and the pilots re-run: the stance enumeration,
  the docking grid, the pusher battery, the reachable-verdict enumeration of the fixed-n rule, the boundary screen in
  guarded single precision, the ceiling of the solver comparison and the power of the draft decision rule.

Out of scope: admission tests that are measured where the foundation is first used and therefore sit in other
packages: INT-FSIM-08 with the collision primitives (WP-10), INT-FSIM-09 with the actuator port models (WP-09),
INT-FSIM-10 with the analyses that first use it (A-09, A-14 and A-16, at M3), INT-FSIM-11 with the one-source kernel
test (WP-04), and the second half of INT-FSIM-07, under witness migration (WP-39).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):

  Foundation-defect admission INT-FSIM-05: the `planar_drive` near-straight case, and the …; BD-034 Foundation-defect
  admission INT-FSIM-06: three PPO probes …; BD-035 Foundation-defect admission INT-FSIM-04 and INT-FSIM-07 (refusal
  half): the fixed-witness …; BD-036 Foundation-defect admission INT-FNP-01 and INT-FNP-02: the typed stride converter
  at the …; BD-037 The upstream work items of Appendix F opened as beads labelled `upstream` in this graph and …;
  BD-038 INT-FSIM-18: the worker-process fallback exercised before it is needed …; BD-041 The plan-time pilot for the
  cart docking closed form, re-run from the reproduction bundle with …; BD-042 The plan-time pilot for the static
  stance family, re-run from the reproduction bundle; BD-043 The remaining plan-time pilots of §25.22, re-run and
  recorded …
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Upstream work.** Every upstream work item is a bead labelled `upstream` inside the package whose gate its
switch-over rule re-runs, done in the sibling's repository under that repository's rules. This package holds the bead
that opens them and mirrors them in the sibling trackers; each carries a priority, an interim posture here and a
switch-over rule, so no milestone waits on a sibling change landing.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The mechanisms here are Solid. The
pilots re-run here are the plan-time pilots: they were run at paper scale before this plan was written and are
reproduced in the repository so that their counts and timing bands become records. Every Frontier package that cites
one of them depends on its re-run.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every child bead is closed with a typed reason citing its own test and that test's output; no close reason is shared; no child is closed by the session that implemented it.
- [ ] Every gate below has a row in `registries/gates.toml` (commit, command, output, receipt hash) with status `pass`, or `killed: fallback adopted` with its ADR. `not run` is not a pass. Thresholds are TARGETs until measured.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-02 (admission of frankensim `fs-ga`: group façades, twist and wrench conventions; first needed M0): Power ⟨wrench, twist⟩ is frame-invariant to 10⁻¹² relative; the analytic Jacobian agrees with central differences to 10⁻⁷ relative at 1,000 seeded configurations; a deliberately reversed adjoint fails at least three fixtures.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-03 (admission of frankensim `fs-mbd`: tree kinematics, articulated-body solve, rigid-point motion; first needed M0): A two-joint arm's accelerations equal those from independently differentiated energy to 10⁻¹⁰; an offset inertial sensor's acceleration equals the rigid-point formula; scalar genericity, adjoint support and contact inputs recorded.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-04 (admission of frankensim `fs-mbd` contact steps and `fs-contact` broad phase; first needed M0): A thin step lying between two clear endpoint poses is detected on 1,000 of 1,000 seeded sweeps; a set of 22 contacts returns a typed refusal.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-05 (admission of frankensim `fs-mbd::planar_drive` and the robot catalog builders; first needed M0): Exact straight travel, the near-zero-curvature case of §33.11 row 2, wrapped against unwrapped headings, and incremental against one-shot integration to 10⁻¹². Until it passes, `planar_drive` is not an oracle.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-06 (admission of frankensim `fs-g1-train` (nothing linked: an update rule to compare against); first needed M0): The three probes of §33.11 row 1: the four combinations of advantage sign and clip side against finite differences to 10⁻³; the critic gradient against the current forward output; a packed sequence against separate replays, ratio 1 to 10⁻⁶. Until it passes upstream, neither file is a baseline.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-07 (admission of frankensim sustained contact step; first needed M0 (the refusal); M1 (under witness migration)): The flat-to-edge-to-top transition returns the typed refusal through the airlock at the pin, and completes under witness migration with the energy ledger closed to its stated residual (§33.11 row 4).
- [ ] INT-FSIM-08 (admission of frankensim `fs-scene`, `fs-query`: support and keep-out boxes, convex queries; first needed M0): A box-on-plane support query equals the closed form. A mesh-backed interior is not admitted by this test.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-09 (admission of frankensim `fs-phs`, `fs-tribo`, `fs-matdb`: ports, friction laws, material data; first needed M0): Power across an interconnection sums to zero to 10⁻¹² relative; every pair parameter returns with its provenance record; a missing material pair is a refusal.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-10 (admission of frankensim `fs-symmetry`, `fs-ad`: cyclic cases, implicit sensitivities, gradient checks; first needed M0): The implicit sensitivity of a 2×2 fixed point equals its closed form. Recorded scope: cyclic groups only, no contact-event derivatives.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-11 (admission of frankensim `fs-tilelang` and the instrument-lab kernels (pattern only); first needed M0): No dependency is added. One dialect source compiles through both preludes and matches its CPU twin (the test of GATE-SPARK-00).
- [ ] INT-FSIM-18 (admission of frankensim worker-process fallback; first needed M0): A 2,000-step G1 referee rollout submitted through the worker returns chunk digests equal, bit for bit, to the same job run in process; a worker killed mid-job and resubmitted returns the same result (§33.14).
- [ ] INT-FNP-01 (admission of franken_numpy; first needed M0): An array file written through `fnp-io` reads back identically.
- [ ] INT-FNP-02 (admission of franken_numpy and frankentorch host tensor layouts; first needed M0): The stride conversion at the interchange boundary reads element 3, not element 12, on the 2×2 case (§33.11 row 3).
- [ ] Each crate above ships `CONTRACT.md` with its ten sections and `contract.toml`; `xtask check-contracts` reports its line count under 115% of its cap.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **Until INT-FSIM-05 passes, `planar_drive` is not an oracle**; until INT-FSIM-06 passes upstream, the sibling's PPO
  files are not a baseline; INT-FNP-02 must read element 3, not element 12, on the 2×2 case.
- The plan-time pilots must reproduce every count exactly and every timing ratio inside its recorded band (a Gauntlet
  gate at M0); a pilot that reproduces a different count invalidates the mechanisms scoped by it.
- KILL-FSIM (WP-02) names this package's worker as its fallback (a), which is why the worker is exercised at M0: a
  2,000-step G1 referee rollout through the worker must return chunk digests equal bit for bit to the in-process run,
  and a worker killed mid-job and resubmitted must return the same result.
