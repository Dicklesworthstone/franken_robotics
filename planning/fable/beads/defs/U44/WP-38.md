## Background

**The row.** WP-38: Modules, archetypes and preflight. Crates: `frob-design`, `frob-model`, `frob-analyze`,
`frob-algebra`. Milestone: M1, M3. Key gates: GATE-SKEL-03, -04, -06, -07; GATE-MARROW-03. Waits for: WP-07, WP-11.

A design is assembled from modules, started from an archetype, and refused early when it cannot work. The package
delivers the module format `.fmodule` with its port contract (mechanical, electrical, thermal, signal and contact
ports; parameters; `module.list`, `module.show`, `module.check`), `module.substitute` with the invalidation contract
that says which compiled products survive a substitution, the eighteen archetypes (the first four at M1:
`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`; the remaining fourteen at M3) with the contact laws the later ones
need (`belt`, `skid`, `pad`, `roller-wheel`, `wheel-rolling`), and preflight as `design.check`: stages 1 to 6 with
aggregation, ranked repairs as typed edits and per-stage timing, then stages 7 (place) and 8 (information).

It is one unit of work because an archetype is accepted only through preflight and substitution: each archetype
compiles, passes stages 1 to 6 with no refusal, has its symmetry groups on file, and stands or rolls under its
baseline controller.

**Order.** It waits for WP-07 (the design language and the compiler) and WP-11 (the analyses that preflight
aggregates). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every
child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-SKEL-SUBST** (decided M1). Trigger: A reused product differs from its recompiled value after one repair
  cycle, or leaf-local reuse saves less than 20% of compile time on the archetypes. Fallback, named in advance: Every
  substitution is global; the memo graph keeps subterm reuse by content hash only.
- **KILL-SKEL-ALIAS** (decided M3). Trigger: Stage 8 returns a witness that a hand-built policy defeats, or decides
  fewer than half of the planted cases within budget. Fallback, named in advance: Stage 8 becomes an advisory report
  built on A-14 coverage and refuses nothing.
- Of 100 seeded defects (at least two per refusal code) all must be refused and at least 95 by the stage and code that
  own them, with zero refusals on the eighteen archetypes and on 10³ random valid designs.
- The mass of each archetype must lie within 15% of the mass on file for its reference robot where one exists
  (GATE-SKEL-07).
- Γ_mech and Γ_obs must equal the groups on file for all eighteen archetypes and for 24 planted asymmetries, with the
  correct breaker named every time and zero disagreements (GATE-MARROW-03).
