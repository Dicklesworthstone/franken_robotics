## Background

**The row.** WP-41: Requirement language, fixtures and the two study types. Crates: `frob-spec`, `frob-arena`.
Milestone: M1. Key gates: GATE-ARENA-03, -04, -05; GATE-ATEL-06. Waits for: WP-10, WP-13.

A result is only as good as the statement it is a verdict on, and the statistics that turn episodes into a verdict.
The package delivers the core of the requirement language in `frob-spec` (unit-normalized predicates, temporal
operators, three evaluators that must agree bit for bit), the task file `.ftask` with terms, requirements and
randomization (`task.new`, `task.show`, `task.list`, `task.check`, `task.verdict`), fixture 1 `fixture:cart-sill-dock`
with its bodies, motor, sill, analytic layer and creep rigs (`task.fixture`), the two study types on the CPU, the
anytime-valid e-process and the fixed-n exact study with its reachability pre-analysis (`study.plan`, `study.submit`,
`study.status`, `eval.preanalyze`), the task families of M1 (stand and recover, threshold delivery, timed docking),
and fixture 2 `fixture:quad-split-grip`.

It is one unit of work because a task, its requirement and the study that decides it are one contract (BET-24), and
because of a rule learned from a pilot: a frozen decision rule is enumerated before sampling. One pilot rule with 64
pairs could return only three of its four verdicts; the fourth was first reachable at 86 pairs.

**Order.** It waits for WP-10 (referee rollouts of the fixtures) and WP-13 (the traces and telemetry that monitors
evaluate). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every
child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- Each requirement-language refusal (FROB-REF-520, -535, -536, -523, -538, -525, -526, -539 and -528) must be
  triggered by its planted task file, and the three evaluators must agree on 10⁶ random traces per operator, including
  multi-rate signals and windows with rounding.
- The fixed-n planner must reproduce the enumeration at n = 64 and n = 86 exactly and refuse an unreachable verdict;
  FROB-REF-553, -555, -542, -543 and -556 each fire on their planted violation, 100 of 100.
- On fixture 1 the creep rigs must flip at their stated thresholds and both negative controls must be caught.
