## Background

**The row.** WP-42: First mission slice [F]. Crates: `frob-mission`, `frob-spec`, `frob-lemma`, `frob-cert`.
Milestone: M1. Key gates: GATE-AUGUR-01 to -07; GATE-LEMMA-04. Waits for: WP-36, WP-41.

The thesis says a mission can be answered for a whole family of designs at once, with a certificate, and that an
amendment withdraws exactly what it invalidates (BET-19). The first slice shows it where the answers are known
exactly, on the CPU, with no GPU and no learned component. The package delivers the mission schema `.fmission` and its
admission with the quantifier order echoed (`mission.new`, `mission.check`), the admission of a second linear-program
solver and of the interval and sum-of-squares airlock entries, the `frob-mission` core (inner and outer design sets,
cells, polarity, proof rules on finite and polyhedral fragments, plain proof objects), the docking family as a finite
game over causal histories with one velocity packet and its scenario-tree comparator, the stance family as body–load
feasibility with vertex witnesses and dual exclusions in lifted and projected form, the pusher battery, the
late-packet alias witness, the mission bundles and the verbs `prove.bundle`, `prove.verify`, `prove.list`,
`mission.synthesize`, `mission.refute`, `mission.distinguish` and `mission.show` with budget stops, the mutation
corpus with the checker-independence campaign, and the slice driven by a fresh agent through the CLI and MCP.

It is one unit of work because the claim is end to end: a query is admitted, answered, bundled, and accepted by
`frob-verify` built from an empty environment on a host with no GPU.

**Order.** It waits for WP-36 (the claim product and the checkers of the mission proof kinds) and WP-41 (`frob-spec`,
the docking fixture and the study types). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-mission` (layer 5; Frontier; cap 14): Mission queries, inner and outer design sets, region certificates, probe
  and experiment suggestion.
- `frob-spec` (layer 4; Solid; cap 8): The task and mission languages: terms, requirements, randomization,
  quantifiers.
- `frob-lemma` (layer 6; Solid, Frontier; cap 10): Claims, certificate production, bundle writing, theorem cards, the
  reflection route.
- `frob-cert` (layer 6; Solid; cap 12): The certificate bundle reader and checkers; the `frob-verify` binary; standard
  library only.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M1.** Everything above; GATE-AUGUR-01 to -07 and GATE-LEMMA-04, whose known answers include the stance boundary at
  exactly 3/14 m, the docking family's 561 of 561 cells, and the pusher battery's [1/2, 2] and ∅ for the same plant.
  KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT stands from this milestone on.

Out of scope: amendments, transport, separation and probes (WP-59); family queries on the GPU and region certificates
(WP-66); synthesis lanes (WP-67); the revalidation engine (WP-63); the re-runs of the plan-time pilots this slice was
scoped by (WP-37).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT** (decided Standing, from M1). Trigger: One certificate accepted by `frob-verify` is shown
  false within its scope. Fallback, named in advance: The procedure of §25.5.5; if the cause is the rule and not an
  implementation defect, the proof kind is removed and its cells become `Tested`.
- **A family certificate is implemented only when it travels**: in a `.frobcert` bundle that `frob-verify` accepts on
  a machine with neither the agent nor a GPU, and whose registered mutations it rejects. One accepted false
  certificate fails GATE-LEMMA-04.
- **The first reading of KILL-AUGUR-TIE** is taken on the docking slice: if a conventional scenario-tree program gives
  the better answer per engineering hour, that program ships behind the same verbs, schema and checker.
- **Stop decision of M1**: the mission chapter is re-planned before physics is added if a fresh agent cannot complete
  the docking and stance amendments of GATE-AUGUR-07 after one repair cycle, because the mapping from sensors and
  actuators to the query is then the defect.
- An unresolved cell is never reported as a verdict; a budget stop returns `unresolved` with what was decided.
