## Background

**The row.** WP-44: Phylum: variants, buckets, cohorts and the head-to-head [S], [F]. Crates: `frob-phylum`,
`frob-kernels`. Milestone: M2. Key gates: GATE-PHYLUM-02, -03, -04; GATE-KIN-06; GATE-SPARK-10. Waits for: WP-15,
WP-18, WP-19.

The first lever of the thesis is deciding many designs per turn, which needs many different bodies in one batch
(BET-18). The package delivers per-world parameter variants (level P-A: model, scene and task slots with a parameter
row), model variants inside padded capacities (level P-B: one model table per slot, masks, `K-variant-gather`), size
buckets and `phylum.plan`, variants on devices (parameter variants, topology variants on one captured graph, a mixed
batch of three body plans, admission of a variant into a free slot), admission and edit-to-first-step latency on the
warm, cool and cold paths (`phylum.sweep`), the control arm of the head-to-head (nine padded per-family templates with
lockable joints and per-design parameter tables), and the mixed-batch head-to-head experiment with arms A, B, C and D
as a fixed-n study, run and published with all four arms.

It is one unit of work because the cohort engine is kept or replaced by one experiment, and everything else in the
package is what that experiment compares.

**Order.** It waits for WP-15 (the batched step and the packed lowering that variants index into) and WP-18 and WP-19
(the devices on which the head-to-head is measured). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in
`deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:

  design-family buckets; the batch-independence test.
- `frob-kernels` (layer 3; Solid; cap 26): The kernel library: manifests, Rust skeletons, generated arithmetic, exact
  and guarded predicates.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M2.** Variants at levels P-A and P-B, buckets, the device paths, the latency measurements and the head-to-head;
  GATE-PHYLUM-02, -03 and -04; GATE-KIN-06; GATE-SPARK-10; the decisions KILL-PHYLUM and KILL-BUCKET. One bead lands
  at M1: the pilot with no GPU code (padding overhead and bucket count of the first archetypes and of 1,000 generated
  designs under the bucket set).

Out of scope: design-family lanes (level P-C), campaign quotienting, the reuse contract and the morphology generator
(WP-63); the quality-diversity archive and maps per dispatch (WP-62); specialized kernels for survivors (WP-46);
family policies trained on a generator (WP-61).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-44): The head-to-head on 512 topologies with four arms (OPEN-PHYLUM-3). Class asked: E1, repeated
  per arm. If not approved: KILL-PHYLUM is decided on the pilot. Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written
  approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-PHYLUM** (decided M2). Trigger: GATE-PHYLUM-03 fails: below 0.5 of homogeneous throughput on 512 designs,
  more than eight buckets, or arm B covers at least 95% of the mutation corpus and is faster. Fallback, named in
  advance: Per-family templates become the engine; cohorts are formed by exact tree with per-design parameter tables;
  the table-driven path remains for admission and preview on `batch-cpu`.
- **KILL-BUCKET** (decided M2). Trigger: More than eight buckets are needed to hold Ω₃ ≤ 2 on the reference family
  corpus, or the mixed batch of GATE-SPARK-10 runs below 0.5 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates after one
  optimization pass. Fallback, named in advance: At most nine padded per-family templates, each a homogeneous
  environment, scheduled in turn; parameter variants are unaffected.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-LIMBKERNEL (limb-archetype kernels): the
  pilot's falsifier holds, or coverage is below 80% with 16 archetypes, or the gate's ratio fails after one repair
  cycle. Fallback: the table-driven kernel for every design, with per-design specialization for survivors under its
  own gate.
- **One tolerance and one kill threshold on two workloads.** A mixed batch must reach at least 0.70 of the harmonic
  mean of its homogeneous rates (three body plans in one bucket, and cohorts of at least 256 designs); below 0.5 the
  kill gates fire. Both have one fallback, per-family templates, and the head-to-head is the deciding experiment.
- **OPEN-PHYLUM-3** is the envelope question for the head-to-head; **OPEN-SPARK-3** fixes the bucket set (links {12,
  18, 24, 36, 48, 64} by default), which the M1 pilot may revise.
- Admitting a variant must change no other world's digest (GATE-SPARK-10); a batch of 256 parameter variants must
  equal 256 homogeneous runs bit for bit (GATE-KIN-06).
