## Background

**The row.** WP-47: Performance harness and parity gates. Crates: `frob-conformance`. Milestone: M2. Key gates:
GATE-SPARK-19, -20, -22. Waits for: WP-34, WP-18.

A performance figure is admitted only by a paired lower bound under a complete disclosure block. The package delivers
the performance harness (the ordering of units, the paired lower bound, the disclosure block, the refusal of a report
that omits a decimation), the throughput parity rows measured by the loop profile, optimize one lever, measure,
disclose, and the `sim.estimate` cost and memory model, which prints the envelope class a job needs and refuses a job
that does not fit before anything is allocated.

It is one unit of work because the harness, the rows it admits and the estimator that predicts them are calibrated
against each other: of 100 A/A campaigns at most 9 may clear a threshold of 1.02, and time estimates must fall inside
their intervals on at least 90% of held-out runs.

**Order.** It waits for WP-34 (the incumbent figures that are the denominators) and WP-18 (the CUDA backend whose rows
are measured first). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block
every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request R1 of the envelope register (shared with WP-34): Incumbent and parity measurements on both cards and on
  all cores. Class asked: E1 per gate (GATE-SPARK-03 and GATE-SPARK-20 here). If not approved: Single-card and
  eight-core rows only; the other rows are recorded `not run`. Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written
  approval is recorded on row R1 of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01); a job above the class in force is
  refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`). R1 is first needed at M0, so its ruling is read at the M0 exit and
  re-read here.
- **KILL-PARITY** (decided M2). Trigger: At M2 exit, after one profile-guided optimization pass, the paired lower
  bound of a physics row (1, 7 or 8 of §36.3) is below 0.33 of the incumbent on the same card. Fallback, named in
  advance: Feature work on the device tier stops until the row is inside tolerance; the arithmetic of §2 and §39 is
  redone with measured rates before the partial gate, where shipping the layer is decided. Between 0.33 and 0.67: a
  recorded defect, one further pass, a disclosure on every Performance claim. The three physics rows: row 1, the
  reference quadruped Q (12 actuated joints, contact capacity 32), physics only on `gpu-cuda`, one RTX 4090, 8,192
  worlds, world-steps/s at decimation 10; row 7, the reference humanoid H (29 actuated joints, contact capacity 64),
  the same on flat ground; row 8, H on a heightfield. The tolerance 0.67 is a step at most 1.5 times slower than the
  incumbent's, which lengthens training by a factor between 1.15 and 1.44 because the step is 0.29 to 0.87 of
  training wall-clock on incumbent stacks. "The arithmetic of §2 and §39" is the ten-fold budget: the product of the
  per-lever factors that the thesis and the ten-fold test charge in full, recomputed with the measured rates.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER** (decided M1 on the rival's ledger in WP-34; re-read at M2 on our own profile by N39b-10, a
  child of this package). Trigger: a mechanism's removable cost is under 10% of the measured loop. Fallback, named in
  advance: it is not funded as a speed lever; it may be funded for trust, coverage or correctness with that reason
  written and no speed figure.
- **Stop decision of M2**: if a physics row is below 0.33 of the incumbent on the same card after one profile-guided
  pass, feature work on the device tier stops and the ten-fold arithmetic is redone with measured rates before the
  partial gate.
- **Two figures for one quantity.** Strong scaling on two cards has a tolerance of 1.25 (this package's gate and the
  two-device gate of WP-48) and an ambition of 1.6, which is also the threshold of KILL-SHARD.
- A figure without a complete disclosure block is refused (`FROB-REF-862`), a ratio without a paired lower bound is
  refused (`FROB-REF-863`), and a job that does not fit memory is refused before allocation (`FROB-REF-602`).
