## Background

**The row.** WP-50: Fidelity ledger, sampled referee, energy audit, tier correction and model-form checks [S], [F].
Crates: `frob-dyn`, `frob-chronicle`, `frob-opt`. Milestone: M2, M3. Key gates: GATE-FID-03 to -08; GATE-CHRON-08,
-09; GATE-CORTEX-15. Waits for: WP-22, WP-39.

The trust ladder of WP-22 says how far a fast tier disagrees with the referee on a battery; this package says what
that is worth during real campaigns. It delivers the referee's own coverage report per robot class
(`fidelity.coverage`), the sampled background referee (`fidelity.shadow`: address-hashed sampling, snapshots, one-step
defects, the coverage figure on every receipt), the energy ledger on batched tiers (`K-ledger-step`) with the audit
for energy created by the simulator (`fidelity.energy`), the tier-correction ledger (control variate and doubly robust
correction), rank agreement between tiers on the 40-design set, and the model-form rank check (`fidelity.modelform`).

It is one unit of work because each mechanism answers the same question from a different side, whether a result
obtained on a fast tier survives the referee, and they share the fixtures: planted tier-sensitive policies, planted
energy exploits, planted model-form winners.

**Order.** It waits for WP-22 (the frozen battery, the ledger, digests and snapshots) and WP-39 (the transactional
referee that the sampled referee runs). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

- `frob-opt` (layer 4; Solid, Frontier; cap 14): Comparison, lineage, optimization, the archive, predictions, the
  cascade, decision cores.

Out of scope: the promotion rule that consumes these records (WP-25); rank robustness of finalists inside the cascade

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):

  The referee's own coverage report per robot class (`fidelity.coverage`); BD-214 The sampled background referee
  (`fidelity.shadow`): address-hashed sampling, snapshots …
- M3: BD-264 The tier-correction ledger: control variate and doubly robust correction; BD-265 Rank agreement between
  tiers on the 40-design set (`eval.rank-agreement` on a tier pair); BD-266 The model-form rank check
  (`fidelity.modelform`); BD-268 The background referee at its gate
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** Solid: coverage, the energy ledger and

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-SHADOW** (decided M3). Trigger: GATE-FID-07 fails on cost or on coverage after one repair cycle, or no
  planted defect is detected. Fallback, named in advance: The referee runs at promotion time only, plus an offline
  audit of sampled snapshots after each run.
- **KILL-DR** (decided M3). Trigger: ρ_ep below 0.7 on 200 paired addresses in every stratum tested, or GATE-CHRON-09
  coverage fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: Promotion reports the label only, from referee
  episodes alone; the ledger keeps recording ρ_ep.
- **BET-34 (referee coverage)**: coverage below 90% in a reference class falsifies the bet for that class; an
  adjudicator is then named, or claims are restricted to the classes the referee covers (stop decision of M2).
- **Shared fixtures are run once.** The model-form check is recorded by GATE-FID-05, GATE-CORTEX-15 and GATE-ATEL-10,
  and the tier correction by GATE-FID-08 and GATE-CHRON-09; each gate is compared with the threshold in its own row.
- **KILL-RANK is not this package's.** Agreement between physics tiers has no kill gate: no population is ordered
  across a tier pair whose τ is below 0.5, which is the per-stratum rule of GATE-FID-04.
- A numerical fault is never counted as a physical failure and never dropped from a denominator (GATE-CHRON-08).
