## Background

**The row.** WP-52: Viewer, stills and dashboard. Crates: `viewer/`, `frob-tui`, `frob-chronicle`. Milestone: M2, M3.
Key gates: GATE-TETHER-14; GATE-CHRON-13; INT-FTUI-01. Waits for: WP-20, WP-43.

People and agents need to look, and whatever they see must be a stored value, never a picture of one. The package
delivers stills and pixel inspection (`telemetry.stills`, `telemetry.inspect-pixel`, `telemetry.export-html`), the
viewer page with its overlays, and the terminal dashboard `frob-tui` on frankentui.

It is one unit of work because every view answers to the same rule: on analytic scenes the overlay value under a probe
pixel equals the telemetry channel within its quantization, and every number the dashboard displays is reproduced by
its source verb.

**Order.** It waits for WP-20 (the ray kernels that stills are rendered by) and WP-43 (the job events and artifacts
the dashboard and viewer read). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic
would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-tui` (layer 7; Frontier; cap 6): The `frob-tui` dashboard on frankentui.
- `frob-chronicle` (layer 6; Solid; cap 18): Telemetry, episode files, replay and forks, digests, the energy ledger,
  the job and chunk store, the memo store, summaries, stills.
- `viewer/`: the Three.js viewer and the HTML export (cap 12 thousand lines of JavaScript). An optional renderer route
  is admitted as a pattern by INT-F3D-01.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M2.** Stills, pixel inspection and the viewer page; GATE-CHRON-13 (the cost of telemetry levels T0 to T2).
- **M3.** The dashboard and the viewer overlays; GATE-TETHER-14; INT-FTUI-01; INT-F3D-01; the decision KILL-TUI.

Out of scope: the telemetry levels and streams themselves (WP-13, WP-51); RGB and splat scenes, which are M6 items
(WP-74).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M2: BD-191 Stills and pixel inspection …
- M3: BD-300 The dashboard `frob-tui` (INT-FTUI-01) and the viewer overlays (INT-F3D-01)
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** Solid: stills, pixel inspection and the
viewer. Frontier: the dashboard, whose foundation frankentui is admitted by a gate that is also its pilot
(INT-FTUI-01): one asupersync package or none in its graph, the job pane rendered from a recorded event stream equal
to its stored snapshot, and idle processor use of at most 2% of one core. None of these blocks a milestone.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-TUI** (decided M3). Trigger: FrankenTUI cannot be admitted with one asupersync package, or the snapshot tests
  cannot be made stable. Fallback, named in advance: No dashboard; `job.list` and `session.show` carry the same
  numbers.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-REPLAYPACK (the single-file replay packet
  of `telemetry.export-html`): its gate fails after one repair cycle, or the page cannot be held to its byte budget of
  8,388,608 bytes. Fallback: `run.export` to `.frun`, stills with overlays and the live viewer; no single-file packet.
- A view creates no evidence: a packet or a still may state the claim class of its source and nothing higher.
- frankentui's optional executor pins an exact asupersync version and is forbidden here until an upstream change
  relaxes the pin (FD-FTUI-01, priority P3).
