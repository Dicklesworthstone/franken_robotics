## Background

**The row.** WP-53: Habitat II: clearance complexes, retrofit catalogue, world bank, film patches, rigid proxies [S],
[F]. Crates: `frob-scene`, `frob-contact`. Milestone: M3, M4. Key gates: GATE-HAB-04 to -06, -10, -11, -12, -14;
GATE-TRAC-05, -07. Waits for: WP-23, WP-16.

A place has to be accepted, measured for passage, kept in memory by the thousand, and treated as something that can
itself be changed. The package delivers scene acceptance (`scene.accept`), optical and friction properties as separate
maps with fixtures and movable objects, the film-friction law (Habitat's thickness state and Traction's cone row,
`K-film`), rigid proxies (a rug as a free slab, a cord as a fixed ridge or a free rod, clutter as rigid bodies), the
mesh path with enclosures and a bounding-volume hierarchy with thin-feature detection, clearance complexes `.fclear`
with two-sided cells, path witnesses and component cuts (`contact.clearance`, analysis A-18), train and test hygiene
(`scene.split`, building-level holdouts, mirrored layouts and edited variants), the world bank (`scene.bank`), and at
M4 the priced retrofit catalogue with `scene.retrofit`.

It is one unit of work because all of it is what the M3 tasks, adversaries and the partial gate need from a place, and
it shares the fixtures of seven archetypes.

**Order.** It waits for WP-23 (the archetypes and hazard lists) and WP-16 (batched contact, which the film row, the
proxies and the mesh path extend). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked
epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-scene` (layer 2; Solid, Frontier; cap 28): Scene packs, the scene compiler, terrain and interior generators,
  import and acceptance; optical and fixture properties; clearance complexes; the retrofit catalog; living-world
  state.
- `frob-contact` (layer 3; Solid, Frontier; cap 22): Collision geometry, detection, contact laws (convex compliant,
  the four-dimensional cone, joint rows, film friction, the hard-contact law), wheels, tracks, terrain mechanics.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M3.** Everything but retrofits; GATE-HAB-04, -05, -06, -10, -12 and -14; GATE-TRAC-05 and -07; the decisions
  KILL-FILM, KILL-MESHGPU, KILL-FCLEAR and KILL-BANK-BREADTH.
- **M4.** The retrofit catalogue `registries/retrofits.toml` with priced kits and the search on a minimal
  counterexample world; GATE-HAB-11; the decision KILL-RETROFIT.

Out of scope: advected films, slosh and articulated proxies (WP-73); real places (WP-32); the feasibility tags of
generated scenes, which are computed from the clearance complex but carried with the generators (WP-23).

acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-53): The world bank resident on the M5 Ultra, far above 16 GiB. Class asked: E1, memory to the
  device budget of §36.10. If not approved: A bank that fits the default; GATE-HAB-12 reports the smaller count.
  Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused
  before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-FILM** (decided M3). Trigger: On the wet-floor battery the film law changes fewer than 10% of episode
  outcomes against a constant-coefficient patch, or adds more than 5% to step time. Fallback, named in advance: Static
  condition cards on the lockstep tiers; the law is kept on the transactional profiles for audits.
- **KILL-MESHGPU** (decided M3 at the earliest, before the work package). Trigger: Mesh traversal on a GPU breaks
  fixed launch shapes or adds more than 15% to the step. Fallback, named in advance: GPUs see thin geometry as
  analytic primitives only; meshes stay on the CPU tiers.
- **KILL-FCLEAR** (decided M3). Trigger: One false witness or false cut survives a repair cycle, or more than 30% of
  doorway queries on the archetype battery stay `unresolved` after refinement. Fallback, named in advance: The
  route-graph traversability check for a nominal footprint, labeled heuristic.
- **KILL-BANK-BREADTH** (decided M3). Trigger: Held-out success does not rise with scene breadth beyond its confidence
  sequence. Fallback, named in advance: The bank serves evaluation, qualification, replay, clearance and retrofit
  search and large scenes; training keeps per-card resident sets.
- **KILL-RETROFIT** (decided M4). Trigger: On the home battery kits flip fewer than 30% of the failures attributed to
  a building feature, or more than 10% of kit verdicts reverse on the referee. Fallback, named in advance: The catalog
  remains the edit vocabulary of Nemesis; the search verb is withdrawn.
- **Two gates, one film fixture.** The three squeeze times are within 2% in GATE-TRAC-05 (the law) and in GATE-HAB-06
  (the law as scenes consume it): one fixture, measured once.
- **KILL-FILM is the film-friction law's gate, not the advected film's** (that is KILL-ADVECT, in WP-73). If KILL-FILM
  fires, both fall to static condition cards.
- The 6 mm case must answer `unknown`, never `clear` and never `penetrates`; every clearance certificate is re-checked
  offline.
- OPEN-HAB-4: retrofit prices are `assumed` and legality constraints are marked unchecked in every report.
