## Background

**The row.** WP-55: Nemesis [S], [F]. Crates: `frob-nemesis`. Milestone: M3, M4. Key gates: GATE-NEMESIS-01 to -08.
Waits for: WP-24, WP-51.

A design is not accepted because it passed; it is accepted because a search that could have broken it did not. The
package delivers the search space and the legality set (`frob.legality/1`, with the hazard registries as the
scene-edit vocabulary), the solvability oracle (`nemesis.oracle`), the artifact guard, the falsification score and the
search (`nemesis.search`), shrinking to a minimal counterexample world `.fmcw` (`nemesis.shrink`), the hard-world set
(`nemesis.hardset`), rare failures in two lanes (certification by bounded-weight estimation with a defensive mixture,
`nemesis.bound`; exploration by splitting), scene-set coverage by submodular selection (`nemesis.cover`), and at M4
the screening audits (`nemesis.audit`).

It is one unit of work because a counterexample is admitted only through the whole chain: legal, solvable, not a
simulator artifact, shrunk, replayable.

**Order.** It waits for WP-24 (the tasks and verdicts a world is judged by) and WP-51 (forks and replay, which
shrinking and the guard use). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic
would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-nemesis` (layer 5; Solid, Frontier; cap 10): Adversarial world search, shrinking to minimal counterexample
  worlds, bounded-weight rare-event estimation.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M3.** Legality, the oracle, the guard, the search, shrinking, the hard-world set, both rare-failure lanes and
  coverage; GATE-NEMESIS-01 to -06 and -08; the decisions KILL-NEMESIS, KILL-RARE and KILL-MCW.
- **M4.** The screening audit (`nemesis.audit`) and its gate GATE-NEMESIS-07, which the cascade's own gate
  GATE-ATEL-09 relies on. The seed bead that applies the audit to the cascade's discarded strata, with the planted
  winner, is carried by WP-62.

Out of scope: separation of two designs by a minimized world pair (WP-59); retrofits found on counterexample worlds
(WP-53); the cascade that applies the audit (WP-62).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every child bead is closed with a typed reason citing its own test and that test's output; no close reason is shared; no child is closed by the session that implemented it.
- [ ] Every gate below has a row in `registries/gates.toml` (commit, command, output, receipt hash) with status `pass`, or `killed: fallback adopted` with its ADR. `not run` is not a pass. Thresholds are TARGETs until measured.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-01 (Known answer; earliest M3): Legality and oracle: 100 of 100 planted illegal edits refused; 0 of 100 planted impossible traps counted; at least 95 of 100 planted solvable traps receive a witness.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-02 (Design comparison; earliest M3): Planted traps of nominal probability at most 3 × 10⁻⁵, 30 paired repetitions: a first promoted failure with at least 3 times fewer simulated steps than card-uniform randomization at a 95% lower bound; at least 70% of reported counterexamples `solvable-causal` or `plant-feasible`.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-03 (Known answer; earliest M3): Shrinking: planted causes recovered exactly in at least 95 of 100 trials; every `.fmcw` replays with identical digests in 100 of 100; on unplanted failures the share of worlds with at most three edits is recorded.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-04 (Known answer; earliest M3): Bounds: over 10⁴ synthetic studies with known p the 95% upper bound covers p in at least 95%; the defensive mixture covers under a mis-centred proposal; non-nominal episodes are refused.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-05 (Design comparison; earliest M3): Exploration on planted failures of probability 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁵ (one with two separate modes), equal episode budget, 30 repetitions: at least 10 times fewer episodes than crude sampling at 10⁻⁵; both modes found in at least 80% of repetitions.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-06 (Known answer; earliest M3): Guard: 100 of 100 planted artifact failures labelled and none in the hard-world set; at least 95 of 100 genuine planted failures survive promotion.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-07 (Known answer; earliest M4): Screening audit: a planted winner removed by a biased screen is reported in 100 of 100 runs; the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of 1,000 synthetic campaigns.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-08 (Known answer; earliest M3): Coverage: lazy and plain greedy agree; on 1,000 random instances of at most 20 scenes the brute-force optimum is at or below the certificate and greedy is at or above (1 − 1/e) of it.
- [ ] Each crate above ships `CONTRACT.md` with its ten sections and `contract.toml`; `xtask check-contracts` reports its line count under 115% of its cap.

has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-55): A certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ (OPEN-NEMESIS-3). Class asked: E2. If not approved: Certify to
  10⁻⁵; report 10⁻⁶ as `Estimated`. Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above
  the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-NEMESIS** (decided M3). Trigger: GATE-NEMESIS-02 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance:
  The search methods are removed; Nemesis keeps the legality profile, the oracle, the guard and shrinking, applied to
  failures that card randomization finds.
- **KILL-RARE** (decided M3). Trigger: In the exploration pilot the gain is below 10 times at 10⁻⁵, or the coverage of
  any interval offered as a bound is below 85%, or a planted mode is missed in more than 20% of repetitions. Fallback,
  named in advance: Crude nominal sampling with the binomial bound alone; splitting is removed; tilting is kept only
  inside `nemesis.search`.
- **KILL-MCW** (decided M3). Trigger: On unplanted failures fewer than half of shrunk worlds have at most three edits,
  or fewer than 90% replay. Fallback, named in advance: Nemesis ships full failing worlds with replay and single-edit
  contrasts ranked by margin; the format stays.
- **The oracle guards the denominator**: 0 of 100 planted impossible traps may be counted as failures, and 100 of 100
  planted illegal edits must be refused.
- **OPEN-NEMESIS-3** is the envelope question for a certified rate of 10⁻⁶; the default certifies to 10⁻⁵.
- A bound is offered only on nominal episodes: non-nominal episodes are refused, and importance weights that miss the
  failure region trip the effective-sample-size floor.
