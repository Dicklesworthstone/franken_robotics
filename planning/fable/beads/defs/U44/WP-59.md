## Background

**The row.** WP-59: Mission queries II: amendments, transport, separation, probes [F]. Crates: `frob-mission`,
`frob-opt`. Milestone: M3. Key gates: GATE-AUGUR-06, -08 to -13. Waits for: WP-42, WP-55.

The first slice answered fixed queries; real work amends them. The package delivers monotone transport of verdicts
with its falsification harness, the region-certificate pilot on the 12-parameter box, the alias and jaw fixtures with
`mission.refute` on the blind payload task, probe and instrument design (`mission.distinguish`,
`scene.probe-request`), separation of two designs by a minimized world pair (`mission.separate`), and checker
independence repeated over every proof kind in use at M3.

It is one unit of work because each mechanism is a way of saying something about a mission without re-deciding it from
nothing, and each is defeated the same way: by one case where the shortcut inherits or issues a wrong verdict. The
widened sole is the standing counterexample to naive monotone transport.

**Order.** It waits for WP-42 (the mission core, the bundles and the checker) and WP-55 (the adversarial search and
minimization that separation uses). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked
epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-AUGUR-REGION** (decided M3). Trigger: The pilot of §25.6 (region certificates on the 12-parameter box,
  four paving arms at 10⁴ boxes) meets its falsifier (more than 90% undecided in the
  best arm, or more than 95% of decided volume from one monotone bound), or GATE-AUGUR-09 fails after one repair
  cycle. Fallback, named in advance: Region certificates are removed; family answers use dual witnesses, monotone
  bounds and pointwise static analyses; untested regions are reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`.
- **KILL-AUGUR-REUSE** (decided Standing, from M3). Trigger: One incorrectly inherited verdict from an implication
  rule. Fallback, named in advance: That rule is removed; reuse falls back to the dependency-keyed memo graph of §28
  (the memo graph of WP-43 and WP-63: a verdict is reused only when every input it was computed from is unchanged).
- **KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE** (decided M3). Trigger: Minimization changes the failure mechanism in more than 20% of
  planted cases, or a conventional scenario tool with the same generator matches the result at equal cost. Fallback,
  named in advance: Ship the Nemesis search and minimization without the design-pair query; keep counterexamples as
  regressions.
- **KILL-AUGUR-PROBE** (decided M3). Trigger: The generator never beats the programmed expert plus direct measurement
  at matched correctness on the coupled-layout experiment, or issues one false lower bound. Fallback, named in
  advance: `experiment.suggest` ships the expert grid and the direct measurement; the experiment grammar is parked.
- **KILL-AUGUR-ALIAS** (decided M3). Trigger: Generated alias witnesses are contradicted by a permitted probe in more
  than 5% of audited cases. Fallback, named in advance: Witnesses are labelled experiment-specific only; universal
  observability claims are refused.
- **KILL-FAMILYCERT** (decided M3). Trigger: At the partial ten-fold gate no family certificate has decided a cell
  that the generic comparator of D-17 leaves undecided at equal cost. Fallback, named in advance: Mission answers come
  from the generic solvers alone and still ship in the bundle format; the specialized projections are removed. (D-17
  is the program rule that a specialized method names the generic method it must beat; for the mission families the
  comparator is the generic solver run on the same lowering, for example the scenario-tree linear program for the
  docking family. A tie is an acceptable outcome that ships the generic method.)
- **OPEN-AUGUR-4**: whether a quantifier-elimination or SMT engine is a production dependency. Default: comparator
  only; the product uses in-tree elimination for small linear systems, and some family cells stay unresolved that a
  general solver would close. KILL-AUGUR-TIE depends on it.
- Zero false transports, zero incorrectly inherited verdicts and zero false accepts are the pass criteria; one
  counterexample fires the corresponding kill gate.
- KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT applies to both arms of the partial gate.
