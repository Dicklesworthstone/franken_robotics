## Background

**The row.** WP-60: The rank-agreement pilot and the partial ten-fold gate. Crates: `frob-conformance`. Milestone: M3.
Key gates: GATE-CORTEX-12 (pilot); GATE-TENFOLD-03 to -06, -10, -11, -13. Waits for: WP-34, WP-54, WP-57, WP-59.

M3 exits on one observable: the partial ten-fold gate recorded, whatever the ratio. The package delivers the
pre-registration of the rank-agreement experiment (design set, strata, the τ thresholds 0.7 and 0.5 fixed before data,
the compute request), the rank-agreement and ladder pilots on an incumbent engine or on `batch-cpu`, and the partial
gate in four parts: the protocol (sealed legged and wheeled briefs, strata, the decision rule with its reachable
verdicts, the ceilings, the adjudicating evaluator), the incumbent arm (the strongest composed rival at pinned
versions, warm, driven through its own tools and the adapter), our arm (fresh agents on the full MCP surface), and
adjudication with the record.

It is one unit of work because the gate is one experiment with one frozen rule, and the rank-agreement pilot is the
other early measurement the thesis stands on: whether a cheap rung orders designs the way an expensive one does
(BET-17).

**Order.** It waits for WP-34 (the incumbent harness and baseline), WP-54 (the sealing and blinding discipline), WP-57
(the adapter that is the rival's access path) and WP-59 (the mission queries that the `envelope` and `sensing` strata
use). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child,
so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request R6 of the envelope register: The rank-agreement pilot (OPEN-PROG-4) and the partial gate's campaigns
  (GATE-CORTEX-12, GATE-TENFOLD-06; approval wanted at M1, runs by M3). Class asked: E2. If not approved: Cheap rungs
  are `uninformative` and neither select nor discard; the partial gate runs its CPU strata. Nothing above E0 starts
  before the owner's written approval is recorded on row R6 of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01); a job
  above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`). The CPU strata are the not-approved
  fallback and are admitted under E0; the device strata are then `not run` with the reason (OPEN-TENFOLD-4).
- **KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED** (decided M3 for the classes run; M7). Trigger: On a confirmatory cell R̂ is below 3, or at
  the partial gate the futility monitor stops the cell. Fallback, named in advance: The speed claim for that class is
  withdrawn from §2, the README and every report; speed-only work packages for the class are re-planned; the product
  is described for that class by the capabilities of §39.10 that do not depend on speed, and by its measured ratio.
  (§2 is the plan's statement of the speed thesis. The capabilities that never depended on speed: any world re-runs
  alone on the CPU from its address; comparisons carry error control that survives an agent's retries; family answers
  are checked by an auditor that shares no code with their producer; failures arrive as minimal replayable worlds
  with an interventional explanation; every result names its claim, its class and its evidence; the energy ledger
  closes. The comparison with incumbents is then stated as parity of speed with a different kind of evidence.)
- **KILL-TENFOLD-TIE** (decided M3; M7). Trigger: On a stratum, arm C reaches the same accepted scope with a
  restricted mean time within a factor of 1.5 of arm A's; or the composed incumbent's recovery ratio is at least 0.8
  on elapsed time and on compute (BET-14, §39.22). Fallback, named in advance: Say so; ship the layer over that engine
  for that stratum behind the same verbs, schemas and checker; keep engine work for the engine-internal gaps only (two
  documented, two unverified: §39.10); spend the research budget on the first mission the program cannot resolve.
  (The two documented engine-internal gaps: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay, and the Metal tier. The two
  unverified ones, checked before they are relied on: replay of one world outside its batch, and solver-internal
  certificates. The ratio against arm B is then the layer's ratio, and no claim is made that the mechanisms need
  this engine.)
- **KILL-TENFOLD-CERT** (decided Standing, from M3). Trigger: One false certificate from an arm. Fallback, named in
  advance: That arm is disqualified from the scoped claim; for arm A the proof kind is suspended and
  KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT applies.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK** (decided Standing, from M3). Trigger: Any leak from sealed confirmation worlds. Fallback,
  named in advance: The affected cells are void and their families retired; if the extent cannot be bounded the gate
  is void and is re-run on a rotated suite.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ** (decided M3; M7). Trigger: No neutral simulator passes GATE-TENFOLD-05 for a stratum. Fallback,
  named in advance: Cross-home adjudication, labelled; if neither home referee can run the other arm's answers, the
  stratum is `descriptive`.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-REACH** (decided M3; M7). Trigger: A cell's pre-analysis fails a reachability condition of §39.7.
  (The conditions: at least ten paired campaigns per arm; enough mission families that the exact guard, a
  binomial(m, ½) test that the median per-family ratio exceeds 5, can reject at the cell's adjusted level, its critical
  counts at α = 0.05 being 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12, 12 of 16, 17 of 24 and 22 of 32, so a cell of four
  families, whose best tail is 1/16 = 0.0625, is refused as confirmatory; and for a population result at least 32
  independent families, 46 where four confirmatory cells share the level.)
  Fallback, named in advance: The cell is opened as `descriptive` and says so before any sealed run; it is enlarged
  for the next gate.
- **KILL-AUGUR-TIE** (decided M3). Trigger: On the strata of the M3 partial gate that use §25, a short robust program
  over a conventional solver reaches the same checked scope at comparable turnaround. Fallback, named in advance: Ship
  that program behind the same verbs, schema and checker; retire any claim of exclusive expressivity on those
  families; spend the research budget on the first mission the program cannot resolve.
- **Stop decision of M3**, by the rule written before the gate runs: a measured ratio under 3 on a mission class
  withdraws the speed claim for that class; under 3 on every class, speed work is no longer funded, the product is
  positioned on replay, qualification, certificates and the evidence ledger, and the family-policy spend of M4 is
  decided again.
- **OPEN-PROG-2**: the adjudicating simulator and the neutral party. Default: a pinned incumbent CPU engine that
  neither arm trains in, run by a fresh-context session; every ratio so measured is labelled `self-adjudicated` and
  the partial gate is a rehearsal.
- **OPEN-PROG-4**: compute for the rank-agreement experiment, about 5 device-hours where specialists are reused and
  about 45 otherwise.
- The pre-analysis known answers (10.081, 9.677, 1.8215, 0.78568; the counts 32, 46, 49 and 182) must reproduce, and
  the percentile bootstrap run as a negative control must wrongly certify ten-fold in 78.6% ± 3 points of rare-tail
  campaigns.
