## Background

**The row.** WP-61: Family policies, inheritance and distillation [F]. Crates: `frob-learn`. Milestone: M4. Key gates:
GATE-CORTEX-11, -12, -13, -14. Waits for: WP-44, WP-48, WP-60.

The second lever of the thesis is training few designs out of the many that are decided. The package delivers family
policies per morphology class (link tokens, the mapping table `frob.policy/1`, pretraining on a `GeneratorId` with a
`DesignSplit`; `train.family`), the family-policy experiment on held-out parametric designs with its fine-tuning
rungs, inheritance along design edits (`train.inherit`, `policy.apply`), distillation from baseline, specialist and
sampling-controller teachers (`train.distill`), pilots of the other learning algorithms against PPO at equal
wall-clock, and the rank-agreement experiment on our own engine per morphology class and task family
(`eval.rank-agreement`).

It is one unit of work because the three mechanisms are alternatives for one quantity, the training cost of a new
design, and are compared on the same held-out designs: with no adaptation, with fine-tuning, with a parent's policy,
with a teacher.

**Order.** It waits for WP-44 (the generator's designs in mixed batches), WP-48 (the PPO battery and the two-device
update) and WP-60 (the rank-agreement pilot and the partial gate, after which the family-policy spend is confirmed or
decided again). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block
every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):

  family-policy experiment on held-out parametric designs, with the fine-tuning rungs; BD-322 Inheritance along design
  edits (`train.inherit`, `policy.apply`); BD-323 Distillation from baseline, specialist and sampling-controller
  teachers (`train.distill`); BD-324 Pilots of the other learning algorithms of §23.22 against PPO at equal
  wall-clock; BD-328 The rank-agreement experiment on our engine, per morphology class and task family …
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The package is Frontier (BET-15 family

sampling-controller teacher and from a baseline; dimensionless against raw SI policy inputs across a size sweep
(construction C-21); policy transport on 12 parent–child edit pairs, 4 of them blind (construction C-23); and, per
alternative algorithm, its own pilot against PPO at equal wall-clock.

has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-61): Pretraining of one family policy (OPEN-CORTEX-1). Class asked: E3, approved per run with its
  ceiling and kill rule written first. If not approved: The variant of M4 with no family policy. Nothing above E0
  starts before the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts
  (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-FAMILY** (decided M4). Trigger: At GATE-CORTEX-11, return with no adaptation below 70% of specialists on more
  than half of the held-out set and fine-tuning above 20% of specialist steps; or the pilot's falsifier holds.
  Fallback, named in advance: No family policy: specialists with inheritance from the nearest parent, and the baseline
  screens.
- **KILL-INHERIT** (decided M4). Trigger: Median parameter-edit cost above 60% of from-scratch steps, or the inherited
  arm more than 5% worse in over 20% of edits. Fallback, named in advance: Every child trains from the class prior or
  from scratch; the mapping table is kept for loading policies.
- **KILL-DISTILL** (decided M4). Trigger: Students stay below 70% of their teachers' requirement pass rate after one
  repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: Distillation is limited to the privileged-teacher case; warm starts come
  from behaviour cloning only.
- **KILL-ALTALGO** (decided M4 at the earliest, per algorithm, before funding). Trigger: For any one of the other
  algorithms of §23.22, its pilot's falsifier holds. Fallback, named in advance: That algorithm is not built; PPO
  under the rules of §23.6 remains the trainer.
- **OPEN-CORTEX-1** (due at M3; blocks GATE-CORTEX-11): the envelope for pretraining. Proposal: one class (quadruped),
  200 designs, at most 60 GPU-hours of pretraining and 40 for specialists, on `trj`. Fail-closed default: no
  pretraining.
- **The variant of M4 with no family policy** (DESIGN). It applies if the envelope stays at its default, if the
  pre-funding pilot meets its falsifier, or if KILL-FAMILY fires. The rungs are then the static check, the baseline
  controller, the sampling controller, gait orbits and per-design specialists that inherit from the nearest parent;
  GATE-CORTEX-11 is recorded as failed or `not run`, BET-15 as falsified or untested, and the ten-fold record states
  the factor that was lost. Every other artifact of M4 lands unchanged.
- **Stop decision of M4**: if KILL-RANK fires for every class and KILL-FAMILY fires as well, the lever 'train few' is
  gone; the ceilings of the ten-fold arithmetic are recomputed with the measured trained fraction and the sealed suite
  is re-stratified before it is sealed.
- Published pretraining sizes of 2·10¹² steps would take 23 to 230 days on two cards and are not planned in any
  envelope.
