## Background

**The row.** WP-61: Family policies, inheritance and distillation [F]. Crates: `frob-learn`. Milestone: M4. Key gates:
GATE-CORTEX-11, -12, -13, -14. Waits for: WP-44, WP-48, WP-60.

The second lever of the thesis is training few designs out of the many that are decided. The package delivers family
policies per morphology class (link tokens, the mapping table `frob.policy/1`, pretraining on a `GeneratorId` with a
`DesignSplit`; `train.family`), the family-policy experiment on held-out parametric designs with its fine-tuning
rungs, inheritance along design edits (`train.inherit`, `policy.apply`), distillation from baseline, specialist and
sampling-controller teachers (`train.distill`), pilots of the other learning algorithms against PPO at equal
wall-clock, and the rank-agreement experiment on our own engine per morphology class and task family
(`eval.rank-agreement`).

It is one unit of work because the three mechanisms are alternatives for one quantity, the training cost of a new
design, and are compared on the same held-out designs: with no adaptation, with fine-tuning, with a parent's policy,
with a teacher.

**Order.** It waits for WP-44 (the generator's designs in mixed batches), WP-48 (the PPO battery and the two-device
update) and WP-60 (the rank-agreement pilot and the partial gate, after which the family-policy spend is confirmed or
decided again). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block
every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-learn` (layer 4; Solid, Frontier; cap 18): The environment API and its reset semantics, learners, networks,
  family policies, inheritance, the FrankenTorch bridge.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M3.** Three pilots, each on record before its mechanism is funded (the family-policy prior on 200 generated
  designs, inheritance along design edits, and distillation from a sampling-controller teacher and a baseline), and
  the owner decision OPEN-CORTEX-1 on the envelope for pretraining.
- **M4.** Family policies, inheritance, distillation and the rank-agreement record; GATE-CORTEX-11, -12, -13 and -14;
  the decisions KILL-FAMILY, KILL-INHERIT, KILL-DISTILL and, per algorithm, KILL-ALTALGO.

Out of scope: equivariant networks (WP-29); the ladder that allocates by the rank-agreement record (WP-62);
geometric-algebra tree policies and skill transport, which are Moonshot items (WP-79).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Its requests are five rows of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01), each with its branch if not approved:
  - R7, pretraining of one family policy (OPEN-CORTEX-1, decided by N23b-24): class E3, approved per run with its
    ceiling and kill rule written first; the family-prior pilot N23b-19 (about 100 GPU-hours) and `train.family`
    run under the per-run rows R7.n that N23b-24 writes. If not approved: the variant of M4 with no family policy
    (N40-34).
  - R13, the inheritance pilot N23b-20 (15 edits × 3 arms × 3 seeds plus the ten-edit chain with twins, about 70
    GPU-hours): above E2's 48 device-hours, so E2 amended by the owner to the pilot's ceiling, with the ceiling and
    falsifier written first. If not approved: the pilot is closed `withdrawn` (`not run: no envelope`, citing
    R13), BD-322 is not funded, and KILL-INHERIT (N23a-30) records its fallback with that reason.
  - R14, the distillation pilot N23b-21 (about 10 GPU-hours beyond the sampling-controller pilot): E1 for
    `gate:KILL-DISTILL`. If not approved: the pilot is closed `withdrawn` (`not run: no envelope`, citing R14),
    BD-323 is not funded, and KILL-DISTILL (N23a-21) records its fallback.
  - R16, the six pilots of BD-324 (about 10 GPU-hours each, about 60 in all): E2, one `campaign:` scope per
    algorithm, the six scopes BD-324 names. If not approved: each pilot is `undetermined`, the algorithm is not
    built (pilot first), and KILL-ALTALGO (N23b-23) leaves PPO under its rules as the trainer.
  - R20, the comparison of asynchronous against synchronous two-card training (N36-12, a bead held directly by
    EPIC-M4; the row names this package because it holds the asynchronous-sharding pilot of BD-324 whose follow-on
    the campaign is): at least ten pairs, each two two-card trainings to threshold, sized before the first pair
    from the measured time to threshold of that pilot. Class expected: E2 for one `campaign:` scope named by
    N36-12, distinct from R16's `campaign:altalgo-async`; above 48 device-hours, E2 amended by the owner. If not
    approved: the comparison is recorded `not run` with the envelope reason, the asynchronous variant is not
    offered, and synchronous sharding remains the two-card training mode.
  R13, R14, R16 and R20 are reserved identifiers of the register, each filed by the bead named, with its estimate,
  before that bead's first run above E0.
  Nothing above E0 starts before the owner's written approval of its row (N44-01; a bead above E0 is not ready
  until then, N41-17); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-FAMILY** (decided M4). Trigger: At GATE-CORTEX-11, return with no adaptation below 70% of specialists on more
  than half of the held-out set and fine-tuning above 20% of specialist steps; or the pilot's falsifier holds.
  Fallback, named in advance: No family policy: specialists with inheritance from the nearest parent, and the baseline
  screens.
- **KILL-INHERIT** (decided M4). Trigger: Median parameter-edit cost above 60% of from-scratch steps, or the inherited
  arm more than 5% worse in over 20% of edits. Fallback, named in advance: Every child trains from the class prior or
  from scratch; the mapping table is kept for loading policies.
- **KILL-DISTILL** (decided M4). Trigger: Students stay below 70% of their teachers' requirement pass rate after one
  repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: Distillation is limited to the privileged-teacher case; warm starts come
  from behaviour cloning only.
- **KILL-ALTALGO** (decided M4 at the earliest, per algorithm, before funding). Trigger: For any one of the other
  algorithms of §23.22, its pilot's falsifier holds. Fallback, named in advance: That algorithm is not built; PPO
  under the rules of §23.6 remains the trainer. (The six algorithms: SAC, TD3, evolution strategies, privileged
  teacher and student, adaptive randomization, asynchronous sharding. Each pilot runs against PPO on the reference
  quadruped task at equal wall-clock on an incumbent stack, three seeds, about 10 GPU-hours each; the falsifier is
  that the arm beats PPO neither in time to the threshold nor in final requirement pass rate. The rules of §23.6 are
  the PPO battery P-1 to P-11 of GATE-CORTEX-07, WP-48.)
- **OPEN-CORTEX-1** (due at M3; blocks GATE-CORTEX-11): the envelope for pretraining. Proposal: one class (quadruped),
  200 designs, at most 60 GPU-hours of pretraining and 40 for specialists, on `trj`. Fail-closed default: no
  pretraining.
- **The variant of M4 with no family policy** (DESIGN; defined by N40-34 under EPIC-M4). It applies if the envelope stays at its default, if the
  pre-funding pilot meets its falsifier, or if KILL-FAMILY fires. The rungs are then the static check, the baseline
  controller, the sampling controller, gait orbits and per-design specialists that inherit from the nearest parent;
  GATE-CORTEX-11 is recorded as failed or `not run`, BET-15 as falsified or untested, and the ten-fold record states
  the factor that was lost. Every other artifact of M4 lands unchanged.
- **Stop decision of M4**: if KILL-RANK fires for every class and KILL-FAMILY fires as well, the lever 'train few' is
  gone; the ceilings of the ten-fold arithmetic are recomputed with the measured trained fraction and the sealed suite
  is re-stratified before it is sealed.
- Published pretraining sizes of 2·10¹² steps would take 23 to 230 days on two cards and are not planned in any
  envelope.
