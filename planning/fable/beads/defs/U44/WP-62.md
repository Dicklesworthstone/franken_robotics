## Background

**The row.** WP-62: The ladder, the cascade and the decision cores [S], [F]. Crates: `frob-opt`, `frob-verbs`.
Milestone: M3, M4. Key gates: GATE-ATEL-07 to -10, -13, -14, -16; GATE-PHYLUM-08. Waits for: WP-28, WP-50, WP-61.

Given many candidate designs and a compute budget, something must decide which candidates earn an expensive
evaluation, and that decision must be auditable. The package delivers the ladder of rungs R0 to R5 (a fixed halving
table, and a calibrated layer with decision cores DC-02 and DC-03 that must beat it), the decision-core tables DC-01
to DC-11 in `registries/cores/` (`cores.show`), the composition of decision cores with its nine interference tests,
rank robustness of finalists inside the cascade (analysis A-19), the quality-diversity archive with maps per dispatch
(`opt.archive`, `phylum.map`), and the six-stage co-design cascade (`opt.cascade`, `report.campaign`).

It is one unit of work because the cascade is the composition of all the rungs and cores, and its claim is about the
composition: a rung is trusted only as far as its measured rank agreement allows, a rung whose screening audit is
missing or failed is set to `uninformative`, and no audit episode is ever cancelled.

**Order.** It waits for WP-28 (optimization, racing and the archive's verbs), WP-50 (tier correction and the
model-form check that rungs consume) and WP-61 (family policies and the rank-agreement record, which decide what the
cheap rungs may do). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block
every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every child bead is closed with a typed reason citing its own test and that test's output; no close reason is shared; no child is closed by the session that implemented it.
- [ ] Every gate below has a row in `registries/gates.toml` (commit, command, output, receipt hash) with status `pass`, or `killed: fallback adopted` with its ADR. `not run` is not a pass. Thresholds are TARGETs until measured.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-07 (Design comparison; earliest M3 (pilot); M4): The rank-agreement experiment per morphology class: Kendall τ on the top band at least 0.7 for `selective` and at least 0.5 for `discard-only`, and ρ_ep at least 0.7, all at the lower bootstrap bound, on 40 designs. A class below 0.5 is `uninformative`.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-08 (Design comparison; earliest M3 (pilot); M4): Layer 2 against the fixed table: held-out coverage at least 1 − α − 0.03 per stratum; audited top-k recall no lower than halving's; a paired 95% lower bound above 1 on GPU-seconds to the same accepted decision; a planted over-scored design caught at the next rung; synthetic drift trips the alarm.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-09 (Known answer; earliest M4): Atelier does not trust a rung whose screening audit (§26.10, GATE-NEMESIS-07) is missing or failed: in 100 of 100 planted campaigns a stage with no audit record, a missed planted winner or an audited miss rate above its tolerance has its rung set to `uninformative` in the affected strata and its results labelled `screen-biased`; a planted unsound prune is caught in 100 of 100; no audit episode is ever cancelled.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-10 (Known answer; earliest M4): A-19: every planted cone-excess and energy-pumping exploit is typed `model-conditional`; honest winners are typed `model-robust`; the stage costs at most 10% of campaign compute.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-13 (Known answer; earliest M3 (DC-01, DC-09, DC-10); M4 (the rest)): Each decision core: its table regenerates bit-identically; its null calibration holds (PO-ATEL-01 to -05, -10); its fallback is demonstrated by forcing each trigger; its shadow run records both actions.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-14 (Known answer; earliest M4): The nine interference tests of §28.19.3 pass on a replayed campaign.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-16 (Known answer; earliest M4): The cascade end to end on a seeded population: stage controls all run; the report prints every mode, every fallback and the phase ledger; merge of two agents' archives in both orders gives identical bytes.
- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-08 (Design comparison; earliest M4): Maps per dispatch: on the benchmark of §39, campaigns reach the acceptance target in at most half the serial rounds of one-design-per-turn search at equal compute, with compute reported beside rounds.
- [ ] Each crate above ships `CONTRACT.md` with its ten sections and `contract.toml`; `xtask check-contracts` reports its line count under 115% of its cap.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-62, WP-28): Design campaigns for the cascade and the surface A/B. Class asked: E2 per campaign. If
  not approved: Campaigns sized to the default, with fewer briefs and wider intervals. Nothing above E0 starts before
  the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-RANK** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-ATEL-07 fails for every morphology class tested. Fallback, named in
  advance: R1 is `uninformative` everywhere and every candidate that survives static analysis is scored on the higher
  rung; the ladder's claim shrinks to stopping and elimination among trained candidates; §39 records the lost factor.
- **KILL-LADDER** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-ATEL-08 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: The
  fixed halving table is the allocation rule; the audit lane is kept as measurement.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-SKEL-CELL (the composed screen from
  capability tables): top-10 recall below 90% or ρ below 0.75 means no automatic discard from the screen, and one
  unsound exclusion suspends the exclusion rule; fallback, the tables remain a sizing report. KILL-AUGUR-BRACKET
  (utility brackets): one false stop, or less than 3 times at matched decisions, or the label changes the agent's next
  action in under a tenth of comparisons; fallback, brackets are advisory labels and no stop is automatic.
  KILL-BUDGETCURVE (budget curves): the pilot shows no crossing, or regret at equal compute falls by less than 30%;
  fallback, fixed-budget scores.
- **Every decision core has four obligations**: its table regenerates bit-identically, its null calibration holds, its
  fallback is demonstrated by forcing each trigger, and its shadow run records both actions.
- Merging two agents' archives in both orders must give identical bytes (GATE-ATEL-16).
