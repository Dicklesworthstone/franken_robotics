## Background

**The row.** WP-63: Revalidation, the memo graph and quotienting. Crates: `frob-verbs`, `frob-chronicle`,
`frob-phylum`. Milestone: M3, M4. Key gates: GATE-ATEL-11, -12; GATE-PHYLUM-01, -05, -06, -07; INT-FSIM-16;
INT-FSQL-02. Waits for: WP-43, WP-44.

A large share of a design campaign repeats work: the same analysis on an unchanged subterm, the same episode at the
same address, the same design up to a symmetry, the same verdict after an amendment that does not touch it. The
package delivers the memo graph in front of analyses with shadow recompute, memoized episodes under class D2 on one
fingerprint, campaign quotienting (the similarity canonical form, orbit representatives, the mission-observable
digest; `phylum.quotient`), the invalidation contract for reuse across designs (`phylum.reuse`), the morphology
generator with `GeneratorId` and `DesignSplit` (`phylum.generate`), the revalidation engine behind `result.revalidate`
and `mission.revalidate` with dependency grades 1 to 4 and polarity, paired finite-difference design gradients
(`phylum.gradient`), and design-family lanes (level P-C).

It is one unit of work because every mechanism here is a reuse rule, and reuse rules share one failure: serving a
result that is no longer true. Each is therefore tested against full recomputation (BET-31, BET-33).

**Order.** It waits for WP-43 (the chunk store and artifact graph that hold memoized bytes and dependency keys) and
WP-44 (variants and cohorts, over which quotients, lanes and the generator are defined). (The order between packages
is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real
dependencies.)

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-QUOTIENT** (decided M3). Trigger: One false quotient survives a repair cycle, or digest classes save less
  than 5% of dispatches on the campaign corpus. Fallback, named in advance: Orbit representatives only; the digest is
  kept as a cache key with no claim.
- **KILL-REUSE** (decided M4). Trigger: Any unsafe reuse in GATE-ATEL-11, or grade 3 saves less than a fifth of the
  recheck cost grade 2 leaves. Fallback, named in advance: Grades 1 and 2 only.
- **KILL-FDGRAD** (decided M4; the pilot runs before funding). Trigger: The pilot's falsifier fires (variance ratio
  below 2 at every horizon of 50 steps or more, or sign agreement below 70%), or no end point is confirmed by a
  specialist. Fallback, named in advance: Rank-based search (CMA-ES, racing); the stencil is kept as a sensitivity
  report for A-16.
- **KILL-FAMILYLANE** (decided M4). Trigger: Measured whole-step gain below 1.15 on the mixed workload, or overhead h
  ≥ f·(1 − 1/r). Fallback, named in advance: The general masked lane only, with shared structure tables kept.
- **A shadow mismatch stops serving within one hit**, and an input missing from a key must be caught by the shadow
  recompute within 64 hits (INT-FSIM-16).
- **The frozen 30-amendment campaign** serves two gates over one engine (GATE-ATEL-11 here and GATE-AUGUR-12 in
  WP-59): every decision must equal full recomputation, with zero unsafe reuse, and unnecessary invalidations at most
  20% of unaffected verdicts.
- **The speedup bound of a family lane** is 1/(1 − f + f/r + h), where f is the fraction of the strong baseline's time
  that is improved, r the factor on that fraction and h the added overhead as a fraction of baseline time. The ceiling
  is computed from the M2 profile before the lane is funded, and the lane is killed if the measured whole-step gain is
  below 1.15 on the mixed workload or h reaches f·(1 − 1/r).
- OPEN-ATEL-3: whether the sibling's recompute, value-of-information and racing crates pass admission on T. Default:
  local implementations against the same known answers.
