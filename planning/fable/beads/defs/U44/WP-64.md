## Background

**The row.** WP-64: Gait orbits with continuation [F]. Crates: `frob-gait`. Milestone: M4. Key gates: GATE-GAIT-01,
-02, -03; INT-FSIM-13. Waits for: WP-27, WP-39.

A periodic gait is a fixed point of a return map, and a fixed point can be followed as the body changes, which would
rank designs by gait cost without training (BET-26). The package delivers the gait atlas with max-plus timing
(analysis A-04, `gait.atlas`), gaits as hybrid orbits with return maps, saltation and Floquet multipliers
(`gait.orbit`), labelled derivatives through events (`gait.gradient`), and continuation of orbits in morphology with
orbit screening (`gait.continue`).

It is one unit of work because the orbit, its stability and its derivative are computed from one linearization across
events, and the screening claim needs all three: the orbit exists, it is stable, and it follows the design.

**Order.** It waits for WP-27 (the baseline controllers that close the loop an orbit is computed for) and WP-39 (the
event-located transactional step, without which a return map has no well-defined event times). (The order between
packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry
the real dependencies.)

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-GAIT** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-GAIT-03 fails. Fallback, named in advance: Orbits serve stability
  diagnostics and warm starts only; the atlas keeps supplying clocks and reward references; no design is ranked by an
  orbit.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package, both at M4.** KILL-FREEPERIOD (autonomous
  orbits; its pilot N22c-35 runs at M3): the section and bordered forms disagree beyond tolerance on more than 5% of autonomous orbits after one repair cycle, or the
  bordered iteration fails to converge on more than a quarter; fallback, autonomous loops are analysed on an event
  section only. KILL-STRIDESEQ (stability under switching between gait modes): the pilot's falsifier holds after one
  repair cycle, or cycle enumeration exceeds its budget on the reference gaits; fallback, no switching verdict is
  issued and multi-mode gaits are tested by finite-disturbance recovery over sampled admitted sequences, class
  `Tested`.
- **A restricted stability computation must say so**: the planted transverse instability must be found, and a
  computation restricted to a symmetric subspace is marked incomplete.
- A gradient through a grazing contact or a fold is labelled or refused, never returned as if regular; no case is
  refused for simultaneity alone.
- If KILL-GAIT fires, orbits still serve stability diagnostics and warm starts, and the atlas keeps supplying clocks
  and reward references; no design is ranked by an orbit.
