## Background

**The row.** WP-65: Energy-budget tier and runtime shields [F]. Crates: `frob-shield`, `frob-control`. Milestone: M4.
Key gates: GATE-SHIELD-01 to -04. Waits for: WP-27, WP-13.

A learned policy needs a bound that does not depend on the policy (BET-25). The package delivers the energy-budget
safety tier `.fenergy` with the exact-integer monitor `K-energy-monitor` and its proof kind (`shield.budget`), runtime
shields with a backup controller (`shield.fit`, `shield.check`), shields evaluated on in-envelope and shifted worlds,
and the measured shield margin with its drift alarm (`shield.report`).

It is one unit of work because the two tiers answer one question at two strengths: the energy tier is an exact
statement under listed premises, and the shield is a calibrated statement on a family of worlds, and each is honest
only with its premises and its coverage printed.

**Order.** It waits for WP-27 (control programs and the backup controller a shield falls back to) and WP-13 (the
energy ledger against which the budget is audited). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in
`deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-ENERGY** (decided M4). Trigger: An exceedance under the premises survives one repair cycle at GATE-SHIELD-01.
  Fallback, named in advance: The claim is withdrawn; the monitor remains as an alarm of class `Estimated`.
- **KILL-SHIELD** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-SHIELD-03 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: The
  energy tier stays; barrier rows become reward penalties and alarms; nothing is called a shield.
- **OPEN-SHIELD-2**: the calibration level α and the per-episode violation budget. Default: α = 10⁻³ and a budget of
  10⁻³ per episode.
- **Premises are checked, not assumed**: a budget with a planted unlisted spring is refused, and a payload-only budget
  must show an exceedance.
- **Shifted worlds are reported separately** and never pooled into the in-envelope bound; a shield must keep at least
  85% of unshielded return at no more than 5% added step cost.
- Nothing here authorizes operation of a physical robot: every artifact carries the flags, all false.
