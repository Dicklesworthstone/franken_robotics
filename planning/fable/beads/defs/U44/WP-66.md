## Background

**The row.** WP-66: Mission queries at scale and region certificates [F]. Crates: `frob-mission`, `frob-cert`,
`frob-lemma`. Milestone: M4. Key gates: GATE-AUGUR-14, -15, -16, -19; GATE-LEMMA-04. Waits for: WP-59, WP-44.

M1 answered mission queries on CPU fixtures; M4 answers them over design families on the GPU without giving up
exactness. The package delivers the ceiling-before-speed check on every registered speed comparison, mission queries
over families on the GPU (the 65,536-row screen and a 1,024-design family batch under the exact-integer or guarded
kernel class), quotients, checked cuts and cell refinement in family search, region certificates on small sub-boxes,
product-system dominance, label queries on supplied command sets, and mission bundles for family answers re-checked on
a second machine.

It is one unit of work because scale must not change the answer: the inner, excluded and unresolved labels must be
identical on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, with zero final errors against exact CPU arithmetic, and every certified
cell still travels in a bundle the offline auditor accepts.

**Order.** It waits for WP-59 (the amended-query machinery and the region-certificate pilot's decision) and WP-44 (the
variants and cohorts in which a family batch runs). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in
`deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Test plan

None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-AUGUR-CUTS** (decided M4). Trigger: One false cut on the frozen catalog, or less than 70% of evaluation cost
  saved; or one quotient merge with different replayed traces. Fallback, named in advance: Cuts off: exhaustive
  evaluation with the static screen as an ordering heuristic. Quotient off: memoize on full model identity only.
- **KILL-AUGUR-DOMINANCE** (decided M4). Trigger: One mislabelled joint outcome, or a conventional reachability tool
  closes the same cells at similar cost. Fallback, named in advance: Paired comparison by replay under the studies of
  §24; the product lowering is kept only as the rule for space-indexed shared inputs.
- **KILL-AUGUR-LABELS** (decided M4). Trigger: Deriving command sets costs more than labelling every part, or an inner
  set contains a failing command. Fallback, named in advance: The query is kept for supplied sets through a
  conventional solver; extraction from contact is parked.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-WEAKVAR (weak-variable enclosure for
  constraints that read many parameters): the comparison of its gate fails, or more than 90% of the volume of
  constraints that read more than six parameters stays unresolved at budget. Fallback: such a constraint receives no
  region certificate and is checked pointwise.
- **KILL-COUPLING** (§25.29; its first clause is read here at M4 by N25ex-01). Trigger: one violated transported
  bound that survives a repair cycle, or no skill on the tray fixture with a budget below its slack. Fallback:
  `mission.transport` carries universal claims only, and every chance or expected-value claim is re-measured on the
  target in the qualification lane (§24.13). The coupling budgets themselves are carried by this package's claim-kind
  bead; GATE-AUGUR-25 is a late gate and is not listed above.
- **Ceiling before speed.** Every registered speed comparison records the measured f, r and h on the strong baseline
  and is refused when 1/(1 − f + f/r + h) is below the claimed ratio; with h = 0.02 no r reaches ten-fold unless f is
  at least 0.92.
- **KILL-AUGUR-REGION** was decided at M3 on the pilot (WP-59); if it fired, the region-certificate bead of this
  package closes as superseded and family answers use dual witnesses, monotone bounds and sampled coverage labelled
  `Estimated`.
- One mislabelled joint outcome, one false cut or one quotient merge with different replayed traces fires the
  corresponding kill gate.
