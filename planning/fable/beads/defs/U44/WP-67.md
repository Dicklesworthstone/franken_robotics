## Background

**The row.** WP-67: Synthesis lanes: co-design, trajectory optimization, bounded exact linkages [F]. Crates:
`frob-synth`, `derive/frob-derive`. Milestone: M4. Key gates: GATE-AUGUR-17, -18. Waits for: WP-66, WP-05.

A mission query can be answered constructively: by producing the design. The package delivers three lanes behind
`mission.synthesize`: the co-design fixed-point lane (strategy `codesign`), bounded exact linkage synthesis through
the derivation subprocess that emits a `.fmodule` (strategy `linkage`), and trajectory optimization with design
variables (strategy `refine`).

It is one unit of work because the lanes share one crate and one acceptance route: whatever a lane proposes is
accepted only by the referee and the mission checker, never on the lane's own word.

**Order.** It waits for WP-66 (family queries and certificates, which accept or reject what a lane proposes) and WP-05
(the derivation toolchain that the exact linkage lane reaches as a subprocess). (The order between packages is
recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real
dependencies.)

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M4: BD-360 The co-design fixed-point lane of `frob-synth` (`mission.synthesize` with strategy `codesign`); BD-361
  Bounded exact linkage synthesis through the derivation subprocess, emitting a `.fmodule` …; BD-362 Trajectory
  optimization with design variables (strategy `refine`)
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The package is Frontier. Pilots on
record first: mass placement in a chassis volume and robustness to the loss of one actuator, by hand; monotone
co-design on a 20-variant sweep of wheel radius, mass and gearbox; trajectory optimization with design variables, 20
candidates per mission from an external solver; bounded exact linkage synthesis on 240 three-pose dyad instances
against multi-start numerical optimization; and placement and sizing along a prescribed motion on the rear-drive cart
and one quadruped.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN** (decided M4). Trigger: The lane mis-ranks more than 20% of top designs against the referee,
  or prunes one independently feasible design. Fallback, named in advance: Restrict to the closure analysis A-10 for
  actuator, battery and compute selection.
- **KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE** (decided M4). Trigger: A certified linkage binds or changes circuit on the referee, or more
  than 25% of template instances exceed the caps. Fallback, named in advance: Multi-start numerical optimization of
  linkage dimensions with the same circuit and clearance check, classed `Tested`.
- **KILL-SYNTH-TRAJOPT** (decided M4). Trigger: GATE-AUGUR-18 fails, or solves exceed the budget at 12 degrees of
  freedom. Fallback, named in advance: Continuous optimization of §28 with the baseline controller in the inner loop.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-SYNTH-REGRESSOR (the placement lane): the
  design-comparison part of its gate fails, or more than 15% of designs infeasible on every library motion pass the
  mission in closed loop, in which case the motion-count screening signal is withdrawn first. Fallback: the
  mass-placement program and analysis A-24 as they stand, with candidates started from class templates.
- **Zero certified linkages may change circuit on the referee** across 240 instances; one that binds or changes
  circuit fires KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE.
- If the symbolic foundation is not available at M4, the linkage lane is the numerical fallback of its kill gate from
  the start, classed `Tested`.
- The co-design fixed point must equal its closed form and must reject the additive-peak rule on the bus fixture
  (GATE-AUGUR-17).
