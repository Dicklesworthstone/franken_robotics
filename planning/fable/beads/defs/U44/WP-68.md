## Background

**The row.** WP-68: Ground: admission, clocks, identification, frozen predictions, bands, the kit [F]. Crates:
`frob-ground`. Milestone: M1 (the reality line), M5. Key gates: GATE-GROUND-01 to -09; GATE-GROUND-10 when data is
admitted. Waits for: WP-13, WP-41.

Simulation claims and claims about the physical world are different claims, and this package is the only door between
them. It delivers, at M1, the `reality:` line on every report, which reads `no-claim` when no dataset has been
admitted; at M3 the published-anchor comparison under the Parity label; and at M5 the calibration lane: data admission
with the dataset manifest `frob.ground.dataset/1`, cells and sealed validation and holdout splits (`ground.ingest`),
the synthetic rehearsal with planted parameters, clock fitting, identifiability analysis and excitation design
(`ground.excite`), identification with actuators first and card updates from logs (`ground.identify`), frozen
predictions with sealed trials and holdout retirement (`ground.predict`, `ground.score`), conformal reality bands per
calibrated cell with decision core DC-07 (`ground.band`), drift detection that retires a calibration, as-built
ingestion, and the replication kit (`ground.kit`).

It is one unit of work because a gate may need only what a simulator has: every gate here runs on the synthetic
rehearsal, and the one gate that needs real data is an import lane whose resting state is `no-claim`.

**Order.** It waits for WP-13 (the reports and telemetry that carry the line) and WP-41 (tasks and requirements, which
define the cells a prediction is frozen for). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-ground` (layer 5; Frontier; cap 10): Calibration against measurements: clock fitting, identifiability, frozen
  predictions, reality bands.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M1.** The `reality:` line and its negative battery of 50 planted misuses, all refused; GATE-GROUND-01, which then
  recurs at every exit.
- **M5.** The calibration lane on the synthetic rehearsal; GATE-GROUND-02 to -09; the Reality lane's first record, or
  a recorded no-claim, which is the exit condition of M5; five kill decisions. GATE-GROUND-10 is tied to no milestone
  and is recorded when data is admitted.

Out of scope: the bench rigs that would supply real data (WP-69); real-place ingestion of a building (WP-32); the
structure loop that consumes as-built data (WP-70).

acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-GROUND** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-GROUND-04 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: No
  identification; Ground keeps admission, clocks, frozen predictions and bands on direct measurements with prior
  cards.
- **KILL-BAND** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-GROUND-06 or -07 fails. Fallback, named in advance: Bands are withdrawn;
  reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table.
- **KILL-CLOCK** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-GROUND-02 or -05 fails. Fallback, named in advance: Real logs are scored
  on time-free metrics only; no crossing time, latency or delay is reported.
- **KILL-ASBUILT** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-GROUND-08 fails. Fallback, named in advance: Scans are not ingested;
  as-built data enters as scalar bench measurements, each a direct measurement of one card field.
- **KILL-KIT** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-GROUND-09 fails. Fallback, named in advance: The kit ships as an archive of
  inputs, records and hashes, with no reproduction claim.
- **OPEN-GAUNT-1**: which real-robot dataset, if any, is admitted first, with what licence and provenance. Default:
  `reality: no-claim` everywhere, and the second-simulator stand-in is labelled `synthetic`.
- **Freeze before read**: a prediction is frozen before the sealed trial is opened, for every trial, and the three
  protocol mutations are refused (GATE-GROUND-05).
- No band is issued for a shifted cell, a short cell or an unbuilt design; on the rehearsal the mean in-cell coverage
  must be within 0.01 of 19/21.
- A replication kit refuses any input that carries or inherits the private mark, and any asset whose licence forbids
  redistribution (`FROB-REF-899`); there is no override flag.
