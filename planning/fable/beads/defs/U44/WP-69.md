## Background

**The row.** WP-69: Bench packages A to F of §30.17, optional imports. Crates: —. Milestone: None; sourcing may start
at M0. Key gates: GATE-GROUND-10. Waits for: WP-68.

These six packages are imports, not software: physical rigs whose measurements would let the Reality lane issue its
first real record. None is scheduled against a milestone, none gates a release, and each leaves `reality: no-claim` in
place if it never runs. They are: A, an actuator stand (one actuator of a catalogue class with a known payload;
current, encoder, optionally torque; done when a sealed prediction of a held-out trajectory is scored and the card
gains `measured` fields); B, a ramp and material pairs (an incline and a drag sled; slip angle and drag at two normal
loads; done when friction per pair has its interval and the sixth of the eight structural aliases of identification is
shown separated); C, an instrumented cart (the cart and sill fixture with 60 mm and 75 mm wheels; current, encoder,
crossing time; done when a frozen prediction and the A/B ranking are scored on a sealed day); D, one built link (a
printed or machined link with declared fiducials; a scan and a scale reading; done when the as-built loop closes and
the tolerance audit is produced); E, a calibrated release (named robot, surface and task cells; whole-robot logs; done
when bands are issued per cell from held-out predictions with limits stated); F, revalidation (independently acquired
traces for the cells of E; done when each ranking reversal and calibration limit is reported).

It is one package because the six share one rule: the software lane is complete without them, and they are admitted
through it.

**Order.** It waits for WP-68, whose admission, clock, prediction and band verbs are what a rig's data passes through.
Sourcing of hardware may start at M0; nothing waits for it. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`,
never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:

What lands at each milestone:
- **M5.** Nothing is due. The row is tied to no milestone; it is filed under M5 because the lane that would receive
  its data lands there. Each rig, if acquired, lands whenever its completion condition is met.

Out of scope: every software mechanism of the Reality lane (WP-68); building scans (WP-32).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads: none. Each rig becomes a bead only when the owner decides to acquire it (OPEN-GROUND-2).
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** Not applicable in the usual sense:
there is no mechanism to pilot. The synthetic rehearsal of WP-68 is the rehearsal of every rig's protocol, so a rig's
data arrives into a lane that has already passed its gates on planted parameters.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every child bead is closed with a typed reason citing its own test and that test's output; no close reason is shared; no child is closed by the session that implemented it.
- [ ] Every gate below has a row in `registries/gates.toml` (commit, command, output, receipt hash) with status `pass`, or `killed: fallback adopted` with its ADR. `not run` is not a pass. Thresholds are TARGETs until measured.
- [ ] GATE-GROUND-10 (Reality; earliest when data is admitted): The first real cell: a frozen prediction scored on sealed data and a band issued. An import lane: it blocks nothing, and its resting state is `no-claim`.
- [ ] GATE-GROUND-10 is recorded only when real data is admitted; until then its status is `not run` and every report prints `reality: no-claim`. No milestone exit reads this gate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None: no code. A rig's protocol (rig description, excitation plan as data, instrument list) is validated by the
dataset-admission tests of WP-68.
### End-to-end script and logging
None of its own. When a rig's dataset exists, the end-to-end script of the Reality lane (WP-68) is run on it in place
of the synthetic rehearsal, with the same structured logging.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **OPEN-GROUND-2**: whether any bench hardware is acquired. Default: none; packages A to F never run and Reality
  stays `no-claim`.
- A dataset lacking licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty is refused at admission (`FROB-REF-873`),
  so a rig must record them at acquisition time.
- Nothing measured on a rig authorizes operation or fabrication; the flags stay false.
- The epic stays open at backlog priority while OPEN-GROUND-2 keeps its default. It is closed as not pursued only by
  an owner decision, and that closes no gate.
