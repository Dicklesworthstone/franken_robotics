## Background

**The row.** WP-70: Foundry I: loads, sizing and the structure loop [F]. Crates: `frob-foundry`. Milestone: M5. Key
gates: GATE-FOUNDRY-01 to -06; INT-FSIM-15. Waits for: WP-11, WP-22.

A design that walks in simulation with assumed link masses is not yet a design: the links must carry the loads the
walking produces, and their real mass changes the walking. The package delivers load cases and spectra extracted from
T2 telemetry (`foundry.loads`), the beam-rung sizing check with analysis A-15 structural margin (`foundry.size`),
stress, buckling, modes and cumulative fatigue damage, topology optimization of links (`foundry.optimize`), and
inertia fed back into the model as a typed edit with a convergence test.

It is one unit of work because it is a loop and is accepted as one: loads, sizing, optimization, feedback of inertia,
and a fixed point that must be reached or refused (BET-29, generative structure).

**Order.** It waits for WP-11 (the static analyses that A-15 joins) and WP-22 (replayable telemetry from a trusted
tier, which is where load cases come from). (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a
blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M5: BD-400 Load cases and spectra extracted from T2 telemetry (`foundry.loads`); BD-401 The beam-rung sizing check
  and A-15 structural margin (`foundry.size`); INT-FSIM-15; BD-402 Stress, buckling, modes and cumulative fatigue
  damage; BD-403 Topology optimization of links (`foundry.optimize`); BD-404 Inertia fed back into the model as a
  typed edit, with the convergence test
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** The package is Frontier. The stage-2
pilot is its own gate, GATE-FOUNDRY-05: a mass saving of at least 7% is the kill threshold and 15% the target, with
convergence within five iterations and a coarse solve of at most 120 s per link class. It is run before the
three-dimensional optimizer is built out.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-LOADS** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-FOUNDRY-02 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in advance: Load
  cases are declared by hand from statics with a stated dynamic factor; spectra and fatigue read `no-claim`.
- **KILL-TOPOPT** (decided M5). Trigger: GATE-FOUNDRY-05 fails: saving under 7%, no convergence, or over budget.
  Fallback, named in advance: Beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback; three-dimensional optimization
  becomes an offline option or is dropped; A-15 is unaffected.
- **Two planted shortcuts must be refused**: the assembled-peak mutation and the equal-limb mutation; and
  superposition must equal direct solves (proof obligation PO-FOUNDRY-01).
- **The feedback loop can diverge or cycle**; both are refused, and the planted-gain loop must reach its fixed point
  within 10⁻⁶.
- The window-split mutation of the fatigue count reproduces its error of 10.7% and must be caught.
