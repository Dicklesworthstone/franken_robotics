## Background

**The row.** WP-73: Living worlds II: advected films, slosh, articulated proxies; coupled islands [F], [M]. Crates:
`frob-scene`, `frob-contact`, `frob-fsim`. Milestone: M6. Key gates: GATE-HAB-07, -08, -09; INT-FSIM-17. Waits for:
WP-53.

Real floors get wet where a robot has walked, pots slosh, cords and rugs move. The package holds four epics that make
such failures visible: robot-advected films (with the admission of the multiphysics bridge, INT-FSIM-17), payload
slosh by an equivalent mechanical model of sprung masses, articulated proxies (a cord as a chain of links, a rug as
hinged plates), and Cosserat rods, cloth and coupled multiphysics islands on the CPU referee for finalists only (with
the cloth oracle INT-FTX-01 run as a worker process).

It is one package because the four are decided by one question, measured by one pilot: how many of the failures of a
full deformable model do cheap proxies already reproduce. If the answer is at least 90%, the islands are not built.

**Order.** It waits for WP-53: static film patches under the film-friction law and rigid proxies are the Solid
baseline that each epic must beat. (The order between packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked
epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real dependencies.)

## Scope

Crates and other units, with layer, tier of contents, scope cap (thousands of lines, DESIGN; 115% opens a scope
review) and role:
- `frob-scene` (layer 2; Solid, Frontier; cap 28): Scene packs, the scene compiler, terrain and interior generators,
  import and acceptance; optical and fixture properties; clearance complexes; the retrofit catalog; living-world
  state.
- `frob-contact` (layer 3; Solid, Frontier; cap 22): Collision geometry, detection, contact laws (convex compliant,
  the four-dimensional cone, joint rows, film friction, the hard-contact law), wheels, tracks, terrain mechanics.
- `frob-fsim` (layer 0; Solid; cap 20): The airlock: adapters to FrankenSim's volatile crates in feature groups; the
  `remote` mode and the worker binary.

What lands at each milestone:
- **M6.** For each funded epic its plan, pilot, kill decision and then the mechanism behind a feature flag;
  GATE-HAB-07 and -08; GATE-HAB-09, which reports the proxy-adequacy number before the island epic; INT-FSIM-17;
  INT-FTX-01. Default order of funding: advected films, payload slosh, cable and rug proxies, then rods, cloth and
  islands.

Out of scope: the Solid film law and rigid proxies (WP-53); scene dressing and populated interiors (WP-32).

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M6: BD-413 Epic: robot-advected films; INT-FSIM-17; BD-414 Epic: payload slosh by an equivalent mechanical model;
  BD-415 Epic: articulated proxies, a cord as a chain of links and a rug as hinged plates; BD-416 Epic: Cosserat rods,
  cloth and coupled multiphysics islands on the CPU referee, for finalists …
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** Frontier: advected films, slosh,
articulated proxies. Moonshot: coupled islands and far-field reuse across forks. Pilots on record before each epic is
funded: tracked low-friction length over the admitted parameter ranges (films); first-mode frequency over the admitted
container range against the step-frequency band (slosh); the proxy-adequacy pilot on a pinned incumbent, the share of
full-model failures the proxies reproduce (islands); and a two-room thin-film grid on the CPU with 100 forks
(far-field reuse).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** No request above the default E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device) is registered. A run that
  needs more is refused (`FROB-REF-761`) until a request with its class and its not-approved fallback is registered
  and approved (N44-01).
- **KILL-ADVECT** (decided M6, with GATE-HAB-07). Trigger: The advected film fails its mass gate after one repair
  cycle, or costs more than 15% of the step, or design rankings on the spill battery agree with static patches at a
  rank correlation of 0.95 or above. Fallback, named in advance: Static film-friction patches under the law of §18;
  advection is not built.
- **KILL-SLOSH** (decided M6). Trigger: The frequency gate fails after one repair cycle, or sprung masses change no
  ranking on the carried-container battery. Fallback, named in advance: Rigid ballast, with a spill metric computed
  from payload acceleration.
- **KILL-PROXY** (decided M6). Trigger: The proxy pilot's falsifier fires (Solid proxies already reproduce the
  failures of the articulated ones), or the cost exceeds its target with no route to less. Fallback, named in advance:
  Solid proxies in training batches; articulated proxies in evaluation batches only, or not at all.
- **KILL-LIVING** (decided M6, before the island work package). Trigger: The proxies reproduce at least 90% of the
  full-model failures (GATE-HAB-09), or an island costs more than 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity with no route to
  less. Fallback, named in advance: The Solid tier and the Frontier proxies are the product; coupled islands are not
  built; reports say deformable matter is represented by proxy.
- **Kill gates from later subsections, decided inside this package.** KILL-FARFIELD (far-field field reuse across
  forks): one digest mismatch that survives a repair cycle, or the pilot's falsifier, or no admitted living-world
  fixture reaches a reuse share of at least 0.8. Fallback: full-replay forks, which are exact and need no dependency
  tracking.
- **Per-world mutable scene state** (film thickness, ruts) must not leak across worlds: no cross-world leak in 10⁶
  world-episodes, or batch-composition invariance is lost.
- An island is not built if it costs more than 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity with no route to less.
- The bridge must refuse when its feature is off and must refuse a throughput-tier request: coupled physics is for the
  referee and finalists only.
