## Background

**The row.** WP-76: Sealed suite, rival arms and the freeze. Crates: `frob-conformance`. Milestone: M7. Key gates:
GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04, -05, -11, -12. Waits for: WP-60, WP-62, WP-66.

The tournament measures the thesis against the strongest composed rival, and its value lies in what is fixed before
anyone looks. The package holds three epics: the sealed suite (missions in named strata, sealed worlds including
externally authored ones, the crossover reuse count); the tournament decision rule (the pooled ratio of restricted
mean times, 24 mission families per cell for a finite-suite result and at least 32 for a population result, with the
reachable verdicts enumerated before sampling); and the incumbent arms (the strongest composed rival at pinned
versions, cold and warm, including a short robust program over an incumbent engine).

It is one unit of work because it is the freeze: the six steps of freeze and seal in order, a published manifest and
advocate statement for every arm, the rival audit complete before sealing, and every pin frozen.

**Order.** It waits for WP-60 (the harness and protocol proven at the partial gate), WP-62 (the cascade, which is our
arm's main pipeline) and WP-66 (mission queries at scale, for the strata that use them). (The order between packages
is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry the real
dependencies.)

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row R9 of `registries/compute_envelope.toml`, N44-01, shared with WP-77; owner decision OPEN-SPARK-2): The tournament: 24 mission families per cell by default, and at least 32 for a cell that
  declares a population result (§39.7, §39.20), on both machines. Class asked: E2 per campaign, and E3 where an arm
  pretrains. If not approved: Fewer strata, each declared `not run` with the reason. Nothing above E0 starts before
  the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ** (decided M3; M7, both readings by N39a-11, a bead of WP-60). Trigger: No neutral simulator
  passes GATE-TENFOLD-05 for a stratum. Fallback, named in advance: Cross-home adjudication, labelled; if neither home
  referee can run the other arm's answers, the stratum is `descriptive`.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-REACH** (decided M3; M7). Trigger: A cell's pre-analysis fails a reachability condition of §39.7.
  (The conditions: at least ten paired campaigns per arm; enough mission families that the exact guard, a
  binomial(m, ½) test that the median per-family ratio exceeds 5, can reject at the cell's adjusted level, its critical
  counts at α = 0.05 being 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12, 12 of 16, 17 of 24 and 22 of 32, so a cell of four
  families, whose best tail is 1/16 = 0.0625, is refused as confirmatory; and for a population result at least 32
  independent families, 46 where four confirmatory cells share the level.)
  Fallback, named in advance: The cell is opened as `descriptive` and says so before any sealed run; it is enlarged
  for the next gate.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK** (decided Standing, from M3, by N39b-04, a bead of WP-60; its standing status is reviewed at
  the M3 and M7 exits). Trigger: Any leak from sealed confirmation worlds. Fallback, named in advance: The affected
  cells are void and their families retired; if the extent cannot be bounded the gate is void and is re-run on a
  rotated suite.
- **Every pin and T are frozen for the length of the tournament**; a pin move or a toolchain train voids the runs it
  spans.
- **A changed digest voids an arm**: each arm's frozen configuration must reproduce its development-fixture figure
  within 10%, and the frozen rival must reproduce its home-task training time within 20% on `trj` and win at least one
  easy-win development family per stratum.
- **M5 and M6 do not gate M7**: a sealed stratum that needs a mechanism which has not landed is declared `not run` in
  the report, with the reason.
- OPEN-PROG-2 (the neutral party and the adjudicating simulator) must be confirmed before the suite is sealed, and
  OPEN-PROG-1 (visibility and licence) before the pins are frozen.
