## Background

**The row.** WP-77: Tournament, adjudication and publication. Crates: `frob-conformance`, `docs/`. Milestone: M7. Key
gates: GATE-TENFOLD-07, -08, -13; NS-24. Waits for: WP-76.

This package runs the experiment and publishes it. It holds three epics: our arm (fresh agents on the released
surface, cold and warm runs separated, every phase of every campaign in the phase ledger); neutral adjudication of
accepted answers; and publication of the result.

Its North-Star scenario is NS-24, the sealed ten-fold tournament: missions in named strata are sealed, and some worlds
come from an evaluator independent of both arms; the rival is the strongest composed incumbent workflow, improved by a
competent baseline engineer on development fixtures and then frozen; both arms get the same agent model, token budget,
hardware envelope and prior information, in fresh contexts; a neutral adjudicator releases paired receipts after both
arms close; the report gives, per stratum, the ratio of restricted mean times with its interval, cold and warm
separately, the crossover reuse count, and a phase ledger for each arm that sums to wall-clock; every submitted
certificate is replayed by `frob-verify`, and one false certificate disqualifies that arm from the scoped claim; where
the composed-incumbent arm ties, the report says so.

It is one unit of work because M7 exits on one observable: the measured ratio published with its phase ledger.

**Order.** It waits for WP-76: nothing runs before the suite is sealed and every arm is frozen. (The order between
packages is recorded in `related`, never in `deps`: a blocked epic would block every child, so the child beads carry
the real dependencies.)

## Technical approach

An epic implements nothing: it contains its child beads, which name it in `parent`, and it is where the package's
acceptance is read. Each bead has one package (the owner of its crate at its milestone, or of the crate where its gate
is measured); exit reviews have none.

Seed beads (the table of contents, assigned by crate, title and gate; a seed's own `parent` governs where it differs,
and N44-03 regenerates this list from the graph):
- M7: BD-431 Epic: our arm: fresh agents on the released surface, cold and warm runs separated; BD-432 Epic: neutral
  adjudication of accepted answers; BD-433 Epic: publication of the result
- Beads drafted from the chapters for what no seed covers (`N<chapter>-<nn>`: mechanism parts, registries, end-to-end
  tests, pilots, kill-gate decisions) attach through their own `parent` field.

**Frontier and Moonshot content, and the pilot that must be on record first.** None. No mechanism is built or killed
at M7; three written rules are applied.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the children's tests run beside the code with `cargo test -p <crate>`, without mocks. The package's
structural tests are in N44-04 (`wp_children_have_one_parent`, `wp_gate_ids_exist`, `wp_tier_tag_has_pilot`).
### End-to-end script and logging
The children's scripts under `scripts/e2e/` demonstrate the package through the real `frob` binary, with the
structured logs of STD-E2E. `scripts/e2e/wp_table.sh` (N44-05) prints this row with its children, the open count and
each gate's status, and fails if a gate above has no record when the package is declared complete.
### Known answers and negative controls
Carried by the children and their chapter registries. A gate above counts only when its registered negative control
has been shown to fail (GATE-GAUNT-01).

## Considerations

- **Envelope.** The package needs more than the default machine envelope E0 (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device).
  Request (row WP-76, WP-77): The tournament: 24 mission families per cell by default, and at least 32 for a cell that
  declares a population result (§39.7, §39.20), on both machines. Class asked: E2 per campaign, and E3 where an arm
  pretrains. If not approved: Fewer strata, each declared `not run` with the reason. Nothing above E0 starts before
  the owner's written approval (N44-01); a job above the class in force is refused before it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).
- **KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED** (decided M3 for the classes run; M7). Trigger: On a confirmatory cell R̂ is below 3, or at
  the partial gate the futility monitor stops the cell. Fallback, named in advance: The speed claim for that class is
  withdrawn from §2, the README and every report; speed-only work packages for the class are re-planned; the product
  is described for that class by the capabilities of §39.10 that do not depend on speed, and by its measured ratio.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-TIE** (decided M3; M7). Trigger: On a stratum, arm C reaches the same accepted scope with a
  restricted mean time within a factor of 1.5 of arm A's; or the composed incumbent's recovery ratio is at least 0.8
  on elapsed time and on compute (BET-14, §39.22). Fallback, named in advance: Say so; ship the layer over that engine
  for that stratum behind the same verbs, schemas and checker; keep engine work for the engine-internal gaps only (two
  documented, two unverified: §39.10); spend the research budget on the first mission the program cannot resolve.
- **KILL-TENFOLD-CERT** (decided Standing, from M3). Trigger: One false certificate from an arm. Fallback, named in
  advance: That arm is disqualified from the scoped claim; for arm A the proof kind is suspended and
  KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT applies.
- **A tournament is not stopped by an interim number.** It is voided and re-run only for a breach of protocol: a pin
  moved during the freeze, sealed material read by an arm, or a rival arm changed after its freeze.
- **After publication the positioning follows the measured ratio**: per stratum, and by auditability where the ratio
  is under 3. If the replication kit does not recompute every published number, the result is labelled `unreplicated`
  and the word 'ten-fold' is not used.
- The report also states the count of certificates that changed a decision, which answers OPEN-LEMMA-5.
