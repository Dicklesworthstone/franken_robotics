## (e) Contradictions and things that could not be turned into work as written

Each is pinned as a `DX` record in NAB-41 so that the correction cannot regress.

1. **The reproduction archive's digest is stale (H.5).** The text gives 241,568 bytes and SHA-256
   `0326e89d…9a4f`. The file in the working tree is 275,178 bytes with SHA-256 `9620da42…7980` (179 entries).
   Git history holds four revisions; the cited digest is the one two commits earlier (161 entries). NAB-15
   records all four revisions, pins the cited one, and makes every re-run name the revision it ran.
2. **The first-pass list cites bead numbers of an earlier numbering (B.1).** For example the trust ladder is
   cited at two numbers that in the seed set are the self-collision analysis and `explain.run`. NAB-43 drops
   the stale numbers and names carriers by current key.
3. **The "not yet placed" list is out of date (B.8, B.6.3).** It says no chapter contains its sixteen ideas.
   Seven were placed by later-added subsections (closed-form loop closures, far-field reuse, controller-budget
   curves, task audit by foil bodies, the item bank, placement and sizing along a motion, visibility under
   self-occlusion), each with drafted beads. Nine remain, which equals the tally "9 not yet placed" of B.2.
   NAB-49 records the standing of all sixteen and asks the owner for the nine dispositions.
4. **Three ideas of the refuted-or-declined list have no entry in the killed-idea register (B.7 against C.2).**
   B.7 says each of its ideas is treated in the register with the calculation or evidence that refuted it.
   "Multi-agent design debates" (the markets half is in entry 63), "custom stator and gearbox co-design" and
   "live editing in the viewer" have none. NAB-40 enters them as declined records with the reason marked as
   not recorded, proposes a default wording, and asks the owner to confirm. No refutation was invented.
5. **The glossary omits one subsystem (Appendix A against §0.5).** 22 of the 23 subsystem names are defined;
   `Reflex` is missing. NAB-06 adds the row from the subsystem table and a completeness test.
6. **A glossary row describes an earlier state of the hypothesis register (Appendix A against §0.8).** The row
   says "H-01 to H-24: supported, narrowed or refuted"; the register has four states (ACTIVE, NARROWED,
   REFUTED, DEFERRED) and rows to H-29. NAB-05 corrects the row and tests it against the register's states.
7. **Two texts name different robots for one unretained check (H.5 against K.3).** The checks table says the
   CPU incumbent rate was measured on "a reference quadruped at 2 ms"; the review log says "for a humanoid".
   NAB-15 stores the table's wording and requires the re-run to record the model.
8. **A reference to marks that no source carries (H.6 against H.3).** H.6 speaks of public "sources marked M
   or U"; H.3 has 39 P, 92 F, 30 S and 2 U readings and none marked M or R. NAB-14 enters the standard
   references of H.4 (cited from memory) with mark M and the search-summary anchor figures with mark R when
   they are registered.
9. **Lifecycle compression's gate is stated at two milestones (C.1.4 item 12 against C.3 item 7).** The queue
   row says "the gate is recorded at M8"; C.3 says the gate register places gate and kill gate at M6 and that
   the register governs. NAB-21 keeps the queue's wording verbatim with the register's milestone beside it.
10. **Appendix H defines no schema for licences.** The brief asks for a licence per source; the appendix
    records licences only by saying which are not verified. NAB-09 adds `licence` and `licence_verified`
    fields with the values `owner`, `n/a`, an SPDX id or `unknown`; NAB-13 marks the five asset collections
    unverified.
11. **Several named public sources have no URL.** Three are stated as such; others are named by document and
    version only. They are entered with `unlocated = true` and an exact title; no URL was invented (a test
    compares every locator with this unit's text).
12. **Figures described but not quoted.** For a few readings the appendix says what was read ("P for robot and
    step counts") without the value. NAB-13 leaves `value` empty for the first re-read to fill instead of
    guessing.
13. **Counts in the matrix.** A first count of the EV tables that filtered rows by a leading word dropped three
    rows; the tables have 41, 59 and 43 rows (143). All 143 printed scores equal (Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort
    rounded half up to one decimal; every table is sorted; C-01 to C-40 all occur. No discrepancy in the plan.
14. **Priorities.** NAB-49 (an owner decision that blocks nothing) is priority 1 at M0, one step less urgent
    than the default; every other bead follows the default for its milestone.

Nothing in the four appendices was impossible to turn into work. The two largest judgments this unit made are
stated here so they can be overruled: (i) "a bead for re-scoring at each milestone exit" was read as one bead
per exit (nine), each widened from the matrix to all decision records, with a shared procedure bead; (ii) the
killed-idea register's revival conditions (the brief asks for "the condition under which it would be
revived") were written per entry from each entry's "survives" line and the kill gates it names, since the
appendix states the rule but not the 67 conditions.
