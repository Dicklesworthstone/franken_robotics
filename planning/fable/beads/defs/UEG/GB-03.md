## Background

A legged robot must carry its own actuators. Heavier actuators need more torque, which needs heavier
actuators: the mass spiral. Whether the spiral converges is a property of the design, decided by one number,
the loop gain κ. Analysis A-10 (co-design closure, preflight stage 6) refuses a design whose spiral does not
close (FROB-REF-116), and the co-design fixed-point lane of mission synthesis must return the same fixed point
(GATE-AUGUR-17: "the co-design fixed point equals the GB-03 closed form"). Construction C-12 treats sizing as a
monotone map on a partial order, iterated from the bottom element (Kleene iteration) to its least fixed point;
this card is the scalar case in closed form, so that the iteration has something to be checked against.
Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Marrow (the iteration), Atelier (A-10).

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-03.toml`; test `gb_03`; controls. Out: Kleene iteration with its infeasibility witness
(BD-052); A-10 itself (BD-103); the co-design lane (BD-360); the structure loop that adds a second gain (GB-38).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Actuator mass m_a = τ_req/ρ_τ with torque density ρ_τ (N·m/kg). Required torque
τ_req = c·m·g·L (c a dimensionless duty factor, m total mass, g = 9.81 m/s², L leg length). Total mass
m = m_f + n·m_a for n actuators and structure mass m_f. Hence m = m_f + κ·m with κ = n·c·g·L/ρ_τ, so
m = m_f/(1 − κ), and the design closes only if κ < 1. Kleene iteration m₀ = m_f, m_{k+1} = m_f + κ·m_k is the
geometric series m_f·Σ κʲ.

**Substituted (illustrative), with goldens.** n = 12, c = 0.25, ρ_τ = 15 N·m/kg.

**Intuition.** Kleene iteration on this loop is a geometric series with ratio κ. Leg length is not free: every
kilogram of structure costs 1/(1 − κ) kilograms of robot.

**Assumptions.** Required torque proportional to total mass and leg length; one torque density across the
catalogue; actuators as the only mass that scales with load. A discrete catalogue makes the map a step function
with the same fixed-point test.

**Flip.** κ reaches 1 at L = 0.51 m. Longer legs need a denser actuator or a different load path.

**Lane A.** The closed forms in `f64`; the partial sums of the series compared with (1 − κ^(k+1))/(1 − κ).

**Lane B.** (1) `frob-algebra` Kleene iteration on the scalar chain with f(m) = m_f + κ·m, tolerance 10⁻¹²:
converges to 14.584346135 kg at L = 0.30 m in a number of steps consistent with ratio κ (error after k steps
≤ κᵏ·m); at L = 0.52 m (κ = 1.0202) it returns the infeasibility witness, never a number. (2) A-10 on a
`synthetic` twelve-actuator design whose card data reproduce c, ρ_τ and L: passes at 0.30 m, reports κ = 0.5886
and the amplification 2.43, and refuses with FROB-REF-116 at 0.52 m with the repair "shorter leg or denser
actuator" as typed edits.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_03_reference`: the six closed-form goldens.
- `gb_03_kleene_matches_closed_form`: iteration against m_f/(1 − κ) within 10⁻¹² at κ = 0.5886.
- `gb_03_kleene_rate`: successive increments have ratio κ within 10⁻¹².
- `gb_03_diverges_at_052`: witness returned; A-10 refuses with FROB-REF-116.
- `gb_03_discrete_catalogue`: fixed point exists and is the least one (starting above it converges downward
  to the same value only if the map is evaluated from ⊥; the test asserts the from-⊥ result).
- `gb_03_controls`: (a) a single pass (m_f + κ·m_f = 9.53 kg) reported as the answer must miss 14.58 kg;
  (b) κ computed with degrees in place of a dimensionless c, or with g omitted (κ = 0.06), must miss.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02's script runs `frob design check --stages 6 --json` on the `synthetic` design and compares the reported
closure mass and κ with the goldens; logs both values, the refusal code in the divergent case and the repairs.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-077: the budget fixed point by Kleene iteration matches this closed form. KA-262: with loop gain 0.59 each
kilogram of structure costs 2.4 kg of robot (see GB-10 for the 20.8 kg fill error that becomes about 50 kg).
Metamorphic: scaling m_f scales m by the same factor; κ is invariant to it. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The structure loop adds a second gain λ and the combined loop converges at κ + λ (GB-38); this card holds
  actuators as the only load-dependent mass.
- κ close to 1 passes the test and yields an unusable robot (53 times the structure mass at L = 0.50 m); the
  budget lint, not this card, is what refuses it.
