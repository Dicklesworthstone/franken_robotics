## Background

Comparing two designs or two policies is the most frequent decision an agent makes in this system. The
anytime-valid study (construction C-15) evaluates both arms on the same world addresses (same scene, same
disturbances, same seeds), forms the paired difference per address, and multiplies a betting e-process: a
non-negative process that is a supermartingale when the arms are equal. By Ville's inequality the chance that it
ever exceeds 1/α under the null is at most α, so the agent may look after every pair and stop as soon as the
threshold is crossed. This card says how soon that is, so that a study can be budgeted before it runs and so
that an agent sees why pairing matters. Tier: Solid. Subsystem: Arena.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-06.toml`; test `gb_06`; controls. Out: the e-process engine and the study verbs
(BD-124 on the CPU, BD-262 on devices); the promotion table of GB-14; the qualification levels of GB-29.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** For a standardized paired effect δ (mean paired difference over its standard deviation), a
well-tuned betting e-process grows at about δ²/2 nats per pair, so it crosses 1/α after about
N(δ, α) = 2·ln(1/α)/δ² pairs. An unpaired comparison with effect δ/3 needs 9 times as many.

**Substituted (illustrative), with goldens.** α = 0.05, ln(1/α) = 2.995732273553991.

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N | δ = 1 | 5.991464547107982 | about 6 | rel 10⁻¹² |
| N | δ = 0.5 | 23.96585818843193 | about 24 | rel 10⁻¹² |
| N | δ = 0.2 | 149.7866136776995 | about 150 | rel 10⁻¹² |
| ⌈N⌉, the planner's `expected_pairs` | δ = 1, 0.5, 0.2 | 6, 24, 150 | — | count |
| Unpaired penalty at a third of the effect | (1/3)⁻² | 9 | nine times | exact |
| Level of qualification request i | α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] | count grows with ln(1/α_i) | (see GB-29) | — |

**Intuition.** Clear wins stop early. Pairing by world address raises δ by removing scene and disturbance
variance, and that matters more than the choice of test.

**Assumptions.** An anytime-valid study with a bound on the paired difference fixed in advance; a well-tuned
bet; a controlled determinism class, since paired statistics are refused otherwise (a pair whose two arms did
not see the same world is not a pair). The count is an expectation and not a guarantee.

**Flip.** Unpaired comparison with a third of the effect size needs nine times the episodes. Inside the
qualification lane the level is α_i, not α, and the count grows with ln(1/α_i).

**Lane A.** The formula in `f64`. In addition a seeded Monte Carlo of the definition, in the engine's own form
(BD-124): paired differences d drawn N(δ·σ, σ²) truncated to the declared span s = 4σ, margin m = 0, the
e-process Eₙ = ∏(1 + λ·d̃ᵢ) with d̃ = d/s ∈ [−1, 1] and a fixed bet λ ∈ [0, 1) chosen as the maximizer of the
sample mean of ln(1 + λ·d̃) on a grid of 1,000 values over a separate seeded pilot stream of 4 × 10⁴ draws (so
the bet is fixed before the evaluated stream). The formula is the growth rate δ²/2 of an uncapped bet, and it
holds only while the span does not cap the bet: the tuned bet in d̃ units is about 4·δ/(1 + δ²), below 1 only
for δ below about 0.27. Hence two regimes, both asserted:
- δ ∈ {0.2, 0.1} (bet not capped): the **mean** stopping time over 2,000 seeded runs (runs that have not crossed
  by 2 × 10⁴ pairs count as 2 × 10⁴ + 1) lies in [0.9·N, 1.2·N]; measured at plan time 1.03 to 1.06·N. The
  median is not used: a first-passage time is right-skewed and its median sits near 0.8·N.
- δ ∈ {1, 0.5} (bet capped at λ → 1 by the span of 4σ): the mean stopping time exceeds N (measured at plan
  time about 2.6·N, 15 pairs in median against 6, and 1.4·N); the test asserts mean > N and logs the ratio.
  This is the card's "the count is an expectation and not a guarantee" made concrete: a clear win still stops
  early, but later than the formula when the declared span is loose.

**Lane B.** `frob-arena`'s anytime engine (BD-124) on the same seeded streams with its registered reference rule,
the fixed bet λ = ½: its e-values and stopping times equal lane A's evaluation of the same product at λ = ½ run
for run (bit-identical, since both consume the same values in address order); and under δ = 0 it crosses 1/α in
at most 5% of 10⁴ runs of length 500 (measured 3.6% at plan time; reported with its binomial interval).
Surface: `frob study plan <manifest> --json` (N33b-19, CPU at M1; the key is optional for this verb) on a
`synthetic` manifest whose `anytime` block declares `level` 0.05, `margin` 0, span 4σ and `planning_effect` 0.5
returns, in the plan's cost estimate, `expected_pairs` = ⌈2·ln(1/α)/δ²⌉ = 24 with the label
`approximation: gaussian-growth`; the manifests with `planning_effect` 1 and 0.2 return 6 and 150. The same
manifest without `planning_effect` returns no `expected_pairs` field (N33b-19: absent, never a default).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_06_reference`: 5.9915, 23.9659, 149.7866 and their ceilings 6, 24, 150; 9.
- `gb_06_scaling`: N(δ/2, α) = 4·N(δ, α) exactly; N(δ, α²) = 2·N(δ, α) exactly.
- `gb_06_montecarlo_band`: means inside [0.9·N, 1.2·N] at δ = 0.2 and 0.1; means above N at δ = 1 and 0.5;
  seeds fixed in the card file.
- `gb_06_mechanism_runs_match` (λ = ½, run for run), `gb_06_mechanism_null_rate`.
- `gb_06_controls`: (a) a fixed-n one-sided z-test peeked after every pair at nominal 5% crosses under the null in
  more than 15% of runs of length 64 (about 29% measured), which the null-rate assertion must reject; (b) unpaired
  streams at δ = 0.2 (independent world addresses per arm, standard deviation three times the paired one, span
  12σ) need at least 6 times the pairs of the paired stream in median (about 7.9 in median and 9.0 in mean
  measured, against the formula's 9), and a study whose arms do not share world addresses is not accepted as a
  paired study by the engine.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob study plan <manifest> --json` for the three effects (three `synthetic` manifests, one
`[[surface]]` entry each, mapping the card's ⌈N⌉ outputs to the estimate's `expected_pairs`) and asserts 6, 24
and 150 and the label `approximation: gaussian-growth`; logs α, δ (`planning_effect`), the span, the returned
count and label, the bet rule identifier, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the evidence block.
N33b-19's own script asserts the same three calls (its step 5a).

### Known answers and negative controls

The betting e-process row of the mathematics compendium (Eₙ = ∏(1 + λᵢ·d̃ᵢ) with predictable bets on bounded
paired differences; a supermartingale under the null). CX on peeking: a nominal 5% test peeked after every pair
reaches 17.4% after 64 looks (GB-30 carries the exact figure). Determinism: the Monte Carlo is seed-explicit;
streams are generated by address so that lane A and lane B consume identical values.

## Considerations

- The formula is the Gaussian-growth approximation; heavy-tailed differences stop later. The study plan reports
  it as an expectation with that caveat.
- δ is unknown in advance; the plan's use of the card is to show the order of magnitude and the value of
  pairing, not to promise a stopping time.
