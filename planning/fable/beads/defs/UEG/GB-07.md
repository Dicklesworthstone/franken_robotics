## Background

A scene is stored as a sampled signed-distance field in 16-bit samples, cut into bricks. Memory, not throughput,
limits how many distinct places can be resident on a device, and that fixes an architectural decision
(ADR-HAB-05): scenes are shared between worlds and indexed, never copied one per world, and bricks are content-
addressed in a pool so that repeated modules (rack bays, desks) are stored once. Whether deduplication pays is
a HYPOTHESIS tested at GATE-HAB-13, with kill gate KILL-POOL as its fallback (if measured brick deduplication on
the battery is below 1.5, variants keep private bricks). This card is the memory arithmetic that motivates the
decision and bounds what a narrow band can save. Tier: Solid. Subsystem: Habitat.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-07.toml`; test `gb_07`; controls. Out: the scene byte model that lane B calls
(N19c-16, `crates/frob-scene/src/bytes.rs`: this card depends on it, re-derives its figures independently in

KILL-POOL decision (N19c-17, N19c-18); per-world state memory (GB-41).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Samples = V/s³ for volume V and spacing s; bytes = 2 × samples for a 16-bit field. A narrow band
of t voxels around surfaces keeps a fraction φ = min(1, (A/V)·t·s) of the volume, for surface area A. Labels add
about one eighth.

**Substituted (illustrative; the surface areas are assumed), with goldens.**

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home samples | 10 × 10 × 3 m, s = 2 cm | 37,500,000 | 37.5 million | count |
| Home bytes | × 2 | 75,000,000 | 75 MB | count |
| Warehouse samples | 60 × 40 × 8 m, s = 2 cm | 2,400,000,000 | 2.4 × 10⁹ | count |
| Warehouse bytes | × 2 | 4,800,000,000 | 4.8 GB | count |
| Warehouse at 4 cm | s = 4 cm | 300,000,000 samples, 600,000,000 bytes | 3.0 × 10⁸, 600 MB | count |
| Band fraction, home | A = 1,000 m², t = 8 and 20 | 8/15 = 0.5333… and 1 | 0.53 to 1 | exact |
| Band bytes, home | — | 40,000,000 to 75,000,000 | 40 to 75 MB | count |
| Band fraction, warehouse | A = 35,000 m², t = 8 and 20 | 7/24 = 0.29167 and 35/48 = 0.72917 | 0.29 to 0.73 | exact |
| Band bytes, warehouse | — | 1.4 × 10⁹ to 3.5 × 10⁹ | 1.4 to 3.5 GB | count |
| Band bytes with labels, home | × 9/8 | 45,000,000 to 84,375,000 | 45 to 84 MB | count |
| Band bytes with labels, warehouse | × 9/8 | 1,575,000,000 to 3,937,500,000 | 1.6 to 3.9 GB | count |
| One home per world | 8,192 × 75 MB | 614,400,000,000 bytes | 614 GB | count |
| Halving the spacing | (1/2)⁻³ | 8 | eight times | exact |

**Intuition.** Halving the spacing costs eight times the memory, and a band saves least where surfaces are
densest. Memory limits the number of distinct places, not the number of worlds, so scenes are shared and
indexed.

**Assumptions.** Dense or banded storage with no deduplication; a band width that includes brick rounding.

**Flip.** A narrow band saves a factor of one to five at 2 cm, not an order of magnitude. Deduplicating bricks
across repeated modules and variants could change the number of scenes per device by more than the band does.
That is a HYPOTHESIS tested at GATE-HAB-13, with KILL-POOL as its fallback.

**Lane A.** Integer and rational arithmetic (volumes in cubic centimetres to avoid floats: 300 m³ at 2 cm is
300·10⁶/8 samples).

**Lane B.** `frob-scene`'s size estimator, which is the byte model of N19c-16 (`frob_scene::bytes`, the
functions the scene compiler and `sim.estimate` use to refuse a pack before allocating), on a dense box of
10 × 10 × 3 m at 2 cm returns 75,000,000 field bytes exactly, plus a
separately reported directory and label overhead; its model functions give `band_fraction(1000, 300, 8, 0.02)`
= 8/15 and `band_bytes` = 40,000,000 without labels and 45,000,000 with them, exactly. On a compiled `synthetic`
banded scene (volume 10 × 10 × 3 m, no walls, ten horizontal 10 × 10 m planes 16 cm apart: 1,000 m² of
surface) the recount of compiled bricks (`recount`, N19c-16) returns a band fraction inside the model's interval
for t = 8 to 20, [8/15, 1]: brick rounding can only add samples, never remove them. Surface:
`frob scene show <pack> --json` reports `field_bytes`, `band_fraction` and `dedup_ratio` (band bricks ÷ unique
bricks, at least 1); `--bytes` adds the whole recount record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The card file validates with all five fields and the thirteen outputs.
- [ ] `gb_07` passes lane A exactly; lane B reproduces the dense and model band figures exactly and the
      recounted band fraction of the ten-plane scene lies in [8/15, 1].
- [ ] A request for 8,192 private 2 cm homes on a 24 GB device is never admitted, and the 614 GB requirement is
      named: the byte model gives 614,400,000,000 bytes and a resident count of 0; `sim.estimate` (BD-219),
      which the card's test calls, answers with `memory.fits` false and exit code 2 (the estimator reports the
      finding and does not itself refuse), its scene group `memory.scenes` carrying the figure and its repairs
      putting the shared variant first and never offering fewer worlds. The refusal of the same request by a run
      verb at admission, before any allocation, is `FROB-REF-602` (the memory estimate exceeds the device
      budget), exit code 6; BD-219's own tests assert it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_07_reference`: every count above by integer equality.
- `gb_07_band_fraction_caps_at_one`: t = 20 in the home gives exactly 1.
- `gb_07_mechanism_dense_box`, `gb_07_mechanism_banded_planes`.
- `gb_07_eightfold`: the estimator's bytes at 1 cm are exactly 8 times those at 2 cm for the dense box.
- `gb_07_controls`: (a) bytes computed as 1 per sample (an 8-bit field) give 37.5 MB and must miss; (b) the
  600 MB figure attributed to 2 cm (it belongs to 4 cm) must miss the warehouse golden by a factor of 8;
  (c) a band fraction not clamped to 1 gives 1.333 for the home at t = 20 and must fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 compiles the dense `synthetic` box with `frob scene compile`, reads `frob scene show --json`, and
compares `field_bytes` with 75,000,000; logs the pack identity, bytes, band fraction and dedup ratio.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-459 (dense scene memory: home 9 MB at 4 cm, 75 MB at 2 cm, 0.6 GB at 1 cm; warehouse 0.6 GB, 4.8 GB, 38 GB);
KA-461 (8,192 worlds each with its own 2 cm home is 614 GB, 26 times a 24 GB card). Metamorphic: bytes scale
with volume linearly and with spacing as s⁻³. Determinism: integer arithmetic, D0.

## Considerations

- The surface areas are assumed; the measured band fraction of each interior archetype is recorded by the gate,
  and a measured value outside 0.29 to 1 does not fail this card, it replaces the illustration.
- Gigabytes here are 10⁹ bytes; device budgets elsewhere are stated in gibibytes and the estimator prints both.
