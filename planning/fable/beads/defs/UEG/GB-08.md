## Background

The project's headline claim (bet BET-13) is a ten-fold reduction in the time an agent needs to resolve a
design mission, measured on a sealed suite against the strongest composed rival. The measure is the restricted
mean time RMT = E[min(T, H)]: the time T to an accepted answer, capped at the deadline H of the stratum, with an
unresolved mission (a late refusal or a failure) counted at the full deadline. The ratio is
R = RMT_rival ÷ RMT_candidate. Restricted means were chosen over medians or means of resolved missions because
those reward an arm for abandoning hard missions. A consequence follows by arithmetic and shapes the whole
programme: coverage binds before speed does. This card is that arithmetic, and the tournament's decision rule
reads the same bound before any run. Tier: Solid. Subsystem: the ten-fold programme.

## Scope

## Technical approach

**Equation.** RMT = E[min(T, H)] over the sealed missions of a stratum; R = RMT_rival/RMT_candidate. If the
candidate leaves a fraction q unresolved at the deadline, RMT_candidate ≥ q·H, while RMT_rival ≤ H, so R ≤ 1/q.
With resolved fraction (1 − q) at mean time t̄: RMT = (1 − q)·t̄ + q·H.

**Substituted (illustrative; exact in rationals), with goldens.** H = 8 h.

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RMT candidate | 88% resolved at mean 0.5 h, 12% missed | 7/5 = 1.40 h | 1.40 h | exact |
| RMT rival A | half resolved at mean 5 h | 13/2 = 6.5 h | 6.5 h | exact |
| R against rival A | 6.5/1.4 | 65/14 = 4.642857… | 4.6 | exact |
| RMT rival B | resolves nothing | 8 h | 8 h | exact |
| R against rival B | 8/1.4 | 40/7 = 5.714285… | 5.7 | exact |
| Cap 1/q | q = 0.12 | 25/3 = 8.333… | 8.33 | exact |
| Share of RMT that is misses | 0.96/1.40 | 24/35 = 0.685714… | 0.96 h of 1.40 h | exact |
| Three more points of coverage | q = 0.09, t̄ = 0.5 h | 47/40 = 1.175 h | 1.175 h | exact |
| Halving the solve time | q = 0.12, t̄ = 0.25 h | 59/50 = 1.18 h | 1.18 h | exact |
| q for R = 10 against rival B at t̄ = 0.5 h | (0.8 − 0.5)/(8 − 0.5) | 1/25 = 0.04 | q ≤ 4% | exact |

**Intuition.** A missed mission costs the whole deadline: the 12% of misses are 0.96 h of the candidate's 1.40 h.
Coverage binds before speed does. At these values three points of coverage are worth as much as halving the
solve time.

**Assumptions.** One common deadline per stratum; a late refusal and a failure both count as unresolved;
correctness is a gate outside the ratio, and one false certificate disqualifies the arm.

**Flip.** Against a rival that resolves nothing, a ratio of ten at a mean solve time of 0.5 h needs q ≤ 4%. A
stratum in which the rival also resolves most missions quickly has no ten-fold in it at any q.

**Lane A.** Exact rational arithmetic (`frob-cert` rationals).

**Lane B.** The restricted-mean and ratio functions of the thesis-arithmetic module (the same code the
tournament's pre-analysis calls): given per-mission records (resolved flag, time), return RMT and R as
rationals and the cap 1/q; the card's missions are synthesized as 100 records per arm (88 at 0.5 h and 12
unresolved; 50 at 5 h and 50 unresolved; 100 unresolved). A record with T > H is clamped to H; a resolved record
carrying a false certificate makes the arm `disqualified` and R undefined.

## Acceptance criteria

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_08_reference`: the ten rationals.
- `gb_08_mechanism`: the three arms from 100 records each.
- `gb_08_bound_property`: R ≤ 1/q for random q ∈ (0, 1], random times in [0, H], any rival.
- `gb_08_no_tenfold_when_rival_fast`: rival resolving 90% at mean 0.6 h gives RMT_rival = 1.34 h and
  R = 1.34/RMT_candidate < 10 for every candidate with RMT ≥ 0.134 h.
- `gb_08_controls`: (a) mean over resolved missions only (0.5 h against 5 h, ratio 10) must miss 4.64;
  (b) unresolved missions dropped from the candidate (RMT 0.44 h, ratio 14.8) must exceed the cap 8.33 and be
  rejected by the bound check; (c) a median-based ratio is rejected as an undeclared estimator.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the card's tests and logs each rational with its decimal expansion. The ratio functions have no
verb of their own before the tournament tooling exists; the script records `surface: none` for this card.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-009: completion cap on the ratio of restricted mean times: R ≤ 1/q; with 12% unfinished the ratio cannot
exceed 25/3 ≈ 8.33. Metamorphic: scaling every time and H by one factor leaves R unchanged. Determinism:
rational arithmetic, D0.

## Considerations

- The card assumes one deadline per stratum. Strata with different deadlines are never pooled before the ratio
  is formed; the pooled estimator is the tournament bead's concern.
- The bound is a ceiling on any claim: a report of R above 1/q for its own q is an arithmetic error and is
  refused at the report boundary.
