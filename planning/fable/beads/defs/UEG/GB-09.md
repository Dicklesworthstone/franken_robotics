## Background

Before any mechanism is funded the programme computes the most each single lever could deliver. Three levers
are tempting: (a) a faster simulation step, (b) a cycle with no training that decides one design per agent turn,
(c) pruning designs with a cheap rung so that few are trained. Each has a ceiling given by simple arithmetic on
measured shares of time, and each ceiling is below ten. The thesis is therefore a conjunction: many designs
decided per agent turn and few trainings (bets BET-13 and BET-17; if the measured denominators lower the
ceilings further, the mechanisms ship as a layer on an incumbent, BET-14). The rule "ceiling before speed" is
doctrine: the bound is computed before the experiment is funded. This card holds the three ceilings.
Tier: Solid. Subsystem: the ten-fold programme.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-09.toml`; test `gb_09`; controls. Out: the calculators (N00-17); the measurement of
the real denominators on the incumbent at M0 and M1 (BD-040, BD-143); the record of the 7.8-to-7.7 correction
(NEG-05).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** (a) A step that takes a share s of training wall-clock: speed-up at most 1/(1 − s) (Amdahl).
(b) One design per agent turn of latency t against an incumbent turn of C + t: at most (C + t)/t. (c) Compute
per decision: C ÷ (c + c_T·f), with c the cheap rung per design, c_T the training rung and f the fraction of
designs trained.

**Substituted, with goldens.** FACT inputs (source register; reread = pending): on one incumbent stack the
nested rates are 94 k, 88 k and 82 k env-steps/s for step, step with inference, and training; on another the
full environment step runs at 347 k env-steps/s against 1.20 M for physics alone; a published training time of
410 s. Illustrative inputs: C = 300 s, t = 60 to 120 s, c = 38 GPU-s.

**Intuition.** Each tempting single lever stops short of ten: a faster step, a no-training cycle that decides
one design per turn, or pruning alone. The thesis is therefore a conjunction: many designs per turn and few
trainings.

**Assumptions.** Fixed sample counts for (a); the same agent latency in both arms for (b); training at parity
for (c). The 300 s and 38 GPU-s values are illustrative. The denominators are measured by this programme at M0
and M1 before any mechanism is funded against them.

**Flip.** A measured phase ledger in which training is a much smaller share than published lowers every
ceiling, and the mechanisms then ship as a layer (BET-14). A cheap rung at 10 GPU-s per design raises the
admissible training fraction to 7.6%.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals.

**Lane B.** The three ceiling calculators of the thesis-arithmetic module on the card's inputs; each returns a
rational and the name of the binding quantity. The calculators accept a phase ledger (a list of named phase
durations that must sum to the wall-clock, else refuse) so that the measured incumbent ledger can be substituted
for the card's inputs without code change; the test feeds a `synthetic` ledger whose shares are 82/94 and
12/94.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_09_reference`: the eleven rationals.
- `gb_09_mechanism`: the same from the calculators; binding quantities named `step share`, `agent latency`,
  `cheap rung`.
- `gb_09_no_f_reaches_ten_above_41`: for c = 41 + 1/1000 and every f ≥ 0 the ratio is below 10.
- `gb_09_monotone`: ceiling (c) is decreasing in f and in c.
- `gb_09_controls`: (a) the ceiling printed as 7.8 with the share printed as 0.87 fails the print-consistency
  check (1/(1 − 0.87) rounds to 7.7); (b) multiplying ceilings (a) and (c) (7.7 × 10.8 = 83) is rejected: the
  two share one denominator and do not compose; (c) a ledger with a missing phase is refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the tests and logs every ceiling with its inputs, their tags (FACT or illustrative) and the
`citable` flag. When the measured incumbent ledger exists the script also prints the ceilings recomputed from
it, labelled `measured`, beside the card's.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-010 (engine-speed ceiling 1/(1 − s): about 7.7 at s = 87%); KA-013 (compute per decision: 10.8 at f = 0, ten
needs f ≤ 0.7%, none above 41 GPU-s); KA-818 (Amdahl limits on incumbent stacks: 7.7 and 1.4). Determinism:
rational arithmetic, D0.
