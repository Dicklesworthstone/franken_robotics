## Background

The design language derives mass and inertia from geometry: a body is a Region (a shape) with a material and a
fill. Decision ADR-SKEL-03 is that no physics field has a silent default, and in particular a body has no
default fill: the author writes `solid` or `{shell: t}`, and a missing fill is refused (FROB-REF-105). The
reason is this card: reading a sheet-metal part as a solid block overstates its mass by an order of magnitude,
and rescaling the density to repair the mass leaves the inertia wrong, because a shell keeps its mass at the
walls. The rejected alternatives were "default solid" (wrong for torsos and covers) and "default shell" (wrong
for wheels, feet and brackets). Proof obligation PO-SKEL-02 requires shell moments to equal their closed forms
exactly (a shell is the difference of two solids), and GATE-SKEL-07 measures it. Tier: Solid. Subsystem:
Skeleton.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-10.toml`; test `gb_10`; controls. Out: the geometry and inertia functor (BD-062); the
fill fields of `.fbot` (BD-055); the mass spiral that amplifies the error (GB-03).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Mass = ρ·V for `solid` and ρ·(V_o − V_i) for `{shell: t}`, V_i being the volume of the Region
offset inward by t. For a box a × b × c about its vertical (c) axis the squared radius of gyration is
k² = (a² + b²)/12 when solid and k² = [V_o·(a_o² + b_o²) − V_i·(a_i² + b_i²)]/[12·(V_o − V_i)] for the shell,
with a_i = a − 2t and so on.

**Substituted (illustrative inputs; exact rational arithmetic), with goldens.** Torso 0.42 × 0.20 × 0.10 m,
aluminium alloy at ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, wall t = 2.5 mm.

| Output | Golden (rational; decimal) | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| V_o | 21/2500 m³ = 0.0084 | — | exact |
| V_i (0.415 × 0.195 × 0.095) | 0.007687875 m³ | 0.007688 | exact |
| Solid mass | 567/25 = 22.68 kg | 22.7 kg | exact |
| Shell mass | 153819/80000 = 1.9227375 kg | 1.92 kg | exact |
| Mass ratio | 11.795681937… | 11.8 | exact |
| k² solid | 541/30000 = 0.0180333… m² | 0.0180 m² | exact |
| k² shell | 716033/30384000 = 0.0235661203… m² | 0.0236 m² | exact |
| k² ratio | 1.3068088906… | 31% larger | exact |
| Mass error | 20.7572625 kg | 20.8 kg | exact |
| Error through the spiral at κ = 0.59 | 20.7572625/0.41 = 50.6274695… kg | about 50 kg | rel 10⁻¹² |

**Intuition.** Reading sheet metal as a block overstates mass by an order of magnitude. Rescaling the density to
repair the mass leaves the inertia wrong, because a shell keeps its mass at the walls.

**Assumptions.** A uniform wall; a box, whose inward offset is a box; internal components modelled as their own
bodies or declared masses.

**Flip.** A wall thicker than half the smallest dimension is a solid written as a shell and is refused
(FROB-REF-105). For wheels, feet and brackets the solid reading is correct, which is why the rule is "no default
fill" and not "default shell".

**Lane A.** Exact rationals from the four inputs.

**Lane B.** Compile two `synthetic` one-body designs through `frob-model` (the same Region, `solid` and
`{shell: 2.5 mm}`); read the body's mass and its inertia about the vertical axis through the centre of mass;
assert mass and I_zz/m equal the goldens exactly (the functor computes box moments in rationals). Then: a
body with no fill refuses with FROB-REF-105 and the repair "add fill: solid or {shell: t}" as a typed edit; a
shell with t = 0.051 m (above half of 0.10 m) refuses with FROB-REF-105 ("a solid written as a shell"); t =
0.050 m exactly is the boundary and is refused as well (the inner box is empty). Surface: `frob design show
<design> --section mass --json`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_10_reference`: the ten goldens.
- `gb_10_mechanism_solid`, `gb_10_mechanism_shell`: exact mass and k².
- `gb_10_full_inertia_tensor`: shell I_xx, I_yy, I_zz each equal (m_o·(·) − m_i·(·))/12 with the outer and
  inner dimensions (the difference of two solids), and the pseudo-inertia is positive definite.
- `gb_10_refusals`: missing fill; shell thicker than half the smallest dimension; boundary t = c/2.
- `gb_10_controls`: (a) a default-solid build accepts the missing fill and reports 22.68 kg: the test asserts a
  refusal instead; (b) density rescaled to 228.9 kg/m³ (= 2,700/11.7957) with `solid` gives the shell's mass
  and k² = 0.01803 m², which must miss 0.02357 m² (the control of KA-261); (c) the shell computed as surface
  area times t (0.292 m² × 2.5 mm × ρ = 1.971 kg) must miss 1.9227375 kg (corners double-counted).

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs `frob design show --section mass --json` on both designs and `frob design check --stages 1` on the
three refusal fixtures; logs mass, k², the refusal code and the repair.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-261 (the torso box solid and shell: 22.68 kg, 1.923 kg, 0.01803 and 0.02357 m², ratio 11.8; exact rational
comparison; controls: a solid default fill, and density rescaled in place of a shell); KA-262 (through the
spiral a 20.8 kg error becomes about 50 kg of robot); KA-263 (a shell thicker than half the smallest dimension
is refused). Determinism: rational arithmetic, D0; identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- The spiral amplification uses the rounded gain 0.59 as printed; with the unrounded 0.5886 it is 50.46 kg. The
  golden states which.
- Non-box Regions (cylinder, sphere, capsule) have their own closed forms under PO-SKEL-02; this card is the
  box case only.
