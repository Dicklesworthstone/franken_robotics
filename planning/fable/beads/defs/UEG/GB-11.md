## Background

A motor winding heats by I²·R, and copper's resistance rises with temperature, so the heating rises with
temperature: positive feedback. The actuator model therefore carries winding temperature with temperature-
dependent resistance (ADR-SINEW-04; the rejected constant-resistance model overstates the continuous rating and
has no runaway at all), and an evaluation states its thermal initial condition explicitly as `cold`,
`soaked(T)` or `steady(task)` (ADR-SINEW-08; the rejected practice is starting every episode at ambient, which
never meets derating). Preflight stage 6 refuses an operating point with no thermal equilibrium or with one
above the limit (FROB-REF-132; repairs: lower current, larger sink conductance, a different gear ratio).
GATE-SINEW-05 holds equilibrium, time constant, critical current and time to limit within 0.5% of the closed
forms. This card is those closed forms on one winding. Tier: Solid. Subsystem: Sinew.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-11.toml`; test `gb_11`; controls. Out: the L2 winding model (BD-072); the static
thermal library (N15a-14); thermal initial conditions (N15a-15); the two-node housing network.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** C·dT/dt = I²·R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)] − G·(T − T_amb), with heat capacity C, conductance to ambient G,
resistance R₀ at T₀ and temperature coefficient α. Let s = I²·R₀·α and P_amb = I²·R₀·[1 + α·(T_amb − T₀)]. For
s < G the rise above ambient settles at θ∞ = P_amb/(G − s) with time constant τ = C/(G − s) and
θ(t) = θ∞·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) from cold. For s ≥ G there is no equilibrium, and θ(t) = (P_amb/(s − G))·(e^((s−G)·t/C) − 1).
The threshold is I_crit = √(G/(R₀·α)). The electrical time constant is L/R. Under torque holding with flux
coefficient k_f (per kelvin) the current needed is I = I₀/(1 + k_f·θ).

**Substituted (illustrative; none measured), with goldens.** R₀ = 0.20 Ω, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K,
C = 120 J/K, T_amb = T₀ = 25 °C, limit 155 °C (rise 130 K).

**Intuition.** Resistance that rises with temperature is positive feedback. It lengthens the time constant,
lowers the continuous rating by 19%, and above a finite current removes the equilibrium. Under torque control
the continuous rating and the runaway threshold are four percent apart (15.9 A and 16.5 A). A 20 s episode from
cold warms this winding by 12.8 K and meets none of it, while the electrical state settles more than ten
thousand times faster.

**Assumptions.** One lumped thermal node; constant current, or constant torque in the last case; a copper
coefficient linear in temperature; fixed ambient. Card values are Estimated unless calibrated.

**Flip.** A housing node in series replaces G by G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha) and lowers every threshold. A duty
whose root-mean-square current is below 18.6 A passes. Evaluation that starts at `steady(task)` instead of cold
puts the cliff inside the episode.

**Lane A.** Closed forms in `f64`; the two torque-hold values by bisection on I₀ (the balance
g(θ) = I(θ)²·R₀·(1 + α·θ) − G·θ has a root at θ = 130 K; and the largest I₀ for which g has any root in θ > 0).

**Lane B.** The static thermal library of `frob-actuate` on a card with these fields: `equilibrium`,
`time_constant`, `time_to_limit`, `continuous_current`, `critical_current`, `torque_hold_tangency`; then the L2
winding model integrated on the referee for 20 s and for 400 s at 20 A (rise 12.81 K and crossing of 155 °C at
384.6 s within 0.5%). Surface: `frob actuator thermal --card <card> --current 20 --json`; at 35 A the verb
returns `equilibrium: none` and FROB-REF-132.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_11_reference`: the fifteen goldens.
- `gb_11_mechanism_static`: library values; `gb_11_mechanism_integrated`: 20 s and 400 s runs.
- `gb_11_two_node_lowers_thresholds`: with G_wh = 2 W/K and G_ha = 1.3333 W/K in series (0.80 W/K combined) the
  static results are unchanged; with G_ha = 1.0 W/K (0.6667 combined) I_crit falls to 29.12 A.
- `gb_11_steady_start`: starting at `steady(task)` for a 15 A duty (rise 45/(0.80 − 0.17685) = 72.2 K) the 35 A burst reaches the
  limit sooner than from cold.
- `gb_11_controls`: (a) constant resistance (α = 0) gives 100 K, 150 s, 22.8 A and an equilibrium at 35 A, each
  of which must miss; (b) starting every episode at ambient hides the limit in a 20 s episode: the test asserts
  the 400 s run does cross it; (c) a wrong sign of k_f (+0.0011) moves the torque-hold limit to 21.2 A and must
  miss 15.9 A.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob actuator thermal` at 20 A and 35 A and compares rise, τ, time to limit and the refusal; logs
each measured value against its golden and the 0.5% gate threshold.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-305 (the card at 20 A: 80 W, s = 0.314 W/K, rise 164.7 K), KA-308 (I_crit = 31.9 A), KA-309 (35 A: no
equilibrium, growth rate (s − G)/C = 0.00136 s⁻¹, limit at 61 s; constant-R gives an equilibrium 306 K above
ambient and 83 s), KA-312 (12.8 K after 20 s). Tolerance of the gate: 0.5%. Determinism: closed forms D0; the
integrated run within its class on each tier.

## Considerations

  its fields.
- The tangency is sensitive to k_f; the bead reports both the root and the θ at which it occurs.
