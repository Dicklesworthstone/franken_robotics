## Background

A brushless motor's torque is limited by current and its speed by voltage: the back-EMF grows with speed until
the inverter has no voltage left. Above that base speed a drive can still produce torque by injecting negative
d-axis current, which opposes the magnet flux (field weakening) at the cost of q-axis (torque-producing)
current. An actuator model without the voltage limit grants peak torque at any speed; one with the limit and
no field weakening grants none above the no-load speed. The verb `actuator.envelope` and the envelope analysis
(A-11, preflight stage 6; refusal FROB-REF-131 for an operating point outside the torque, speed and voltage
envelope) use the model of this card, and GATE-SINEW-06 holds base speed and the weakened torque within 0.5% of
these closed forms. Tier: Solid. Subsystem: Sinew.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Non-salient lossless machine in the rotor frame, electrical speed ω_e, flux linkage λ, inductance
L, current limit I_max. Voltage limit V_lim = V_bus/√2 (the circle inscribed in the voltage hexagon, as used by
this card). Voltage magnitude: ω_e·√((λ + L·i_d)² + (L·i_q)²) ≤ V_lim. Base speed (i_d = 0, i_q = I_max):
ω_b = V_lim/√(λ² + (L·I_max)²). No-load speed (i = 0): ω₀ = V_lim/λ. Above base speed, on both limits,
i_d = [(V_lim/ω_e)² − λ² − (L·I_max)²]/(2·λ·L), i_q = √(I_max² − i_d²). Top speed (i_d = −I_max):
V_lim/(λ − L·I_max) when λ > L·I_max. Torque is proportional to λ·i_q.

**Substituted (illustrative), with goldens.** λ = 0.010 V·s, L = 100 µH, I_max = 40 A, V_bus = 48 V.

| V_lim | 33.94112549695428 V | 33.9 V | rel 10⁻¹² |

| Inscribed-circle conservatism at a hexagon vertex | 1 − √3/2 = 0.1339745962155614 | at most 13.4% | rel 10⁻¹² |

**Intuition.** Negative d-axis current buys speed with torque. At 1.5 times base speed a model with field
weakening delivers 59% of peak torque and a model without it delivers none, because that speed is above the
no-load speed.

**Assumptions.** L_d = L_q; R = 0; linear magnetics; the circle inscribed in the voltage hexagon, which is
conservative by at most 13.4% of voltage at a vertex; bus voltage from GB-13.

**Flip.** λ ≤ L·I_max makes the top speed unbounded in this model. Resistance and saliency move every figure,
which is why the referee enforces the exact hexagon and the full equations.

**Lane A.** Closed forms in `f64`; in addition verify at the weakened point that both constraints are active:
i_d² + i_q² = I_max² and ω_e²·((λ + L·i_d)² + (L·i_q)²) = V_lim² to 10⁻¹² relative.

**Lane B.** `frob-actuate`'s envelope routine on a `synthetic` card with R = 0, L_d = L_q = 100 µH and the
inscribed-circle option: base speed, no-load speed, the (i_d, i_q) pair and torque fraction at 1.5·ω_b, top
speed. With the field-weakening option off the routine returns zero available torque at 1.5·ω_b. With the
exact hexagon (vertex magnitude √(2/3)·V_bus and face-midpoint magnitude V_bus/√2 in the power-invariant
convention this card uses) the available voltage reported by the routine must be at least the inscribed-circle
value at every angle (property test over 360 angles). Surface, in the verb's own form (BD-073): `frob actuator
envelope <card> --v-bus 48V --shape circle --json` returns V_lim, base speed, no-load speed and top speed, and
the boundary point at ω_e = 4,727.03 rad/s with (i_d, i_q) = (−32.22, 23.70) A and both limits binding, which
is a torque fraction i_q/I_max of 0.5925 (the verb takes speeds as `--speeds a:b:n` and a single operating
point as `--point τ,ω`; it has no `--card` or `--speed` flag).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_12_reference`: the eight goldens; both constraints active at the weakened point.
- `gb_12_mechanism`: envelope values; zero torque without field weakening.
- `gb_12_continuity_at_base_speed`: at ω_b the weakened formula gives i_d = 0 and i_q = I_max to 10⁻⁹.
- `gb_12_hexagon_dominates_circle`: exact-hexagon voltage ≥ inscribed-circle voltage at 360 angles, with
  equality at the six face midpoints and a ratio of 2/√3 = 1.1547 at the vertices.
- `gb_12_unbounded_when_lambda_small`: λ = 0.003 V·s (below L·I_max = 0.004) returns `top_speed: unbounded`
  with the model named, never a number.
- `gb_12_controls`: (a) no voltage limit: 100% torque at 1.5·ω_b must miss 59%; (b) V_lim = V_bus (the factor
  1/√2 dropped) gives ω_b = 4,457 rad/s and must miss; (c) the sign of i_d flipped (+32.2 A) raises the voltage
  magnitude above V_lim, which the active-constraint check catches.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob actuator envelope` at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 times base speed and at 6,000 rad/s (beyond top
speed: zero torque) and compares with the goldens; logs speed, i_d, i_q, torque fraction, limits active. The
gate's static rows are not recorded there: `scripts/e2e/sinew_static_sizing.sh` (N15a-26) runs the same card
through the same verb, with the FROB-REF-131 refusal and the 10⁴-point property run, and writes the record of
GATE-SINEW-06.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-315: base speed of the non-salient lossless card V_lim/√(λ² + (L·I_max)²) = 3,151 rad/s electrical, within
0.5% (GATE-SINEW-06). Metamorphic: scaling V_bus scales every speed by the same factor and leaves the current
pair at a fixed speed ratio unchanged. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Electrical speed is mechanical speed times the pole-pair count; the card states electrical values only.
- The lossless model is the known answer, not the model of record: with R > 0 the figures shift and the
  referee's full equations govern.
