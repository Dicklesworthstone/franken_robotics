## Background

Referee episodes are expensive and fast-tier episodes are nearly free. Construction C-25 couples them: run both
tiers on the same world addresses, and use the cheap outcome to cancel variance in the expensive one (a control
variate), or audit a randomly chosen subset on the referee and correct the cheap prediction by the audited
residual, inverse-weighted by the audit probability (a doubly robust correction). The tier-correction ledger
records every audit probability before dispatch. The correction is unbiased for any cheap prediction, so it is
safe to run before the correlation is known; whether it is worth running is a measurement, and kill gate
KILL-DR returns promotion to the plain paired e-process if contact chaos keeps the correlation low. Gates
GATE-FID-08 and GATE-CHRON-09 check the estimator and its ledger. This card says how much correlation is needed
for a given saving and what a poor prediction costs. Tier: Solid arithmetic for a Frontier estimator.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-15.toml`; test `gb_15`; controls. Out: the estimators and the ledger (BD-264); the

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Control variate: θ̂ = Ȳ_hi(n) − β·(Ȳ_lo(n) − Ȳ_lo(N)), n referee episodes paired with the same n
fast episodes, N ≥ n fast episodes in all. At the optimal β = Cov(Y_hi, Y_lo)/Var(Y_lo) its variance is
Var(Y_hi)/n·(1 − ρ²·(1 − n/N)), ρ being the tier correlation. Doubly robust correction:
Zᵢ = mᵢ + Aᵢ·(Yᵢ − mᵢ)/pᵢ with Aᵢ ~ Bernoulli(pᵢ) set by the system and mᵢ the frozen cheap prediction. Then
E[Zᵢ | past] = E[Yᵢ | past] for any mᵢ, and Var(Zᵢ) = Var(Yᵢ) + E[(1 − pᵢ)/pᵢ·(Yᵢ − mᵢ)²].

**Intuition.** Coupling pays only at high correlation, and a poor cheap prediction costs variance and never
bias. The estimator is safe to run before ρ is known; whether it is worth running is the measurement.

**Assumptions.** Audit probabilities logged before dispatch and bounded below by p_min; the prediction frozen
before any high-tier outcome exists; the same addresses on both tiers.

**Flip.** Contact chaos that drives ρ below 0.7 for whole episodes sends promotion back to the plain paired
e-process (KILL-DR). Re-simulating short segments restores correlation and estimates a different quantity,
which the report names.

**Lane A.** The closed forms; plus a seeded simulation of the definitions on a bivariate normal with ρ = 0.9
(n = 64, N = 6,400, 4,000 replications): the empirical variance ratio of θ̂ to the plain mean lies in
[0.17, 0.23]; and for the binary case (Y ~ Bernoulli(½), m = Y ± ½ clipped, i.e. m = ½ everywhere, p = 0.1,
10⁵ draws) the sample mean of Z is within 3 standard errors of ½ and its variance within 3% of 0.25 + 2.25.

**Lane B.** `frob-opt`'s estimators on the same seeded streams reproduce lane A's estimates to 10⁻¹² (same
arithmetic order declared); the ledger refuses an audit whose probability was not logged before dispatch and
one below p_min; the estimator reports, in the receipt fields BD-264 defines (`estimate { method, theta,
interval, level, rho, replaced_count, ess_share, p_min, g_hash, n_audited, N }`), ρ as `rho`, the realized saving
as `replaced_count` against `n_audited` (their ratio), and, where the stratum's ρ_ep is under 0.7 on at least 200
paired addresses or the effective-sample share is under 30%, the switch-off trigger by name (the card's
"fall back"; otherwise the correction stands). Surface, in the verb's own form (BD-264; result reference
positional; a `run` verb, so each call carries its own key): `frob sim run <N16b-06's honest control> --tier
batch-cpu --key gb15-sim --json`, then `frob eval promote r:<that result> --method dr --key gb15-dr --json`,
which reports ρ, N, the number audited, p_min (1/20 by default), the estimate θ̂ with its interval and
`replaced_count`; `--method paired --key gb15-paired` beside it gives the baseline. The surface step asserts the
receipt: `estimate.method` is `dr`, `p_min` is 0.05, 1 ≤ `n_audited` ≤ `N`, `rho` lies strictly inside (−1, 1)
and the interval contains θ̂. It logs, as a metric and not as an assertion, the card's ratio
1 − ρ̂²·(1 − n/N) at the receipt's own values beside `n_audited`/`replaced_count`. The window [0.17, 0.23] is not
asserted through the verb: no stored result carries a planted correlation (N16b-06's `synthetic_tier_pairs` is a
library generator), and one promotion is one draw of θ̂, not a variance; the planted case is asserted by the
mechanism tests on the seeded streams above.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_15_reference`: savings, thresholds, 0.1981, 2.25, 9.
- `gb_15_cv_simulation`, `gb_15_dr_unbiased_with_bad_prediction`, `gb_15_dr_variance_formula`.
- `gb_15_limits`: N = n gives ratio 1 exactly (no extra cheap episodes, no saving); ρ = 0 gives 1; p = 1 gives
  no added variance.
- `gb_15_mechanism_matches`, `gb_15_mechanism_ledger_refusals`.
- `gb_15_controls`: (a) a prediction tuned after seeing audited outcomes (m set to Y on audited addresses)
  biases the estimate; the simulation detects the bias at 5 standard errors and the ledger's freeze check
  refuses it; (b) audit selection by outcome (auditing only predicted failures) with weights omitted is biased
  and caught; (c) β fixed at 1 with ρ = 0.5 and unequal tier variances raises the variance above the plain
  mean, which the reported saving below 1 exposes.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs `frob sim run <N16b-06's honest control> --tier batch-cpu --key gb15-sim --json`, then `frob eval
promote r:<that result> --method dr --key gb15-dr --json` and `--method paired --key gb15-paired`, and asserts the
receipt fields listed under Surface; logs the exact command lines, exit codes, ρ̂, n, N, the minimum audit
probability, the estimate and its interval, `replaced_count`, and the card's ratio at those values (a metric).
The planted case (ρ = 0.9, n = 64, N = 6,400, ratio in [0.17, 0.23] over 4,000 replications) is run by the
script's test lanes (`gb_15_cv_simulation`, `gb_15_mechanism_matches`), not through the verb.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-016 (gain 1/(1 − ρ²): 2 at ρ = 0.7, 3 at 0.82, 10 at 0.95); KA-132 (control variate variance and the
thresholds ρ ≥ 0.894 and 0.949); KA-352 (1.3, 2.0, 5.3, 10.3 times fewer referee episodes). Determinism:
closed forms D0; simulations seed-explicit by address.

## Considerations

- The saving is in referee episodes, not wall-clock; the fast-tier episodes are not free at very large N.
- Segment re-simulation changes the estimand; a report that uses it must say so in its claim line.
