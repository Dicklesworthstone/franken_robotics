## Background

Phylum runs robots of different sizes in one GPU batch by padding every world to a common capacity. Padding
costs work: the largest robot, not the mean, sets the cost of a padded batch. Splitting the cohort into size
buckets (decision ADR-PHYLUM-02: a small fixed set of capacities, at most eight buckets, chosen by an exact
planner) buys back padding at the price of one more launch plan per bucket. The verb `phylum.plan` reports the
padding overhead and refuses when more than eight buckets would be needed to keep waste under the limit
(FROB-REF-741). Kill gate KILL-BUCKET falls back to at most nine padded per-family templates if mixed-topology
buckets fail on devices; GATE-PHYLUM-03 measures the overhead. This card is the pay-off arithmetic. Tier:
Solid. Subsystem: Phylum.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-17.toml`; test `gb_17`; controls. Out: the padding and bucket arithmetic module
(N17a-04); the exact planner (N17a-05, BD-171); the KILL-BUCKET decision (N21b-19).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** With per-world work scaling as Lᵖ (L the size, here degrees of freedom), a cohort padded to
L_max costs Ω_p = L_maxᵖ/E[Lᵖ] times the unpadded work, and 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ for r = E[L]/L_max. With stage
shares w_p of the unpadded step (Σ w_p = 1), Ω = Σ w_p·Ω_p. Splitting into B buckets wins when
N·τ·(Ω − Ω_b) > (B − 1)·c_L, for N worlds, τ the unpadded cost of a world-step, Ω_b the residual overhead after
bucketing and c_L the launch overhead of one plan.

**Substituted (illustrative; shares and c_L are HYPOTHESES), with goldens.** 90% quadrupeds at L = 18, 10%
humanoids at L = 35; shares ½ cubic, 3/10 quadratic, 1/5 linear; τ = 0.5 µs; c_L = 0.45 ms.

| Output | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| r = E[L]/L_max = 19.7/35 | 0.562857142857… | 0.56 | exact |
| Ω₁, Ω₂, Ω₃ | 1.776649746; 2.958222652; 4.495978524 | 1.78; 2.96; 4.50 | rel 10⁻⁹ |
| Ω (weighted) | 3.490786006781391 | 3.5 | rel 10⁻⁹ |
| One bucket, N = 8,192 | N·τ·Ω + c_L = 14.74826 ms | 14.8 ms | rel 10⁻⁶ |
| Two buckets, N = 8,192 | N·τ + 2·c_L = 4.996 ms | 5.0 ms | exact |
| N = 512: one; two | 1.34364 ms; 1.156 ms | 1.35 ms; 1.16 ms | rel 10⁻⁵ |
| N = 128: one; two | 0.67341 ms; 0.964 ms | 0.67 ms; 0.96 ms | rel 10⁻⁵ |
| Crossover N* = c_L/(τ·(Ω − 1)) | 361.33 worlds | 360 worlds | rel 10⁻⁴ |
| Crossover with c_L ten times smaller | 36.13 worlds | 36 worlds | rel 10⁻⁴ |
| Ω₃ with 1% of designs at L = 66 (89% at 18, 10% at 35) | 23.273487930808 | 23.3 | rel 10⁻⁹ |

**Intuition.** The largest robot, not the mean, sets the cost of a padded batch, and each bucket buys back
padding at the price of its own launches. Buckets win at thousands of worlds and lose at a hundred.

**Assumptions.** The stage shares and c_L are HYPOTHESES that the M2 profile replaces; padded slots do full
work; the two-bucket case has no padding left.

**Flip.** A launch overhead ten times smaller moves the crossover to 36 worlds. A tree-sparse factor that skips
masked slots removes most of the cubic term and makes one bucket viable at larger N.

**Lane A.** Rational arithmetic for r and the Ω_p (for example Ω₃ = 35³/(0.9·18³ + 0.1·35³) =
42,875/9,536.3), `f64` for the times.

**Lane B.** `frob-phylum`'s padding module on the size histogram {18: 0.9, 35: 0.1} with the three shares
returns the same Ω_p and Ω; the bounds 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ hold; the planner with buckets {18, 35} against one
bucket {35} chooses two buckets at N = 8,192 and 512 and one bucket at N = 128, with the pay-off inequality
printed. Surface: `frob phylum plan --sizes <histogram> --worlds 8192 --json` reports `padding_overhead = 3.49`
for one bucket and the chosen plan. A histogram of nine sizes with a waste limit that needs nine buckets refuses
with FROB-REF-741.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_17_reference`: the ten goldens.
- `gb_17_bounds`: 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ for p = 1, 2, 3 (1.7766 ≤ Ω_p ≤ 1.7766, 3.156, 5.608).
- `gb_17_mechanism_overheads`, `gb_17_mechanism_planner_choice`, `gb_17_mechanism_741`.
- `gb_17_crossover_monotone`: the pay-off inequality's left side is linear in N and crosses once.
- `gb_17_controls`: (a) Ω computed from the mean size (35/19.7 = 1.78 for every p) must miss 3.49; (b) padded
  slots assumed free (Ω = 1) never choose buckets and must fail the planner test at N = 8,192; (c) launch
  overhead ignored always chooses buckets and must fail at N = 128.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob phylum plan` at N = 8,192, 512 and 128 and compares the overhead and the chosen bucket count;
logs Ω_p, Ω, the per-plan times and the inequality's two sides.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-368 (padding overhead for three populations; for 90% at 18 and 10% at 35: r = 0.56, Ω₁ = 1.78, Ω₂ = 2.96,
Ω₃ = 4.50); KA-371 (the worked card at 8,192 worlds). Metamorphic: scaling all sizes by one factor leaves every
Ω_p unchanged. Determinism: D0; the verb never runs physics.

## Considerations
