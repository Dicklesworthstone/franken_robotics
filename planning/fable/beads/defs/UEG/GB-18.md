## Background

Doctrine: a speed experiment is funded only after its ceiling has been computed on the strong baseline's
critical path. A request for a speed experiment without its computed ceiling, or with a ceiling below its goal,
is refused (FROB-REF-866). The general bound is Amdahl's law with an overhead term. It applies to running a
family of designs in one batch (what the rival pays to switch designs is the fraction that can be saved), to
design-family lanes inside the step (level P-C; kill gate KILL-FAMILYLANE falls back to the general masked
lane), and to the specialized mission solver measured at plan time. GATE-AUGUR-14 checks that every registered
speed comparison of the mission subsystem records f, r and h and is refused below its claim. This card
evaluates the bound on each case. Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Phylum and the ten-fold programme.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** If a fraction f of the strong baseline's matched wall time is improved by a factor r and the
method adds relative overhead h, then speed-up ≤ 1/(1 − f + f/r + h).

**Substituted (illustrative unless tagged FACT), with goldens.**

**Intuition.** The bound is computed before the experiment is funded. A mixed batch is worth what the rival
pays to switch designs, which is unpublished; lanes inside the step are a modest optimization.

**Assumptions.** A serial cost model on the strong baseline's critical path, which also caches graphs, factors
and kernels; f, r and h measured, not assumed. A pruning ratio is never multiplied by a speed-up measured on
work already pruned.

**Flip.** A measured switch cost near zero in the incumbent closes the family-batch gap. Overlapped queues void
the serial model, and the critical path is then measured directly.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals.

**Lane B.** The ceiling calculator of N36-05 (the same function the speed-experiment admission calls): given a phase
ledger of the baseline with the improved phases marked, r per phase and h, it returns the bound and refuses
when the claimed speed-up exceeds it. Fixtures: the two switch-cost ledgers (256 switches of 1 s or 0.1 s plus
10 s of stepping), the constraint-stage ledger, and the solver ledger (842.8 µs composed batch; 575.7 µs
untouched). A speed experiment registered with goal 10 on the 0.1 s ledger is refused with FROB-REF-866 and the
computed ceiling 2.74 in the payload.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_18_reference`: every row.
- `gb_18_mechanism_ledgers`: the four ledgers; `gb_18_mechanism_866`.
- `gb_18_bound_monotone`: increasing in f and r, decreasing in h; r → ∞ limit equals 1/(1 − f + h).
- `gb_18_controls`: (a) a composition that reports 50 for the pruning example (a pruning ratio
  multiplied by a speed-up measured on work already pruned, or the 50 s that remain read as the ratio) must miss
  20: the calculator accepts only total time before over total time after on one ledger (1,000 s over 50 s); (b) 1.345 ÷ 0.94 = 1.4309 must miss 1.46396 and is recorded as the
  withdrawn derivation; (c) a ceiling computed on the weak baseline (no cached graphs: 2 s per switch, bound
  40.2) is rejected because the ledger is not the strong baseline's.

### End-to-end script and logging

The ceiling refusal is raised by the performance harness's campaign admission (N36-05, BD-216: a campaign of
kind `speed-experiment` must reference a ceiling record filed first and not below its goal), and that harness
deliberately has no agent verb (`predict.register` registers predictions of outcomes and is not this path). So
NEG-02's surface step for this card is the conformance test binary: it runs `gb_18_mechanism_866`, which
submits a `speed-experiment` campaign declaration on the 0.1 s ledger with goal 10, and one with no ceiling
record, and reads the JSON status line, expecting FROB-REF-866 twice with the computed ceiling 2.7385 in the
first payload; logs the exact command line, exit code, f, r, h, the bound, the goal and the refusal payload.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-014 and KA-373 and KA-809 (speed-up at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h): 13.5 at f = 0.97, r = 40, h = 0.02; 6.7
for 85% improved without limit; with h = 0.02 no r reaches ten unless f ≥ 0.92). Determinism: rational
arithmetic, D0.
