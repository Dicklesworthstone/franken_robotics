## Background

A design campaign that sweeps module choices over the slots of a symmetric chassis evaluates many designs that
are the same robot turned or mirrored. Bet BET-31 (campaign quotienting) is that algebra spent at campaign time
deletes these simulations exactly and before any run, by simulating one representative per orbit of the
symmetry group and transporting the result to the others with the recorded group element. A second, structural
quotient merges designs that the dynamics cannot tell apart (identical ballast slugs that enter rigid-body
dynamics only through their count and their first and second moments). Kill gate KILL-QUOTIENT withdraws the
mechanism if a quotient ever loses a winner; GATE-PHYLUM-01 checks the counts and bit-identical replay. The
group that acts is the observation-preserving subgroup of chassis and task: one asymmetric sensor takes the
factor from twelve to two. This card is the counting. Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Marrow, Phylum.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-19.toml`; test `gb_19`; controls. Out: orbits, Burnside counts and subgroup lattices
(BD-049, N13-17); campaign quotienting and representatives (BD-285); the projection quotient (N10b-12).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Orbit counting (Burnside): the number of distinct assignments of k module types to a slot set X
under a group Γ is |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), c(g) being the number of cycles of g on X.

**Substituted (exact arithmetic), with goldens.**

| Output | Derivation | Golden | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ten leg types on six hexapod slots under the dihedral group of order 12 | identity 10⁶; two rotations by ±60° (one 6-cycle) 2·10; two by ±120° (two 3-cycles) 2·10²; half turn (three 2-cycles) 10³; three vertex reflections (four cycles) 3·10⁴; three edge reflections (three cycles) 3·10³; sum 1,034,220 over 12 | 86,185 | count |
| Reduction factor | 10⁶/86,185 | 11.6029… (printed 11.6) | exact |
| Only the sagittal mirror left (a sensor broke the rest) | (10⁶ + 10³)/2 | 500,500 (factor 1.998, printed 2.0) | count |
| Three types under the full group | (729 + 6 + 18 + 27 + 243 + 81)/12 | 92 | count |
| Four feet with states open, stick, slip under C₂ × C₂ | (3⁴ + 3·3²)/4 | 27 strata | count |
| Eight equal ballast slugs in 24 collinear slots: placements | C(24, 8) | 735,471 | count |
| Distinct (count, Σx, Σx²) classes | enumeration | 24,546 | count |
| Slots {1, 4} and {2, 3} | Σx = 5 both; Σx² = 17 and 13 | must not merge | exact |

**Intuition.** Algebra spent at campaign time deletes simulations exactly and before any run. The factor is at
most the group order, and one asymmetric sensor takes it from twelve to two.

**Assumptions.** The acting group is the observation-preserving subgroup of chassis and task; results are
transported back with the recorded group element; the projection quotient is a structural rule checked by
bit-identical replay.

**Flip.** A front-only lidar counted with the mechanical group merges designs that the closed loop
distinguishes. A projection that drops Σx² merges {1, 4} with {2, 3} wrongly.

**Lane A.** Integer arithmetic: the cycle-index sum, and brute-force enumeration for the small cases (3 types:
all 729 colourings canonicalized under the 12 group elements give 92 classes; the strata: 81 states under the
four elements give 27); the ballast classes by enumerating all 735,471 subsets and collecting (Σx, Σx²) in a
set (the count is the same for slots numbered 0…23 or 1…24).

**Lane B.** `frob-algebra`: build D₆ acting on six slots from a 6-cycle and a reflection; `orbits` with k = 10
returns 86,185 and a canonical representative function whose image on all 10⁶ colourings has exactly 86,185
elements; the subgroup generated by the one reflection that fixes no slot (three 2-cycles: the sagittal mirror
of a layout with no leg on the mirror plane) returns 500,500. `frob-phylum`'s mission-observable digest on the ballast
family returns 24,546 distinct digests and distinct digests for {1, 4} and {2, 3}; that digest arrives with
campaign quotienting (BD-285, M3), so at M1 this one assertion is compiled `#[ignore]` with the reason
"needs the mission-observable digest of BD-285" and logged `pending`, and it is un-ignored and must pass as part
of BD-285's acceptance (the Burnside, brute-force and lane-A ballast assertions close here at M1). Surface, in the verb's own
form (N13-17): `frob algebra orbits --family D6 --slots 6 --types 10 --json` returns count 86,185 of total
1,000,000; `--family C2 --action sagittal --slots 6 --types 10` returns 500,500; `--family C2xC2 --slots 4
--types 3` returns 27; `--family D6 --slots 6 --types 3 --representatives` lists all 92 representatives.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_19_reference_burnside`: 86,185; 500,500; 92; 27.
- `gb_19_reference_bruteforce`: 92 and 27 by canonicalization; 86,185 by canonicalizing 10⁶ colourings.
- `gb_19_reference_ballast`: 735,471 placements, 24,546 classes.
- `gb_19_mechanism_orbits`, `gb_19_mechanism_representatives`, `gb_19_mechanism_ballast_digest`.
- `gb_19_transport_roundtrip`: for 10³ random colourings, applying the recorded group element to the
  representative returns the original.
- `gb_19_controls`: (a) counting under the rotation subgroup only gives (10⁶ + 20 + 200 + 10³)/6 = 166,870 and
  must miss; (b) a wrong cycle index (reflections all with three cycles) gives 83,935 and must miss; (c) the
  mechanical group used with a front-only lidar gives 86,185 where 500,500 is right; (d) a digest of
  (count, Σx) alone gives fewer than 24,546 classes and merges {1, 4} with {2, 3}.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob algebra orbits` in the forms above for the three hexapod cases and the 27 strata, and
`frob phylum quotient` on the ballast family once that verb exists (M3; `not run` before); logs the exact
command lines, exit codes, group order, cycle counts, orbit counts and the digest count.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-123, KA-233, KA-365 (86,185, a factor of 11.6; control: a wrong cycle index); KA-697 (86,185 and 500,500
representatives; control: deduplication under the mechanical group when a sensor breaks it); KA-127, KA-367,
KA-648, KA-698 (735,471 placements in 24,546 classes; control: two placements with equal first moment and
different second moment merged). Determinism: integer arithmetic, D0; canonical representatives are
lexicographically least and identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- The mirror that survives a forward sensor is the sagittal one; on six radial slots it has either two fixed
  slots (four cycles, 10⁴ fixed colourings, giving 505,000) or none (three cycles, 500,500) depending on
  whether legs sit on the mirror plane. The card's 500,500 is the layout with no leg on the plane; the test
  covers both and the archetype states which it is.
- The ballast enumeration visits 735,471 subsets; it runs in well under a second and is kept in the default
  test set.
