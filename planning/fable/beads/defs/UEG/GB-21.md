## Background

On a wet or greasy floor a foot does not meet the floor at once: it rides a liquid film that must be squeezed
out before the surfaces touch and boundary friction is available. How long that takes depends strongly on pad
size and viscosity, so a friction map of constant coefficients cannot represent the difference between a small
foot on water and a large flat foot on oil. Decision ADR-TRAC-06 adds a film-friction law: Habitat keeps one
film-thickness value per contact sample and Traction turns it into a cone row (kernel `K-film`). Bet BET-36 is
that such living-world mechanisms change decisions; kill gate KILL-FILM returns the lockstep tiers to static
condition cards if the law changes fewer than 10% of episode outcomes on the wet-floor battery. Gates
GATE-TRAC-05 and GATE-HAB-06 both hold the three squeeze times of this card to 2% (the bound of the chapter
that owns the law; an earlier 1% in the scene chapter was reconciled to 2%). A film state outside the card's
calibrated range under `--strict-data` is refused (FROB-REF-242). Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Traction, Habitat.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Stefan's law for two rigid parallel discs of radius a approaching through a Newtonian film of
viscosity η under load N: dh/dt = −2·N·h³/(3π·η·a⁴). Integrated: 1/h² − 1/h₀² = 4·N·t/(3π·η·a⁴). From a thick
film (h₀ → ∞) the time to thickness h is t = 3π·η·a⁴/(4·N·h²).

**Substituted (illustrative; the viscosities are orders of magnitude), with goldens.** Target h = 10 µm.

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water, small foot | η = 1 mPa·s, a = 2 cm, N = 100 N | 0.03769911184307752 s | 0.038 s | rel 10⁻¹² |
| Cooking oil, same foot | η = 60 mPa·s | 2.261946710584651 s | 2.3 s | rel 10⁻¹² |
| Water, large foot | a = 6 cm, N = 400 N | 0.7634070148223197 s | 0.76 s | rel 10⁻¹² |
| Ratio large to small (a⁴/N) | 81/4 | 20.25 | "scales with a⁴" | exact |
| Ratio oil to water | 60 | 60 | "and with viscosity" | exact |
| From a finite film h₀ = 1 mm, water, small foot | t·(1 − h²/h₀²) | 0.03769534193… s (0.01% less) | — | rel 10⁻⁹ |

**Intuition.** The time scales with a⁴ and with viscosity. Against a stance of 0.3 s a small foot on water
reaches boundary friction almost at once, while oil and large flat feet ride a film for the whole stance. A map
of constant coefficients cannot represent that difference.

**Assumptions.** Rigid parallel circular plates; a Newtonian film; constant load; the combined roughness sets
where the film law hands over to boundary friction. Outputs are Estimated.

**Flip.** A textured or drained sole, with a much smaller effective radius, grips at once and the law changes
nothing. If it changes fewer than 10% of episode outcomes on the wet-floor battery, KILL-FILM returns the
lockstep tiers to static condition cards.

**Lane A.** The closed form in `f64`, and a fourth-order Runge–Kutta integration of dh/dt from h₀ = 1 mm to
10 µm with 10⁵ steps in the variable u = 1/h² (in which the law is linear, du/dt = 4N/(3π·η·a⁴)), agreeing
to 10⁻⁹.

**Lane B.** `frob-contact`'s film update on one contact sample: thickness state initialized at 1 mm under the
three loads reaches 10 µm at the three times within 2% on every tier (the law's exact per-step update is
1/h²_{n+1} = 1/h²_n + 4·N·Δt/(3π·η·a⁴)); friction available before the handover thickness is the film value
and after it the boundary value; an input with η outside the interface card's calibrated range under
`--strict-data` refuses with FROB-REF-242. Surface (`contact.probe` in BD-230's film form, quantities with their
units as in the registry example): `frob contact probe --film --eta 1mPa.s --radius 20mm --normal 100N --h0 1mm
--until-h 10um --json` returns the squeeze time with the evidence block (class `Estimated`).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_21_reference`: the six goldens; the integration cross-check.
- `gb_21_mechanism_three_times`, `gb_21_mechanism_handover`, `gb_21_mechanism_242`.
- `gb_21_scaling`: doubling a multiplies the time by 16; doubling N halves it; halving h quadruples it.
- `gb_21_step_independence`: the exact update gives the same time to 10⁻¹² for Δt = 0.5, 1, 2 and 2.5 ms.
- `gb_21_controls`: (a) a constant wet-friction coefficient has no time dependence and gives squeeze time 0:
  the stance-length test (0.3 s on oil must still be on the film) fails it; (b) an explicit Euler update in h
  at Δt = 2.5 ms is unstable or wrong by more than 2% for the oil case's first steps from 1 mm and is caught;
  (c) scaling the small-foot time by contact area (a² in place of a⁴: 0.0377 s × 9/4) predicts 0.085 s for
  the large foot and must miss 0.76 s.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob contact probe --film` for the three cases (and once out of domain with `--strict-data`) and
compares the squeeze time against the goldens with the 2% threshold and the refusal FROB-REF-242; logs the exact
command lines, exit codes, η, a, N, h₀, h, the time, the regime at handover and the provenance class.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-404 and KA-438 (squeeze of a film to 10 µm: 0.038 s, 2.3 s, 0.76 s; proof obligation PO-TRAC-02; tolerance
2% at both gates). Determinism: the per-sample update is exact in the 1/h² variable up to rounding and belongs
to its tier's class.

## Considerations

- The viscosities are orders of magnitude, not measurements; which measured film tables enter the catalogue and
  under what licence is an owner decision (OPEN-TRAC-3).
- Real soles are compliant and textured; the rigid-disc law is an upper bound on film time for a smooth pad and
  the card says nothing about hydroplaning at speed.
