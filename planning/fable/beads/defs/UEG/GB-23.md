## Background

Physics, sensors and policies run at different rates, and the rates rarely divide each other. If each process
is scheduled by a floating-point timer or by "every k-th step", the long-run rate drifts and the pattern of
late ticks is an accident of rounding, which breaks replay and makes a 30 Hz policy silently run at 30.77 Hz.
The clock contract fixes this: every period is a rational number of seconds; the base unit is 1/Q s with Q the
least common multiple of the denominators; each process has an integer period and an integer phase
accumulator; and a firing that falls strictly inside a physics step is handled by the profile's declared rule
(`hold`: realized at the next step boundary, on throughput tiers; a substep boundary on the referee and the
strict profile). The schedule is then exact, declared and replayable, and accumulators and queues live inside
every checkpoint. Gates GATE-SENS-04 and GATE-SENS-05 check it on every tier. This card is the standard example.
Tier: Solid. Subsystem: Sensorium.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Base unit 1/Q s. Process i has integer period Nᵢ = Tᵢ·Q quanta and fires when n ≡ φᵢ (mod Nᵢ).
Under `hold`, a firing at quantum q with physics period N_p is realized at step ⌈q/N_p⌉, with lateness
⌈q/N_p⌉·N_p − q quanta.

**Substituted (exact arithmetic), with goldens.** Physics 400 Hz, IMU 100 Hz, policy 30 Hz.

| Output | Golden | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Q = lcm(400, 100, 30) | 1,200 | exact |
| Periods in quanta (physics, IMU, policy) | 3, 12, 40 | exact |
| Policy instants (quanta) | 0, 40, 80, 120 | exact |
| Realized at physics steps | 0, 14, 27, 40 | exact |
| Realized at quanta | 0, 42, 81, 120 | exact |
| Lateness (quanta; ms) | 0, 2, 1, 0; 0, 5/3 = 1.6667, 5/6 = 0.8333, 0 | exact |
| Pattern period | 120 quanta = 0.1 s; step intervals 14, 13, 13 | exact |
| Decision instants in a half-open 20 s window [0, 20) | 600 | count |
| Shortcut "every thirteenth step": rate | 400/13 = 30.769230… Hz (2.5641% high) | exact |
| Shortcut: decision instants in [0, 20) (steps 0, 13, …, below 8,000) | 616 (2.6667% more) | count |

**Intuition.** Rates that do not divide each other are ordinary. Integer phase accumulators keep the long-run
rate exact and make the jitter a declared pattern that replays.

**Assumptions.** Rational periods; the profile's rule for events inside a step; accumulators and queues inside
every checkpoint.

**Flip.** A base unit finer than the declared limit is refused at admission. A pair of rates that divide, such
as 400 Hz and 50 Hz, has no lateness and needs none of this.

**Lane A.** Integer arithmetic: lcm, ceilings, and a loop over 24,000 quanta counting firings.

**Lane B.** `frob-sense`'s scheduler built from the three rational rates: `Q`, the integer periods, the first
seven realized policy steps (0, 14, 27, 40, 54, 67, 80), the lateness sequence, and the count of 600 over a
20 s episode on the `hold` profile; on the referee profile the same instants are realized at substep
boundaries with zero lateness; a checkpoint taken at quantum 41 and restored reproduces the remaining schedule
exactly. Surface: `frob sense schedule --rates physics=400,imu=100,policy=30 --horizon 0.1 --json` prints the
table of instants, realized steps and lateness.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_23_reference`: every row.
- `gb_23_mechanism_schedule`, `gb_23_mechanism_referee_zero_lateness`, `gb_23_mechanism_checkpoint_restore`.
- `gb_23_long_run_rate_exact`: over 10⁶ s (simulated arithmetically) the number of policy firings is exactly
  30 × 10⁶.
- `gb_23_dividing_rates_no_lateness`: 400 Hz and 50 Hz: every lateness is 0.
- `gb_23_base_unit_limit`: rates 400 Hz and 1000/3 Hz give Q = 2,000 (admitted); adding a rate with period
  1/1,000,003 s exceeds the declared limit and is refused at admission.
- `gb_23_controls`: (a) "every thirteenth step" gives 616 firings in 20 s and must miss 600; (b) a
  floating-point accumulator t += 1/30 in `f32` over 20 s produces 600 or 601 firings depending on rounding and
  is rejected by the exact-count assertion run over 10³ horizons; (c) realizing a late firing at the previous
  boundary (floor in place of ceiling) acts before the instant and fails the causality check.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob sense schedule` for 0.1 s and for 20 s and compares the realized steps and the count; logs Q,
the periods, the first twelve rows of the schedule and the count.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-201 (rational clocks: 1,200 base units per second and periods of 3, 12 and 40); KA-471 (400 Hz physics and
a 30 Hz policy under `hold`: nominal decisions at 0, 40, 80, 120 quanta realized at step boundaries 0, 14, 27,
40). Determinism: integer arithmetic, D0, identical everywhere.

## Considerations

- A decimation that is not an integer (40/3 steps per tick) is reported as a rational mean wherever a
  world-steps figure is derived from a control rate (see GB-40).
- Lateness is at most one physics step minus one quantum; tasks whose requirements are sensitive to 1.67 ms
  must use the referee or the strict profile.
