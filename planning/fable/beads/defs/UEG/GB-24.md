## Background

The docking fixture is the project's smallest complete example of a mission answered over a family with
certified facts: a cart must dock exactly in three control ticks although its actuator gain is unknown, and it
receives one velocity reading in time to use it. The first command is therefore an experiment: it must move the
cart enough that the reading tells the two possible worlds apart, while leaving enough actuator authority to
finish in either world. The result is a closed-form trade between sensing tolerance and actuator authority, a
verdict that holds for a whole family and not for one simulation, and a demonstration that a late packet is
the same as no sensor (construction C-19; theorem card T-23). Gates GATE-SENS-13 and GATE-AUGUR-03 check the
boundary and the grid; the same boundary screens exact-integer and guarded single-precision kernels on GPUs.
Tier: Solid arithmetic; the mission machinery around it is Frontier. Subsystems: Augur, Sensorium.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Three ticks of 1 s from rest. Acceleration g·u_k with hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2} and |u_k| ≤ U for
U ∈ [½, 1]. Target (x₃, v₃) = (1, 0) exactly. One reading of v₁ with error in [−ε, ε], delivered before the
second command. With p = u₀ the remaining commands are forced: u₁ = 1/g − 2p, u₂ = p − 1/g (from
v₃ = g·(u₀ + u₁ + u₂) = 0 and x₃ = g·(5u₀ + 3u₁ + u₂)/2 = 1). The limits |u₁|, |u₂| ≤ U in both worlds leave
1 − U ≤ p ≤ (U + ½)/2. The two worlds give v₁ = p and v₁ = 2p; their reading intervals are disjoint when
ε < p/2. Hence a robust causal policy exists exactly when ε < (U + ½)/4, equality excluded.

**Substituted (exact arithmetic), with goldens.**

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bound | U = ¾ | 5/16, reached with probe p = 5/8 | exact |
| Commands at the bold probe | p = 5/8; g = 1; g = 2 | (5/8, −1/4, −3/8); (5/8, −3/4, 1/8) | exact |
| Commands at the timid probe | p = ¼; g = 1; g = 2 | (¼, ½, −¾); (¼, 0, −¼) | exact |
| Tolerance at the timid probe | p/2 | ε < 1/8 (the bold probe buys 5/2 times more) | exact |
| Grid U = ½ + i/32 (i = 0…16), ε = j/64 (j = 0…32) | admitted iff 2j < 32 + i | 561 cells: 344 admitted, 217 excluded, 9 on the boundary (even i) | count |
| Packet after the third command is committed | — | all 561 excluded | count |
| Third gain 33/32 admitted, at U = ¾, ε = 1/64 | — | worst terminal error 1/65 m: every cell admitted for two gains is wrong for three | exact |
| FACT (executed boundary screen, 65,536 cases) | unguarded `f32`; guarded with exact fallback | 452 false admissions and 23,438 false exclusions; none | count |

**Intuition.** Information has a clock. The first command is an experiment, sensing tolerance trades against
actuator authority, and a late packet is the same as no sensor.

**Assumptions.** An exact terminal condition; a strict inequality evaluated in exact integer arithmetic; the
hypothesis set, the delivery deadline and the error units as declared.

**Flip.** Any change to the declared support of g, to the deadline or to the tolerance invalidates the verdict.
Unguarded single precision on the boundary gives hundreds of false admissions; a guard band with exact fallback
gives none.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals: derive the commands, check the limits and the terminal state in both worlds for
both probes; classify the 561 cells by the integer predicate 2j < 32 + i and, independently, by brute force:
for each cell, search p on the rational grid of step 1/128 within [1 − U, (U + ½)/2] for a value with
p/2 > ε (existence equals the predicate on this grid because the optimum p = (U + ½)/2 is a grid point).

**Lane B.** The docking family solver of `frob-mission` on the same grid returns the same 344/217 partition,
an inner witness (the probe and the decision rule) for each admitted cell and a dual or alias exclusion for
each excluded one; with the delivery deadline moved after the third command it returns the alias witness (two
worlds, identical transcripts, disjoint safe actions) for every cell; with g ∈ {1, 33/32, 2} at U = ¾,
ε = 1/64 it returns `unsatisfiable` with the pair (1, 33/32) as witness. Surface: `frob mission synthesize
fixtures/missions/docking/docking.fmission --key gb24-syn --json` (the registry's form, BD-135: the mission file,
which BD-129 writes, is the argument and declares the 17 × 33 grid as its family; `mission.synthesize` is of
kind `run`, so the call carries an idempotency key, and without one it is refused with FROB-REF-805). Its
answer has 344 inner and 217 excluded cells, the nine boundary cells among the excluded, and the BLAKE3 digest
of its 561 labels in N20b-13's row-major order equals the label-vector digest N20b-13 commits under
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/docking/` (the comparison N25c-22's gate script also makes). This is the
card's one surface entry: the docking fixture is a Gauntlet fixture with no manifest in the `task.fixture`
registry (N24b-03), so `frob task fixture fixture:docking` would answer FROB-REF-802 for an unregistered name
and is not called.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_24_reference_commands`: both probes, both gains; terminal state (1, 0) exactly.
- `gb_24_reference_grid`: predicate and brute force agree on all 561 cells.
- `gb_24_reference_boundary`: the nine cells with even i and j = 16 + i/2 are excluded.
- `gb_24_mechanism_partition`, `gb_24_mechanism_late_packet`, `gb_24_mechanism_third_gain`.
- `gb_24_authority_limits`: at U = ½ the only admissible probe is p = ½ (interval [½, ½]) and the bound is ¼.
- `gb_24_controls`: (a) a non-strict comparator admits the nine boundary cells (353 admitted) and must miss
  344; (b) the predicate evaluated in `f32` without a guard band misclassifies at least one cell of a 65,536-row
  refinement of the boundary (the planted defect reproduces the false-admission class); (c) a policy that
  ignores the reading succeeds in one world only and is rejected as a witness.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the surface command above (the family query, under its key); compares the counts, the
label-vector digest and the bound at U = ¾; logs the exact command line, exit code, the grid counts, the
witness for one admitted and one excluded cell, and the evidence block (claim Proof, class Certified where a
bundle is produced; otherwise Known answer, Tested).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-501 (docking grid: 344 of 561 admitted, 217 excluded, nine exactly on the boundary); KA-637 (admitted iff
ε < (U + ½)/4; at U = ¾, ε = 5/16 both worlds can read 15/16); KA-656 (the 65,536-row guarded screen: no error
after exact fallback, identical bytes on all placements). Determinism: exact integer arithmetic, D0 on every
backend.
