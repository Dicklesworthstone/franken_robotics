## Background

Most safety arguments about a learned policy depend on the policy and on the contact model, so they die at the
next retraining. Bet BET-25 is that one class of claim does not: if a physical guide puts an absorber on every
forward path, then the device stops safely whenever the absorber can absorb all the energy that can reach it,
whatever the controller does and whatever the contact law is. Construction C-31 states the claim as an energy
budget with every source listed on the port graph; the `.fenergy` artifact carries the budget in decimal
strings with integer scales; the runtime monitor (`K-energy-monitor`) is one integrator and one comparison per
tick in exact integer arithmetic; proof obligation PO-SHIELD-01 and proof kind `energy-budget` let the offline
auditor re-check the inequality. Kill gate KILL-ENERGY keeps only an Estimated alarm if the claim cannot be
held. GATE-SHIELD-01 requires this card to reproduce. The mechanism is Frontier and its paper-scale pilot
(N22b-09) precedes it. The labels follow the control chapter: 0.25 J is external work (gravity over the
stroke), 0.10 J is stored energy (a series spring), and reserve is a further 0.10 J. Subsystem: Reflex.

## Scope

## Technical approach

**Equation.** ½·m_cap·v² + ∫ max(P_elec, 0) dt + E_stored + E_gravity + E_other + E_reserve ≤ W_abs,min, with
m_cap the effective mass including reflected rotor inertia, P_elec the drive's electrical power until cutoff,
and W_abs,min the least work the absorber takes over its stroke. Read as an exclusion: a crossing that needs a
potential rise above the initial energy plus all allowed external work has no controller.

**Substituted (illustrative; exact decimal arithmetic), with goldens.** g = 9.81 m/s².

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic | m_cap = 8 kg, v = 0.4 m/s | 0.64 J | 0.64 J | exact |
| Drive until cutoff | 20 W × 25 ms | 0.50 J | 0.50 J | exact |
| External work (gravity) | 8 × 9.81 × 0.040 × 0.08 (8% slope, 40 mm stroke) | 0.251136 J | 0.25 J | exact |

**Three readings of the gravity term (DESIGN; the same statement is in the pilot N22b-09 and in the proof-kind
beads N12-17 and N22b-14, so that no two beads give "the card" different totals).** The plan prints
8 × 9.81 × 0.040 × 0.08 = 0.25 J and derives 1.49 J, 50.5 ms and 45.5 ms from it; the product is 0.251136 J.
(1) *Printed*: the term entered as the declared 0.25 J (250,000 in units of 10⁻⁶ J): total 1,490,000, margin
510,000, longest cutoff delay 50,500 µs, or 45,500 µs with the reserve. Known answers KA-153 and KA-568 are
asserted on this reading, to the printed digits. (2) *Product*: the term as the product its basis gives, 251,136:
total 1,491,136, margin 508,864, delay 50,443.2 µs (50.4432 ms), or 45.4432 ms with the reserve. This is the
governing reading and the one in the Golden column above, because a bound may not be rounded down and the
format's integer scale holds the product exactly. (3) *Outward*: wherever the term is written with fewer digits
than its basis it is rounded up, never down: 0.2512 J at four decimals (total 1.4912 J, 50.44 ms), 0.26 J at
two (total 1.50 J, 50.0 ms). No bead may quote 1.49 J or 50.5 ms without saying "printed".

**Intuition.** No term depends on the policy or on the contact law, so the evidence survives every retraining
and the monitor is one integrator and one comparison per tick. The margin is thin: a quarter of the absorber.

**Assumptions.** A physical guide puts the absorber on every forward path; the port graph lists every source;
m_cap includes reflected rotor inertia; the absorber envelope and drive power are measurements, so the claim is
Estimated in simulation and Reality is `no-claim` until bench data arrive.

**Flip.** A rotor of 5 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m² behind a 50:1 reduction on a wheel of radius 0.05 m adds 50 kg of effective
mass, six times the cart, and the budget fails. A budget that omits the gravity or spring term is rejected by
the checker.

**Lane A.** Exact decimal (rational) arithmetic.

**Lane B.** `frob-shield`, in the integers `shield.budget` works in (units of 10⁻⁶ J and 10⁻⁶ s; a delay is
rounded down to the time step, since a bound is never rounded up; BD-337). The cart with the 8% slope in its
scene (the product reading: the builder derives 251,136 µJ, above the declared 0.25 J, so the derived value
enters with the finding `declared-below-derived` and the exit code is 2) is admitted with sum 1,491,136, margin
508,864 and longest cutoff delay 50,443 µs (the table's exact 50,443.2 µs rounded down); with the reserve term
the delay is 45,443 µs. The same cart on a scene with no slope (the printed reading: the gravity term is the
declared 0.25 J) is admitted with sum 1,490,000, margin 510,000 and delays 50,500 µs and 45,500 µs, which is how
the card reproduces KA-153 and KA-568 as GATE-SHIELD-01 asks. With the rotor
term added it is not admitted (exit code 2, a result and not a refusal, BD-337) naming the kinetic term and
the largest admissible reading; a file whose port graph has a gravity or spring port with no budget term is
refused by the completeness check (FROB-REF-451, exit code 3); the integer monitor fed a `synthetic`
power trace of 20 W for 25 ms then 0 integrates exactly 0.50 J and never trips, and fed 20 W for 51 ms trips at
the tick where the running total passes the admissible drive energy; the `energy-budget` bundle verifies
offline with `frob-verify`. Surface, in the verb's own form (`shield.budget` builds and admits a budget for a
design and a boundary; kind `run`, so every call takes its own idempotency key), on the `synthetic` fixtures of
`fixtures/shield/energy/` that N22b-18 ships for `shield_energy_budget.sh` (one set of fixtures for the card,
not a second copy here): `frob shield budget drop-stop-cart-slope --boundary dock_stop_A --reading-max 0.39 --key
gb26-1 --json` (the product reading; the reading domain's upper end 0.39 m/s plus the declared reading error
0.01 m/s is the card's 0.4 m/s) writes the `.fenergy` artifact and returns the integer terms, the sum, the
margin and the admissible cutoff delay; `drop-stop-cart` under key `gb26-2` is the printed reading; `--reserve
0.10` on each (keys `gb26-3`, `gb26-4`) gives the reserve variants; the geared-rotor variant of the cart (key
`gb26-5`) is the flip.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_26_reference`: the twelve goldens.
- `gb_26_printed_reading`: with the gravity term at the declared 250,000 the integers are 1,490,000; 510,000;
  50,500 µs; 45,500 µs (the values N22b-09 asserts in `card_a_integers` and `card_a_cutoff`).
- `gb_26_mechanism_admit`, `gb_26_mechanism_reserve`, `gb_26_mechanism_rotor_not_admitted` (exit code 2,
  not 3),
  `gb_26_mechanism_omitted_term_rejected`, `gb_26_mechanism_monitor_trip`, `gb_26_mechanism_bundle_verifies`.
- `gb_26_exclusion_any_radius`: the sill exclusion holds for wheel radii 0.02 to 0.5 m (the potential rise does
  not depend on radius).
- `gb_26_controls`: (a) a budget without the gravity term (1.24 J) is rejected by the checker, not admitted
  with a larger margin; (b) m_cap without reflected rotor inertia admits the rotor design and must be caught
  by the port-graph completeness check; (c) a monitor that integrates signed power (crediting regeneration)
  under-counts on a trace with −10 W segments and fails the max(P, 0) test.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the five `frob shield budget` calls listed under Surface (product reading, printed reading, the two
reserve variants, and the rotor variant: not admitted, exit code 2, with the largest admissible reading), each
with its own key, then assembles the bundle of the product-reading budget and runs `frob-verify` on it as step 8
of N22b-18's script does; logs the exact command lines, exit codes, each term with its label, the sum, the
absorber, the margin, the cutoff delay in microseconds, any finding and the verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-153 (energy budget arithmetic at a guided stop) and KA-568 (the drop-stop card: 0.64 + 0.50 + 0.25 + 0.10
against 2.00 J). GATE-SHIELD-01 adds no exceedance over 10⁴ randomized contact sequences with μ in [0, 5],
restitution in [0, 1] and every contact law; that sweep is the mechanism's test, and this card supplies its
budget. Determinism: exact integer monitor, D0 on every backend.
