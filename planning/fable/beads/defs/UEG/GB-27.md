## Background

A legged gait is a periodic orbit of a hybrid system: smooth flow interrupted by impacts that reset the
velocity. Bet BET-26 is that such orbits, found by shooting and continued in design parameters, screen designs
far more cheaply than rollouts, because stability becomes a number (the Floquet multipliers, eigenvalues of the
derivative of the return map) instead of a judgment of a rollout. Computing that derivative across an impact
needs more than the Jacobian of the reset: a perturbed trajectory hits the guard at a slightly different time,
and that shift carries the flow before the impact into the state after it. The correct linear map is the
saltation matrix (construction C-16; theorem card T-17). Leaving the time-shift term out gets the sign and the
area change wrong. Near a grazing impact, an ambiguous event order, a change of active set or frictional
degeneracy the derivative does not exist and is refused (FROB-REF-263, with the offending state and
directions). Gates GATE-GAIT-01 and GATE-GAIT-02 check multipliers and gradients; the mechanism is Frontier
with pilot N10b-03. Subsystem: Reflex (gaits).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** For flow ẋ = f(x), guard g(x, t) = 0 and reset x⁺ = R(x⁻, t), the saltation matrix is
Ξ = D_xR + (f⁺ − D_xR·f⁻ − D_tR)·D_xg/(D_tg + D_xg·f⁻), f⁻ and f⁺ being the vector field just before and after
the event. A periodic gait is a fixed point of the return map P; its Floquet multipliers are the eigenvalues
of DP. For a rimless wheel with spoke half-angle α (n spokes, α = π/n) and spoke length ℓ on a slope γ, the
return map on the squared post-impact rate is ω₊² ↦ cos²(2α)·(ω₊² + (4g/ℓ)·sin α·sin γ).

**Substituted (illustrative), with goldens.** g = 9.81 m/s².

**Intuition.** The event time moves with the perturbation, and that shift carries the flow before the impact
into the state after it. Leaving it out gets the sign and the area change wrong. Stability of a gait is then a
number and not a judgment of a rollout.

**Assumptions.** Isolated events with transverse crossing; rigid impacts on the referee, while compliant tiers
use products of step Jacobians and no Ξ; multipliers probe local stability only.

**Flip.** A grazing event, or two events not tied by a symmetry inside the event-time resolution, returns
`refused` and no matrix. Six spokes give a multiplier of ¼ and twelve give ¾: more spokes, slower convergence.

**Lane A.** The formula evaluated with 2 × 2 matrices in `f64`; cross-check by finite differences: integrate
the ball analytically from (y₀, v₀) just before impact to a fixed time after it for perturbations of 10⁻⁶ in
each state, and compare the difference quotient with Ξ (agreement to 10⁻⁵); iterate the rimless-wheel map from
ω₊² = 1 and 4 and confirm convergence to 2.0950 with error ratio ½ per step.

**Lane B.** `frob-gait`: the tangent map across the ball's impact equals Ξ to 10⁻⁹; a ball with v⁻ → 0
(grazing: |D_xg·f⁻| below the resolution) returns FROB-REF-263 with the state and direction; `gait.orbit` on
the rimless-wheel fixture finds the fixed point and reports one multiplier of 0.5 within 10⁻⁶ (the gate's
closed-form comparison). Surface: `frob gait orbit --fixture rimless-wheel --spokes 8 --slope-deg 4
--length 0.5 --json`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_27_reference_saltation`: Ξ entries and determinant.
- `gb_27_reference_finite_difference`: difference quotients match Ξ within 10⁻⁵.
- `gb_27_reference_rimless`: multipliers ½, ¼, ¾; fixed point 2.0950 and 1.4474.
- `gb_27_mechanism_tangent`, `gb_27_mechanism_grazing_refused`, `gb_27_mechanism_orbit_multiplier`.
- `gb_27_energy_consistency`: det Ξ = r² (phase-area contraction equals the restitution squared).
- `gb_27_controls`: (a) the reset Jacobian diag(1, −0.8) used as the tangent map has determinant −0.8 and
  first entry +1 and must miss; (b) the time-shift term with f⁺ and f⁻ swapped gives a coupling entry of the
  wrong sign (−8.829) and is caught by the finite-difference check; (c) a multiplier estimated from one
  rollout's envelope instead of DP is not a fixed-point derivative and fails the 12-spoke case (0.75).

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 calls `frob gait orbit` for 6, 8 and 12 spokes and compares the multiplier and the fixed-point rate;
logs the orbit residual, the multipliers, the event count and the evidence block.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-093: rimless wheel with spoke half-angle α on a slope γ: the return map on the post-impact rate is
ω₊² ↦ cos²(2α)·(ω₊² + (4g/ℓ)·sin α·sin γ); the multiplier is cos²(2α), exactly ½ for eight spokes.
Determinism: closed forms D0; the orbit solve within the referee's class.

## Considerations

- Multipliers probe local stability only; a gait with all multipliers inside the unit circle can still fail
  for large disturbances, and the orbit record says so.
- Compliant tiers have no rigid events; there the tangent map is a product of step Jacobians and this card's Ξ
  is the limit the referee is checked against.
