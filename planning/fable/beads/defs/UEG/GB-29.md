## Background

An agent that may resubmit until a test passes will eventually pass by chance. The blinded qualification lane
makes retries safe instead of forbidding them: each request of a campaign is committed before its worlds are
drawn, runs on fresh sealed world clusters that serve no other request, and is tested at a level that shrinks
with the request index so that the levels of all requests ever made sum to less than α. Error control then
holds for any number of adaptive retries (proof obligation PO-ARENA-02; theorem card T-26), the index advances
for every committed request whether or not the agent likes the answer (invariant INV-QUAL-03), and all agents
on a claim family share one index sequence. GATE-QUAL-01 runs 500 adaptive retries on no-signal worlds and
requires that false qualification stays within the level. Opening a fresh campaign would reset the index, so it
needs the capability `qual:admin`. This card is the level schedule and its price. Tier: Solid. Subsystem:
Arena.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Request i is tested at α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)]. Because 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1), the levels
telescope: Σ_{i ≤ m} α_i = α·m/(m + 1), and the limit is α. For tests whose sample size is proportional to
ln(1/level), request i needs ln(1/α_i)/ln(1/α) times the units of a single test at α.

**Substituted (exact arithmetic), with goldens.** α = 0.05.

| Output | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |

**Intuition.** Error control holds for any number of adaptive retries, because the level is spent whether or
not the agent likes the answer and the worlds are fresh each time. Retrying is allowed and is never free.

**Assumptions.** The request is committed before the draw; no cluster serves two requests; the index advances
for every committed request; all agents on a claim family share one index sequence.

**Flip.** An agent that could open a fresh campaign would reset its index and void the bound, which is why
opening one needs `qual:admin`. Reading confirmation worlds does not break the error rate; it breaks the
held-out population, and the family is retired.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals for levels and partial sums; `f64` logarithms for the unit ratios.

**Lane B.** `frob-arena`'s level ledger: for a campaign with α = 0.05, requests 1 to 500 are assigned exactly
α/[i·(i + 1)] as rationals; the cumulative spend after m requests equals α·m/(m + 1); a request that is
committed and then abandoned still advances the index; two agents on one claim family draw indices from one
sequence (interleaved 1, 2, 3, … with no repeats); `frob eval ledger q:<campaign> --json` prints index, level and
cumulative spend; an attempt to open a second campaign for the same claim family without `qual:admin` is refused. A seeded
simulation of 500 adaptive retries on no-signal worlds (each a one-sided exact test at its level) over 10⁴
campaigns gives a false-qualification frequency of at most 0.05.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_29_reference_levels`, `gb_29_reference_telescoping` (Σ_{i ≤ m} α_i = α·m/(m + 1) for m = 1…10⁴, exact),
  `gb_29_reference_unit_ratios`.
- `gb_29_mechanism_ledger_levels`, `gb_29_mechanism_abandoned_request_advances`,
  `gb_29_mechanism_shared_index`, `gb_29_mechanism_new_campaign_needs_admin`.
- `gb_29_mechanism_500_retries`: the seeded simulation.
- `gb_29_controls`: (a) a constant level α per request gives 51% after 14 null submissions and must fail the
  rate test; (b) an index that advances only on failed requests lets an agent keep level α₁ forever and is
  caught by the abandoned-request test; (c) Bonferroni for a fixed m = 10 (α/10 each) is valid only for ten
  requests: the eleventh has no level, and the test asserts the lane never runs out.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs `frob eval ledger q:<campaign> --json` (the campaign positional, as in the verb's registry example)
on a `synthetic` campaign with ten committed requests and compares index, level and cumulative spend with the
card (request 10 at 1/2,200; spend 0.05·10/11 = 1/22); logs the exact command line, exit code, the campaign id,
the indices and the levels as rationals.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-621: cost of a retry relative to one test at α = 0.05: about 1.23, 2.57, 4.08 and 5.15 times the units at
requests 1, 10, 100 and 500; for the fixed-n rule of fixture 1 a tie is first reachable at n = 99 and 178
at the levels of requests 1 and 10 (GB-30), and by the same rule at n = 328 at the level of request 500
(ln(α₅₀₀/4)/ln 0.95 = 327.8; at request 100 the rule gives 266). Determinism: rational arithmetic D0; the simulation
is seed-explicit.

## Considerations

- The unit ratio assumes sample size proportional to ln(1/level); exact fixed-n rules grow in steps (GB-30) and
  rare-failure bounds grow the same way (request 10 needs 76,959 clean episodes for 10⁻⁴; GB-32).
- The series α/[i·(i + 1)] is one valid spending sequence; a different registered sequence would change the
  table and needs its own card.
