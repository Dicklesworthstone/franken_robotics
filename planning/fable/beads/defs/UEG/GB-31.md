## Background

A mission query asks a question over a family of designs and a set of loads, and its answer is a set with a
boundary, not a simulation of one design: designs inside carry a witness (an explicit force allocation at
every mission vertex), designs outside carry a dual vector that proves no allocation exists (a Farkas
certificate, proof kind `lp-farkas`), and both are checked by a few exact products in the offline auditor.
The stance family is the first such query (construction C-32): four point feet carry a load whose centre of
mass may be anywhere in an envelope; how narrow may the stance be? The tempting shortcut, "the support polygon
contains the load and total actuator capacity suffices", accepts 1,596 bodies of a validation grid that the
exact model rejects. A second lesson is that the answer depends on who allocates the forces: the same body is
feasible with independently commanded feet and infeasible on four equal passive springs. Decision ADR-AUGUR-03:
the lifted linear program is the first implementation, since a specialized projection reached the same optimum
in one solve and has no speed advantage. Gates GATE-AUGUR-02 and GATE-AUGUR-05 check the family. Tier: Solid
arithmetic inside a Frontier subsystem. Subsystem: Augur.

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Feet at (±a, ±b) with vertical forces f₊₊, f₊₋, f₋₊, f₋₋ (first sign x, second y), each in
[0, cᵢ]. Load W with centre of mass (x, y). Balance: Σf = W; a·(f₊₊ + f₊₋ − f₋₊ − f₋₋) = W·x;
b·(f₊₊ − f₊₋ + f₋₊ − f₋₋) = W·y. With X = W·x/a and Y = W·y/b: f₊₊ − f₋₋ = (X + Y)/2 = W·x/(2a) + W·y/(2b).
One free parameter t = f₊₊ gives f₋₋ = t − (X + Y)/2, f₊₋ = W/2 + X/2 − t, f₋₊ = W/2 + Y/2 − t; the state is
feasible iff max(0, (X + Y)/2, W/2 + X/2 − c₊₋, W/2 + Y/2 − c₋₊) ≤ min(c₊₊, c₋₋ + (X + Y)/2, W/2 + X/2,
W/2 + Y/2). With equal ratings c, the envelope corner is feasible exactly when (X + Y)/2 ≤ c.

**Substituted (exact arithmetic), with goldens.** W = 400 N, a = 1/3 m, envelope half-sizes 150 mm × 75 mm (so
moments ±60 N·m, ±30 N·m), c = 160 N.

| Output | Golden | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Corner condition | f₊₊ − f₋₋ = 90 + 15/b ≤ 160 | exact |
| Minimum half-width | b = 3/14 m | exact |
| Forces at b = 3/14, corner (60, 30), order [++, +−, −+, −−] | (160, 130, 110, 0) N | exact |

The weak screen: for all five load states the centre of pressure (x, y) lies inside the rectangle |x| ≤ a,
|y| ≤ b, and Σcᵢ ≥ W.

**Intuition.** The answer is a set of designs with a boundary, witnesses inside it and a dual vector outside
it, checked by a few exact products. A support polygon is not that answer, and neither is a result that omits
who allocates the forces.

**Assumptions.** Fixed pose; coplanar contacts; a vertical load; independently commanded normal forces; mission
vertices that do not depend on the design.

**Flip.** A passive suspension, a mass that depends on the design (the midpoint of two feasible designs can be
infeasible) or a force floor for continuous contact each change the set. A lifted linear program reaches the
same optimum in one solve, so no speed is claimed for a specialized solver.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals: the interval for t in every one of the 11,520 states; the weak screen; the
counts.

**Lane B.** `frob-mission`'s stance family on the same inputs: boundary b = 3/14 from the lifted program and
from the projection (identical rationals); an `lp-primal` witness at b = 3/14 and b = ¼; an `lp-farkas`
exclusion at b = 1/5 whose multipliers reproduce 165 > 160 in the checker; the authority model
`passive-equal-spring` at b = ¼ returns `excluded` with the spring allocation; the grid query returns 2,254 and
114 and agrees with lane A on all 11,520 states; `frob-verify` accepts the bundle. Surface: `frob mission
synthesize --mission stance --json` and `frob prove verify`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_31_reference_boundary`, `gb_31_reference_allocations`, `gb_31_reference_dual`,
  `gb_31_reference_springs`, `gb_31_reference_grid` (2,254; 114; 1,596).
- `gb_31_mechanism_lifted_equals_projected`, `gb_31_mechanism_witness_checks`,
  `gb_31_mechanism_farkas_checks`, `gb_31_mechanism_authority_models`, `gb_31_mechanism_grid`.
- `gb_31_corrupted_certificates`: six corrupted bundles (a force above its rating, a sign flipped in the dual,
  a moment equation dropped, a vertex omitted, a stale design hash, a multiplier of the wrong sign) are each
  rejected by the checker.
- `gb_31_controls`: (a) the support-polygon-plus-total-capacity screen accepts 1,596 bodies wrongly;
  (b) convex interpolation between two feasible designs whose mass depends on the design is not a certificate
  (the midpoint counterexample is infeasible); (c) a certificate that omits the authority model accepts the
  passive-spring body and is rejected for the missing field.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the stance query, verifies its bundle offline and compares the boundary, the witnesses and the grid
counts; logs b as a rational, the allocations, the dual multipliers, the counts and the evidence block (claim
Proof, class Certified, with the auditor build and bundle hash).

## Considerations

- Lateral friction, contact switches and coupled torque limits are outside this family and belong to other
  owners; the certificate states its assumptions as inputs.
- No speed is claimed for the projection; a specialized kernel is earned only through repeated changed queries
  with real timings.
