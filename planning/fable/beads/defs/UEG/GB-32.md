## Background

Finding failures and certifying their absence are different purchases. Nemesis searches for failures with
adversarial and importance-weighted sampling, which is cheap and has no bound on its error; a statement that
the failure rate is below p⋆ needs nominal episodes, and their number is fixed by arithmetic. Construction C-27
keeps two lanes: a certification lane (bounded-weight estimation with a defensive mixture, verb
`nemesis.bound`, decision core DC-04) and an exploration lane (multilevel splitting on forks). Bet BET-32 is
that bounded-weight importance sampling refutes and tightens but never buys absence cheaply; invariant
INV-NEMESIS-03 forbids a bound from episodes selected by an adversary or a battery; kill gate KILL-RARE falls
back to crude nominal sampling with the binomial bound alone. GATE-NEMESIS-04 checks the counts. A certified
10⁻⁶ needs compute above the default envelope; if not approved, 10⁻⁵ is certified and 10⁻⁶ reported as
Estimated. Tier: Solid arithmetic. Subsystem: Nemesis.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-32.toml`; test `gb_32`; controls. Out: `nemesis.bound` (BD-276); splitting (BD-277);
the DC-04 policy (N26a-26); the record of the 76,963-to-76,959 correction (NEG-04).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** n independent nominal episodes with no failure give p ≤ 1 − δ^(1/n) at confidence 1 − δ, so the
least n for a target p⋆ is n = ⌈ln δ/ln(1 − p⋆)⌉, about ln(1/δ)/p⋆. With a defensive mixture of nominal share
β the importance weights are at most 1/β, and no observed failure certifies only p ≤ ln(1/δ)/(β·n). Splitting
with L levels of conditional probability p₀ and N trials per level has a squared coefficient of variation near
L·(1 − p₀)/(p₀·N).

**Substituted (exact counts; the throughput is illustrative), with goldens.** δ = 0.05.

**Intuition.** Finding failures and certifying their absence are different purchases. Exploration is cheap and
has no bound on its error; absence has a fixed price that no sampler discounts without assuming where the
failures live.

**Assumptions.** Independent nominal episodes; a failure predicate frozen before sampling; a bound scoped to
its tier and randomization, of class Tested.

**Flip.** At a tenth of the throughput the 10⁻⁶ claim costs 21 hours: certify 10⁻⁵ and report 10⁻⁶ as
Estimated. Failures driven by static parameters alone gain nothing from forks, since a fork without fresh
randomness is a copy.

**Lane A.** The counts by exact integer search: the least n with (1 − p⋆)ⁿ ≤ δ, decided in exact rational
arithmetic at n and n − 1 (high-precision logarithms only propose the candidate), so that the rounding rule
cannot be confused with the approximation.

**Lane B.** `frob-nemesis`, through the one count function `rare::zero_failure::required(p_star, level)`
(N26a-21; no second entry point computes the count) and the verb in its owner's grammar (BD-276, N26a-30):
`frob nemesis bound <candidate> --task <task> --tier batch-cpu --target 1e-4 --delta 1/20 --mode certify
--estimate --key gb32-1 --json` reports 29,956 required episodes (and 299,572 and 2,995,731 for 1e-5 and 1e-6)
without drawing; with `--delta 1/2200` (0.05/110, the level of the tenth qualification request, which is
`Level::request(1/20, 10)` in the function) it reports 76,959; a bounded-weight run (`--mode estimate --shift
<μ> --epsilon 1/10`: the defensive mixture of BD-276 needs its shift or a `--proposal`, and ε is the card's β;
importance sampling never certifies absence, so `--mode certify` with a mixture is refused as
`certify-by-importance-sampling`) with zero failures in 29,956 episodes reports an upper bound no lower than
its floor ln 20/(0.1 × 29,956) = 1.00004 × 10⁻³, and the reply prints that floor; a request for a bound from
battery-selected or adversary-selected episodes is refused with FROB-REF-560, exit code 3 and no estimate in
the envelope (the lane firewall of N26a-23, which carries INV-NEMESIS-03; repairs: draw fresh nominal episodes
under a new commitment, or remove the offered set and let the verb sample); a bound with one observed failure
uses the exact binomial upper limit for k = 1 and is larger; splitting returns an estimate labelled Estimated
with its coefficient of variation and no bound.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_32_reference_counts`: 29,956; 299,572; 2,995,731; 76,959; verified by (1 − p⋆)ⁿ ≤ δ < (1 − p⋆)ⁿ⁻¹.
- `gb_32_reference_mixture`, `gb_32_reference_splitting`.
- `gb_32_mechanism_plan_counts`, `gb_32_mechanism_request_level`, `gb_32_mechanism_mixture_bound`,
  `gb_32_mechanism_selected_refused`, `gb_32_mechanism_one_failure`.
- `gb_32_coverage_simulation`: with true p = 10⁻⁴, the event "29,956 clean episodes" has probability
  (1 − 10⁻⁴)²⁹⁹⁵⁶ = 0.04999… ≤ 0.05 (computed, not sampled).
- `gb_32_controls`: (a) the approximation ln(1/δ)/p⋆ rounded up gives 29,958 and 76,963 and must miss the exact
  goldens; (b) a count that undercounts (rounding ln(1/δ)/p⋆·(1 − p⋆/2) down gives 29,955) leaves
  (1 − p⋆)ⁿ > δ and must fail the exactness check (the existing control "rounded down from ln(1/δ)/p⋆" gives
  29,957, errs on the safe side and cannot fail, so this undercounting control replaces it); (c) importance
  weights applied without the 1/β bound claim 10⁻⁴ from the mixture run and are rejected.

### End-to-end script and logging

first repair and the evidence class.
