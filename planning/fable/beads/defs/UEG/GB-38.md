## Background

Sizing a link to the loads it carries changes its mass; the changed mass changes the loads; the loads are
measured by re-running the simulated campaign. This loads–mass loop must converge inside a small budget or be
refused. Decision ADR-FOUNDRY-07 holds actuator cards fixed inside the structure loop and re-selects actuators
as an outer step with full revalidation. The reason is this card: the actuator mass spiral (GB-03) and the
structure loop are two geometric series, and run in one loop their gains add, so convergence is slow and a
structural change is amplified more. The rejected alternative is one combined loop. A loop that diverges,
cycles or exceeds its iteration budget is refused (FROB-REF-260; repair: the beam rung as a cheaper sizing
check, or an under-relaxed step). GATE-FOUNDRY-06 measures convergence on fixtures. Tier: Frontier (pilot with
the structure-loop bead). Subsystem: Foundry.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-38.toml`; test `gb_38`; controls. Out: inertia fed back as a typed edit with the
convergence test (BD-404); the beam rung (BD-401); the actuator spiral (GB-03).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** Link mass S sized to a stress cap and other mass M₀ satisfy S = A + λ·(M₀ + S), with A the mass
at zero load and λ the sensitivity of sized mass to carried mass. Fixed point S* = (A + λ·M₀)/(1 − λ); the
error contracts by λ per iteration. Run in one loop with the actuator spiral the gain is about κ + λ. The
estimated contraction is λ̂_k = ‖Δ_k‖/‖Δ_{k−1}‖ for successive increments Δ, and the bound on the remaining
error is λ̂/(1 − λ̂)·‖Δ_k‖. Budget: five iterations; stop when the bound is below 2% of S.

**Substituted (illustrative), with goldens.**

**Intuition.** Two geometric series in one loop converge slowly. That is why actuator cards are held fixed
inside the structure loop and re-selected as an outer step with full revalidation.

**Assumptions.** A linear model near the fixed point; the campaign re-run on the same world addresses, so the
map is deterministic; a budget of five iterations and a 2% test.

**Flip.** λ̂ ≥ 1 on two successive iterations is refused (FROB-REF-260), with the beam rung as the repair. Fresh
random worlds at each iteration put a noise floor above 2% and the test cannot pass. A discrete stock size
produces cycling, which one under-relaxed step may cure.

**Lane A.** Exact rationals: iterates, fixed points, λ̂ from successive differences (exactly 3/20), the error
bound λ̂/(1 − λ̂)·|Δ_k| against the true remaining error (equal for a linear map).

**Lane B.** `frob-foundry`'s loop driver on a `synthetic` sizing map S ↦ A + λ·(M₀ + S): converges within the
budget, reports λ̂ = 0.15, the bound, and stops at the first iteration whose bound is under 2% of S (iteration
3 for the first fixture: bound 0.15/0.85 × 0.045 = 0.0079 kg against 0.047 kg); with λ = 1.05 it refuses with
FROB-REF-260 after two successive ratios ≥ 1 and names the beam rung; with a map that adds seeded noise of 3%
per evaluation (fresh worlds) it exhausts the budget and refuses, and with fixed world addresses the same map
converges; with a discrete stock (S rounded up to multiples of 0.5 kg around a fixed point at a boundary) it
detects a 2-cycle and applies one under-relaxed step.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_38_reference_fixed_points`, `gb_38_reference_iterates`, `gb_38_reference_contraction_estimate`,
  `gb_38_reference_combined_gain`.
- `gb_38_mechanism_converges_and_stops`, `gb_38_mechanism_260_divergent`, `gb_38_mechanism_noise_floor`,
  `gb_38_mechanism_cycle_underrelax`.
- `gb_38_error_bound_is_tight`: for the linear map the bound equals the true remaining error to 10⁻¹².
- `gb_38_controls`: (a) a stop rule on the increment alone (|Δ| < 2%) stops one iteration early at λ = 0.74
  while the true error is 2.8 times the increment (0.74/0.26), and must fail the bound check; (b) actuators
  re-selected inside the loop (gain 0.74) do not meet 2% in five iterations from a 10% first step (remaining
  error 0.74⁴ × 10% × 2.85 = 8.5%), and the test asserts the refusal; (c) the campaign re-drawn with new seeds
  each iteration is the noise-floor defect.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 runs the card's tests; where `frob foundry size --iterate --json` exists it runs the two linear fixtures
and compares iterates, λ̂ and the stop iteration; logs each iterate, increment, ratio and bound.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-743: loads–mass loop with M₀ = 12 kg, A = 0.6 kg, λ = 0.15, start 4.0 kg: iterates 3.0, 2.85, 2.8275,
2.8241, 2.8236; fixed point 2.8235 kg; λ̂ = 0.15. Determinism: rational arithmetic D0; the campaign on fixed
world addresses is deterministic by construction.

## Considerations

- "Five iterations contract the first step to 0.74⁴" counts four ratios between five iterates; the card file
  states the convention.
- The linear model is local; far from the fixed point the driver trusts only the measured λ̂ and its bound.
