## Background

Simulator throughput is quoted in two units that differ by an integer factor and are routinely confused. A
world-step is one physics step of one world. An env-step is one control decision of one environment, which
spans d physics steps (the decimation, equal to the control period divided by the physics step). A training
rate in env-steps/s multiplied by d can exceed a physics-only rate in world-steps/s on the same card only by
mistake. Invariant INV-PERF-01 fixes the ordering that any honest report satisfies, and the performance
harness rejects a report that violates it (GATE-SPARK-19); a Performance figure without its complete
disclosure block is refused (FROB-REF-862). The same confusion in rewards and discounts changes what is
optimized, which is why running reward terms are rates (per second, not per control step) and the discount is
exp(−β·Δt). Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Spark (performance), Cortex.

## Scope

In: `registries/cards/GB-40.toml`; test `gb_40`; controls. Out: the performance harness with unit ordering,
paired lower bound and disclosure block (BD-216); time-aware rewards and physical discounting (BD-194).

## Technical approach

**Equation.** World-steps/s = d × env-steps/s. Ordering in env-steps/s on one device and workload:
training ≤ full environment step ≤ physics only. A reward term stated as a rate r (per second) accumulates
r·Δt per control step; a discount stated as a rate β gives a factor exp(−β·Δt) per control step.

**Substituted (illustrative, with one FACT), with goldens.**

| Output | Inputs | Golden | Printed | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| World-steps inside training | 400 k env-steps/s at d = 10 | 4,000,000 world-steps/s | 4.0 M | exact |
| Ordering check against physics-only 2.0 M world-steps/s | 4.0 M > 2.0 M | rejected | "cannot stand beside" | exact (verdict) |
| FACT: incumbent physics | 1.20 M env-steps/s at d = 4 | 4,800,000 physics steps/s | 4.8 M | exact |
| FACT: incumbent full environment step | 347 k env-steps/s | physics share 347/1,200 = 0.28917 | 29% | exact |
| Per-step penalty of 1 over 10 s | 50 Hz; 100 Hz | 500; 1,000 | 500; 1,000 | exact |
| The same penalty as a rate of 50 per second | 50 Hz; 100 Hz | 500; 500 | "rates" | exact |
| Discount per step for the same horizon, β = 1 s⁻¹ | exp(−0.02); exp(−0.01) | 0.9801986733; 0.9900498337 (both give e⁻¹⁰ over 10 s) | exp(−β·Δt) | rel 10⁻¹² |
| A fixed per-step discount γ = 0.99 over 10 s | 50 Hz; 100 Hz | 0.99⁵⁰⁰ = 0.006570; 0.99¹⁰⁰⁰ = 4.317 × 10⁻⁵ | — | rel 10⁻⁶ |
| Non-integer decimation (30 Hz policy, 400 Hz physics) | 40/3 | mean world-steps per control tick 40/3, as a rational | "(GB-23)" | exact |

**Intuition.** A rate without its decimation is not a number. The same confusion in rewards and discounts
changes what is optimized, which is why running reward terms are rates and the discount is exp(−β·Δt).

**Assumptions.** Reference workloads at h = 2 ms and d = 10; incumbents measured at matched settings and at
their native settings, reported separately.

**Flip.** A task whose rational clocks make d non-integer reports the mean world-steps per control tick as a
rational. A comparison at native settings alone is a comparison of settings.

**Lane A.** Exact integer and rational arithmetic; `exp` in `f64` for the discount rows.

**Lane B.** The harness's report validator: a report with `training = 400 k env-steps/s`, `d = 10` and
`physics_only = 2.0 M world-steps/s` on the same device and workload is rejected with the ordering violation
named (INV-PERF-01); the same report with `physics_only = 5.0 M` is accepted; a figure with no unit field, or
with `steps/s`, is rejected; a figure without its disclosure block is refused with FROB-REF-862; the task
compiler lowers a reward term declared as `rate = 50 /s` to 1.0 per step at 50 Hz and 0.5 per step at 100 Hz,
and a discount declared as β to exp(−β·Δt) with Δt taken from the task's rational clock.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gb_40_reference`: every row.
- `gb_40_mechanism_ordering_rejected`, `gb_40_mechanism_ordering_accepted`, `gb_40_mechanism_unit_required`,
  `gb_40_mechanism_862`, `gb_40_mechanism_reward_rate_lowering`, `gb_40_mechanism_discount_lowering`.
- `gb_40_return_invariant_to_control_rate`: a `synthetic` constant-penalty episode of 10 s with reward and
  discount declared as rates (r = 50 s⁻¹, β = 1 s⁻¹) has discounted returns at 50 Hz and 100 Hz that agree
  within 1% (the step sums r·Δt·(1 − e^(−β·T))/(1 − e^(−β·Δt)) differ by 0.50%, the discretization term of order
  β·Δt), while the per-step declaration differs by a factor of about 2.
- `gb_40_controls`: (a) env-steps reported as world-steps (the 400 k against 2.0 M confusion) passes a naive
  check and must be rejected by the validator once `d` is read; (b) a per-step penalty at two control rates
  (500 against 1,000) is flagged by the task lint as a rate-dependent reward; (c) a non-integer decimation
  truncated to 13 overstates env-steps by 2.56% (GB-23) and is rejected in favour of the rational.

### End-to-end script and logging

NEG-02 feeds the two `synthetic` reports to the harness's validation entry point (`frob eval run --battery
perf-selftest --json`, or the conformance test binary where that battery is not yet registered) and expects
one rejection and one acceptance; logs each figure with unit, d and the ordering verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-806: a report with 400 k env-steps/s of training at d = 10 beside 2.0 M world-steps/s of physics on the
same card is rejected by the ordering check (INV-PERF-01). Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The incumbent's own documentation mixes the two units; its figures are recorded with the unit its source
  used and converted once, in the register, with the decimation stated.
- The return-invariance test holds for rate-declared terms; event rewards (a bonus at docking) are not rates
  and are exempt by type.
