## Background

The second robot taken from the sibling physics library's own catalogue is a KUKA iiwa 7: a seven-revolute
collaborative arm in the shoulder-elbow-wrist layout. It is on file "with provenance, under the suite's
licence" as the arm of the `mobile-manipulator` archetype: the template's presets mount it on a Stretch 3 or
TIAGo base, and its workspace and singular configurations are the fixtures of the workspace and singularity
analyses and of the derived inverse-kinematics kernel. Like the sibling's G1, it is constructed by a builder
function at the pinned sibling commit and consumed through the airlock, not imported from a file. A sibling
routine is not an oracle because it exists: the builder's numbers are admitted by a test of their own before
anything relies on them, and nothing here is a Reality claim about the physical arm.

## Scope

In: `assets/robots/iiwa7/` (manifest with `role = "sibling-catalogue"`, the provenance record, validation
golden); the arm's kinematic known answers; the mass on file for the arm's share of the
`mobile-manipulator@iiwa7-*` presets. Out: the airlock (BD-007); the lowering of a catalogue builder's
model to a design term, which is the `fsim-catalog` format of `design.import` (NEG-32); the admission of the
builder (the catalogue smoke of INT-FSIM-05, BD-033); workspace, singularities and the inverse-kinematics
kernel (BD-105); the archetype template and presets (N14b-04).

## Technical approach

**Source record.** Sibling repository and the commit recorded in the stack lock; the builder's source path,
line range and function name (read at the pin and stored); `licence = the suite's` with its text hash. No
`source/` directory: the asset is regenerated by calling the builder through the airlock and lowering the
result to a design term with `frob design import --format fsim-catalog --builder iiwa7 --name iiwa7 --key
asset-iiwa7-<sibling commit> --json` (NEG-32; NEG-15's import recipe: the verb writes no file, the key makes a
re-run an idempotent replay with the same `DesignId`, and `import.fbot` is the design's `term` section, `frob
design show <DesignId> --section term --json`, with a pinned `DesignId`).

**Provenance of the numbers.** The manifest must say where the builder's link lengths, masses and inertias
come from (a public description file, a datasheet, or values typed by the sibling's authors), as recorded in
the sibling's source comments or documentation at the pin. If the sibling states no provenance, the manifest
records `parameters_provenance = "unstated at pin"` and every card field derived from them is `assumed`.

**Expected facts (recalled, mark M; the builder at the pin governs).** Seven revolute joints with alternating
axes; reach of about 0.8 m from the shoulder; arm mass on the order of 24 kg; joint ranges of roughly ±170°
and ±120° alternating; rated payload 7 kg.

**Validation.** (1) Counts: 7 actuated joints, 8 bodies including the base link; the builder's base is
fixed (n_v = 7 in the builder's model). The design format has no field for a robot bolted to the world, so
the lowered design has a free root (n_v = 13 when compiled alone), carries the loss-report entry "fixed
base: anchor by task or scene", and the arm's analyses use the seven joint coordinates. (2)
Mass properties: exact inertia consistency per link; total mass written to `mass_on_file`. (3) Joint limits
present and finite for all seven. (4) Kinematic structure: the first three axes intersect at the shoulder
point and the last three at the wrist point to within 10⁻⁹ m (the property that makes the arm's position and
orientation problems separable and that the inverse-kinematics kernel assumes); the distances
shoulder–elbow (d₁) and elbow–wrist (d₂) are extracted and stored. (5) Jacobian rank 6 with a one-dimensional
null space at a generic configuration; rank 5 at full extension; rank loss at the shoulder singularity (wrist
centre on the first axis) and at the wrist singularity (the fifth and seventh axes aligned). (6) Wrist-centre
workspace outer radius d₁ + d₂ to 10⁻⁹ m.

**Use.** The arm of the two `mobile-manipulator` presets; fixtures for A-05 and A-06 and for the certified
inverse-kinematics pilot on a seven-joint chain; the arm's share of the presets' mass rows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The manifest records sibling commit, path, lines and builder, and the parameters' provenance or its
      absence.
- [ ] Validation steps 1 to 6 pass and their values are stored; `import.fbot` has a pinned `DesignId`.
- [ ] The admission record of the builder (its counts and total mass at the pin) exists before any analysis
      cites the arm.
- [ ] The arm's mass on file is written into the manifest field that both `mobile-manipulator` presets read;
      the presets and their mass rows are N14b-04's, so this bead checks the field (present, sourced, equal to
      the validated total) and does not wait on them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `robot_iiwa7_provenance_complete`.
- `robot_iiwa7_counts`: 7 joints, 8 bodies; n_v 7 in the builder's model, 13 for the lowered design compiled
  alone; the fixed-base entry is in the loss report.
- `robot_iiwa7_spherical_shoulder_and_wrist`: axis intersections within 10⁻⁹ m.
- `robot_iiwa7_jacobian_ranks`: 6 generic; 5 at full extension; rank loss at the two named singular poses.
- `robot_iiwa7_workspace_outer_radius`: d₁ + d₂.
- `robot_iiwa7_mass_properties_consistent`, `robot_iiwa7_joint_limits_valid`, `robot_iiwa7_design_id_pinned`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/assets_licences.sh` (NEG-16): builds the arm through the airlock, lowers it, runs validation;
logs sibling commit, provenance status, d₁ and d₂ in metres, ranks at the four poses and the total mass.

### Known answers and negative controls

For an arm with a spherical shoulder and a spherical wrist: wrist-centre reach d₁ + d₂; rank 5 at the elbow
singularity. Controls: a lowering that perturbs one axis by 1 mm (the intersection test fails and the
inverse-kinematics kernel's precondition is reported unmet); the builder used as an oracle before its
admission record exists (the row is `blocked: admission`). Determinism: exact rank at rational sample
configurations; pinned identity per sibling commit.

## Considerations

- Recalled figures are expectations only; the builder at the pin replaces them.
- A sibling's newer clean head is never rolled back locally; a changed builder is adopted through the
  switch-over rule and regenerates the goldens.
