## Background

`quad-mammal` is the reference legged body of the project: a dog-like quadruped with three joints per leg. It
is the workload behind most planning figures (the "Q" workload of the performance tables), the first archetype
with a floating base, and the body on which the symmetry machinery is easiest to get wrong in an instructive
way: mechanically it has two mirror planes, but as soon as a forward-facing sensor is mounted the closed loop
has only one. It lands at M1 with the first templates. This bead fixes its zoo golden: the pinned default
instance and the expectation file that the zoo test and script recompute.

## Scope

In: `zoo/quad-mammal.fbot`, `zoo/quad-mammal.expect.toml`, its control variants. Out: the template and its
presets (BD-109); the symmetry analysis (BD-102); the baseline controller and the standing row (BD-116); the
gait atlas (BD-332); reference-robot imports (the `ROBOT-…` beads).

## Technical approach

**Morphology.** Torso; `mirror(xz, mirror(yz, leg))`; each leg is hip abduction, hip pitch, knee (three
revolute joints) and a foot end effector with contact law `foot-patch`. Actuated coordinates 12; floating base
6; n_v = 18; bodies 13 (torso plus three links per leg); end effectors 4. Proprioception only by default (IMU,
joint encoders, foot contact switches); a power bus with a battery card; every fill, material, contact law,
joint limit and current convention written (no physics default exists).

**Symmetry.** Γ_mech as built: C₂ × C₂, generated by the sagittal mirror (left ↔ right) and the transverse
mirror (front ↔ rear), acting on legs as the permutations (FL FR)(RL RR) and (FL RL)(FR RR). Default Γ_obs:
C₂ × C₂ (proprioception preserves both). Breakers: `add-sensor forward-lidar` → Γ_obs = C₂ (sagittal mirror
only), breaker the lidar mount; `add-payload 2 kg` on the left side at mid-length breaks the
sagittal mirror and keeps the transverse one, so both groups become C₂ generated by the transverse mirror,
breaker the payload.

**Control variants.** (a) `variants/lidar-assert-c2xc2.fbot`: forward lidar mounted and `assert_obs: C2xC2`
kept → refused with FROB-REF-109 (declared symmetry differs from computed), breaker the lidar mount.
(b) `variants/missing-fill.fbot`: the torso's fill removed → FROB-REF-105. (c) `variants/one-sole.fbot`: one
foot's interface card changed → Γ_mech reduced although the encoder permutation still exists.

**Reference robots on file.** Unitree Go1, Go2, A1; ANYmal B and C; Spot (BSD-3 models); Barkour for import
parity only. They enter as themselves by import; presets of the template approximate their masses for the
mass gate. No reference robot is the source of a Reality claim.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `zoo_quad_mammal` (generated by NEG-30): identity, morphology, symmetry with both breakers, six stages,
  five known answers, three controls.
- `zoo_quad_mammal_strata_bruteforce`: the 81 foot-state tuples canonicalized under the four group elements
  give 27 classes.
- `zoo_quad_mammal_policy_tie_counterexample`: under the larger group C₂ × C₂ with a forward lidar, a tied
  policy would have to walk backwards as the mirror image of walking forwards while seeing only forwards; the
  test checks that the weight-sharing constructor refuses Γ_mech when Γ_obs is smaller.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/zoo_goldens.sh` (NEG-31) runs this archetype at M1: templates, new, show, symmetry with breakers,
check stages 1 to 6, `algebra orbits` for the strata, and the three variants; the log carries `DesignId`,
`ModelId`, groups, stage statuses and times.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-096 (27 strata and 162 transitions); KA-270 (Γ_mech as built); KA-276 and CX-133 (forward lidar with
`assert_obs: C2xC2` refused, FROB-REF-109); CX-118 (Γ_mech of order 4, Γ_obs of order 2 with one forward
lidar). Determinism: pinned identities; integer counts.
