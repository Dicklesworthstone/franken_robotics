## Background

Gradients of a rollout with respect to state, controls and design parameters are wanted for trajectory
optimization, gait continuation and sensitivity analysis. FACT (reported, re-read at admission test INT-FSIM-03
before any gate cites it): FrankenSim's `fs-mbd` is plain `f64`, is not generic over a differentiation scalar,
and emits no articulated adjoints. Its `fs-ad` crate has forward-mode duals, implicit-function sensitivities
(`ift.rs`) and gradient checks, with recorded scope "cyclic groups only, no contact-event derivatives". Upstream
register priority: **P3**.

This project therefore owns its derivatives: derivative programs generated by `frob-kir` on smooth kernels, and,
through contact, estimators that each carry a derivative label. A derivative through a step is offered only
where it exists. Near a grazing contact, an ambiguous simultaneity or a changing active set the request is
refused with the offending state, and a one-sided estimate, a generalized derivative or a smoothed surrogate
each carry their own label. The label set (C-16, nine labels): `smooth`, `saltation`, `discrete-step` (the
time-stepping map's derivative with the active set unchanged in each step), `fixed-mode` (the mode sequence held
fixed by fiat: a conditional derivative), `one-sided`, `generalized`, `smoothed`, `finite-difference`, `refused`.

The referee has no derivative of its own, so a gradient computed on a fast tier cannot be checked against the
tier that adjudicates. That is what the upstream item would supply. Rejected: differentiating FrankenSim by
finite differences as the permanent referee gradient (cost grows with the number of parameters, and the noise
floor near contact is exactly where a check is needed); a local dual-number fork of `fs-mbd`.

## Scope

In: in FrankenSim, a reverse-mode pass through the contact-free articulated step and through the contact step
at a fixed active set, with a refusal near mode boundaries. Here: the gradient comparison. Out: derivative
programs and labelled estimators on this side (BD-334, BD-353); gradients through contact as a product feature
(the Moonshot epic BD-422); hybrid shooting on articulated models (FD-FSIM-20).

**The change (DESIGN).** For one step x₊ = Φ(x, u, θ), with state x = (q, v), controls u and design parameters θ
(masses, inertias, link lengths, joint axes), an entry that takes the cotangent x̄₊ and returns
x̄ = (∂Φ/∂x)ᵀ·x̄₊, ū = (∂Φ/∂u)ᵀ·x̄₊ and θ̄ = (∂Φ/∂θ)ᵀ·x̄₊, without forming the Jacobians.

1. *Contact-free.* The adjoint of the recursive Newton–Euler pass and of the articulated-body solve, in the
   same tree sweeps as the forward pass, reversed.
2. *Contact at a fixed mode.* With the active set and the stick or slip state of every contact held fixed, the
   step's solve is a smooth linear system, and its adjoint is one transposed solve (the implicit-function rule:
   for F(z, p) = 0, dz/dp = −(∂F/∂z)⁻¹·∂F/∂p, and the adjoint solves with (∂F/∂z)ᵀ).
3. *Honesty at the boundary.* The entry returns its result with the mode it assumed and the margins to the
   nearest mode change (smallest normal force, smallest distance to the cone, smallest gap). Below declared
   margins it refuses instead of returning a derivative of the wrong branch.

Additive: a new entry; the forward step and its values are unchanged.

**Interim posture here.** Derivative programs from `frob-kir` on smooth kernels; labelled estimators through
contact; design gradients by paired finite differences. The referee offers no derivative.

**Fixture (one reproducible failing fixture, DESIGN).** `tests/upstream/FD-FSIM-05/`, opened by BD-037 and kept current
by this bead: one test tagged `FD-FSIM-05`, compiled as the `frob-conformance` integration test `upstream_fd_fsim_05` and
marked `#[ignore]` so that the default suite stays green, with `expected.toml` beside it holding the signature
recorded at the pinned commit (`observed_at`, the failing assertion and the observed value). It fails at the pin
and passes once the change is adopted; a fixture that passes at the pin no longer demonstrates the limit and is
itself a failure of the registry check. It asks `RefereeDynamics` for adjoint support (recorded as absent at the
pin by INT-FSIM-03) and, when present, runs the duality test below through the airlock.

**Switch-over (the compatibility train).** When the change is on the sibling's `main`: (1) move the sibling's pin
in `franken-stack.lock` to a clean commit that contains it (a pin only moves forward; a sibling's newer clean head
is never rolled back locally); (2) write a new resolution receipt (`frob.stack-receipt/1`) under `receipts/`
with `cargo xtask check-stack --write-receipt --reason pin-move --gate INT-FSIM-01` (plain `check-stack` only
verifies and writes nothing); (3) re-run that sibling's admission gates, the parity gates and the
gates this item names; (4) relock (`Cargo.lock` regenerated and committed, builds `--locked` on the one toolchain
T) and append the record, with the receipt hash, to `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`. Only then does the row's `status`
become `adopted` and the interim posture end. No PASS is recorded on a dirty sibling tree, and neither a pin nor
T moves while a ten-fold tournament is running. If a gate named here does not exist yet when the change lands,
the pin still moves with the gates that do exist, the row stays `landed`, and the gate's first run is made
against the new pin.

**What the switch-over re-runs.** The rule is "compare gradients" (DESIGN, thresholds are TARGETs): on 10³
seeded contact-free states of each reference robot, the upstream adjoint and this project's derivative program
agree to 10⁻⁶ relative; on the gait battery's regular cases, the fixed-mode adjoint and the labelled estimator
have cosine at least 0.95 (the criterion of GATE-GAIT-02: at least 72 of 80 regular cases at cosine 0.95 against
central differences, and all 20 grazing and fold controls labelled or refused). A disagreement is recorded with
the first differing component and blocks adoption of the referee gradient, not of anything else. This
project's side of both comparisons is the labelled orbit gradient of `gait.gradient` (BD-334) and the derivative
programs of `frob-kir`.

**Fallback if the change cannot land.** The referee stays without a derivative; fast-tier gradients are
checked by finite differences in `f64` at two step sizes with a Richardson check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In FrankenSim (added):
- `ballistic_adjoint_is_exact`: a free body under gravity for time T = 0.5 s; expected ∂x(T)/∂v₀ = T·I, so the cotangent of the initial velocity for a unit cotangent on final position is 0.5 per axis, to 10⁻¹².
- `pendulum_energy_gradient_through_a_step`: E = ½·m·L²·θ̇² + m·g·L·(1 − cos θ) with m = 1 kg, L = 1 m, g = 9.81 m/s², from θ = 0.3 rad, θ̇ = 0.5 rad/s. The cotangent x̄₊ = ∇E(x₊) is pulled back through one step: for h = 1 ms the result equals central differences of E(Φ(x)) (step 10⁻⁶) to 10⁻⁸ relative; as h → 0 (h = 10⁻⁹ s) it tends to ∂E/∂θ = m·g·L·sin 0.3 = 2.899053 and ∂E/∂θ̇ = m·L²·θ̇ = 0.5 within 10⁻⁶ relative.
- `duality_on_two_link_arm` and `duality_on_g1`: the inner-product test above.
- `fixed_mode_adjoint_matches_central_difference`: a block sliding on a plane with μ = 0.5, far from stick; relative error at most 10⁻⁶.
- `refuses_at_stick_slip_boundary`: tangential speed within the declared margin of zero; expected the typed refusal.
Here:
- `referee_gradient_carries_a_label`: every value returned through the airlock has one label of the set.
- `gradient_comparison_records_first_difference`: a planted sign flip in one derivative-program term is reported with its component.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/upstream_fd_fsim_05.sh --phase pre|post` (plain shell, seed-explicit, idempotent, non-zero on failure).
Phase `pre`, valid at any pin before adoption: `cargo xtask check-stack`; `frob doctor --json` (its `upstream`
section shows this item with its status); `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test upstream_fd_fsim_05 -- --ignored`,
asserting that the fixture fails with the signature in `expected.toml`; then the interim posture's own tests,
asserting that they pass. Phase `post`, after the pin move: the same fixture must pass, the gates named in this
bead are re-run, and the registry row must read `adopted` with a receipt newer than the pin move. Phase `post` also runs
`frob gait gradient <orbit> --params <p1,p2,p3> --metrics cot --tier referee --key fdf05-g1 --json` on a contact-free
orbit fixture and the same with `--key fdf05-g2` on a regular stance orbit of the gait battery (the inputs of the
`gait.gradient` row of VERBS-gait: an orbit, at least three design parameters, the metrics, `--tier`; a run verb, so
each call carries its own idempotency key), asserting the label and the agreement metrics.

Logging per STD-E2E: the script sources the shared helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/upstream_fd_fsim_05/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`FD-FSIM-05`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). It also records the sibling's pinned commit, the stack-receipt hash, the phase, each measured
value with its unit and threshold, digests where state is compared and, on failure, the first differing value
and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the
gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the ballistic sensitivity T·I; the pendulum energy gradient; the implicit sensitivity of a 2×2 fixed point against its closed form (the admission test INT-FSIM-10).
- Negative controls: an adjoint with one transposed block missing must fail duality; a fixed-mode adjoint evaluated across a stick-to-slip change must be refused, and a mutant that returns a number there must be caught by the grazing controls.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; same-platform class.

## Considerations

- A fixed-mode derivative is a true derivative only where the mode is locally constant; that is why the margins are part of the return value. Generalized derivatives at mode changes stay a research item on this side.
- `fs-mbd` being non-generic means the adjoint is hand-derived there; the duality test is the guard against a hand-derived error.
- The item is P3 and Frontier-adjacent: no claim of the product depends on a referee gradient.
- No milestone waits on this bead. Its milestone label says when its switch-over gate first exists, not that the
  milestone's exit depends on it. Until the switch-over has been run, the interim posture above is the design of
  record. M1 depends only on FrankenSim, asupersync and FrankenSQLite, and no upstream work item is on its
  critical path.
- If this change never lands, the program still ships on the interim posture, at the cost stated there. A change
  that does land is adopted only through its switch-over rule: never by a local patch, a fork or a vendored copy,
  and never by rolling a sibling's newer clean head back. Neither a pin nor T moves during the ten-fold
  tournament of M7.
- The priority in the upstream register states the item's value to this project and its place in this program's
  queue. The risks carried at program level are the cost and ordering of upstream work and collisions with other
  agents in the same sibling; the mitigations are file reservation, small commits and the sibling's own gates.
