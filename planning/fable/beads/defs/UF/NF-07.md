## Background

FrankenSim ships a catalog builder for the Unitree G1 humanoid in `fs-mbd::robot_models`, and this project uses
it: the M0 demonstration is a referee rollout of that model whose digest must equal FrankenSim's own. FACT
(`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/robot_models.rs:398–403` at `483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21): the builder states
that fixed rubber hands, head, pelvis contour, logo, waist supports and sensor attachments are omitted and that
"their nonzero masses are not lumped into retained links". **It keeps 30 links, whose masses sum to
31.96314202 kg** (summed from the source table). FACT (the pinned upstream description of the same robot;
reported, to be re-read from the primary source before a gate cites it): four welded links, the head, the logo
and the two hands, add **1.377 kg, for 33.34014202 kg over 38 links**.

The omission is stated, so this is a limit and not a hidden defect. It still matters. At 9.81 m/s² the reduced
model weighs 313.5584 N and the source assembly 327.0668 N: a static support rated at 320 N passes on the
reduction and fails on the robot. This project's rule for imports is that a body of nonzero mass is never
dropped without a declared reduction, and every result on a reduced model cites the reduction. The plan lists
this among six later foundation facts and says the corresponding upstream work items are "added to Appendix F
by description": fold or declare the omitted masses of the G1 catalog. The plan gives it no `FD-` number and no
register priority; N33c-47 assigns `FD-FSIM-34` at the lowest register priority, P3 (see Identifier below).

Rejected: using the 30-link builder as the reference for import parity of the humanoid archetype (a 4% mass
error in the reference); correcting the masses in a local copy of the builder.

## Technical approach

**Where the work is done, and under whose rules (binding).** The change is made in the `frankensim` repository
(`../frankensim` beside this one; head `483d09c682f2` and toolchain channel `nightly-2026-07-06` when this was written
on 2026-11-21, both FACT to re-read), by an agent of this program working there under that repository's own
`AGENTS.md`, gates and conventions. For FrankenSim those rules include its layer checks, its contracts, its unsafe-capsule discipline, its ban on
in-process foreign kernels in production paths and its ban on branches and worktrees (FACT:
`frankensim/AGENTS.md`, `frankensim/docs/ACCELERATOR_DOCTRINE.md`), so the work is committed on `main` in small
steps that each pass its gates. FrankenSim moves fast (FACT: 409 commits touched `crates/fs-mbd` in the thirty
days to 2026-11-21, and its checkout had 32 modified paths), so every file named in this bead is re-read at the
current head before it is edited.

1. Read that repository's `AGENTS.md` and its coordination rules first. They govern; where they differ from this
   bead, they win, and the difference is written into this bead's closing note.
2. Other agents work in the same repository. Before editing, reserve every file to be touched through Agent

4. It lands on that repository's `main` through its own tests and gates. Nothing in this repository patches,

5. The work is tracked twice: this bead, and a mirrored bead in the sibling's tracker whose identifier is stored

   The row (`id = "FD-FSIM-34"`, `bead = "NF-07"`, register priority P3) and the mirrored bead are opened by

**The report.** `tests/upstream/NF-07/REPORT.md` (also the body of the mirrored bead): the evidence above with
file, lines and commit; the 30 retained masses and their sum; the four welded links with their masses once
re-read from the primary source; the two weights; and `observed_at` with the roll-up value the admission probe
recorded at the pin.

**The change (DESIGN), either form, FrankenSim's choice; both additive.**

1. *Fold.* A second builder (or an option of the present one) that folds each omitted fixed attachment into its
   parent link. For bodies i with mass mᵢ, centre of mass cᵢ and inertia Iᵢ about cᵢ, all in the parent's
   frame, the folded body has m = Σ mᵢ, c = (Σ mᵢ·cᵢ)/m and
   I = Σ [Iᵢ + mᵢ·(‖dᵢ‖²·1 − dᵢ·dᵢᵀ)] with dᵢ = cᵢ − c (the parallel-axis rule). The 30-link topology is
   unchanged; the total mass becomes the source's.
2. *Declare.* The present builder returns, beside the model, a reduction record: the list of omitted bodies
   with their masses and parents, and the omitted total. Nothing about the model changes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In FrankenSim (added):
- `folded_g1_total_mass`: equals the sum of the 38 source masses (33.34014202 kg as reported), to 10⁻⁹ kg.
- `fold_two_bodies_closed_form`: a 2 kg body at x = 0 and a 1 kg body at x = 0.3 m, both with negligible own inertia: folded mass 3 kg, centre of mass at x = 0.1 m, inertia about the folded centre for an axis perpendicular to x equal to 2·0.1² + 1·0.2² = 0.06 kg·m².
- `reduction_record_lists_omissions`: the record's masses sum to the difference between source and retained totals.
- `reduced_builder_unchanged`: bit-identical model.
Here:
- `g1_reduced_rollup`: the 30 masses sum to 31.96314202 kg to 10⁻⁹ kg through the airlock.
- `reduced_profile_cites_reduction`: every result envelope on `g1-reduced-30` carries the reduction with `omitted_mass`.
- `static_support_320n_discriminates`: a support rated 320 N passes on the reduced profile (313.5584 N) and fails on the 38-link source model (327.0668 N).
- `import_without_declared_reduction_is_refused` (N14b-34's test, re-run at the new pin, not written again): an import that drops a body of nonzero mass with no reduction is refused.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/upstream_nf_07.sh --phase pre|post`: phase `pre` runs the admission probe of record,
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- g1-mass --json`
(there is no `frob-conformance` binary, BD-025: the example `crates/frob-conformance/examples/foundation_probe.rs`
is declared in N33c-36 and its subcommand `g1-mass` is N33c-41's, which creates the file if it lands first);
reads N14b-34's profile file `assets/profiles/g1-reduced-30.toml` and asserts 31.96314202 kg from both, with the
same 30 retained names and the same omission list (the roll-up is a weight comparison and not a `design.check`
stage, as N14b-34 states); runs `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --seconds 2 --key nf07-pre-<run> --json` and
asserts that its envelope cites the reduction (`evidence.reduced` with profile `g1-reduced-30`), recording the
state digest; it also runs the `#[ignore]`d fixture `upstream_nf_07` and asserts that it fails at the current
pin (phase `post`: passes). Phase `post` adds the folded total or the generated reduction, and the same run
under a distinct key, `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --seconds 2 --key nf07-post-<run> --json`, to confirm that
the reduced profile's digest is unchanged from the one `pre` recorded.

Logging per STD-E2E: the script sources the shared helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/upstream_nf_07/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`NF-07`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). It also records the sibling's pinned commit, the stack-receipt hash, the phase, each measured
value with its unit and threshold, digests where state is compared and, on failure, the first differing value
and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the
gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: 31.96314202 kg and 33.34014202 kg; 313.5584 N and 327.0668 N at 9.81 m/s²; the two-body fold (3 kg, 0.1 m, 0.06 kg·m²).
- Negative controls: a fold without the offset term mᵢ·(‖dᵢ‖²·1 − dᵢ·dᵢᵀ) gives 0 kg·m² in the two-body case and must fail; a roll-up that silently includes the omitted masses must fail the 31.96314202 kg probe.
- Determinism lanes: mass sums are exact in rationals and are checked to 10⁻¹² relative in double precision on both platforms.
