## Background

The differentials of the exponential map on SE(3), its left and right Jacobians, appear wherever a pose is
perturbed: in integrators, estimators, and every analytic Jacobian. FrankenRobotics reuses FrankenSim's `fs-ga`
as its host representation of SE(3). FACT (`frankensim/crates/fs-ga/src/lie.rs:46` and `:1044–1049` at
`483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21): `fs_ga::Se3::left_jacobian` and `right_jacobian` are computed by a series
that **refuses when the infinity norm of ad exceeds 32** (`SE3_SERIES_MAX_AD_NORM = 32.0`), and the refusal
precedes the series. So a pure translation of 33 m returns `IllConditioned`, although the pose is regular and
`Se3::exp` accepts it (`:766–776`, no such limit).

The differential is in fact exact and trivial there. For a pure translation the twist has zero angular part,
ad² = 0, and the left and right differentials of the exponential are exactly I + ad/2 and I − ad/2, both with
determinant one. For a translation of 33 m along +x and a unit rotation rate about +z, the linear y component
is −16.5 m in one and +16.5 m in the other. A warehouse aisle is longer than 32 m, so a refusal there would
exclude ordinary poses; anchored coordinates keep translations inside a cell small, but a relative pose
between two bodies in one aisle is not anchored.

The plan lists this among six later foundation facts whose upstream work items are "added to Appendix F by
description": a closed-form differential for small rotation and large translation. The plan gives it no `FD-`
number and no register priority; N33c-47 assigns `FD-FSIM-35` at P3, because this project does not depend on the
sibling's Jacobians (see Identifier below).
Rejected: scaling lengths to dodge the limit (the limit is on a dimensionful norm; rescaling changes which
poses are refused, not the defect); reading `IllConditioned` as a singular pose.

**The change (DESIGN).** The Jacobians of SE(3) have a closed form whose only delicate terms depend on the
rotation angle θ = ‖φ‖, never on the translation ρ: with the twist written as (φ, ρ), the left Jacobian is the
block matrix [[J(φ), 0], [Q(φ, ρ), J(φ)]] in the ordering with the angular part first (FrankenSim's own
ordering governs), where J is the SO(3) Jacobian and Q is linear in ρ with coefficients that are functions of θ
alone. Therefore:

1. evaluate the θ-dependent coefficients in closed form, switching to their Taylor series only for small θ
   (a test on θ, not on ‖ad‖);
2. form Q by its linear dependence on ρ, with no limit on ‖ρ‖;
3. keep a refusal only where the map is truly singular (θ at a multiple of 2π away from zero, where the
   Jacobian loses rank) and name it as such.

At φ = 0 this reduces exactly to I ± ad/2. Additive in the register's sense: no previously accepted input
changes its result beyond rounding; inputs previously refused for a large translation are now answered.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In FrankenSim (added, in `fs-ga`):
- `pure_translation_33m`: twist with zero angular part and translation (33, 0, 0) m; applied to a unit rotation rate about +z, the left and right Jacobians give linear y components −16.5 m and +16.5 m (in the order the report records at the pin), all other added components zero, and determinant 1, to 10⁻¹².
- `pure_translation_equals_identity_plus_half_ad`: 10³ random translations up to 10⁴ m; J_left = I + ad/2 and J_right = I − ad/2 exactly up to rounding.
- `small_rotation_large_translation`: θ = 10⁻⁸ rad with ‖ρ‖ = 100 m; agreement with a 60-term exact-rational series evaluation to 10⁻¹².
- `left_right_relation`: J_right(ξ) = J_left(−ξ) on 10³ seeded twists.
- `product_with_inverse_is_identity`: J·J⁻¹ = I to 10⁻¹⁰ on 10³ seeded twists away from singular rotations.
Here:
- `frob_lie_matches_sibling_large_translation`: the differential test.
- `owner_limit_is_not_a_singularity`: a scratch analysis fed the sibling's `IllConditioned` at 33 m must report `unsupported`.
- `aisle_relative_pose_40m`: the relative pose of two bodies 40 m apart in one aisle (a pure translation (40, 0, 0) m, and the same with a yaw of 0.1 rad) through the airlock's façade over the sibling's Jacobians: at the starting pin both return `unsupported` (`FROB-REF-792`, `kind: owner-limit`, N33c-42); after adoption both answer and agree with `frob-lie` to 10⁻¹² relative.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/upstream_nf_08.sh --phase pre|post`: phase `pre` runs the two-pose probe,
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- se3-dexp --json`
(N33c-42's subcommand; there is no `frob-conformance` binary, BD-025), and asserts the recorded outcomes; it runs

airlock made into the sibling's Jacobians (the airlock counts them) and how many results carried the label.
Phase `post` asserts agreement at both probe poses, runs the differential test and `aisle_relative_pose_40m`
(no result carries the label), and re-runs the INT-FSIM-02 lane, asserting that no result carries the label; if
that lane never called the sibling's Jacobians, that assertion is logged as `not exercised` rather than as a pass.
The verb-level form (a referee run of two bodies 40 m apart in one scene) needs a multi-body scene that no
dependency of this bead provides, and is not claimed here.

Logging per STD-E2E: the script sources the shared helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/upstream_nf_08/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`NF-08`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). It also records the sibling's pinned commit, the stack-receipt hash, the phase, each measured
value with its unit and threshold, digests where state is compared and, on failure, the first differing value
and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the
gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: for a pure translation, the differentials are I ± ad/2 with determinant 1; ∓16.5 m at 33 m.
- Negative controls: a Jacobian that swaps left and right must fail the sign of the 16.5 m component; a fix that only raises the constant from 32 to a larger number must fail at 10⁴ m.
- Metamorphic relation: for a pure translation the result is linear in the translation: doubling ρ doubles the off-diagonal block exactly.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, bit-identical. Across the two platforms, bit-identical if `fs-ga`'s elementary functions are its own deterministic implementations rather than the platform `libm` (FACT to recheck at the pin; the pure-translation case uses none and must be bit-identical regardless); otherwise the cross-platform lane compares to 10⁻¹² relative and records the class it found.

## Considerations

- The refusal exists to protect a series from loss of accuracy; the closed form removes the need for it in translation, and keeps accuracy by testing on the angle.
- Twist ordering and left/right conventions differ between libraries; the report records which of the two Jacobians gave −16.5 m at the pin, and the tests assert the convention the sibling documents.
- No milestone waits on this bead. Its milestone label says when its switch-over gate first exists, not that the
  milestone's exit depends on it. Until the switch-over has been run, the interim posture above is the design of
  record. M1 depends only on FrankenSim, asupersync and FrankenSQLite, and no upstream work item is on its
  critical path.
- If this change never lands, the program still ships on the interim posture, at the cost stated there. A change
  that does land is adopted only through its switch-over rule: never by a local patch, a fork or a vendored copy,
  and never by rolling a sibling's newer clean head back. Neither a pin nor T moves during the ten-fold
  tournament of M7.
- The priority in the upstream register states the item's value to this project and its place in this program's
  queue. The risks carried at program level are the cost and ordering of upstream work and collisions with other
  agents in the same sibling; the mitigations are file reservation, small commits and the sibling's own gates.
