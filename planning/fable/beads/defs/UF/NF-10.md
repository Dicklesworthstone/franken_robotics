## Background

A broad phase decides which pairs of bodies could touch during a step by enclosing each body's motion over the
step in a simple bound. If the bound misses part of the motion, a contact inside the missed part is never
tested, and the body tunnels. FrankenSim's contact crate has a helper for a moving sphere. FACT
(`frankensim/crates/fs-contact/src/broad_phase.rs:146–156` at `483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21):
`swept_sphere_aabb` documents that it "**encloses straight centre motion only**" and leaves curved trajectories
to the caller. It is a documented scope limit.

A sample on a rotating link moves on an arc, and the arc bulges beyond the chord between its endpoints by the
sagitta. So the helper is right for prismatic and ballistic segments and wrong, by its own statement, for a
sample on a rotating link: this project uses it only for the former and builds its own bounds for the latter.
The plan lists this among six later foundation facts whose upstream work items are "added to Appendix F by
description": an arc-aware swept bound. The plan gives it no `FD-` number and no register priority; N33c-47
assigns `FD-FSIM-37` at P3 (see Identifier below).

Rejected: padding the chord bound by a fixed margin (no fixed margin covers a long link turning fast);
sub-stepping until the chord is close enough everywhere (cost without a guarantee).

**The counterexample.** A sphere of radius r = 0.05 m rides at orbital radius R = 1 m on a link that turns
through Δθ = 120° in one step. Its centre goes from A to B along an arc. The chord AB passes at distance
R·cos(Δθ/2) = 0.5 m from the axis; the arc reaches R = 1 m. The gap between them is the sagitta
R·(1 − cos(Δθ/2)) = 0.5 m. A bound on the straight segment AB padded by r reaches 0.55 m from the axis on the
bisector; the true swept volume reaches 1.05 m. Any obstacle between 0.55 m and 1.05 m on that side is missed.

**The change (DESIGN).** Two helpers beside the present one, both conservative and both documented with their
assumption:

1. *One rotation.* For a centre that rotates by an angle Δθ of magnitude at most π at orbital radius R about a
   fixed axis: the box of the segment AB dilated by the sagitta R·(1 − cos(Δθ/2)) plus r.
2. *Any motion with a path-length bound.* If L bounds the length of the centre's path over the step, every
   point X of the path satisfies ‖X − A‖ + ‖X − B‖ ≤ L, so the path lies in the segment AB dilated by
   ½·√(L² − ‖B − A‖²), plus r. For the counterexample L = 2π/3 m (the arc length) and the dilation is 0.589 m,
   which contains the sagitta bound.

The present helper is unchanged, and its documentation gains a pointer to the two. Additive.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In FrankenSim (added, in `fs-contact`):
- `sagitta_counterexample`: R = 1 m, Δθ = 120°, r = 0.05 m; the bound reaches at least 1.05 m from the axis on the bisector; the straight-motion helper reaches 0.55 m.
- `path_length_bound_contains_sagitta_bound`: for the same case, ‖B − A‖ = √3 = 1.7321 m, L = 2π/3 = 2.0944 m, dilation ½·√(L² − ‖B − A‖²) = 0.589 m ≥ 0.5 m.
- `half_turn`: Δθ = π at R = 0.5 m; sagitta 0.5 m; the bound contains the whole semicircle.
- `zero_rotation_reduces_to_straight`: Δθ = 0 gives the straight-motion box exactly.
- `contains_sampled_arc_points`: 10³ random (R, Δθ, r); 1,000 points on each arc, all inside.
Here:
- `arc_fixture_1000_variants`: the gate's variants; expected 1,000 of 1,000 detected.
- `chord_only_mutant_fails`: a broad phase using the straight-motion helper on the fixture must miss the obstacle.
- `multi_joint_sweeps_contained`: 10⁴ sweeps of two- and three-joint chains, 1,000 path points each, both implementations.
- `missing_speed_bound_is_refused`: a model with no speed limit and no actuator or load limits.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/upstream_nf_10.sh --phase pre|post`: both phases run the scene of a fast-turning link beside an
obstacle placed outside the padded chord and inside the arc, the hand instance of N18b-45 (reached through
N33c-44), with `frob sim run crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/traction/arc-arm.fbot --scene
crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/traction/arc-hand.fscene --tier batch-cpu --profile strict --seconds 0.01
--seed 12 --key nf10-<phase>-run --json`, then audit that run with `frob contact audit <run> --p 1 --detector
strict --key nf10-<phase>-audit --json` (`contact.audit` takes a run identity, and both verbs are run verbs that
need an idempotency key), asserting that the contact is found and that no missed contact is attributed to a
broad-phase rejection; phase `post` adds the differential table.

Logging per STD-E2E: the script sources the shared helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/upstream_nf_10/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`NF-10`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). It also records the sibling's pinned commit, the stack-receipt hash, the phase, each measured
value with its unit and threshold, digests where state is compared and, on failure, the first differing value
and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the
gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: sagitta R·(1 − cos(Δθ/2)) = 0.5 m at R = 1 m and 120°; reach 1.05 m with r = 0.05 m; path-length dilation 0.589 m.
- Negative controls: the chord-only mutant; a sagitta computed with Δθ/4 in place of Δθ/2 (0.134 m here), which must fail containment of the sampled arc points.
- Metamorphic relation: bounds are covariant under a common rigid motion of axis, arc and obstacle.
- Determinism lanes: bounds are computed in `f64` with outward-rounded final dilation; identical twice on one machine.
