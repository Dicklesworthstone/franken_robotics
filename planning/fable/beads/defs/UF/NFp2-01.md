## Background

The referee tier is FrankenSim's articulated dynamics and contact solve, consumed through the airlock crate
`frob-fsim`. Upstream work item FD-FSIM-02 lands a persistent contact time stepper in FrankenSim (its Moreau–Jean
lane: a stepper that owns the active set, the warm-start impulses and the tangential memory from step to step,
with exact checkpoint and restore). The plan's switch-over rule for that item is one sentence: "re-baseline the
fidelity ledger once".

The **fidelity ledger** records, per stratum, the distribution of disagreement between a faster tier and a slower
one on the frozen standard battery; an **envelope** is the frozen tolerance of a (stratum, quantity), immutable
once written (N16bp1-01). GATE-FID-01 (Fidelity, M2, TARGET) reads the (`batch-cpu`, `referee`) strata: median
and 95th-percentile disagreement at most 2% and 5% for base displacement over 2 s, 3% and 8% for energy per metre,
5% and 12% for stance impulse. When the referee's stepper changes, the referee's outputs change, and the numbers
frozen against the old stepper no longer describe the referee in use.

This bead is the fidelity side of the switch-over. It is separate from FD-FSIM-02 for an order-of-work reason:
the upstream change can and should start long before the M2 ledger exists, while the re-baseline needs both the
adopted change and the ledger's measurement (BD-212). A bead never waits on what waits on it, so the step that
needs both comes after both.

DESIGN, how "re-baseline once" is done without editing a frozen envelope: the stratum key contains the kernel
versions, and the new stepper is a new referee kernel version, so every (fast, `referee`) stratum of the new pin is
a new stratum with no envelope. The re-baseline is the measurement and freeze of those new strata, recorded once
with a written rationale under the cause `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` of the ledger's re-baseline record. The old strata
and their envelopes stay as history. Rejected: overwriting the old envelopes (immutability is the countermeasure
against tuning after outcomes); leaving the new strata unfrozen (every promotion against the referee would then
be refused with `FROB-REF-821`).

## Scope

In: the precondition check; the re-measurement of the frozen battery against the referee at the new pin; the one
re-baseline record and its document; the freeze of the new strata; the reading of GATE-FID-01 against them; the
case in which the change was adopted before the M2 freeze; the script.
Out: the upstream change, the pin move and the referee-side re-runs (FD-FSIM-02); the measurement driver, the
gate evaluator and the M2 freeze (BD-212); envelopes, the freeze routine and `record_rebaseline` (N16bp1-01); the
pairing of runs (N16bp2-01); the same step for witness migration (FD-FSIM-12, whose own switch-over uses the cause
`upstream:FD-FSIM-12`).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** The row of FD-FSIM-02 in `registries/upstream.toml` reads `landed` or `adopted` with a
   stack receipt newer than the pin move; FD-FSIM-02's referee-side record is on file (the referee's contact
   known-answer battery, the thin-barrier and sill fixtures and INV-KIN-02 on 10⁴ forced retries, all passing at
   the new pin). If any is missing the script stops and records nothing.
2. **Which case.** (a) BD-212 froze the M2 envelopes against the old stepper: go on to step 3. (b) The change was
   adopted before BD-212 measured: the M2 measurement was made against the new stepper, there is nothing to
   re-baseline, and this bead records exactly that: the pin at which GATE-FID-01 was first measured, and that the
   cause `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` is unused. It then closes.
3. **Re-measure.** Run BD-212's measurement on the same `BatteryId` against the referee at the new pin; for each
   reference class, `frob eval run <class design> --battery standard --class <c> --tier referee --key rb02-<c>
   --json` (the run seeds are the frozen battery's, fixed by its `BatteryId`; `eval.run` has no seed input, and an
   earlier text passed a `--run-seed` that its row does not list). The fast side is not re-run unless its kernels changed: the pairing
   hook matches the new referee episodes with the stored `batch-cpu` episodes by address. The pairs land in new
   strata, because the referee's kernel version differs.
4. **Document and record.** `docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline-FD-FSIM-02.md`: per class and quantity the old and
   new median and 95th percentile, the old and new envelope bounds, the stack receipt hash of the new pin, and
   the physical reason for each change that exceeds the old envelope (for example persistent contacts resolved by
   one law, no longer by an event solve called step after step). Its hash is written with
   `record_rebaseline("GATE-FID-01", "upstream:FD-FSIM-02", doc_hash, changed)`. A second call for this cause is
   refused by the store. From the script the function is reached through a Cargo example target of its crate, the
   pattern of the gate recorder: `cargo run -p frob-chronicle --example record_rebaseline -- --gate GATE-FID-01
   --cause upstream:FD-FSIM-02 --doc docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline-FD-FSIM-02.md` (neither a verb nor a binary;
   N16bp1-01 defines the function and exposes this target, which BD-212's `m2-baseline` record uses as well).
5. **Freeze.** After the record exists (a re-baselined threshold reaches the ledger only through strata frozen
   after it), the new strata are frozen with the freeze action, the battery run with `--freeze-envelopes` in a
   session holding the `fidelity:admin` scope, under its own key (`frob eval run <class design> --battery standard
   --class <c> --tier referee --freeze-envelopes --key rb02-frz-<c> --json`; the key of step 3 would return that
   run's stored receipt and freeze nothing), by N16bp1-01's bound rule: the three gated quantities at the GATE-FID-01
   95th-percentile thresholds in force, every other quantity at its measured 95th percentile rounded up to two
   significant digits, task decisions with no tolerance.
6. **Read the gate.** GATE-FID-01 is evaluated on the new strata with BD-212's evaluator. Pass: recorded against
   the new pin (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FID-01 --receipt <hash>`), and
   FD-FSIM-02's row becomes `adopted`. Fail: the gate row is recorded as failed at the new pin, the row stays
   `landed`, the pin move is not rolled back (a sibling's newer clean head is never rolled back locally), and the
   KILL-CONTACT trigger is re-read on the new numbers by its decision bead. Thresholds are not edited here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With FD-FSIM-02 adopted after the M2 freeze: every (`batch-cpu`, `referee`) stratum of each reference class has a new stratum at the new referee kernel version with a frozen envelope, and the old strata and their `EnvelopeId`s are unchanged, byte for byte.
- [ ] Exactly one re-baseline record exists for (GATE-FID-01, `upstream:FD-FSIM-02`); it cites the document's hash and the stack receipt; a second attempt is refused, naming the first document.
- [ ] The document lists, per class and quantity, the old and new median, 95th percentile and bound.
- [ ] GATE-FID-01 is recorded against the new pin with measured values, by a session that did not implement the work, or recorded as failed with the consequences of step 6.
- [ ] With FD-FSIM-02 adopted before the M2 freeze: no re-baseline record for this cause exists, and the bead's closing note names the pin of the first measurement.
- [ ] The script refuses to start when a precondition of step 1 is missing.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/upstream_rebaseline.rs` (real store in a temporary directory; strata labelled
`synthetic`):
- `new_referee_version_is_new_stratum`: pairs written under referee kernel versions v1 and v2 give two strata; freezing v2 leaves v1's `EnvelopeId` unchanged.
- `rebaseline_cause_single_use`: `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` accepted once for GATE-FID-01 after an `m2-baseline` record exists; refused the second time with the first document's hash in the message.
- `case_b_records_nothing`: with no frozen stratum at the old version, the driver writes no re-baseline record and reports case (b).
- `precondition_guard`: a registry row reading `open` stops the driver before any run.
- `gate_read_on_new_strata_only`: the evaluator, given both versions, reads the v2 strata (displacement median 1.8% at v2 and 2.4% at v1 → pass).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/upstream_fd_fsim_02_rebaseline.sh` (plain shell, seed-explicit, idempotent, non-zero on failure):
`preconditions` (`cargo xtask check-stack`; `frob doctor --json` for the registry row); `before` (`frob fidelity
ledger --class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json` per class: the old strata and their envelope
identities); `measure` (the command of step 3); `record` and `freeze` (the commands of steps 4 and 5; the freeze
also once without the `fidelity:admin` scope, expecting `FROB-REF-720`, exit 5); `after` (the same ledger read: old
identities unchanged, new strata frozen); `second-attempt` (the record command repeated, expecting the refusal
naming the first document); `gate` (the gate verdict per new stratum read with `frob fidelity ledger --class <c>
--tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --kernel-versions <new referee version> --json`, exit 2 when a stratum misses its
threshold; then the recorder command of step 6, run by a session that did not implement the work). Log: `target/e2e-logs/upstream_fd_fsim_02_rebaseline/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript; every
line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command line, exit code, wall time and
the envelope's evidence block; `metric` lines per class and quantity with the old and new median and 95th
percentile, their thresholds and units, the old and new `EnvelopeId`s, the sibling's pinned commit and the stack
receipt hash; the refusal of the second attempt with its message; on failure the first stratum whose old
envelope changed, or the first address whose pair is missing, with the row paths; a `summary` line naming
GATE-FID-01 and the case (a or b).

### Known answers and negative controls

- GATE-FID-01's thresholds (2% and 5%, 3% and 8%, 5% and 12%); the gate records measured values.
- Negative controls: a second re-baseline for this cause (refused); a driver that edits an old envelope in place
  (the `before` and `after` identities differ and the script fails); a re-baseline attempted while the registry
  row reads `open` (refused by the precondition).
- Determinism lanes: the re-measurement twice on one machine and across worker counts gives identical pair rows
  on the referee (class D2 within the tier).

## Considerations

- Cost: one referee pass over the battery, 3.3 to 16.7 core-hours for three reference classes at 1.0 to 0.2 times
  real time per core; the freeze run of step 5 is a second battery run and costs as much again unless the store
  serves the stored referee episodes of step 3 by address (BD-212's driver decides; the document records which).
- FD-FSIM-12 (a moving-surface owner upstream) changes the referee's stepper as well and has the same step with
  its own cause; if both land in one pin move, each still records its own document, in the order they landed.
- No milestone waits on this bead: until it runs, results adjudicated by the referee at the new pin are in
  unfrozen strata and their promotion is refused with `FROB-REF-821`, which is the fail-closed state.
- Neither a pin nor the toolchain moves during the ten-fold tournament of M7, so this bead does not run then.
