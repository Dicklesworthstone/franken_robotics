## Background

Upstream work item FD-FDGR-01 makes the owner's drone reconstruction project
(`franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction`) publish a geometry generation in metres, with a scale witness and with
coverage and uncertainty per region. The plan's switch-over rule for that item is: "Run INT-FDGR-01, then
GATE-HAB-03 on one capture." INT-FDGR-01 is the integration gate of the drone scan lane: the sibling's schema
fixtures validate; a geometry generation whose scale status is not metric is refused; coverage masks become the
scene's unknown-space label, so that a clearance answer treats an unscanned region as unknown and never as free;
the privacy inheritance of real-place data applies to the result. GATE-HAB-03 is the Reality gate of real-place
ingestion: one real place ingested end to end, privately, with its scale label and unknown regions respected, and
at least one probe request emitted for it; a place for which no capture exists is recorded as `no-claim`, not as
a pass. With the switch-over the posture of that foundation moves from Pattern (a schema adapter, no crate
dependency) to Subprocess (the sibling's tool is invoked as a binary; still no crate of it in this build graph).

This bead is the gate side of that switch-over. It is separate from FD-FDGR-01 for an order-of-work reason (the
pattern of NFp2-01): the upstream work can start as soon as the M3 schema pilot (N33c-25) has fixed what the
adapter reads, while running the two gates needs the `place` form of `scene.import` with the `fdgr` adapter (the
M5 capstone BD-398) and the gate script (N19c-07). A bead never waits on what waits on it, so the step that needs
both comes after both, and the upstream item no longer waits for the M5 capstone before its work begins.

The drone scan lane is an upper-tier mechanism with its own pilot: the `[[pilot]]` record that N33c-25 writes to
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`; mechanism real-place ingestion,
drone scan lane, `tier = "F"`, `gated_beads = ["NFp2-02"]`). This bead is the drone lane's own mechanism work, so
it is not ready until `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead NFp2-02 --json`
(N38a-09) answers `Fundable`. The record gates this bead only; the importer beads of the twin lane are gated by
N19c-01's record, so a drone-lane `falsifier-holds` blocks this bead and not them.

## Scope

In: the precondition check; the subprocess run of the sibling's tool at the pinned commit; the contract row of the
drone adapter in the importer's contract file; INT-FDGR-01 through the verb at the new pin; GATE-HAB-03 through the
drone lane on one capture (or `no-claim`); the registry row's move to `adopted` and the foundation's posture to
`subprocess`; the script.

Out: the upstream change, its fixture, the pin move and the admission record of the move (FD-FDGR-01); the importer,
the drone adapter and the place form (BD-398, N19c-04, N33c-25); the gate script itself (N19c-07); the contract file
and its enforcement (N19c-09); the KILL-INGEST decision (N19c-08: when this bead runs after that decision, its gate
values are evidence for a later re-reading, never a rewrite of the decision of record); the twin-package route and
its switch-over (FD-FSS-01, NFp2-03).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** The row of FD-FDGR-01 in `registries/upstream.toml` reads `landed`, with the sibling's pin in
   `franken-stack.lock` moved to a clean commit that contains stage 3 (publication); `cargo xtask check-stack`
   reports the sibling on-pin and clean under a stack receipt newer than the move; FD-FDGR-01's fixture
   (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test upstream_fd_fdgr_01 -- --ignored`) passes at the new pin; and
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead NFp2-02 --json` answers `Fundable` for N33c-25's
   drone-lane record. If any is missing the script stops before any `frob` command that writes, and records
   nothing. On `Blocked{falsifier-holds}` it also prints the fallback the record names (mesh and point-cloud import
   with an operator-declared scale and `reality: no-claim`) and its kill gate, KILL-INGEST; on any other `Blocked`
   reason it prints that reason.
2. **The generation, by subprocess.** The script runs the sibling's command-line entry of stage 3 (as named in
   FD-FDGR-01's closing note, from the sibling's checkout at the pinned commit, never a copy) on the sibling's own
   smallest sample capture, writing the generation to a temporary directory. The binary's path, the commit and the
   output's digest go into the log and the receipt. No crate of the sibling enters `Cargo.lock` (checked again).
3. **Contract row.** The admitted triple (adapter `fdgr`, the sibling's commit, the digests of
   `geometry_generation.schema.json` and `scale_model.schema.json`) is added to `registries/ingest_contract.toml`
   (N19c-09). DESIGN, rule for adding it: under OPEN-HAB-5's default ("the walkthrough twin package; the drone route
   when it publishes geometry") the route has now published, so the row is added; if the owner's ruling recorded
   in `docs/adr/OPEN-HAB-5.md` excluded the drone route, the row is added only after a new ruling supersedes that
   record, and until then this bead runs step 4 on the schema fixtures only and leaves the row `landed`.
4. **INT-FDGR-01 through the verb at the new pin.** (a) The eight schema fixtures of N33c-25 through
   `frob --json scene import --kind place --from fdgr <fixture> --key fsw-fx-<a…h>`, each with the outcome
   N33c-25 fixed. (b) The published sample generation: imports (exit 0 or 2) with a scale label equal to the
   sibling's `scale_status` (`witnessed` or `surveyed`), the contract row cited in the result; the same generation
   with its scale status rewritten to `relative_only` (a planted copy) is refused, and a metric task on it is
   `FROB-REF-304`; a clearance query across a region the coverage root marks unobserved is `unresolved`, never a
   witness; a still exported without the private scope and flag is `FROB-REF-731`, exit 5. The value is recorded
   with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate INT-FDGR-01 --receipt <hash> --part M5` by
   a session that did not implement the work.
5. **GATE-HAB-03 through the drone lane.** N19c-07's script on one capture processed by the sibling:
   `scripts/e2e/gate_hab_03_real_place.sh --lane private --from fdgr --package <generation>`, with the owner's
   configured private root; or, if no capture of a real place is admitted, the rehearsal lane on the sample
   generation and the row `no-claim` naming the missing capture. The scale label must be `witnessed` or better for
   a pass. Recorded through the same recorder, by a fresh session.
6. **Adoption.** The row's `status` becomes `adopted`, citing the receipts of steps 4 and 5 and the stack receipt;
   the foundation's `posture` in its `franken-stack.lock` row (N33a-01's text form; `registries/upstream.toml` has no
   posture field and refuses unknown fields) moves from `pattern` to `subprocess`, and a receipt is written for that
   lock with `cargo xtask check-stack --write-receipt --reason admission --gate INT-FDGR-01`; the interim posture of the drone
   lane (schema adapter only) ends. If GATE-HAB-03 could not run on a real capture, the row is still `adopted` with
   the gate `no-claim` (the plan's rule: a missing capture is `no-claim`, not a failure of the switch-over).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With a precondition of step 1 missing, the script stops before any write and nothing is recorded; when the pilot check answers `Blocked{falsifier-holds}`, the stop prints the fallback of N33c-25's record and KILL-INGEST.
- [ ] The generation is produced by invoking the sibling's binary at the pinned commit; its commit and digest are in the receipt; no crate of the sibling is in `Cargo.lock`.
- [ ] The contract file holds the drone adapter's row (or the record says which ruling withholds it), and a generation whose schema digest is not in it is refused with `FROB-REF-303`.
- [ ] INT-FDGR-01 is recorded at the new pin with measured values: the eight fixtures' outcomes, the published generation's label equal to the sibling's status, the non-metric copy refused, the unobserved region `unresolved`, the private mark inherited.
- [ ] GATE-HAB-03 is recorded on one capture through the drone lane with label `witnessed` or better and at least one probe request, or `no-claim` naming the missing capture; never a pass from the rehearsal lane.
- [ ] Both gate values are recorded by a session that did not implement the work.
- [ ] The row of FD-FDGR-01 reads `adopted` with the receipts, and the foundation's posture reads `subprocess`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-scene/tests/fdgr_switchover.rs` (real files in a temporary directory; generations written to the
sibling's schema and labelled `synthetic`):
- `non_metric_scale_is_refused`: a generation with `scale_status = "relative_only"`; expected the import refusal, and a metric task on the pack `FROB-REF-304`.
- `unobserved_region_is_unknown_not_free`: the half-observed corridor; a traversal query through the far half returns `unknown` (CX-355 as a test).
- `privacy_mark_is_inherited`: a scene imported from a private capture is private, and so is every artifact derived from it.
- `label_equals_sibling_status`: `witnessed` → `witnessed`; `surveyed` → `surveyed`; `estimated` → `estimated`; a status string the adapter does not know → `relative-only` (N19c-02's mapping).
- `contract_row_required`: a generation whose schema digest is not in the contract file → `FROB-REF-303` with the admitted rows as the repair.
- `precondition_guard` (the script's helper, in `scripts/e2e/tests/fdgr_switchover_helpers.sh`, plain bash sourcing the STD-E2E helper): a row reading `open`, a dirty sibling tree, or a scratch pilot register whose drone-lane record carries `falsifier-holds` (the pilot check answering `Blocked{falsifier-holds}`, with the fallback printed) stops the script before any command that writes.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/upstream_fd_fdgr_01_switchover.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; header `bead=NFp2-02 milestone=M5
gates=INT-FDGR-01,GATE-HAB-03 plants=coverage-ignored,witnessed-without-model,relative-as-metric`; `roots.private`
of the scratch state and `FROB_PRIVATE_ROOT` point at a temporary directory outside the repository; every `run`
call carries its own `--key`): `preconditions` (`cargo xtask check-stack`; `frob --json doctor` for the registry
row; the fixture test with `--ignored`, expecting a pass; `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots --
fundable --bead NFp2-02 --json`, expecting `Fundable`, its answer and the pilot record's identifier logged); `generate` (the sibling's binary at the pin, step 2);
`contract` (the contract row read back; a generation with an altered schema digest, expecting `FROB-REF-303`);
`int-fdgr-01` (the imports of step 4 with keys `fsw-fx-<a…h>`, `fsw-gen-1`, `fsw-rel-1`; `frob --json scene accept
<id> --key fsw-acc-1`; `frob --json scene fit … --key fsw-fit-1` across the unobserved region; `frob --json artifact
export <still digest> --out exports/ --key fsw-exp-1`, expecting `FROB-REF-731`, exit 5); `gate-hab-03` (N19c-07's
script as in step 5, its own log linked by path and digest); `adopt` (the row and posture read back). Log:
`target/e2e-logs/upstream_fd_fdgr_01_switchover/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line,
exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; it also records the sibling's pinned commit, the binary's
digest, the stack-receipt hash, the generation's digest, the scale label against the sibling's status, the
unknown fraction, each refusal's code, exit code, pointer, observed and required, and, on failure, the first
differing value and the artifact identifiers needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed,
failed and skipped and names INT-FDGR-01 and GATE-HAB-03. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: 0.001 m in 2.000 m is 500 parts per million (the sibling's scale residual for its surveyed
  baseline, read from the generation's scale model); CX-355 (a corridor whose far half was never observed appears
  as empty space in a naive import; with the coverage mask the same query returns unknown).
- Negative controls (each must make the script fail): an importer that ignores the coverage root answers `free` in
  the unobserved half; a generation claiming `witnessed` without a scale model is accepted (it must be refused by
  schema validation); a `relative_only` copy imported as metric; a sibling crate added to `Cargo.lock`.
- Determinism: the import of one generation twice under distinct keys gives the same `SceneId`; the sibling's own
  determinism statement for the reconstruction is recorded with the import, not assumed.

## Considerations

- No milestone waits on this bead: until it runs, the drone lane is a schema adapter (the interim posture of
  FD-FDGR-01), which is the fail-closed state.
- If KILL-INGEST fired at M5 before this bead runs, place imports are viewing-grade; a GATE-HAB-03 pass here at
  `witnessed` is the capture N19c-08 names as the condition for re-opening, and is handed to a new ADR that
  supersedes the decision of record from its date; it does not rewrite it.
- The sibling's command-line entry is the sibling's to name; this bead reads it from FD-FDGR-01's closing note and
  records it with its commit, so a renamed entry is a precondition failure, not a silent change.
- Neither a pin nor the toolchain moves during the ten-fold tournament of M7, so this bead does not run then.
- Real-place data is private: the generation of a real capture lives beneath the private root, and only digests
  and labels are logged.
