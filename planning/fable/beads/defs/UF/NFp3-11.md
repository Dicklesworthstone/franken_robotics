## Background

The referee tier is FrankenSim's articulated dynamics and contact solve, consumed through the airlock crate
`frob-fsim`. FrankenSim's sustained contact step keeps fixed material witnesses and a fixed contact frame for each
body pair, so a wheel rolling over a lip, a sliding sole and a foot rocking from heel to toe are refused by name
inside FrankenSim, and this project's transactional step migrates witnesses above the airlock instead. Upstream
work item FD-FSIM-12 lands an owner for moving witnesses in FrankenSim (a moving-surface mode of the sustained
step: witnesses relocated, the contact frame rebuilt, the tangential memory transported or reset), and the referee
then delegates migration to it. The plan's switch-over rule for that item is one sentence: "re-baseline the
fidelity ledger once".

The **fidelity ledger** records, per stratum, the distribution of disagreement between a faster tier and a slower
one on the frozen standard battery; an **envelope** is the frozen tolerance of a (stratum, quantity), immutable
once written (N16bp1-01). GATE-FID-01 (Fidelity, M2, TARGET) reads the (`batch-cpu`, `referee`) strata: median
and 95th-percentile disagreement at most 2% and 5% for base displacement over 2 s, 3% and 8% for energy per metre,
5% and 12% for stance impulse. GATE-FID-03 (Fidelity, M2, TARGET) is the referee's own coverage: per robot class,
the share of standard-battery scenarios the referee completes, with the refusals broken down by cause
(`contact-cap`, `fixed-witness-gate`, `missing-joint-class`, `kinematic-loop`), the `strict` share and the
promotion wall-clock; coverage below 90% in a reference class falsifies BET-34 for that class, and the `strict`
profile is then the named adjudicator. When the referee stops refusing moving witnesses, both change: the
referee's outputs are no longer the ones the envelopes were frozen against, and scenarios it used to refuse with
the cause `fixed-witness-gate` now complete.

This bead is the fidelity side of the switch-over. It is separate from FD-FSIM-12 for an order-of-work reason, the
one that separates NFp2-01 from FD-FSIM-02: the upstream change can and should start as soon as this project's own
migration exists (M1), while the re-baseline needs the adopted change, the ledger's M2 measurement (BD-212), the
envelope store (N16bp1-01) and the coverage campaign (BD-213). A bead never waits on what waits on it, so the step
that needs all of them comes after all of them.

DESIGN, how "re-baseline once" is done without editing a frozen envelope: the stratum key contains the kernel
versions, and FD-FSIM-12 gives the delegating referee a new referee kernel version, so every (fast, `referee`)
stratum of the new pin is a new stratum with no envelope. The re-baseline is the measurement and freeze of those
new strata, recorded once with a written rationale under the cause `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` of the ledger's
re-baseline record (one of the three causes N16bp1-01's closed list admits). The old strata and their envelopes
stay as history. Rejected: overwriting the old envelopes (immutability is the countermeasure against tuning after
outcomes); leaving the new strata unfrozen (every promotion against the referee would then be refused with
`FROB-REF-821`); re-using FD-FSIM-02's cause for this item (each upstream change that replaces the referee's
contact stepper has its own single use, so that neither can hide behind the other).

## Scope

In: the precondition check; the re-measurement of the frozen battery against the referee at the new pin; the one
re-baseline record for this cause and its document; the freeze of the new strata; the reading of GATE-FID-01
against them; the re-issue of the coverage table and the reading of GATE-FID-03; the case in which the change was
adopted before the M2 freeze; the case in which FD-FSIM-02 was adopted in the same pin move; the move of
FD-FSIM-12's row to `adopted`; the script.
Out: the upstream change, the pin move, the referee-side re-runs and the before-and-after comparison of the
transition fixture (FD-FSIM-12); the measurement driver, the gate evaluator and the M2 freeze (BD-212); envelopes,
the freeze routine and `record_rebaseline` (N16bp1-01); the pairing of runs (N16bp2-01); the coverage campaign,
its ledger table and the verb `fidelity.coverage` (BD-213); the same step for the persistent stepper (NFp2-01,
cause `upstream:FD-FSIM-02`); the capacity side of referee coverage (NFp3-12, for FD-FSIM-13); the decision on
BET-34 (its own bead, which reads the table this bead re-issues).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** The row of FD-FSIM-12 in `registries/upstream.toml` reads `landed` or `adopted` with a
   stack receipt newer than the pin move; FD-FSIM-12's referee-side record is on file (INT-FSIM-07 in its adopted
   form, the referee's contact battery, the thin-barrier and sill fixtures, and the three before-and-after
   differences of the `wheel-over-lip` fixture, all at the new pin); the airlock reports the moving-surface
   capability. If any is missing the script stops and records nothing.
2. **Which case.** (a) BD-212 froze the M2 envelopes against the referee that migrates above the airlock: go on
   to step 3. (b) The change was adopted before BD-212 measured: the M2 measurement was made against the
   delegating referee, there is nothing to re-baseline, and this bead records exactly that (the pin at which
   GATE-FID-01 was first measured, and that the cause `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` is unused), re-issues the coverage
   table (step 7) only if BD-213's rows were written at an older stack-lock digest, and closes. (c) FD-FSIM-02
   was adopted in the same pin move: the new strata are the same for both items. The battery is measured once
   (the driver looks for stored pairs at the new referee kernel version before it starts a run); each item
   writes its own document and its own record, in the order the two changes landed on the sibling's `main`; and
   the strata are frozen once, after both records exist: whichever of NFp2-01 and this bead runs first stops
   after its record with the note `freeze waits for <the other cause>`, and the second freezes. If the first has
   already frozen them, the second writes its record with an empty `changed` list, its document says that the
   thresholds in force were frozen under the other cause, and it reads the gate from the same strata.
3. **Re-measure.** Run BD-212's measurement on the same `BatteryId` against the referee at the new pin; for each
   reference class, `frob eval run --battery standard --class <c> --tier referee --key rb12-<c> --json` (in the
   battery form the design may be omitted when the class is given; the run seeds are the frozen battery's, fixed
   by its `BatteryId`). The fast side is not re-run unless its kernels changed: the pairing hook of N16bp2-01
   matches the new referee episodes with the stored `batch-cpu` episodes by address. The pairs land in new strata,
   because the referee's kernel version differs.
4. **Document and record.** `docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline-FD-FSIM-12.md`: per class and quantity the old and
   new median and 95th percentile, the old and new envelope bounds, the stack receipt hash of the new pin, the
   three differences FD-FSIM-12 recorded on the transition fixture (as context: one fixture is not a stratum), and
   the physical reason for each change that exceeds the old envelope (for example witnesses relocated on both
   bodies at one world contact point inside the solve, where the interim posture cut the interval at each
   migration; or tangential memory transported across an edge that the interim posture reset). Its hash is written
   with `record_rebaseline("GATE-FID-01", "upstream:FD-FSIM-12", doc_hash, changed)`, reached from the script
   through N16bp1-01's example target: `cargo run -p frob-chronicle --example record_rebaseline -- --gate
   GATE-FID-01 --cause upstream:FD-FSIM-12 --doc docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline-FD-FSIM-12.md` (neither a verb nor
   a binary). A second call for this cause is refused by the store, naming the first document.
5. **Freeze.** After the record exists (a re-baselined threshold reaches the ledger only through strata frozen
   after it), the new strata are frozen with the freeze action, the battery run with `--freeze-envelopes` in a
   session holding the `fidelity:admin` scope, under its own key (`frob eval run --battery standard --class <c>
   --tier referee --freeze-envelopes --key rb12-frz-<c> --json`; the key of step 3 would return that run's stored
   receipt and freeze nothing), by N16bp1-01's bound rule: the three gated quantities at the GATE-FID-01
   95th-percentile thresholds in force, every other quantity at its measured 95th percentile rounded up to two
   significant digits, task decisions with no tolerance.
6. **Read GATE-FID-01.** The gate is evaluated on the new strata with BD-212's evaluator. Pass: its receipt is
   recorded against the new pin by a session that did not implement the work (`cargo run -p frob-conformance
   --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FID-01 --receipt <hash>`; the script writes no gate record). Fail: the
   gate row is recorded as failed at the new pin, FD-FSIM-12's row stays `landed`, the pin move is not rolled
   back (a sibling's newer clean head is never rolled back locally), and the KILL-CONTACT trigger is re-read on
   the new numbers by its decision bead. Thresholds are not edited here.
7. **Re-issue the coverage table.** The table `referee_class_coverage` is keyed by (class, battery identity,
   stack-lock digest, transaction numerics identity), so the new pin has no row. GATE-FID-03's Gauntlet entry is
   run at the new pin, as BD-213 defines it: `FROB_TEST_SEED=17 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity
   -- GATE-FID-03` (with `--features gauntlet-mutations` when its controls run); it submits one campaign job per
   class (`frob eval run --battery <battery> --class <class> --tier referee --adjudicate strict-fallback --record
   coverage --key <k> --json`), reads the rows back through `fidelity.coverage`, checks the partition identity
   (completed + refused by the four causes + numerical faults = scenarios) and the adjudicator rule, and writes a
   hash-named receipt. This bead then compares, per reference class, the new row with the row at the old pin:
   - the count refused with cause `fixed-witness-gate` must not rise (a rise is a finding that blocks adoption and
     is reported upstream with the scenarios);
   - it is expected to fall (HYPOTHESIS; the measurement is the test), and a class in which it does not fall is
     named in the record with the scenarios still refused and the upstream reason each carries;
   - a scenario that moved from `fixed-witness-gate` to another cause (`contact-cap` most likely, once the
     transition is no longer the first refusal) is listed as moved, never counted as completed;
   - the referee's share, the adjudicator (`referee` at 0.90 or above, `batch-cpu:strict` below) and the BET-34
     status of the class are re-read from the new row, and a class whose adjudicator changed is named first.
   The old rows are not edited: they are the coverage of the old stack. The gate is recorded from the entry's
   receipt by a session that did not implement the work (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate --
   --gate GATE-FID-03 --receipt <hash>`).
8. **Adopt.** When GATE-FID-01 passed on the new strata and the coverage rows exist with no rise of the cause,
   FD-FSIM-12's row in `registries/upstream.toml` becomes `adopted`, citing both receipts and the document. The
   interim posture then ends for the referee; this project's own migration stays for the `strict` profile.

Determinism: the re-measurement is class D2 within the referee tier (identical pair rows twice on one machine
and across worker counts); the coverage rows are exact counts. Evidence of what this bead writes: claim Fidelity,
class `Tested`, tier `referee`, `reality: no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With FD-FSIM-12 adopted after the M2 freeze: every (`batch-cpu`, `referee`) stratum of each reference class has a new stratum at the new referee kernel version with a frozen envelope, and the old strata and their `EnvelopeId`s are unchanged, byte for byte.
- [ ] Exactly one re-baseline record exists for (GATE-FID-01, `upstream:FD-FSIM-12`); it cites the document's hash and the stack receipt; a second attempt is refused, naming the first document; a record for `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` neither satisfies nor consumes it.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline-FD-FSIM-12.md` lists, per class and quantity, the old and new median, 95th percentile and bound, and the three transition-fixture differences from FD-FSIM-12.
- [ ] GATE-FID-01 is recorded against the new pin with measured values, by a session that did not implement the work, or recorded as failed with the consequences of step 6.
- [ ] A `referee_class_coverage` row exists at the new stack-lock digest for every reference class; the partition identity holds in each; the rows at the old digest are unchanged; GATE-FID-03 is recorded from the entry's receipt.
- [ ] Per class, the count refused with `fixed-witness-gate` at the new pin is not above the count at the old pin, and the comparison (old and new counts by cause, scenarios moved to another cause, share, adjudicator, BET-34 status) is in the record.
- [ ] With FD-FSIM-12 adopted before the M2 freeze: no re-baseline record for this cause exists, and the bead's closing note names the pin of the first measurement.
- [ ] With FD-FSIM-02 adopted in the same pin move: the battery was measured once, two documents and two records exist in landing order, and the strata were frozen once, after both records (or, where the other switch-over had already frozen them, this record's `changed` list is empty and its document says under which cause the thresholds in force were frozen).
- [ ] FD-FSIM-12's row reads `adopted` with the receipts only after both gates were read; with either unread or failed it reads `landed`.
- [ ] The script refuses to start when a precondition of step 1 is missing.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/upstream_rebaseline_witness.rs` (real store in a temporary directory; strata and
coverage rows labelled `synthetic`):
- `cause_fd_fsim_12_single_use`: `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` is accepted once for GATE-FID-01 after an `m2-baseline` record exists, and refused the second time with the first document's hash in the message.
- `two_upstream_causes_are_independent`: with `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` already recorded, `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` is still accepted once; recording it does not make a second `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` acceptable.
- `delegating_referee_is_new_stratum`: pairs written under referee kernel versions v1 (migration above the airlock) and v2 (delegated) give two strata; freezing v2 leaves v1's `EnvelopeId` unchanged.
- `freeze_refused_before_record`: the driver, asked to freeze the v2 strata with no record for the cause, stops with the missing record named and freezes nothing.
- `case_b_records_nothing`: with no frozen stratum at the old version, the driver writes no re-baseline record and reports case (b).
- `case_c_measures_once`: with the pairs of the new version already stored by the other switch-over, the driver starts no battery run and writes its own record; with only its own record present it stops with `freeze waits for upstream:FD-FSIM-02`; with both present it freezes; with the strata already frozen it writes a record whose `changed` list is empty.
- `coverage_row_is_new_key`: a row written at stack-lock digest d2 leaves the row at d1 byte-identical; `fidelity.coverage --class <c>` at the new stack returns the d2 row.
- `fixed_witness_cause_must_not_rise`: old row `{ fixed-witness-gate: 6, contact-cap: 48 }`, new row `{ 2, 51 }` over 200 scenarios with three scenarios moved: accepted, 4 fewer refusals of the cause, 3 listed as moved to `contact-cap`, 1 newly completed; new row `{ 7, 48 }`: the comparison fails naming the class and the cause.
- `partition_identity_checked`: a new row whose completed, refused and faulted counts sum to 199 of 200 is rejected before any comparison.
- `precondition_guard`: a registry row reading `open`, or a missing referee-side record, stops the driver before any run.
- `adopted_requires_both_gates`: the row is set to `adopted` only with a passing GATE-FID-01 receipt on the new strata and coverage rows at the new digest; with either missing it stays `landed`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/upstream_fd_fsim_12_rebaseline.sh` (plain shell, sources the shared helper of STD-E2E, seed-explicit,
idempotent, non-zero on failure). Steps: `preconditions` (`cargo xtask check-stack`; `frob doctor --section
upstream --json` for the registry row; the referee-side record of FD-FSIM-12); `before` (`frob fidelity ledger
--class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json` and `frob fidelity coverage --class <c> --json` per class: the
old strata with their envelope identities, and the old coverage rows by cause); `measure` (the command of step
3); `record` and `freeze` (the commands of steps 4 and 5; the freeze also once without the `fidelity:admin`
scope, expecting `FROB-REF-720`, exit 5); `after` (the same ledger read: old identities unchanged, new strata
frozen); `second-attempt` (the record command repeated, expecting the refusal naming the first document);
`gate-fid-01` (the verdict per new stratum read with `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair
batch-cpu,referee --kernel-versions <new referee version> --json`, exit 2 when a stratum misses its threshold);
`coverage` (the Gauntlet entry of step 7, then `frob fidelity coverage --class <c> --json` per class and the
comparison by cause); `adopt` (the row read back as `adopted`, or `landed` with the unread gate named). The two
recorder commands of steps 6 and 7 are run afterwards by a session that did not implement the work; the script
stops at the receipts.

Log: `target/e2e-logs/upstream_fd_fsim_12_rebaseline/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a human-readable transcript
beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`NFp3-11`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`,
`metric`, `refusal`, `end`); `cmd` lines carry the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines carry, per class and
quantity, the old and new median and 95th percentile with their thresholds and units and the old and new
`EnvelopeId`s, and per class the old and new coverage counts by cause, the scenarios moved, the share and the
adjudicator, with the sibling's pinned commit and the stack receipt hash; the refusal of the second attempt is
logged with its message; on failure the line carries the first differing value (the first stratum whose old
envelope changed, the first address whose pair is missing, or the first class whose `fixed-witness-gate` count
rose) and the row paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, the gates
touched (GATE-FID-01, GATE-FID-03) and the case (a, b or c). No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: GATE-FID-01's thresholds (2% and 5%, 3% and 8%, 5% and 12%); the partition identity of a
  coverage row; the adjudicator rule at 0.90; the hand case of `fixed_witness_cause_must_not_rise` (6 → 2 with
  three moved and one completed). The gates record measured values.
- Negative controls: a second re-baseline for this cause (refused); a record attempted under the cause
  `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` for this item when that cause is already used (refused, which shows the causes are not
  interchangeable); a driver that edits an old envelope in place (the `before` and `after` identities differ and
  the script fails); a freeze attempted before the record (refused by the driver); a coverage comparison that
  counts a scenario moved to `contact-cap` as completed (the partition identity fails); a re-baseline attempted
  while the registry row reads `open` (refused by the precondition).
- Determinism lanes: the re-measurement twice on one machine and across worker counts gives identical pair rows
  on the referee (class D2 within the tier); the coverage counts are identical on a second run of the campaign.

## Considerations

- Cost: one referee pass over the battery for the ledger, 3.3 to 16.7 core-hours for three reference classes at
  1.0 to 0.2 times real time per core; the freeze run of step 5 is a second battery run and costs as much again
  unless the store serves the stored referee episodes of step 3 by address (BD-212's driver decides; the document
  records which); the coverage campaign is a third pass of about 200 scenarios per class with the `strict`
  re-runs of what the referee still refuses.
- The expected fall of `fixed-witness-gate` is the reason the upstream item is P1, and it is a hypothesis: for
  tracked and many-sample robots the first refusal after the change is likely to be `contact-cap`, which only
  FD-FSIM-13 lifts (NFp3-12). The record therefore reports moved scenarios separately, so that the two items'
  effects on BET-34 are not confused.
- If FD-FSIM-12 and FD-FSIM-13 land in one pin move, the coverage campaign is run once and both beads read the
  same new rows, each for its own cause.
- No milestone waits on this bead: until it runs, results adjudicated by the delegating referee are in unfrozen
  strata and their promotion is refused with `FROB-REF-821`, and the coverage read at the new stack prints `"not
  measured"` with the campaign as its next action, which are the fail-closed states.
- Neither a pin nor the toolchain moves during the ten-fold tournament of M7, so this bead does not run then.
- Two implementations of migration exist after adoption (upstream for the referee, ours for `strict`); a class
  whose adjudicator is `batch-cpu:strict` keeps being adjudicated by ours, and the document says which classes
  those are.
