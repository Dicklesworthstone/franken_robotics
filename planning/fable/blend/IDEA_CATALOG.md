# FrankenRobotics idea catalog (consolidated across three exercises)

Compiled 2026-11-21 from 16 documents and one archive. 112 distinct ideas after merging about 225 raw entries. This file records what the documents claim; it does not improve or endorse them.

Verdict tally for the 112 rows: 24 CONSENSUS-STRONG, 7 CONTESTED, 13 KILLED, 10 MIXED, 58 UNSCORED. Length is about 21,000 words, roughly twice the brief's estimate, because merged rows keep each lane's variant of the mechanism. Sections 2 to 6 (about 4,500 words) are the compact view.

## 0. How to read this catalog

**Sets and lanes.**

| S3-MOR | same archive, `mor/` passes P01–P03, claims C001–C007 | "Astra" plan | Audited and executed |

CC = Claude, COD = Codex/GPT, AGY = Gemini.

**Convergence count (n).** Number of the nine wizard lanes (set × model) that proposed essentially the same idea. The same model in two sets counts twice, because it ran against a different plan without sight of the other run. A MOR appearance is shown as "+MOR" and not counted. Where a lane proposed an idea and cut it in its own winnowing, it still counts, and the row says so.

**Scores.** 0 to 1000, listed with the scoring model. Calibration, from the Set 1 report: COD mean 690 (range 369–864), CC mean 549 (220–780), AGY mean 861 (685–945). The report says AGY "rubber-stamped 3 subtle mathematical bugs" and that rank ordering should rely on COD and CC. Read every AGY score with that in mind.

**Verdict labels.**
- CONSENSUS-STRONG: 700 or more from every scorer, or proposed by three or more lanes that advanced it.
- CONTESTED: scorers differ by about 250 points or more, or straddle 700 with opposite conclusions.
- KILLED: scored low and conceded, withdrawn by its author, or shown infeasible by a calculation (given in the row).
- UNSCORED: no cross-model score.
- MIXED: a fifth label I added. Scored, below 700 from the strict scorer, no large gap, not killed. Almost all of these were adopted in a narrowed form.
- Where three or more lanes proposed an idea but every one of them cut it, I do not call it CONSENSUS-STRONG; the row says "convergent but cut".

**Evidence level.** Nothing in Set 1 or Set 2 is measured: every ratio there is paper arithmetic, and the authors say so. Only Set 3 contains executed experiments (section 5). "doubt:" marks a claim that looks wrong to me or to the extraction pass; everything else is recorded as claimed.

**Coverage.** I read directly: the Set 1 report, reactions, MOR report, disagreement register and Brenner artifact, and in Set 3 the README, `mor/CLAIMS.md`, `mor/raw/*.md`, `information/*.md`, plus (beyond the list I was given) `study/*.md`, `mor/MOR_CASE.md`, `sill/CRITIC.md`, the native Metal and CUDA result notes and the first part of `pusher/AUDIT_PILOT.md`. The six idea files and five Set 1 score files were read in full by six per-file extraction passes, and I merged their extracts. I checked all 78 Set 1 scores in those extracts against the report's matrix (all match) and spot-checked key numbers against the source files.

### 4.2 Gaps surfaced by review, audit or execution rather than by ideation

- **A speed test can be powerless** (S3 audit, K-112). The locked pusher protocol could show at most 1.46×; its "failure to reach 10×" was a guaranteed outcome counted as evidence.
- **A frozen decision rule may be unable to return some verdicts** (S3, K-112): no practical tie is reachable at 64 pairs.
- **Local contracts hide time correlation** (S3, K-001): two fingers with identical local summaries on a shared bus.
- **In-family checks cannot expose structural error** (S3, K-053): the omitted delayed law passes every residual check and reverses the decision.
- **The actuator authority model changes the hardware decision** (S3, K-074): independent-force and equal-spring models give 155 N and 175 N on the same foot.
- **Widening a hypothesis set by a hair invalidates certificates wholesale** (S3 follow-up): adding gain 33/32 makes it alias with gain 1 at error radius 1/64, and all 34,590 positive decisions on each grid become wrong for the amended mission.
- **Controller clock and rotor inertia flip benchmark outcomes** (S3): success fractions 0.093 and 0.780 become 1.000 and 1.000 with a 1 kHz loop or added inertia.
- **Contact counts diverge inside a same-morphology warp** (COD on CC-01): homogeneity by morphology is not homogeneity by work.
- **A screen can throw away the winner** (COD on TA-P1): audit discarded designs blind, and plant a winner the screen represents poorly.
- **Monitors cannot see a breach until it is too late** (COD on AGY FTA-P3): two friction regimes produce identical histories until first slip; test with indistinguishable pre-slip histories.
- **After the engine is fast, agent think time dominates** (S2-CC): at 30–120 s per serial turn, the levers become population verbs and fewer turns, not faster steps.
- **The plans measure themselves where they trail** (S2-CC): a 500k humanoid steps/s target against a reported 2.67M for MJX.
- **Scorer-proposed additions**: a constrained allocator with refusal reporting under any universal policy; a two-mode sampled-data shield fixture with a verified backup domain; a native paired-intervention replay harness before any browser bundle; "edit to first accepted result" as the unit for batching claims.
