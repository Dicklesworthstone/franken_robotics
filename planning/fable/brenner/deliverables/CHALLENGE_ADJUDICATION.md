### 2.2 What the upheld contests have in common

Most register errors are of three kinds: a number from one source or one assumed setting written as a range
(H-11, H-12, H-21, H-22b); a design choice written as a finding (H-01 "never", H-06 "six parameters", H-08 "double
precision"); and a verdict stronger than its evidence (H-09 "as stated", H-16 "capture the failures", H-18
"NARROWED" without a pack).

### 2.3 Claims rejected inside partly upheld contests

1. H-03: "0.86" is fabricated. It is published (second version of the paper) and audited.
2. H-01: a 6–12× advantage for sampling control in paired design comparisons. It needs five training seeds per
   design and a 20 ms planning model.
3. H-02 and H-21: "15–30%" and "15–40%" fine-tuning costs. Not derivable; the pack supports 2.5–25%, 20% and 50%.
4. H-04: 2.05× from specialization. The 2.5× phase speedup is assumed.
5. H-08: remove double precision. It stays as the default for one failure and as the escalation.
6. H-11: coverage of 3–35% as a surviving number. Both ends assume a referee speed.
7. H-18: REFUTED in the per-design-training regime. Illustrative arithmetic is not an instrumented session.
8. H-19: REFUTED on four to five gaps. Two of the five are not gaps, and the falsifier asks for load-bearing ones.
9. H-22: 5–15× from pairing on continuous metrics. The correlation is asserted.
10. Q-001: 10–30× by a four-stage funnel.

## 5. Revised answer to Q-001

**Q-001 is open.** No matched mission has been run by anyone, on any pipeline, and the record contains no
measurement of time to an accepted decision. The register's sentence "the realistic range is three to ten" is
withdrawn as an answer; it is paper arithmetic about one comparator.

What the record supports:

1. **No single mechanism delivers ten-fold**, and no conjunction has been shown to. This part of the register's
   answer stands.
2. **The denominator is an accepted decision, not a training run.** A design, a controller and an evidence package
   with a 1% failure bound at 95% confidence cost 299 failure-free episodes per accepted design in every arm. Seven
   minutes of quadruped training is one term of that, and on a narrow brief it may not be the largest.
3. **Two comparators give two different questions.**
   - Against the incumbent named in the question (per-design training with a standard outer loop), the candidate
     route is the conjunction the register listed: decide many designs per turn, avoid per-design training with
     family policies and inheritance, stop early, search for failures, and count decisions. Paper arithmetic gives
     about 3–3.5× on quadruped design cycles and about 15× on humanoid ones if per-design training can be replaced
     when designs are ranked. That condition rests on the two least measured links in the record (H-02, H-22a),
     and the nearest published indications are unfavourable (10% zero-shot on variable terrain in a diverse space;
     a rank correlation of 0.40 for an easier comparison).
   - Against a composed incumbent that hosts the same portable methods, every ingredient of that conjunction is
     marked portable in its own evidence pack. The expected ratio on fixed-topology CUDA missions is therefore near
     one, unless an engine-internal property binds. Two such properties are documented (a mixed-topology batch with
     run-to-run replay; a Metal tier), one engine-internal speed question is open (H-04b), and the rest of what an
     engine adds (an independent referee tier, certificates, replay) changes what is accepted, not how fast.
4. **Engine speed is a parity requirement**, with one qualification: whether structure-exploiting assembly and
   solve give more than parity is unmeasured (H-04b; ceiling 2.6× on an incumbent's trace), and it is not counted
   until it is.
5. **Killed as sources of the ten-fold, unchanged:** half precision (H-15); recursion-only kernel specialization
   (H-04); exact rollout reuse (H-09, first half); native coupled multiphysics in every world (H-16); sampling
   control as a stand-in evaluator (H-01); joint paving of high-dimensional boxes by isotropic bisection (H-06).

What the program does about it: build the composed-incumbent pipeline first, on one fixed-topology and one
mixed-topology brief; trace it by phase; run three single-change ablations (information, controller acquisition,
stepping); and report, per stratum, the recovery ratio (B − W)/(B − F) beside the headline ratio. The registered
threshold of the question (at most one tenth of the incumbent's GPU-hours and of its elapsed time, for the median
mission, at matched accepted quality) is unchanged.
