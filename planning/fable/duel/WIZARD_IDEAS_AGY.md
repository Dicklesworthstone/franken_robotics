## 0. Making "10×" Falsifiable: Where Time Actually Goes

Before evaluating ideas, define the measurable end-to-end metric missing from v0.2 (MOR_CASE PQ1, E016):

> **Time-to-Verified-Design (TTVD):** Wall-clock time on one workstation (2× RTX 4090 or Apple M5 Ultra) from a mission specification (terrain + interior archetypes, payload, doorway/stair geometry, duty cycle) to a **referee-promoted, exploit-free, structurally/fatigue-checked, fabrication-ready robot design + controller** passing held-out interior benchmarks.

In the strongest late-2026 incumbent pipeline (MuJoCo Warp / MJX / Isaac Lab + LLM co-design loop + external CAD/FEA), TTVD is governed by a multiplicative product:

```
TTVD = N_designs × (T_compile + T_control(N_steps / Throughput)) × M_debug_rework + T_CAD_FEA
```

- **Physics throughput:** Multi-kernel global-VRAM steppers (MJX, MuJoCo Warp, and v0.2's ~25-launch §14.3 pipeline) spill state, mass matrices, Jacobians, and contacts to HBM every 2 ms substep, capping single-4090 quadruped throughput around ~2–5M world-steps/s and crippling macOS (Newton/Isaac Lab are CPU-only on macOS; v0.2 targets just ≥25% of one 4090 on M5 Ultra).
- **Recompile & single-model lock-in:** Editing a morphology forces 30–180 s of XML/USD/graph rebuild because incumbent GPU engines broadcast one fixed `mjModel` across all worlds.
- **Controller re-acquisition (the largest wall-clock sink):** Every non-trivial morphology edit invalidates the previous RL policy, costing **5–30 minutes of PPO training per candidate** (MJX Playground: ~5 min quadruped on 2× 4090, ~25–30 min humanoid). v0.2's workaround—hand-crafted per-class baseline heuristics (§18.3)—explicitly targets only 80% 5 m flat-ground traversal and breaks on novel hybrids.
- **Credit assignment:** Without gradients through contact (MuJoCo Warp and PhysX are non-differentiable; v0.2 punts contact gradients to unscheduled M6) or causal debugging, agents burn 200–1,000 black-box trials guessing which continuous parameters or discrete components caused failure.
- **Physical fantasy:** Every incumbent stops at rigid capsules/boxes with fictional masses and zero fatigue/manufacturability verification, pushing weeks of CAD/FEA/re-simulation rework outside the loop—even though **FrankenSim already contains ~290k lines of 3-D CutFEM topology optimization, elasticity/fatigue, fabrication constraints, tolerance allocation, and faceted STEP export right next door**.

The five retained ideas attack **five orthogonal factors of this equation** so their gains multiply into a **20×–100× end-to-end TTVD unlock** that no incumbent engine plus a thin agent wrapper can cheaply replicate.

### #2: "Free" Implicit-Function-Theorem (IFT) Contact & Morphology Adjoints on the Symmetry-Reduced Design Manifold

#### 1. Mechanism (Crates, Algorithm, Data Layout)

- **The mathematical unlock (reusing Stage 8's already-factored Hessian):** At every substep, Stage 8 minimizes the strictly convex compliant contact objective Φ(v; z) = ½(v − v*(z))ᵀ A(z) (v − v*(z)) + Σ_c ℓ_c(J_c(z) v; z), where z = (q, v_prev, u, θ) combines state, control, and all continuous morphology/actuator parameters θ. At the converged velocity v_+*(z), optimality gives r(v_+*, z) ≡ ∇_v Φ(v_+*, z) = 0. Because ℓ_c is smooth and convex by construction (C-06), its Jacobian ∇_v r = H = A + JᵀGJ ≻ 0 is **already factored into tree-sparse LDLᵀ inside SRAM on the final Newton iteration**.
- **One-backsub adjoint pass:** Given an upstream cotangent v̄_+ = ∂ℒ/∂v_+ over a horizon of H = 16–32 steps:
  1. Solve H λ = v̄_+ by **one sparse triangular back-substitution reusing the cached LDLᵀ** (~350 FLOPs quadruped, ~1,800 FLOPs humanoid—under 5% of forward-step cost!).
  2. Compute exact pullbacks z̄ += −λᵀ (∂r/∂z) using `frob-kir`'s auto-derived reverse-mode straight-line program (SLP) of `K-cay-step`, `K-rnea`, `K-crba`, `K-act-l1`, and `K-cone-eval` (exact Lie-algebraic cotangents on 𝔰𝔢(3)* via dcayᵀ).
