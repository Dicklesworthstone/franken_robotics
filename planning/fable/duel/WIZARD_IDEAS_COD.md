2026-11-21 · Independent Codex proposal for plan v0.2 · Source review and proposed experiments

## What a tenfold win would mean

**Measure time from a task brief to the first robot and controller that clears a frozen acceptance battery.** A task names a scene population, required success and clearance, limits on mass and actuator demand, and a cost-of-transport target. Budget starts at brief intake and includes agent calls and waits, compilation, policy fitting, rejected proposals, data movement, referee runs and the final evidence package. A candidate that exhausts the budget is censored at that budget and counts as unresolved. Report resolution rate, restricted mean time to resolution at the shared cap, resolved-pair speedups, GPU-seconds, CPU core-seconds and peak memory. Aim for **at least a 10× speedup in restricted mean time at matched or better resolution rate and acceptance quality**, with uncertainty over paired task briefs. Freeze the cap and a per-family minimum resolution rate before timing. Carry every failed brief and unsupported workload in the denominator and publish the per-family result. A component-level 10× elsewhere in this document is a hypothesis about that component.

Use four initial briefs with held-out variants: a loaded legged robot on rubble and steps, a wheeled carrier docking in a tight warehouse, a tracked base on a ramp and stair transition, and a wheel-leg robot crossing thresholds and low-friction patches. Expand to the seven interior archetypes with layout- and parameter-level holdouts. Pin the same admissible geometry, component catalogs, seeded disturbances, evaluation thresholds and output schemas for all arms. Let competitors use their native batching, warm starts and best control workflows. Give them the same controller or model information whenever the task admits that comparison. Independent adjudication uses analytic cases first and a frozen cross-engine evaluation on the common supported subset. Publish oracle disagreements as unresolved with their denominator. A threshold that depends on a FrankenSim-specific outcome also needs a separately reported referee result.

The incumbent arm is the strongest measured complete pipeline per supported brief, selected on a separate tuning set. Start with a morphology-search baseline built from RoboGrammar or a Co-design Gym method, an equally budgeted agent loop in the style of RoboMoRe, native MJX or MuJoCo Warp and Newton/Isaac or Genesis training where supported, and Drake planning where it is competitive. MuJoCo, MJX and MJWarp are related arms with different strengths. Use paired trials on the same Linux configuration, one and two 4090s separately. Report the M5 Ultra path with a separately stated support matrix. Record cold-start and amortized times separately, charging shared pretraining, scene compilation and indexes over the number of delivered tasks. Run the lightest source-supported incumbent first when commissioning an individual gate. Public benchmark numbers establish candidates for the bake-off. They give no denominator for this product claim. ## 1. Compile engineering requirements into morphology search

**Build.** In §12, add typed holes to a bounded `frob-design` term and compile a task brief into predicates in `frob-analyze`. A mount hole carries a frame and its legal joint subgroup and port signature. A contact attachment carries a conservative wrench envelope. Battery, thermal, payload and geometric requirements are typed quantities with explicit tolerances. The agent selects the catalog, allowed constructors and task. In §22.6, `frob-opt` fills holes with best-first branch-and-bound, ordering by expected utility but pruning only with a checked bound over *every* legal completion of the node. An optimistic upper capability bound can rule out a required ramp drawbar force. A conservative lower resource bound can rule out mass. A possible candidate remains a candidate until control and rollout establish performance.

Store canonical term structure in an interned arena of opcodes and child ranges with interned card IDs. Maintain per-node sparse outward-rounded interval boxes and nondominated resource summaries. Collapse mirror and radial duplicates only for symmetry actions that preserve the catalog, the task and every relevant constraint. Use exact rational arithmetic for simple linear inequalities and interval leaves for quantities involving geometry, with witnesses referencing their input hashes. Prune dominated partial nodes only under a recorded monotonicity argument over the same completion interface and admissible domain. For nonmonotone torque or contact cases, derive a separate necessary bound or leave them alive. `frob-model` emits the same resolved model for the existing CPU and GPU tiers. The first slice uses no added GPU code.

The agent runs `frob design solve --task … --catalog … --budget …` and sees a small feasible frontier, best remaining bounds and the assumptions that excluded a family. It can widen a payload, part or topology bound and resume from the surviving summaries. When the budget ends, the system can report the explored region and the unresolved holes. The result saves the agent from manually diagnosing the same mass spiral or geometric incompatibility across related designs and makes the search space legible.

**Lever and competition.** Seek 10× fewer referee-equivalent candidate evaluations per accepted result and report search overhead and wall time. Compare against RoboGrammar-style grammar search, a Co-design Gym search method, and the same external agent with the same part catalog, static checks, as much symmetry deduplication as is valid, and cheap rejected-design filters. RoboGrammar already uses graphs and learned search guidance. Censi already gives mathematical co-design semantics. RoboMoRe already closes an LLM morphology/reward loop. The inference is that obtaining compositional cuts *over unexplored completions*, proof of coverage, and inexpensive resumption across requirement edits requires a shared typed constraint representation. A wrapper with such a representation is a legitimate competitor. A wrapper that calls the simulator on resolved designs plus static lints pays for most independent submissions. [RoboGrammar](https://people.csail.mit.edu/jiex/papers/robogrammar/index.html) · [Mathematical theory of co-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08055) · [Co-design Gym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02366) · [RoboMoRe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00276)

**First gate.** Before training or GPU work, exhaust a frozen 1,024-leaf catalog of wheeled and wheel-leg tree variants for two tasks, including planted asymmetric payload and nonmonotone actuator interactions. Use exact or conservatively checked leaf predicates and independent witness checking. Falsify on the first feasible leaf rejected as infeasible or the first missing nondominated frontier point. Kill the acceleration lane if cached bounds cannot save at least 70% of total search evaluation cost in exact leaf-equivalent units on the blinded second task. Pass here permits a larger M3–M4 rollout bake-off only. Fallback is the plan’s agent-driven typed edits with sound leaf checks. Unqualified branch scores may order suggestions. High confidence the tiny gate is cheap and useful. Medium confidence useful monotone cuts survive richer morphologies.

**Foundation boundary.** The component trait and typed effort/flow data are useful port inputs, and `fs-mbd` evaluates articulated trees. `fs-ivl` and FrankenSymPy can check bounded local inequalities, identities and small algebraic subproblems. The partial-term compiler, completion semantics, antichain search and pruning checker are new. Use Lean only for small generic combinator lemmas whose dependency closures the independent checker accepts. Source notes A, B and D.

## 2. Transfer controllers across typed edits

In §18 and §19, factor a controller into a task-conditioned coordinator, shared role-local blocks and an actuator-constrained residual. At a known correspondence, express errors in the successor body frame with SE(3) adjoint transport for twists and the dual map for wrenches. For limbs that share a valid symmetry action, reuse the matching block including sign and frame changes. Use normalized features for lengths, timing and effort only within their declared scope. Seed new roles from nearest supported modules. Synthesize the new kinematics, stance allocator and driver limits from the child model. Reset unmatched recurrent state and remap phase only when a compatible map is defined. Start with stationary edits to length, gear ratio and mounting. A mirrored pair, removed limb or wheel-to-foot swap needs its own gate.

Store structural mappings as CSR adjacency lists and indexed tables of role IDs, small frame transforms and representation blocks. Bucket inference by role and execution shape, with structure-of-arrays state and shared weights. Keep donor training provenance and per-edit validity in the checkpoint manifest. Select a donor using prior evidence on held-out edit classes, then run a short challenge against model-based synthesis, a generic graph policy and a same-morphology warm start when applicable. Spend residual adaptation only where failure margins improve. An agent gets a working policy quickly for local edits, a breakdown of reused roles and the measured effect of changed roles on the challenge. It can try several part changes inside the same budget.

Universal and modular controllers already transfer across morphologies. The additional bet is that the explicit edit gives an auditable correspondence and lets the system preserve relevant behavior or reuse only affected blocks across a real engineering lineage. ## 3. Target referee work at the decision with randomized audits

**Build.** Move part of §14.5’s promotion job inside §22.4’s paired decision procedure. Freeze a decision question, a bounded referee outcome and a target scene law. For pair i, draw a fresh paired scenario. Compute the cheap tier’s contrast and select a clipped prediction `m_i` using only information available before revealing the referee outcome. Log an audit probability `p_i` with a positive floor before drawing an independent Bernoulli `A_i`. Run both referee arms only on audit. The observation is `Z_i = m_i + A_i * (Y_i - m_i) / p_i`, where `Y_i` is the paired referee contrast on that scenario. Conditional on the scenario and predictions, the audit expectation is `Y_i`. Wrong cheap models inflate uncertainty but preserve that expectation when the seed design, positivity and outcome protocol hold. A predictable bounded-increment confidence sequence for the target contrast can then drive stopping. A predeclared indifference margin determines the outcome. A ranking model can offer likely next tests without controlling their validity.

Store a columnar pair/event table in `frob-arena/chronicle` with scenario and design identities, predictor version, tier outcome, propensity, independent stream key, audit result, evaluation cost and evidence-state update. Allocate the audit before seeing either referee output. Persist the probability and pair order before dispatch, and commit statistical updates in logical order. Retries retain the same audit assignment. For a missing audited outcome, either assign a predeclared bounded failure score if the estimand is operational and that failure belongs to it, or suspend the verdict. For risk constraints use separate bounded failure indicators with simultaneous intervals under a prespecified error allocation. Fix candidates within a confirmation comparison. A new challenger chosen adaptively needs fresh confirmation seeds and its own prespecified error spend. This gives an agent an explicit decision budget and a reason why the next check is useful, including when a novel contact regime warrants spending more on the referee.

**Lever and competition.** Compare against fixed escalation, paired referee-only confidence sequences and multi-fidelity BO or classical control variates with the same predictors, candidate archive and audit budget. Target 10× fewer referee pairs and at least 3× lower end-to-end decision time on the high-agreement strata, at matched stopping error, indifference margin and resolution. If a batch pair costs 0.02 referee pairs, mean audit work costs 0.05 and scheduling/transfer costs 0.01, per-row cost is 0.08 of a referee pair. A 10× decision win then permits only 1.25× as many rows as referee-only. The inverse weights can easily break that budget. Multi-fidelity control variates and anytime inference are established methods. The product bet is that structurally matched seeds, stable replay and live discrepancy data from two tiers make the residual small enough to pay off for changing robot designs. A wrapper with a fully aligned dual-fidelity adapter and the same statistical protocol is a credible rival. [Multi-fidelity control variates in robot learning](https://xinjie-liu.github.io/mfpg-rl/) · [Off-policy confidence sequences](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/karampatziakis21a.html)

**First gate.** Capture or construct a small, paired source table at M2. Freeze a blinded suffix containing a near tie, a genuinely superior robot, a decision reversal between tiers and a high-disagreement slip stratum. Replay online assignment over fixed null simulations and the blinded suffix, with uncensored outcomes available only to the harness. Any stopping-error defect or acceptance of the planted reversal kills the decision rule. If the high-agreement cases fail to beat paired referee-only by 2× in measured or auditable cost after even a favorable offline allocation, drop adaptive auditing from the critical path. Keep paired referee racing and use cheap outcomes only to schedule provisional work. High confidence in the correction under its stated assumptions. Medium-low confidence in 10× because low propensities and rare tail failures drive the range and sample size.

**Foundation boundary.** `fs-rand` supplies deterministic independent streams. `fs-uq` has fixed control-variate and multilevel pieces, `fs-eproc` has betting and paired comparisons, and `fs-voi` has a finite one-measurement Gaussian knowledge-gradient score. Use the latter only for acquisition ordering. Verify the new conditionally randomized, bounded update against its own derivation and null controls. No existing sibling primitive supplies the full stopping and audit protocol. Source note C.

## 4. Compile reusable route feasibility evidence

**Build.** Add a geometric contract to §16’s `ScenePack` and the relevant analyses in §22.2. Build a hierarchical graph of doorway, turn, ramp, threshold, foothold and docking transitions. Each edge has explicitly bounded free space, height and slope intervals and an uncertainty source such as scan registration, unobserved space or fixture state. Motion primitives use SE(2) for planar bases and SE(3) charts for bounded stair or posture envelopes. Evaluate a family by interval-enclosing both the composed pose of its conservative swept hull and the distance field along candidate transitions. Use one-sided geometry margins. For clearance, inflate the moving envelope and use distance lower bounds that include frame, field and interpolation error. For impossibility, construct an optimistic graph that over-approximates admissible continuous moves using necessary occupancy and reach constraints. A disconnect supports a negative result only if coverage and inclusion were checked for that declared motion class. Otherwise return an unresolved route. Doors, moving people, terrain deformation and unobserved scan regions can force abstention. A clear route is scoped to geometry and kinematic constraints. Dynamic feasibility still needs controller and rollout evidence.

Use Morton-indexed bricks with outward-rounded distance bounds, a compact CSR route graph and per-edge interval summaries keyed by geometry, uncertainty policy, posture envelope and adapter version. Store small cleared transition witnesses and disconnected-cut certificates. Cache clearance by verified envelope inclusion so a smaller compatible footprint or a near-neighbor edit can reuse work. Keep witnesses private when the scene is private. In early M3, run a CPU index for one warehouse and a galley with doors fixed at declared angles. Then add a low-clearance terrain profile with anchored uncertainty. `frob scene fit --family … --route …` should identify the exact corner, transition or unresolved scan patch and return a footprint limit or a candidate path the agent can simulate.

**Lever and competition.** Target 10× lower amortized time for 64 design/route queries over related footprints per scene while matching the standalone pipeline’s checked-decision rate. Publish first-query time and break-even count. Compare against precompiled SDFs, cached per-footprint clearance and Drake’s reusable configuration-space regions and GCS path optimization. Reuse across changed footprints and chart families, with formal scope on what a negative or positive result means, is the differentiator. A Drake-based application could implement the same index. Cheap per-pose signed-distance wrappers do not in themselves provide verified coverage, cross-design reuse or separation between blocked and unknown. [Drake geometry optimization](https://drake.mit.edu/pydrake/pydrake.geometry.optimization.html) · [Drake GCS trajectory optimization](https://drake.mit.edu/doxygen_cxx/classdrake_1_1planning_1_1trajectory__optimization_1_1_gcs_trajectory_optimization.html)

Falsify with one incorrectly cleared or excluded route in the supported set. Require the index to break even by 16 near-neighbor queries on the held-out aisle, with comparable decisive coverage at matched error bounds. Keep the scene distance field and the route heuristic when certificates are unsound or mostly unresolved. High confidence for fixed fixtures, short motion primitives and simple footprints. Lower confidence for broad legged mode coverage.

**Foundation boundary.** `fs-rep-sdf` has tiled, adaptive and narrow-band fields. `fs-motion::clearance` exposes chart separation evidence and explicit error terms. `fs-ivl` supplies interval operations and `fs-ga` group transforms. ## 5. Rank repairs by actual replay interventions

**Build.** Replace §23.5’s earliest-anomaly heuristic as a decision-maker with controlled experiments. Keep the heuristic to nominate candidates. Represent a failure as a short, content-addressed trace of active contacts, guards, efforts, saturations and discriminating state. In `frob-chronicle`, retain periodic complete checkpoints plus copy-on-write branch descriptors containing a parent checkpoint, intervention, validity epoch, paired random stream addresses and metrics. In `frob-analyze/opt`, define specific interventions on driver limits, friction cards, gait timing, command inputs and permitted design parameters. Separate a temporary intervention applied at a checkpoint from a permanent edit evaluated from reset. Write down the causal question for each experiment. A change in a diagnostic branch can show that one specified perturbation changes the outcome in that simulated state. Acceptance of a hardware edit needs the full rollout with the edited model.

Reuse a prefix only when the pre-intervention model, actions, scheduling and random streams are identical by construction or by a checked dependency rule. Mass, inertia, controller memory and contacts are global effects for the initial implementation. Any permanent edit touching them starts from reset, with the mapped or re-synthesized controller qualification kept explicit. Bind snapshots to all solver, actuator, estimator, sensor and policy memory. Clear warm starts after a changed intervention when required by the declared transition, and measure the result against a cold replay of that very transition. Randomize and search on diagnostic seeds, then hold out seeds and compare the baseline, the changed design with compatible fixed control, and the changed design with resynthesis. Add targeted paired combinations if interventions interact. Keep the experiment’s effect, horizon, controller choice and unresolved alternatives in the `explain` envelope. An agent sees a ranked edit with the observed difference and can request the matched replay witness.

**Lever and competition.** Target 10× lower time to the first confirmed repair for late failures with many admissible diagnostic interventions, compared with full-episode paired branching and the same sequential race, candidate edits and final validation. Include a stronger arm that saves native checkpoints and uses manually defined patch adapters. Time manual adapter work on previously unseen edit classes as part of brief intake, and report recurring decisions with those adapters already present. If full replay cost is one unit, 0.95 is a reusable prefix and branch overhead is 0.02, the branch itself costs 0.07 units. Independent confirmation, edit compilations and branches that require reset all lower the overall gain. Benchmark those separately. MuJoCo already exposes restorable simulation state. Counterfactual replay is established. The inference is that typed dependencies, semantic controls and explicit tested intervention classes make precise replay experimentation reusable across designs. A state saver alone cannot carry controller semantics across a robot edit or establish when its old prefix is interchangeable. A sufficiently capable incumbent arm can compete via native state restoration and a custom typed patch adapter. [MuJoCo state and stepping](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/programming/simulation.html) · [Counterfactual data augmentation with locally factored dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.02863)

**First gate.** Seed four cases with an early symptom distinct from the planted cause, such as torque saturation after low-friction onset or a temperature cutout followed by tracking loss. Include one edit whose mass change invalidates its old prefix and one unchanged prefix that *must* reproduce. Compare against uncached paired full-run ablations, native checkpointed ablations and the plan’s anomaly order. Kill the affected reuse class on the first divergent unchanged prefix or mislabelled permanent-edit claim. Defer the technique if it fails to put a real fix in the top three for at least three of four planted cases or cut diagnostic time by at least 2× against full replay on the late-failure cases, or if the checkpointed arm matches typed replay on the held-out edits. Keep full paired reruns and explanatory telemetry. High confidence that the narrower causal question is useful. Medium confidence on a 10× workflow because validation and reset-heavy edits dominate some briefs.

**Foundation boundary.** `fs-mbd` has articulated stepping over caller-generated contacts. `fs-contact` supplies relevant solver and collision primitives, `fs-rand` supplies counter-addressed streams and `fs-eproc` supplies pairwise testing. Neither an atomic full-world snapshot nor its edit semantics comes from those primitives. This proposal depends on the M2 Chronicle replay contract and extends it with branch admission and outcome receipts. Source notes A and C.

## Sequence and cuts
