## How to read the tables

- **Source passes** gives the pass tags, then in brackets the passes' own provisional claim IDs. Finer locators (table rows, sections, first or second half of a split claim) are in the ledger field `source_locators`: `P02-row-a` is row a of the P02 checklist, `P02-S3-N1` an item of its section 3, `P05-A3` item 3 of P05's section A, `P01-5a` the first half of P01-5.
- **Evidence** lists E-numbers from `MOR_CASE.md`, then sources exactly as the pass gave them (`https://` dropped here; the ledger keeps the full strings), then plan anchors with line numbers the normalizer located in the target document. An asterisk marks an E-number the normalizer attached because the pass cited the same document (the plan, or an identical URL) without the E-number; these are listed per claim in `evidence_refs_added_by_normalizer`. Square brackets carry the passes' own provenance markers: † quotes relayed by a summarizing fetch (P02), † citations from memory (P03), figures read through a summarizing fetch (all of P01), abstract-only or secondary-source readings (P05), and numbers computed inside a pass.
- **Material?** is the normalizer's classification (would believing the claim change a commitment, a number or a novelty claim in the plan) and the ledger status.
- **What the audit must check** is the ledger field `audit_request`: the source to re-read, the arithmetic to redo, the file to inspect, and the result that would refute the claim.
