## New evidence the passes did not have

These came out of re-reading the primary sources. They are recorded inside the corrected statements in the tables below and in the JSONL, and are listed here because several claims lean on them. Register the ones the lead keeps as E018 onward.

1. **Playground Fig. 13** (arXiv 2502.08844, page 21). The Go1 flat joystick run finishes at about 410 s on one RTX 4090 and about 325 s on two, with a compile region of roughly 45 s inside those times; Appendix B.4 says device topology makes no material difference for this task. Consequences: the incumbent costs about 410 GPU-s, not 600 (C015, C070); the published minutes already include JIT (C004, C006).
2. **Debate2Create versions.** The '4 minutes and $0.50 per round' figure is in v2 only, is modelled from token counts, and was removed in v3 of 23 June 2026 (C001, C006).
3. **mjlab nightly benchmark** (`https://mujocolab.github.io/mjlab/nightly/`, RTX 5090, 4,096 envs, updated 2026-11-21). Go1 velocity: physics-only 1.20M env-steps/s (4.8M physics steps/s), full environment step 347k env-steps/s, 71% non-physics overhead; G1 tracking: 873k, 256k, 71%. A published component measurement on the late-2026 stack exists (C010), and on that stack physics is under a third of the environment step (C003).
4. **MuJoCo Warp documentation.** 'Batched Model Fields' and 'Per-world assets enable heterogeneous worlds': per-world model parameters are already an incumbent capability (C075, C078, C079). 'Is MJWarp on GPU deterministic? No ... Set device to CPU': same kernels on CPU, no GPU determinism (C066).
5. **Isaac Lab default G1 rough run**: 3,000 iterations x 24 steps x 4,096 envs at the published 82,000 FPS is about 1 h on one RTX 4090 (derived). Incumbent training denominators therefore span about 5 min to about 1 h by task and stack (C014, C071).
6. **FrankenLean S26 per-module result**: `Mathlib.Order.FixedPoints` and `Mathlib.Order.OmegaCompletePartialOrder` are accepted; the Lie-group, matrix-exponential, analytic-quaternion and representation-theory modules are blocked (C084).

## What the plan should do about the verified claims in this batch

Each action follows directly from a VERIFIED or PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED claim. Nothing here depends on a claim from another batch.

1. **Fix the units in §28.1** (C068). Define world-step and env-step in Appendix A, state the decimation beside each row, and put the two quadruped rows in one unit. As written the training target implies twice the physics-only target on the same card.
2. **Re-base the §28.1 targets on published incumbent figures** (C004, C069, C070). Replace 'No absolute RTX 4090 figure ... was found' with the Playground Fig. 13 one-card run (about 410 s) and note the mjlab nightly figures. Then say for each row whether it is a tolerance or an ambition. GATE-CORTEX-05 at 15 min is about 2.2x slower than the published run on the same card, the batch-cpu row is 7 to 11x below published MuJoCo C per core, and the humanoid GPU row is below published CPU totals.
3. **Correct the incumbent denominators in the narrative** (C071). NS-01's 'six hours', the 'hours later' of §1.1 and the 'hours long' of §18.1 should read minutes to about an hour, with sources, so that the plan agrees with its own §28.1.
4. **Narrow the GPU and Design rows of §1.1** (C066, C067). Keep only what incumbents document as absent: bitwise replay of a GPU world on a CPU, determinism of MuJoCo Warp on GPU, and a topology change inside a running GPU batch. Drop 'the CPU version is a different code base' and 'morphology is fixed before the simulator starts'.
5. **Add an end-to-end metric with an incumbent arm** (C011, C072, C077). Put a new bet in §2, an end-to-end row in §28.1 and the incumbent harness in the oracle table of §30.2 as an out-of-process tool. Name the adjudicating simulator, freeze and publish the incumbent tool layer before any run, define BET-10's 'design benchmark', and give GATE-ATEL-03 an effect size.
6. **Measure the incumbent before committing to mechanisms** (C010, C005). At M0-M1, time the incumbent loop on trj by phase, starting with what needs no agent: cold start, a topology edit to first step, and training to threshold on one and two cards. Seed the ledger with the published component figures listed above.
7. **Do not adopt the decision rule or the kill gate as drafted** (C012, C013). The rule confirms a true 10x in at most 18% of campaigns; re-specify it on a pooled ratio with at least ten seeds per arm and a censoring rule. Say what a measured ratio between 3 and 10 means, and set the partial gate at M3 (legged and wheeled briefs) and the full gate at M4.
8. **State the Amdahl limits where the plan argues for speed** (C003, C006, C014, C015, C078). On quadruped briefs, simulator speed alone, the NS-06 cycle alone (3.0 to 3.5x) and compute per decision (ceiling about 10.8x at f = 0 against a one-card incumbent) do not reach tenfold. The plan should name the additional factor it is betting on (designs decided per turn, fewer turns to a qualified design, or a cheaper training rung) and the f it needs.
9. **Write down per-world design parameters and contact capacities** (C075, C076, C079). Add one sentence to §14.3 allowing worlds in a batch to carry different parameter values within one topology, record it as parity with MuJoCo Warp, restate NS-06 as designs decided per hour, and give numeric contact and row capacities for each §28.1 workload.
10. **Make the two unguarded rungs visible** (C073, C074). State in §18.6 that no gate tests whether the baseline-controller rung ranks designs as the trained rung does, and in §19.3 that every trained design starts from the baseline-controller warm start with no reuse across designs. Whether to add a rank-agreement gate or policy reuse is decided by claims in other batches.
11. **Add a referee coverage figure at M2 exit** (C080): the share of standard-battery scenarios the referee completes under its 21-contact cap, and the wall-clock of a promotion.
12. **Add a pre-M5 comparison with published figures, correctly labelled** (C081, C082). At M3 compare at least one simulated quantity per reference robot with a published figure under the Parity label, and report the share of card fields marked `generic` or `assumed` in any design used for a benchmark claim.
13. **Cite sources by version** (C001, C007). Cite Debate2Create v2 for the LLM minutes and dollars or drop them; describe the 40 GPU-hours as per 5-round run.
