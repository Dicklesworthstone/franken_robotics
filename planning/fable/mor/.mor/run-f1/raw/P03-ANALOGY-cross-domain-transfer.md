## Finding in brief

Three relational structures account for the order-of-magnitude gains in the seven source fields.

- **R1. Recomputation proportional to the edit**, not to the whole design: engineering change orders in chip design, incremental builds, relative free-energy calculations. Precondition: an edit influences only part of the result.
- **R2. A cheap estimator corrected or filtered by a trusted expensive one**: multi-fidelity funnels, multifidelity Monte Carlo, learned surrogates with verification. Precondition: a cost ratio of 10² or more, and the two must rank candidates alike.
- **R3. Samples steered toward the informative region**: falsification, splitting, sign-off corners, save-state search. Precondition: the event of interest is rare or the informative region is small.

The plan (E015) already has R1 at model compilation and R2 as a tier ladder. It lacks R1 at policy acquisition, an R2 estimator (promotion is pass or fail), any test that adjacent rungs of its ladder rank designs alike, and R3 altogether. No transferred mechanism has published evidence of a tenfold end-to-end gain in robot design. The nearest published figure for the highest-leverage one is about 3×.

Citations marked † are from memory and were not re-fetched in this pass. The others were checked on 2026-11-21.

## Ranking by expected end-to-end leverage

Probabilities are judgments, not measurements.

2. **P03-3, rung agreement.** Not a speedup. It decides whether the plan's seconds-long loop produces rankings at all. P(τ ≥ 0.6) ≈ 0.45. Highest information per GPU-hour.

4. **P03-4.** 3 to 30× fewer referee episodes on energy-type metrics, P ≈ 0.6; little for fall rates, P ≈ 0.2.
5. **P03-7.** Up to the number of continuous parameters, on one sub-problem. P ≈ 0.35.
6. **P03-8.** 1 to 3× on sweep latency. P ≈ 0.5.
7. **Row 3 caches.** 1.2 to 2× on comparisons. P(works) ≈ 0.8.

**Confidence.** Medium on the screen (P03-1) and the rejections (P03-9). Low to medium on every numeric gain: none was measured here, and all depend on phase times that PQ1 has not yet supplied. The levers act on different phases, so partial gains could compound, but this pass found no evidence that any single transfer delivers tenfold.
